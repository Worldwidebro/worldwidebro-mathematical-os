# Worldwidebro Holdings: The Delegation Network

## Mission
Build a self-orchestrating network of mutually-supporting ventures where work flows between sectors, capturing arbitrage at every handoff.

## Vision
A delegation-powered economy where AI agents route opportunities across verticals, creating compounding value through cross-sector collaboration.

## Core Thesis
We don't build 712 separate businesses. We build a **delegation network** where:
- Staffing finds labor → Construction uses it → Real Estate manages the properties → Finance funds the deals
- Each handoff captures margin
- Work self-generates through network effects
- Capital flows to the highest-arbitrage opportunities

## How We Make Money
1. **Labor Arbitrage** (STA): Margin on contractor placement (30-40%)
2. **Project Arbitrage** (CON): Margin on project execution + staffing markup (25-35%)
3. **Data Arbitrage** (RE): Margin on property intelligence + prop-tech services (20-30%)
4. **Capital Arbitrage** (FIN): Spread on financing deals + advisory fees (15-25%)
5. **Delegation Fees**: Internal transfer pricing when ventures hand off work (5-10%)

## Key Metrics
- **Delegation Velocity**: Work items flowing between sectors per week (Target: 50+/week by Month 3)
- **Network Margin**: Total margin captured across all handoffs (Target: 35% blended)
- **Cross-Sector Revenue**: % of revenue from delegation (vs. direct sales) (Target: 60% by Month 6)
- **Placement Velocity**: Time from opportunity creation to work completion (Target: < 72 hours)

## Decision Principles
- **Network over silos**: Every venture must feed at least 2 other ventures
- **Arbitrage first**: Prioritize work that captures margin at handoff points
- **Speed to delegation**: If work can be delegated, delegate within 24 hours
- **Data over intuition**: All delegation decisions backed by Knowledge Graph analytics

## The 5 Foundation Verticals (Launch First)
1. **STA (Staffing)**: Pure labor arbitrage, lowest capital, powers everyone
2. **CON (Construction)**: Project-based revenue, creates jobs for STA, feeds RE
3. **RE (Real Estate)**: Data arbitrage, property management, feeds FIN
4. **FIN (Finance)**: Capital arbitrage, deal sourcing from CON/RE/TECH
5. **OPS (Services/Ops)**: Admin arbitrage, back-office for all ventures

## What We Skip (For Now)
- Healthcare, Manufacturing, Energy (high capital, regulatory burden)
- Focus on low-cap, high-delegation, immediate arbitrage verticals
