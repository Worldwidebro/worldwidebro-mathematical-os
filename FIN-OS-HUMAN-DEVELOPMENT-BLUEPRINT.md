# FIN-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Finance Operating System
**Sector Focus:** Financial services, wealth management, accounting
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: FIN-Specific Stressors

- Regulatory/compliance pressure (high stakes)
- Client money management (trust responsibility)
- Market volatility impact (emotional)
- Constant learning (tax law, market changes)
- Ethical dilemmas (conflicts of interest)

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

- 20+ active client accounts
- Tax/legal advisors (compliance partners)
- 2-3 peer wealth advisors

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Client Asks for Tax Dodge**
- Low: Help them find loophole
- High: Explain law, recommend compliant approach

**Test 2: Market Downturn; Clients Panic**
- Low: Sell everything, protect commission
- High: Educate, hold long-term strategy

**Test 3: Conflict of Interest (Your Product vs. Client's Need)**
- Low: Sell your product anyway
- High: Recommend what's best for client

**Test 4: Client Accuses You of Mismanagement**
- Low: Blame the market, get defensive
- High: Review honestly, acknowledge mistakes

**Test 5: Opportunity to Move Client Money Quietly**
- Low: Move it, don't tell them
- High: Communicate fully, get approval

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (steward wealth responsibly)
- L2-5: Calm under market pressure; sound judgment
- L6: Responsive; consistent communication
- L7: Deep client relationships; peers refer to you
- L8: Innovates strategy; educates clients
- L9: Ethical under pressure; transparent
- L10: Building client wealth legacy; mentoring advisors
