## Flags for follow-up
1. **Candles and subscription tiers are priced above what any researched competitor charges.** This is fine as a luxury-positioning bet, but it's not backed by a comparable — it's a judgment call, not market-validated. Recommend either (a) explicitly justifying the premium in copy (materials, exclusivity), or (b) pricing closer to the researched ceiling until the brand has proof points (reviews, press) to support the premium.
2. **Vibrator/lingerie categories have real outliers** (LELO gold $3,900, La Perla collab $705) that are not standing catalog prices — don't anchor off these.
3. Couples-toy and lubricant pricing in the existing mockup ($199 range would fit Luna Duo; $62 Touch Oil) already land in a defensible position relative to real competitors — no change needed there.
