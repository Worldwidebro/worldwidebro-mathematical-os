# OPS-001 Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) Registration Guide

**Venture:** OPS-001 Staffing Operations  
**Objective:** Register 74 qualified staffing prospects for federal WOTC benefits  
**Value:** $1,200–$9,600 tax credit per employee (federal)  
**Deadline:** Register within 28 days of hire date  
**Process Portal:** wotc.ncworks.gov (NC Works, North Carolina)  

---

## What Is WOTC?

The **Work Opportunity Tax Credit** is a federal tax incentive for employers who hire workers from targeted groups facing barriers to employment.

**Key Facts:**
- Federal tax credit: $1,200–$9,600 per employee
- Valid for 1 year from hire date
- Must register within 28 days of hire date
- No cost to employer; automatic IRS deduction
- North Carolina administered via NC Works Portal (wotc.ncworks.gov)

**Eligible Target Groups** (pick the ONE that matches your prospect):

| Group | Description | Credit |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **TANF Recipient** | Received TANF (Temporary Assistance for Needy Families) in past 2 years | $2,400 |
| **SNAP/Food Stamps** | Currently receives SNAP benefits | $2,400 |
| **SSI/Disability** | Receives Supplemental Security Income (SSI) | $3,600 |
| **Long-Term Unemployed** | Unemployed 27+ weeks; in Unemployment Insurance (UI) program | $2,400 |
| **Ex-Felon** | Convicted felon; hired within 1 year of conviction or release | $2,400 |
| **Disabled Veteran** | Disabled veteran receiving VA disability compensation | $2,400 |
| **Vocational Rehab** | Received VR services or SSA benefits | $1,800 |
| **Summer Youth** | Age 16–17; employed May 1–Sept 15 | $1,200 |

## 5-Step Registration Process

### Step 1: Classify & Document (Days 1–5)

For each of your 74 prospects, verify they fit ONE target group above. Gather evidence:

**Required per prospect:**
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Last 4 digits of SSN
- Target group classification
- Evidence document (letter from DHHS, UI, SSA, DOL, etc.)

**Evidence sources:**
- TANF: Award letter from DHHS
- SNAP: Approval letter or EBT statement
- SSI: Award letter from Social Security Admin
- UI: Unemployment benefit statement (ncesc.com)
- Ex-Felon: Criminal record + court docs
- Disabled Veteran: VA disability award letter
- VR Services: Closure letter from NC DVR
- Summer Youth: Birth certificate + hire date proof

**Action:** Use OPS-001-WOTC-PROSPECT-TEMPLATE.csv to track classification for all 74.

---

### Step 2: Create NC Works Account (Day 6)

1. Go to **wotc.ncworks.gov**
2. Click **Register as Employer**
3. Enter:
   - Business name (Worldwidebro Holdings)
   - Federal EIN (apply at IRS.gov if you don't have one)
   - Business address (Charlotte, NC)
   - Contact email
4. Verify email link sent by NC Works
5. Login and save credentials

---

### Step 3: Register All 74 Prospects (Days 7–8)

**Via Portal Bulk Upload (Recommended):**
1. Download CSV template from wotc.ncworks.gov
2. Fill in all 74 rows using OPS-001-WOTC-PROSPECT-TEMPLATE.csv
3. Upload file → System batch-creates all registrations
4. Verify no errors in system

**Per-Prospect Fields Required:**
- First Name, Last Name, DOB (MM/DD/YYYY)
- SSN (Last 4 digits)
- Hire Date (MM/DD/YYYY; must be within 28 days of today)
- Job Title (e.g., "Staffing Coordinator")
- Wage/Salary (hourly or annual)
- Employment Type (Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal)
- Target Group (select from list)
- Evidence Type (TANF Award Letter, etc.)
- Employer Certification (check "Yes — I have reviewed eligibility")

---

### Step 4: Submit to IRS & Track (Day 9 + 30–90 days)

1. Click **Submit Registration Batch** in portal
2. System validates; if clean, forwards to IRS within 24 hours
3. NC Works processes (1–2 business days)
4. IRS reviews (30–90 days)
5. Check portal for status updates:
   - "Pending IRS Review" (processing)
   - "Approved" (credit available)
   - "Denied" (ineligible; can appeal)
   - "Need More Info" (you respond with docs)

**Success Rate:** Typically 60–80% approval rate if documentation is complete.

---

### Step 5: Claim Tax Credit on Your Return (After Approval)

Once approved by IRS, claim the credit:

**On Quarterly Payroll Taxes (Form 941):**
- File Form 941 next quarterly return after approval
- Line 11d: Enter certified credits
- Credit reduces your federal payroll tax liability

**On Annual Tax Return:**
- File Form 8845 (Disabled Employees) or Form 5884-C (WOTC)
- Report all certified credits from the year
- Claim deduction on business return (1040, 1120, etc.)

**Example:**
```
Prospect: John Smith (TANF Recipient)
Certified by IRS: Yes
Tax Credit: $2,400

Your quarterly payroll tax (before credit): $5,000
Minus WOTC credit: -$2,400
Net tax due: $2,600
```

## Compliance Notes

- **Keep evidence:** Retain TANF letters, UI statements, ID copies for 7 years (IRS audit)
- **Verify SSN:** Mismatched SSN/name = verification fails
- **Hire date accuracy:** Must match payroll records
- **Target group match:** Assigned group must match evidence
- **If denied:** Appeal through portal within 30 days
