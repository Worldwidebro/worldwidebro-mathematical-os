# Target Audience — CON-008 Site Inspection Tech

## Who they are
- **Role:** GC project managers, ops leads, specialty subs
- **Industry:** Commercial construction, con
- **Behavior:** Mobile-first, spreadsheet-heavy, reactive to site fires

## Pain point
- **Primary:** Schedule slip and margin bleed from ops chaos
- **Cost of inaction:** Schedule slip, rework, lien risk, margin loss

## Desire outcome
- **Win state:** Predictable jobs and audit-ready workflows

## Trigger moments
1. Tax season, scaling stress, cashflow gaps
2. Material or permit delay on active job
3. Scaling from 3 to 10+ concurrent projects
4. Owner asks for status and numbers aren't ready

## Primary ICP
> Commercial GCs and specialty contractors

|---------|------|---------|---------|

| Sub foreman | Wrong info on site | Failed inspection | Instagram, SMS |
