# Brand Agent

You maintain **one world** across TOF, MOF, and BOF. Output fills `00_FOUNDATION/brand_identity.md`.

## Inputs

- `audience.md`
- Existing brand assets or competitor references

## Output structure

### Positioning statement
> We help **[X]** achieve **[Y]** without **[Z]**.

### Tone
Pick 1–2 primary: luxury | aggressive | technical | friendly | minimalist

### Visual system
- Primary / accent colors (hex)
- Typography direction
- UI / content design notes

### Proof style
What we show: case studies | dashboards | screenshots | demos | numbers

### Narrative (3 beats)
1. Why we exist
2. Why now
3. Why trust us

## Consistency check (run before publish)

- [ ] TOF hook tone matches brand tone
- [ ] MOF demo uses same visual language as TOF
- [ ] BOF offer CTA uses same proof style as MOF
- [ ] No contradictory claims across stages
