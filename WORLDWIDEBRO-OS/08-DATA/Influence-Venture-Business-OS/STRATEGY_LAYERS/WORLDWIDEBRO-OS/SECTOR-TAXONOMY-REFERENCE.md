## 7 Economic Layers

All 31 sectors organized by function in the ecosystem flow:

### Layer 1: CAPITAL
Provides money and financial instruments.
- **FIN** (Finance & Investments)
- **FI** (FinTech)
- **FS** (Financial Services)

### Layer 2: INFRASTRUCTURE
Powers everything else.
- **TECH** (Technology)
- **ST** (Software Tools)
- **OPS** (Operations)
- **LT** (Logistics & Transportation)
- **SEC** (Security)
- **DATA** (Data & Intelligence)

### Layer 3: ASSETS
Creates tangible value.
- **RE** (Real Estate)
- **CON** (Construction)
- **EN** (Energy)
- **MFG** (Manufacturing)
- **AG** (Agriculture)

### Layer 4: DISTRIBUTION
Creates attention and trust.
- **MC** (Marketing & Communications)
- **MEDIA** (Media)
- **PROFILE** (Personal Brands)
- **COM** (Communities & Memberships)

### Layer 5: PRODUCTS & SERVICES
Serves customers directly.
- **COMM** (Commerce)
- **EC** (E-Commerce)
- **BW** (Beauty & Wellness)
- **FH** (Food & Hospitality)
- **EDU** (Education)
- **ET** (EdTech)
- **HC** (Healthcare)
- **PS** (Professional Services)
- **SPEC** (Special Ventures)

### Layer 6: GOVERNANCE
Rules, compliance, risk.
- **LG** (Legal & Governance)

### Layer 7: VENTURE CREATION
Creates new companies.
- **VS** (Venture Studio)

---

## Cross-Cutting Support

These sectors span multiple layers and enable all others:

- **HCAP** (Human Capital) — staffing, recruiting, talent for all sectors
- **EM** (AI / Emerging Media) — automates Products/Services + Distribution layers
- **SEC** (Security) — foundational to Infrastructure + all layers

## Ecosystem Flow

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CAPITAL (FIN, FI, FS)                                       │
│  Money & financial instruments flow in                      │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  INFRASTRUCTURE (TECH, ST, OPS, LT, SEC, DATA)              │
│  Systems that power all sectors                             │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ASSETS (RE, CON, EN, MFG, AG)                              │
│  Tangible resources created                                 │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PRODUCTS & SERVICES (COMM, EC, BW, FH, EDU, ET, HC, PS, SPEC)
│  Customer-facing offerings                                  │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DISTRIBUTION (MC, MEDIA, PROFILE, COM)                     │
│  Attention, trust, market reach                             │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CUSTOMERS                                                   │
│  Money flows back as revenue                                │
└────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
                    REINVESTMENT → back to CAPITAL
```

**Meanwhile:**
- **GOVERNANCE** (LG) oversees all layers
- **VENTURE STUDIO** (VS) creates new companies entering the flow
- **HCAP** staffs ventures across all layers
- **EM/AI** automates processes across all layers
