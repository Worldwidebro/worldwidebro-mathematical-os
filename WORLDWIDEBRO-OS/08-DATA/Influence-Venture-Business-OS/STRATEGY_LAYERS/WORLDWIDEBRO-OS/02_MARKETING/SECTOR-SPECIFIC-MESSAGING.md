## UNIVERSAL CLOSING FRAMEWORK (All Sectors)

### The 3-Question Close

```
[After discovery call]

Great. Let me confirm I understand:

1. Your main challenge is [pain point], and it's costing you [$ or time]?
2. If we could solve that in [timeline], you'd be interested?
3. Who besides you needs to approve a decision like this?

[Get answers]

Perfect. Here's what I propose: 
[Give specific proposal with price, timeline, ROI]

Do you want to move forward?
```

### The Objection Handler

**Objection: "It's too expensive"**
```
I hear you. Most customers felt the same way before they saw ROI.

Here's the math: You're spending [time/money] manually right now.
This cuts that by [50-70%], so it pays for itself in [2-4 weeks].

Can we do a 2-week trial to prove it?
```

**Objection: "We're already using [competitor]"**
```
Cool, who? [Listen]

Most people using [competitor] find they're missing [key feature/integration].
We're built specifically for [your use case].

Would it be worth 20 minutes to see the difference?
```

**Objection: "We need to think about it"**
```
Totally fair. Here's what I'll do:
I'll send you a demo video you can watch in 5 minutes.
You can forward it to your [finance/operations] person.
Let's reconnect Thursday and see if it makes sense.

Does Thursday at 2pm work?
```

## Call Execution Rules

**Rule 1: Best Time to Call**
- Construction: 7-9am or 3-4pm (not during job site hours)
- Beauty: 10am-12pm or after 5pm (not during client hours)
- Food: 2-3pm or 10-11am (not rush hours)
- Tech: 11am-1pm or 3-5pm (flexible, avoid standup times)

**Rule 2: Voicemail Strategy**
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from Worldwidebro Holdings. 
I was calling about [specific problem in their sector].

I've helped [number] companies like yours solve it. 
When you get a sec, can you call me back at [number]?

[Your name]."
```

**Rule 3: Call Frequency**
- Cold contact: 2-3 calls max, then switch to email
- Warm contact: 3 calls + email follow-up
- Hot lead (asked for demo): Call daily until booked

**Rule 4: After Each Call**
- Update status in ClickUp
- Log notes (what they said, next step, timeline)
- Schedule follow-up (email in 2 days, call in 1 week if no response)
- Note deal probability (0-100%)
