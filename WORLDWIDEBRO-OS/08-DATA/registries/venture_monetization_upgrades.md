## 1. Capabilities Audit & Asset Packaging

The following core capabilities of IZA OS are immediately available for packaging and commercialization:
*   **Ingestion & Data Supply:** Peer-to-peer file synchronization (Syncthing) and document OCR/automation (Stirling-PDF).
*   **Memory & Knowledge Graphs:** Neo4j Graph Queries (cypher-skill), PostgreSQL logging tables, and Qdrant vector databases.
*   **Workflow Automation:** n8n workflow engines, Twenty CRM pipelines, and webhook listeners.
*   **Developer & UI Cores:** Open Design system prototyping, TailwindCSS custom layouts, and gitnexus-cli scanning.

These capabilities are mapped below to a **3-Tier Monetization Upgrade Ladder** to drive fast, progressive cash flows:
1.  **Tier 1: Content & Digital Assets (Instant / Low-barrier):** PDF guides, templates, video tutorials, worksheets, and courses.
2.  **Tier 2: Scalable Services & Consultations (Medium-barrier):** Technical consultations, setup packages, configuration audits, and high-ticket masterclasses.
3.  **Tier 3: Systems & Recurring SaaS (High-barrier):** Sandboxed agents, customized SaaS subscriptions, automated pipelines, and hosted widgets.
