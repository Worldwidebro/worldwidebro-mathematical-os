# PART 4: YOUR 5 STORIES (STAR METHOD)

## The STAR Method

- **Situation**: What was the context?
- **Task**: What were you responsible for?
- **Action**: What did you do? (Use "I")
- **Result**: What happened? (Quantify if possible)

## Story 1: Solving a Technical Problem

**Question**: "Tell me about a technical challenge."

**Template**: "[System] was [problem]. I [analyzed], [identified root cause], [implemented fix], and [measured result]. Performance improved by [X%]."

Keep to 2-3 minutes. Use a real example.

## Story 2: Learning Something New

**Question**: "Tell me about learning a new technology."

**Template**: "I hadn't worked with [technology], but [project required it]. I [learned], [built prototype], [tested], and [delivered successfully]."

## Story 3: Ambiguous Requirements

**Question**: "What do you do when requirements are unclear?"

**Template**: "A client wanted [vague requirement]. I asked [questions], documented [assumptions], got [alignment], built [what was specified], and delivered [exactly right]."

## Story 4: Communication with Stakeholders

**Question**: "Tell me about working with different teams."

**Template**: "[Team A] wanted [X], [Team B] wanted [Y]. I [documented tradeoffs], [got alignment], we [decided together]. Result: [satisfied teams, on schedule]."

## Story 5: Automation and Efficiency

**Question**: "How do you improve processes?"

**Template**: "[Team] was [manually doing task] taking [time]. I [documented], [identified automation], [built it], [automated]. Now [reduced time]. That's [X hours saved]."

## Practice Tonight

- Write out 5 concrete stories (real examples)
- Read each aloud (2-3 min each)
- Practice telling naturally (not reading)
