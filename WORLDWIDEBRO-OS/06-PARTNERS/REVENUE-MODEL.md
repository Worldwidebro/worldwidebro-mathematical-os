# Partner Revenue Model & Reseller Program

## Commission Structure

| Tier | Annual Revenue | Commission % | Support | Co-marketing |
|------|---|---|---|---|
| Reseller | $0–50K | 30% | Email | $0 |
| Channel | $50K–250K | 35% | Slack+Weekly | $500/mo |
| Strategic | $250K+ | 40% | Executive | $2000/mo |

## Auto-Promotion

- $50K annual → Tier 2 (auto, next month)
- $250K annual → Tier 3 (auto, next month)
- Demotion if falls below threshold for 2 consecutive quarters

## Payout Process

**Monthly:**
1. Query Stripe for partner charges
2. Calculate commission (charge × tier_rate)
3. Subtract refunds/chargebacks
4. If >$100, queue for payout

**Execution:**
- Stripe Connect (automatic)
- Net 15 days
- 1099-NEC issued annually

## Contract Terms (MSA)

- Duration: 1 year, auto-renews
- Exit: 30-day notice, no penalty
- Non-compete: 6 months post-exit
- No revenue minimum (0-based)

**Appendix A: Commission by tier (see table above)**
**Appendix B: Partner can resell all "live" ventures (not "planned")**

## Partner Dashboard (Real-time)

- Total deals, won deals, pipeline value
- Commission earned + pending
- Monthly breakdown
- Payout history
- API key manager
- Export deals (CSV)
- 1099 download (Q1 annually)

## Playbook: Week 1–30

| Phase | Duration | Partner Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activation | Week 1 | Learning | Support heavy |
| Ramp | Week 2–4 | Ramping | Weekly sync |
| Growth | Month 2+ | Autonomous | Monthly review |
| Scaling | Month 6+ | Strategic | Tier upgrade |

### Troubleshooting

- **Unresponsive:** Day 7 Slack check-in → Day 10 CRO call → Day 30 exit
- **Low conversion:** Analyze lead quality, test venture focus, offer sales training
- **Custom venture:** CEO approval required, 40% commission
