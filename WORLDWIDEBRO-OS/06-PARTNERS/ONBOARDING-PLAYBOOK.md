# Partner Onboarding Playbook

## Week 1: Activation

**Day 1:**
- Welcome email from CEO
- ServiceNow provisioned (OAuth)
- API key generated
- Partner accepts MSA

**Day 2–3:**
- Training: 3 videos (10m + 5m + 5m)
- Review ventures (CON-001, OPS-001, LT-005)
- API docs provided

**Day 4–7:**
- Submit first test lead
- ServiceNow incident auto-created
- Dashboard shows live deal

## Week 2–4: Ramp

**Week 2:** First deal cycle
- Partner nurtures Week 1 leads
- CRO available for questions
- Weekly sync (30 min)

**Week 3:** First win (target $5–10K)
- Commission auto-calculated
- Incident marked closed
- Celebration email from CEO

**Week 4:** Growth phase
- Pipeline: 5–10 active leads
- Autonomous operation
- Monthly business review

## Month 2+: Scaling

**Tier progression targets:**
- Month 2: $8–10K revenue (path to Tier 2)
- Tier 2: $50K annual (35% commission, dedicated support, $500/mo co-marketing)
- Tier 3: $250K annual (40% commission, executive access, $2000/mo co-marketing)

**Co-marketing opportunities:**
1. Joint webinar: "How [Partner] + Worldwidebro solve X"
2. Case study: Featured on vex-hero-site
3. Integration: Partner product on VEX marketplace (30–40% revenue share)

**Auto-promotions:** Happen end-of-month if revenue threshold reached

**Quarterly business reviews:**
- Revenue analysis (closed, pipeline, forecast)
- Competitive landscape
- Product roadmap feedback
- Next quarter plan

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Unresponsive | Day 7: Slack check-in → Day 10: CRO call → Day 30: Exit review |
| Low conversion | Analyze lead quality, test venture focus, offer sales training |
| Custom venture | CEO approval required, 40% commission |
