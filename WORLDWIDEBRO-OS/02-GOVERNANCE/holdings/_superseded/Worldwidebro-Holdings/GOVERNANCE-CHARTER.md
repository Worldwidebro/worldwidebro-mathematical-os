# Governance Charter: Worldwidebro Holdings

**Effective Date:** 2026-06-18  
**Authority:** CEO, Antwuan Divine Johns  
**Update Cycle:** Quarterly reviews, annual revision  
**Scope:** All 18 OPCOs, 704 ventures, holding company structure

---

## 1. Board Structure

**Worldwidebro Holdings Board:**
- CEO (Antwuan Divine Johns)
- CFO (hired by Month 2)
- Chief Operations Officer (hired by Month 2)
- External Legal Counsel (retained by Month 1)
- Board Secretary (appointed by CEO)

**Board Meetings:** Monthly (first Friday, 2 hours)

**Board Responsibilities:**
- Strategic direction & quarterly reforecasting
- Capital allocation across layers
- Key hires (CEO, CFO, COO only)
- M&A decisions (acquisitions > $100K)
- Risk management & quarterly risk review
- Compliance & legal matters

---

## 2. OPCO Leadership

**Each OPCO has:**
- President (OPCO Head, reports to CEO)
- 2-3 Venture Managers (report to OPCO President)
- 1 Finance Manager (shared across 2-3 OPCOs, reports to CFO)

**OPCO President Responsibilities:**
- 90-day revenue targets by venture
- Monthly P&L review (consolidated)
- Team performance & hiring
- Quarterly strategic review
- Risk escalation

**Venture Manager Responsibilities:**
- Day-to-day operations
- Daily/weekly sales & operational KPIs
- Customer relationship management
- Team performance & morale
- Weekly operational reviews with OPCO President

---

## 3. Venture Status Lifecycle

All ventures must be in one of 6 states at all times:

| Status | Definition | OPCO Owner | Decision Gate |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Idea** | Concept stage, no revenue | OPCO President | Launch decision (Month 1) |
| **Research** | Market validation, < $5K spend | OPCO President | Go/no-go (Month 2) |
| **Building** | Product development, beta testing | OPCO President | Beta launch (Month 2-3) |
| **Launching** | Soft launch, pilot customers | Venture Manager | Public launch (Month 4) |
| **Operating** | Revenue-generating, $0-$50K/mo | Venture Manager | Scale or pause (Month 6+) |
| **Scaling** | Aggressive growth, $50K+/mo | Venture Manager | Maintain or exit (Month 9+) |
| **Archived** | Discontinued, migrated, or sold | CFO | Final closure |

---

## 4. Quarterly Decision Framework

Every 90 days, the Board conducts a portfolio review. Each operating venture gets a decision:

**KEEP** — Continue as is, fund normally  
**SCALE** — Invest more, hire, aggressive growth  
**SELL** — Exit/liquidate, repurpose team  
**MERGE** — Combine with another venture  
**PAUSE** — Suspend operations, preserve team  
**ARCHIVE** — Officially discontinue  

**Decision Criteria:**
- Revenue run rate vs. 90-day target
- Gross margin (should exceed 40%)
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback)
- Team capability & morale
- Market fit signals (NPS, retention, churn)
- CEO assessment + CFO financial review

---

## 5. Financial Controls

**Monthly Reporting:**
- P&L by venture (revenue, COGS, OpEx)
- Cash burn by venture
- 90-day cash runway
- ARR, MRR, and month-over-month growth
- Payroll commitments
- Debt service (if applicable)

**Annual Budgeting (Nov-Dec):**
- Full-year revenue forecast by venture
- Hiring plan by role/OPCO
- Capital expenditure plan
- Contingency reserves (20% of budget)
- Board approval required

**Spending Limits (approvals required):**
- < $5K: OPCO President
- $5K-$25K: CEO
- $25K-$100K: Board approval
- > $100K: Board vote (unanimous)

---

## 6. Compensation & Incentives

**CEO:** Annual salary + quarterly profit bonus (20% of net profit, first $50K reinvested)

**OPCO Presidents:** Annual salary + quarterly venture revenue target bonus (3% of revenue overages)

**Venture Managers:** Base salary + monthly operational bonus (1% of venture revenue, if > monthly target)

**Finance Manager:** Annual salary + quarterly accuracy bonus (on forecast accuracy)

**All employees:** Annual raise review (March), equity grants (if profitable)

---

## 7. Risk Management

**Quarterly Risk Assessment:**
- Market risk (competition, CAC inflation)
- Team risk (key person dependence, retention)
- Financial risk (burn rate, runway, cash)
- Operational risk (systems, data, compliance)
- Legal/compliance risk (contracts, insurance, licenses)

**Red Flag Protocol:**
- If a venture's cash runway < 3 months: CEO + CFO intervention
- If monthly burn > monthly revenue + 50%: Pause decision within 7 days
- If team key person leaves: Emergency succession plan within 24 hours
- If customer loss > 20% MoM: Root cause analysis + correction plan within 7 days

---

## 8. Venture Synergies

Each venture benefits from holding company resources:

**Shared Services (No Direct Charge):**
- Finance/accounting (via Finance Manager)
- Legal (via retained counsel)
- HR/payroll (via shared HR system)
- Marketing templates & playbooks
- Technology infrastructure (Supabase, cloud)
- Data & analytics (DuckDB, Grafana dashboards)

**Cross-Selling Opportunities:**
- OPCOs actively refer customers across ventures (tracked quarterly)
- Marketing can promote 2-3 related ventures in single campaigns
- Sales team access to customer bases across 18 OPCOs

**Capital Allocation:**
- Surplus from one venture can fund growth in another
- Board approves inter-OPCO capital transfers > $10K
- Goal: 3x profit multiplier through synergies

## 9. Reporting & Transparency

**Daily (internal):**
- Cash balance report (automated)
- Sales activity log by venture
- Customer acquisition count

**Weekly (OPCO President → CEO):**
- Revenue & burn
- Team updates (hires, departures, morale)
- Blockers & escalations
- Top 3 customer relationships

**Monthly (Board-wide):**
- Full P&L dashboard (Airtable)
- Venture status updates
- Top performers & struggling ventures
- Hiring plan progress
- Capital deployment

**Quarterly (Board Review):**
- Full portfolio analysis
- Revenue vs. forecast
- Venture decisions (Keep/Scale/Sell/Merge/Pause/Archive)
- Strategic adjustments
- External benchmark (vs. market)

**Annually (AGM):**
- Full annual review
- Year-over-year growth
- Strategic roadmap for next year
- Shareholder communication

## 10. Escalation Matrix

| Issue | Escalation | Timeline | Decision Maker |
|-------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Revenue miss < 20% | OPCO President → CEO | Monthly review | CEO (advise) |
| Revenue miss > 20% | CEO → Board | 7 days | Board decides |
| Team crisis (departure) | Venture Manager → OPCO President → CEO | 24 hours | OPCO President (exec), CEO (approval) |
| Venture failure (bankrupt) | CFO → CEO → Board | 7 days | Board decides (liquidate/pause) |
| Compliance issue | Legal counsel → CEO → Board | 48 hours | Board decides |
| Customer complaint (major) | Venture Manager → OPCO President → CEO | 24 hours | OPCO President (resolve) |

## 11. Amendment Process

**Governance Charter amendments** require:
- CEO proposal (written)
- 30-day review period
- Board vote (unanimous approval required)
- Effective date: 90 days after approval (transition period)

**Emergency amendments** (crisis-driven):
- CEO + CFO joint proposal
- 24-hour board notification
- 48-hour window for objections
- If no objections, effective immediately
