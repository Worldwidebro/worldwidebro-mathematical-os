## How This Works: The Business Model

### Verticals (18 OPCOs)
- Each OPCO focuses on ONE industry
- All ventures within OPCO share learnings
- OPCO leader becomes expert in that vertical
- Cross-OPCO synergies flow naturally

### Horizontals (10 Functions)
- Each Horizontal serves ALL 18 OPCOs
- Eliminating 17 duplicate teams
- Economies of scale on expensive functions
- 1/18th cost per OPCO

### Synergy Multiplier
- Media OPCO generates leads
- Marketplace OPCO converts to customers
- Sales Horizontal closes deals
- Staffing OPCO supplies execution
- Operations Horizontal runs efficiently
- Finance Horizontal manages cash
- Investment OPCO deploys capital
- Real Estate OPCO holds assets

**Result:** Each venture feeds others = exponential growth
