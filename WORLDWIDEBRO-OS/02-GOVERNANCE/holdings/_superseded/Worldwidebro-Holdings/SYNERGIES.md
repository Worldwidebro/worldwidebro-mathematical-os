## Horizontal Functions & Their Synergies

### 1. Finance Horizontal
**Role:** Capital aggregation, allocation, treasury

**Synergies:**
- Collects profit from all 18 OPCOs
- Deploys to highest ROI opportunities
- Funds growth ventures (Construction, Technology, Real Estate)
- Cross-subsidizes at-risk OPCOs

**Example Flow:**
```
Media OPCO profit ($50K) → Finance
Marketplace OPCO profit ($75K) → Finance
Operations OPCO profit ($100K) → Finance
Total available: $225K

Finance deploys:
- $75K to Technology OPCO (highest growth)
- $75K to Construction OPCO (project funding)
- $75K to Investment OPCO (acquisition capital)
```

**3x leverage:** Same $225K capital serves 3 OPCOs

**18x leverage:** Same software serves 18 OPCOs

**Synergies:**

- Economies of scale in sales infrastructure

```
- No cross-sell opportunity
- 4x higher cost
```
