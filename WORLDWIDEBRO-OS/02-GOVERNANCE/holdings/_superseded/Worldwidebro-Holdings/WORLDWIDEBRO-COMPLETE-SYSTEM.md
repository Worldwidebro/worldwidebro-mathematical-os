## Decision Matrix: Keep / Scale / Kill

Every 90 days, review each venture:

```
KEEP
├─ Steady revenue
├─ Positive unit economics
├─ Good team
└─ Action: Support, minimal interference

SCALE
├─ Exceptional revenue
├─ Proven model
├─ Team capacity
└─ Action: More capital, hire, marketing

SELL
├─ Operating but stalled
├─ Distraction from core
├─ Strategic fit poor
└─ Action: Find buyer, transition

MERGE
├─ Overlaps with another venture
├─ Combined form stronger
└─ Action: Consolidate teams, eliminate duplication

PAUSE
├─ Not ready yet
├─ Team not ready
├─ Market not ready
└─ Action: Freeze, revisit next quarter

ARCHIVE
├─ No traction
├─ Wrong market
├─ Wrong team
├─ No path to revenue
└─ Action: Shut down, preserve IP/docs
```
