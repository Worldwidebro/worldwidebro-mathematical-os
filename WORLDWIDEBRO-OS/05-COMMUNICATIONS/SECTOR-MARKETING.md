### Header Image Specs

- **Dimensions:** 600px wide × 200px tall (fits email header across all clients)
- **Format:** PNG (transparency), WebP (optimized), JPEG fallback
- **File size:** <50KB per image (critical for email deliverability)
- **Style:** Gradient background + emoji + minimal text overlay
- **Safe zone:** Keep content in center 400px (edges crop on mobile)

## 5-Email Sequence Visual Strategy

### Email #1: AWARENESS (Day 0)
**Goal:** Grab attention, establish credibility  
**Visual:** Bold gradient + hero emoji + tagline

**Per-Sector Headers:**
- **Healthcare (Teal):** 🏥 "Find Trusted Healthcare Talent" + faint hospital corridor
- **Industrial (Amber):** 🏭 "Scale Your Workforce, Instantly" + faint warehouse
- **IT & Tech (Navy):** 💻 "Find Engineers, Not Headhunters" + faint modern office
- **Admin (Slate):** 📋 "Administrative Excellence, On Demand" + faint organized office
- **Finance (Emerald):** 💰 "Accounting Talent That Scales" + faint professional office
- **Hospitality (Rose):** 👨‍🍳 "Hospitality Staffing Made Simple" + faint kitchen/service

### Email #2: EDUCATION (Day 2)
**Goal:** Show how the process works  
**Visual:** Process flowchart or testimonial

- **Healthcare:** "How Medical Staffing Works" (3-step vetting process)
- **Industrial:** "Our Vetting Process" (safety-first, background checks)
- **IT:** "Technical Screening Explained" (skills assessment, code review)
- **Admin:** "From Request to Hire" (timeline, candidate matching)
- **Finance:** "Credentials Verification" (CPA verification, experience)
- **Hospitality:** "Staff, Scheduled, Delivered" (quick onboarding)

### Email #3: SOCIAL PROOF (Day 5)
**Goal:** Build confidence with real results  
**Visual:** Client testimonial + metrics

- **Healthcare:** "98.7% On-Time Placements" (hospital setting + nurse photo)
- **Industrial:** "27.3% Faster Hiring" (warehouse + ops manager quote)
- **IT:** "Zero Turnover on Vetted Engineers" (dev team + CTO quote)
- **Admin:** "100% Retention After 90 Days" (office + CEO testimonial)
- **Finance:** "85% Hired for Permanent Roles" (office + CFO quote)
- **Hospitality:** "50+ Venues, 2,000+ Staff Placed" (service + manager quote)

### Email #4: URGENCY (Day 8)
**Goal:** Create FOMO, drive decision  
**Visual:** Scarcity + limited-time messaging

- **Healthcare:** "Healthcare Shortage—Act Fast" (shortage stats)
- **Industrial:** "Q3 Scaling Window Closing" (seasonal urgency)
- **IT:** "Senior Engineers Filling Fast" (competition angle)
- **Admin:** "Back-to-Office Season Starts Soon" (seasonal)
- **Finance:** "Tax Season Staffing Fills Up Fast" (seasonal)
- **Hospitality:** "Summer Peak Staffing Starts Now" (seasonal)

### Email #5: CONVERSION (Day 12)
**Goal:** Final push to signup  
**Visual:** Success story + celebration

- **Healthcare:** "Your First Hire Starts Next Week" (success photo + checkmark)
- **Industrial:** "Let's Scale Your Team This Quarter" (team expansion)
- **IT:** "Build Your Engineering Dream Team" (dev team photo)
- **Admin:** "Your Office Runs Smoother" (organized workspace)
- **Finance:** "Close Books Faster, Hire Smarter" (dashboard)
- **Hospitality:** "Your Staffing Problems Are Solved" (busy service photo)
