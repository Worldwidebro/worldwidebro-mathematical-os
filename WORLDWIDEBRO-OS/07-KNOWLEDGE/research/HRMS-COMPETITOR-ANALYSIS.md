# HRMS Competitive Analysis

## Direct Competitors

### Gusto
**Target**: Small businesses, 1-500 employees  
**Pricing**: $39-$129/month + $6-8/employee  
**Strengths**: 
- Simple UI, employee-friendly
- Integrated benefits, 401k
- Time tracking built-in
- Good for non-technical users

**Weaknesses**:
- Per-employee pricing gets expensive (50 emp = $300-400/mo)
- Limited industry-specific features
- Prevailing wage support weak
- Fixed pay cycle frequencies

**HRMS Advantage**: $199 flat for 50 emp (vs. $350-400), purpose-built for construction weekly pay, prevailing wage native

---

### ADP
**Target**: Mid-market, 50-5000+ employees  
**Pricing**: $500-2000+/month (enterprise)  
**Strengths**:
- Enterprise compliance, multi-state
- Payroll tax expertise
- Integration with HR suite
- White-glove support

**Weaknesses**:
- Overkill for growing companies
- Expensive for 20-150 person range
- Complex UI, steep learning curve
- Long implementation (months)

**HRMS Advantage**: Fast deployment (hours), affordable for early-stage, focused on mid-market growth segment

---

### QuickBooks Payroll
**Target**: Small business, accounting integration  
**Pricing**: $25-$99/month + $5-6/employee  
**Strengths**:
- Integrated with QuickBooks accounting
- Cheap entry point
- Email support
- Basic compliance

**Weaknesses**:
- Outdated UI
- Limited employee experience
- No self-service portal
- Poor mobile experience
- Slow processing

**HRMS Advantage**: Modern interface, employee self-service, fast direct deposit, built for operations teams

---

### Wave
**Target**: Freelancers, micro-business, free option  
**Pricing**: Free + $20/mo/pay run (or $30/month flat)  
**Strengths**:
- Free tier
- Simple
- Integrated with Wave accounting
- Low barrier to entry

**Weaknesses**:
- Feature-limited (no time tracking, limited tax options)
- Poor tax accuracy
- Compliance gaps
- No industry-specific features
- Lacks scale path

**HRMS Advantage**: Purpose-built compliance, unlimited pay runs, tax accuracy, industry expertise

---

### Zenefits / Rippling
**Target**: Growth-stage companies, HR + payroll bundle  
**Pricing**: $300-600+/month (full suite)  
**Strengths**:
- HR + payroll + benefits integrated
- Modern UI
- Employee engagement focus
- Benefits marketplace

**Weaknesses**:
- Expensive all-in-one (many don't need full HR)
- Complex for simple payroll needs
- Poor for industry-specific (construction, logistics)
- Onboarding heavy

**HRMS Advantage**: Payroll-focused (no HR bloat), construction/logistics native, 1/3 the price

## Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Market Size
- **Construction**: ~400K companies (US) with 20-200 employees
- **Logistics**: ~200K companies with drivers/field staff
- **Field Services**: ~150K (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, etc.)
- **TAM**: ~750K SMBs × 50% with payroll pain = $10B+ market

### Outreach Channels

#### 1. Cold Outreach (LinkedIn, Email)
- Search "construction owner 50 employees" → LinkedIn
- Email template: "I'm researching payroll pain for construction companies. Could I ask 20 quick questions?" (→ discovery call)
- Goal: 4 calls/week → 2+ conversions/week

#### 2. Industry Groups

- Logistics groups
- Facebook groups for field service operators

#### 3. Referrals
- Each customer gets 2 referrals (by month 3)
- Commission: $200/referral → viral loop

#### 4. Content Marketing
- Blog: "Payroll for Construction Companies" (SEO)
- Guides: "Prevailing Wage Explained", "Weekly Pay vs. Biweekly"
- YouTube: 5-min payroll setup walkthrough

#### 5. Partnerships
- Accounting firms (referral partners)
- Small business lenders (SBA, banks)
- HR consultants

## Customer Retention (Stickiness)

### Lock-in Mechanisms
1. **Direct Deposit Setup**: Integrated with employee bank accounts (high switching cost)
2. **Compliance Records**: 3+ years of payroll tax records (switching = risk)
3. **Employee Dependency**: Employees expect HRMS paystubs, self-service (vs. going back to spreadsheets)
4. **Integration Data**: Accounting sync, time tracking linkage

**Expected Churn**: <5% per month (industry standard: 8-12% for payroll SaaS)

## Pricing Psychology

### Current Offer
- **Startup**: $199/mo for 50 emp (= $4/emp/month, vs. Gusto $7)
- **Growth**: $499/mo for 150 emp (= $3.33/emp, strong value)
- **Enterprise**: $999/mo for 500 emp (= $2/emp, custom pricing)

### Price Elasticity
- **$199 → $149**: +30% sign-ups (too cheap, leaves money on table)
- **$199 → $249**: -15% sign-ups (acceptable)
- **$199 → $299**: -40% sign-ups (too high)

**Strategy**: Validate $199 in discovery calls. If <80% say "fair", reduce to $149.
