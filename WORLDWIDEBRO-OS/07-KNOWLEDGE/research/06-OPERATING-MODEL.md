```

│
├── Revenue Layer

│   ├── SaaS/Subscription ($__K/mo)
│   ├── Licensing/Royalties ($__K/mo)
│   ├── Operator partnerships ($__K/mo)

├── Execution Layer
│   ├── You: Strategic/high-leverage work
│   ├── Operators: Day-to-day execution

    ├── Debt/leverage
    └── Asset acquisition
```
