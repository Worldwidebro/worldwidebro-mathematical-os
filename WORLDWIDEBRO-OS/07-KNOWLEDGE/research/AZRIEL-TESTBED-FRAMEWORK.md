# Azriel Testbed Framework — Phase 1 Validation

**Purpose:** Validate character-first teaching methodology and content atomization system using Azriel YouTube channel.

**Timeline:** June-August 2026 (3 months)  
**Goal:** 12-16 published videos, 1,000+ YouTube subscribers, 100K views, parent community feedback

---

## What We're Testing (Hypotheses)

### Hypothesis 1: Character-First Teaching Works
**Claim:** Teaching character (identity, courage, growth mindset) BEFORE skills increases motivation and outcomes.

**Test:** Document Azriel's growth across:
- Confidence (does he try harder things?)
- Persistence (does he bounce back from failure?)
- Self-concept (does he see himself as capable?)
- Learning application (does he use taught skills independently?)

**Success Metrics:**
- Azriel asks for harder challenges by Week 4
- Azriel attempts things alone (reduced dad-dependency) by Week 8
- Azriel teaches someone else a skill by Week 12

---

### Hypothesis 2: Parents Want Character Content
**Claim:** Parents of 5-12 year olds are hungry for content that develops character, not just teaches skills.

**Test:** Track comment sentiment and engagement type:
- % of comments about character/confidence
- % of comments asking for character-focused content
- Comments from parents saying "my kid applied this at home"

**Success Metrics:**
- 40%+ comments related to character/mindset
- 20%+ comments are parents asking for more character content
- 10%+ comments are parents reporting kid behavior change

---

### Hypothesis 3: Content Atomization Scales
**Claim:** One video creates 50+ assets across platforms (shorts, clips, social posts, etc.)

**Test:** Track production time and asset output:
- Time to record: Track actual hours
- Assets created: Count shorts, clips, posts, quotes
- Repurposing efficiency: How many assets per hour recorded?

**Success Metrics:**
- Produce 1 long-form video in <2 hours
- Create 30+ short-form assets from each video
- Maintain consistent quality across all formats

---

### Hypothesis 4: Bonding + Learning Works Together
**Claim:** Teaching your own child creates authentic, engaging content AND strengthens relationship.

**Test:** Subjective feedback from Azriel + viewer comments:
- Does Azriel enjoy the filming?
- Does relationship improve (more conversations, more initiated time together)?
- Do viewers comment on the authentic dad-kid dynamic?

**Success Metrics:**
- Azriel initiates 2+ filming sessions per week by Month 2
- Comments frequently mention "love the relationship" or "goals with my dad"
- Azriel reports feeling "proud" of the content

## Measurement System

### Azriel's Personal Growth Metrics

**Confidence Tracker (Subjective)**
- Rate 1-10: "How confident am I trying new things?"
- Measure before/after each video
- Trend should increase over 12 weeks

**Effort Tracker (Behavioral)**
- How many times does Azriel attempt something difficult?
- How many times does he ask "Can I try that?"
- Target: 2x/week by Month 3

**Persistence Tracker (Behavioral)**
- How long does he stick with a challenge before asking for help?
- Baseline: Gives up in <2 minutes
- Target: Persists 5-10 minutes by Week 8

**Teaching Moments (Count)**
- How many times does Azriel explain/teach something to others?
- Baseline: 0
- Target: 2-3/week by Month 3

---

### YouTube Channel Metrics

**Growth Metrics**
- Subscribers (baseline: 0, target: 1,000 by Week 12)
- Views per video (baseline: 100-500, target: 1,000-5,000)
- Total views (target: 50K by end of Phase 1)
- Watch time (target: 10,000 hours)

**Engagement Metrics**
- Like rate (target: 5%+ of viewers)
- Comment rate (target: 3%+ of viewers)
- Share rate (tracking, target: 0.5%+)
- Subscriber retention (target: 90%+ watch next video)

**Content Quality Metrics**
- Production quality improving? (audio, lighting, editing)
- Story telling improving? (hook, pacing, closing)
- Authenticity sustained? (no artificial feels)

---

### Audience Feedback Metrics

**Comment Analysis (Weekly)**
- % about character/confidence
- % asking for specific content
- % from parents reporting application at home
- Sentiment analysis (positive/neutral/negative)

**Survey (End of Phase 1)**
- 10-question survey sent to subscribers
- "What character quality do you want your kids to develop?"
- "Would you take a paid course on this?"
- "What topics do you want next?"

**Viral Indicators**
- Any videos shared to Reddit, parenting forums?
- Are parents recommending to other parents?
- Any press/influencer shares?

## Red Flags: When to Pivot

**Red Flag 1: Low Engagement**
- Engagement <2% = content not resonating
- Pivot: Shift to more entertainment, less educational

**Red Flag 2: Azriel Not Enjoying**
- Azriel resisting filming = authentic moment lost
- Pivot: Make filming more play-based, less structured

**Red Flag 3: Quality Drop**
- Production quality declining = burnout
- Pivot: Reduce frequency, hire editor, simplify process

**Red Flag 4: No Parent Interest**
- No parents asking for paid content
- Pivot: Re-focus on skills (school-relevant), less character
