### RISK #4: Unit Economics Collapse at Scale

**Honest Math:**

```
Month 1: 50 students @ $49/mo = $2,450 gross
  - Platform fees (5%) = -$122
  - Stripe (2.9%) = -$71
  - Your time (20 hrs @ $100/hr) = -$2,000
  - Net: -$743 (you lose money)

Month 2: 30 of 50 stay (40% churn) + 20 new = 50 students
  - To replace churn, need 20 new customers
  - Customer acquisition cost = $2,000-5,000
  - Revenue = $2,450, but CAC = $3,000
  - **Negative unit economics**

Month 6 Reality:
  - Churn is constant (40%/month)
  - To grow to 100 members, need to add 40 members/month
  - At $3,000 CAC, that's $120,000/month in customer acquisition spend
  - But you only have $4,900/month in revenue
  - The math doesn't work
```

**The Hidden Killer:**
- Course completion rate is 10% (industry average)
- Most students quit after Chapter 1
- Churn happens at Month 2 (they got what they wanted)
- You need to replace the entire customer base every 2-3 months

**Probability of Failure:** 85% (unit economics are fundamentally broken)

## 🎯 RED TEAM RECOMMENDATIONS

### BEFORE LAUNCH: 3-Week Validation Sprint

**Week 1: Market Validation (5 hours)**
```
1. Find 20 construction contractors (LinkedIn, Facebook groups, Reddit)
2. Offer free access to General Contracting course
3. Track: Completion rate, time spent, satisfaction
4. Ask: "Would you pay $49/mo for this?"
5. Gate: Proceed only if 12+ say "yes" AND 60%+ complete chapter 4
```

**Week 2: Distribution Testing (10 hours)**
```
1. Run 3 small ad tests in parallel:
   - $100 Google Ads to "general contracting course"
   - $100 Facebook ads to contractors
   - Outreach to 10 contractor associations for partnership
2. Track: Cost per lead, conversion rate
3. Gate: Proceed only if CAC < $100
```

**Week 3: Pricing Validation (2 hours)**
```
1. Survey your 20 beta users:
   - Would you pay $29/mo?
   - Would you pay $49/mo?
   - Would you pay $199 one-time?
2. Choose pricing based on highest conversion + retention combo
3. Gate: Don't launch at $49/mo if data shows $29/mo converts better
```

**Success Criteria Before Paid Launch:**
- ✅ 12+ people say "yes, I'd pay"
- ✅ 60%+ complete chapter 4
- ✅ NPS score +30 or higher
- ✅ CAC can be < $75 with testing
- ✅ Pricing chosen based on actual demand, not guessing

---

### IF YOU LAUNCH (Against Red Team Advice)

**Plan for Failure Modes:**

1. **Expect 40-60% Churn Monthly**
   - Budget for constant customer replacement
   - Know that your LTV is probably 2-3 months, not 6

2. **Budget for $150-300 CAC Minimum**
   - Don't assume organic works
   - Have paid ads budget from day 1

3. **Have Escape Plan from Skool**
   - Export member list weekly to Supabase
   - Keep email in separate platform (Kit, not Skool)
   - Be ready to migrate to Kajabi in Month 3 if needed

4. **Hire for Momentum, Not Scale**
   - Don't hire 4 people to create 9 courses
   - Hire 1 marketing person in Month 2 to get distribution working
   - Hire 1 content creator in Month 3 if course 1 validates

5. **Pivot Quickly if Unit Economics Break**
   - If month 2 CAC > $100, kill paid launch immediately
   - If month 2 churn > 50%, kill the model
   - Pivot to: cohort-based courses, instructor platform, or b2b licensing

## 🎬 BETTER APPROACHES

### OPTION A: Cohort-Based Courses (Higher Probability of Success)

```
Price: $300 per person (4-week cohort)
Format: 10 live sessions + peer learning
Size: 20-30 students per cohort
Revenue per cohort: $6,000-9,000
Cost: You + 1 TA assistant = $2,000
Margin: 60%+
Repeat: Run new cohort every 4 weeks = $24-36K/mo at 2 cohorts

Why This Works:
- Higher retention (peer accountability)
- Easier distribution (word-of-mouth cohort fills up)
- Better unit economics
- You're the instructor (personal brand > algorithm)
```

**Probability of Success:** 70%

---

### OPTION B: Marketplace Model (Network Effects)

```
Position as "platform for construction pros to teach"
You're not creating content, facilitating it
Commission: 30% on each course sold
Example: 
  - 100 contractors create courses
  - Average $200/mo per course
  - Your commission: $100/course
  - Revenue: $10,000/mo (100 courses)

Why This Works:
- Network effects (more teachers = more students)
- You're not bottleneck (creators drive growth)
- Monetizes your venture portfolio (let them teach)
- Defensible (platform > content)
```

**Probability of Success:** 60%

---

### OPTION C: Specialized Bootcamp (High-Touch)

```
Format: 8-week intensive bootcamp
Price: $2,000 per student
Class size: 15-20 students
Format: Live group sessions + 1:1 mentoring
Revenue: $30-40K per cohort
Cost: Instructor + 2 mentors = $8K
Margin: 50%+
Repeat: 2-3 cohorts per year = $60-120K/year

Why This Works:
- Premium positioning (bootcamp > course)
- Selective admissions (quality > scale)
- Higher perceived value
- Better outcomes (high-touch) = better testimonials
```

**Probability of Success:** 65%
