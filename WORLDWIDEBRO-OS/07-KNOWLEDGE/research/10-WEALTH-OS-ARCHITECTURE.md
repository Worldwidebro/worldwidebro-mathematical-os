## System Interconnections

```
Attention
    ↓
Sales
    ↓
Revenue
    ↓
Profit
    ↓
Capital
    ↓
Assets
    ↓
Ownership
    ↓
Legacy
```

**Each system feeds the next.**

**If any system breaks, the flow stops.**
