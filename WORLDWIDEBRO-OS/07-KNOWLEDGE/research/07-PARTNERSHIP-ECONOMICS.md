# Partnership Economics

**Purpose:** Structure partnerships that incentivize operators while keeping ownership.

---

## 4 Partnership Structures

### Structure 1: Revenue Share Agreement

**You own:** 100% of venture  
**Operator gets:** % of monthly revenue  
**Example:** Venture generates $10K/mo → You take $6K, Operator takes $4K

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Your equity | 100% |
| Their equity | 0% |
| Your capital | You provide all |
| Their capital | Time only |
| Revenue split | You: [%], Them: [%] |
| Term | [1-3 years?] |
| Renewal | [Y/N] |

**Best for:** Proven business model, operator is pure executor

---

### Structure 2: Equity Partnership

**You own:** 51% (control)  
**Operator owns:** 49% (aligned)  
**Capital:** Usually 50/50 split

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Your equity | 51% |
| Their equity | 49% |
| Your capital | 50% |
| Their capital | 50% |
| Revenue split | Dividend from profits |
| Control | You have majority board seat |
| Term | Long-term (5-10 years) |

**Best for:** Growth venture, operator brings capital + commitment

---

### Structure 3: Operator Agreement

**You own:** 70%  
**Operator owns:** 30%  
**Your capital:** You provide, they work

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Your equity | 70% |
| Their equity | 30% |
| Your capital | You provide |
| Their capital | Sweat equity |
| Revenue split | Dividend from profits |
| Control | You make decisions |
| Term | [2-5 years?] |

**Best for:** Early-stage, operator needs incentive

---

### Structure 4: Franchise/License

**Operator:** Owns local business  
**You:** Collect royalties  
**Capital:** Operator funds theirs, you fund franchisor costs

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Operator ownership | 100% (local territory) |
| Your ownership | IP/methodology |
| Operator capital | Full investment |
| Your capital | Minimal |
| Revenue | Royalty % [5-8%] + franchise fees |
| Control | Franchisor standards |
| Term | [3-10 years] |

**Best for:** Proven, replicable model

## Decision Matrix

### Use Structure 1 (Revenue Share) when:
- [ ] Model is proven
- [ ] You're providing capital
- [ ] Operator is pure executor
- [ ] You want full control
- [ ] Early stage or test venture

### Use Structure 2 (Equity Partnership) when:
- [ ] Operator brings capital
- [ ] Long-term growth potential
- [ ] You want aligned incentives
- [ ] Operator is cofounder-level
- [ ] Scaling venture

### Use Structure 3 (Operator Agreement) when:
- [ ] You fund venture
- [ ] Operator brings execution
- [ ] Medium-term growth
- [ ] Standard venture
- [ ] You maintain control

### Use Structure 4 (Franchise) when:
- [ ] Model is tested
- [ ] Operator has capital
- [ ] Multiple territories
- [ ] Standardized execution
- [ ] Scaling phase
