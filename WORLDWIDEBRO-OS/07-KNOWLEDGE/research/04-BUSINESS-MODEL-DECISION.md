# Business Model Decision

**Purpose:** Which ONE business model are you building?

---

## The 6 Options

### 1. Software/SaaS

**Model:** Recurring subscription for software access  
**Revenue:** Monthly subscription × user count  
**Capital:** High (product dev)  
**Time:** 120+ days to first revenue  
**Scalability:** Very high  
**Defensibility:** High (software moat)  

**Candidates from your assets:**
- Venture OS dashboard
- Repository intelligence SaaS
- Knowledge graph as service

---

### 2. Licensing/Royalties

**Model:** One-time fee for IP, recurring royalties  
**Revenue:** License fees + % of revenue  
**Capital:** None (you own IP)  
**Time:** 30-60 days to first revenue  
**Scalability:** Very high  
**Defensibility:** IP-based  

**Candidates:**
- Venture OS framework licensing
- Automation systems licensing
- Operating system licensing

---

### 3. Services/Consulting

**Model:** You deliver the service  
**Revenue:** Fee per engagement or monthly retainer  
**Capital:** None  
**Time:** 7-30 days to first revenue  
**Scalability:** Medium (limited by time)  
**Defensibility:** Experience/track record  

**Candidates:**
- Fractional CTO
- AI Systems Consulting
- Systems Architecture

---

### 4. Agency/Outcomes

**Model:** You deliver outcomes (deliverables)  
**Revenue:** Project-based or % of value  
**Capital:** None  
**Time:** 30-90 days  
**Scalability:** Medium-high (can hire team)  
**Defensibility:** Results track record  

**Candidates:**
- Automation builds
- Systems implementation
- Venture setup/launch

---

### 5. Marketplace

**Model:** You facilitate transactions, take take-rate  
**Revenue:** % of volume transacted  
**Capital:** Medium (platform dev)  
**Time:** 60-180 days  
**Scalability:** Very high  
**Defensibility:** Network effects  

**Candidates:**
- Operator marketplace
- Venture capital marketplace
- Skills marketplace

---

### 6. Hybrid

**Model:** Combination of above  
**Revenue:** Multiple streams  
**Capital:** Medium-high  
**Time:** 60-180 days  
**Scalability:** Very high  
**Defensibility:** Multiple moats  

**Example:**
- Consulting → Fractional CTO (recurring)
- Fractional CTO → Your methodology → SaaS
- SaaS → Licensing to other agencies

---
