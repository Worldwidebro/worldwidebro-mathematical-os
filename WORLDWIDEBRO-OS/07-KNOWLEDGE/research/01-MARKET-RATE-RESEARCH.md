# Market Rate Research

**Purpose:** Price based on value created, not hours.

## The 5 Market Rates

1. **Labor** - What someone pays you ($$/hr or $$/mo)
2. **Agency** - What businesses pay for outcomes ($$ based on value)
3. **Product** - What customers pay recurring (SaaS $/mo)
4. **Acquisition** - What multiples apply (2-10x multiples)
5. **Capital** - What money costs (6-25% depending on source)

---

## Service 1: AI Systems Consulting

**Labor Market:** $50-500+/hr  
**Agency Market:** Value saved × 10-30%  
**Typical:** $2,500-$25,000 per engagement  

---

## Service 2: Fractional CTO

**Labor Market:** $2K-$15K+/month  
**Agency Market:** Value prevented × 10-30%  
**Typical:** $2,500-$15,000/month

## Service 3: Automation Build

**Labor Market:** $2K-$50K+ per project  
**Agency Market:** Hours saved × hourly value × 10-30%  
**Typical:** $5K-$25K+ per project

---

## Service 4: Repository Intelligence

**Labor Market:** $1K-$15K per engagement  
**Agency Market:** Dev hours saved × 10-30%  
**Typical:** $2.5K-$15K per assessment

---

## Pricing Formula

**Value Created × 10-30%**

Example: Save client $100K/year → Charge $10K-$30K
