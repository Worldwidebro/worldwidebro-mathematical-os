## About This Book

**Adventure Atlas** is a heartwarming picture book about noticing the magic in everyday life.

It's for curious 7-year-olds (and parents who want them to remember their year).

### What You'll Learn

✓ Self-awareness — noticing your own feelings  
✓ Mindfulness — paying attention to the present  
✓ Gratitude — appreciating everyday moments  
✓ Creativity — expressing yourself through art  
✓ Growth — understanding adventures get bigger as you grow  

### What Happens Next

After reading, your child will want to make their own Adventure Atlas.

Let them.

It becomes a memory that lasts forever.
