## Back Matter

### What Is An Adventure?

An adventure doesn't have to be scary or far away.

An adventure is:
- Something you notice
- Something you feel
- Something that makes you smile or learn
- Something worth remembering

### Make Your Own Adventure Atlas

You can make one too! Here's how:

1. Get a blank notebook
2. Go do something fun (play, explore, learn, create)
3. Draw what happened
4. Write about how you felt
5. Keep it forever

### Fun Questions to Ask Yourself

- What made me happy today?
- What did I learn?
- Who made me laugh?
- What was hard but I did it anyway?
- What am I proud of?

### Share Your Adventures

Did you make an Adventure Atlas?

Take a photo and show your friends!

Adventures are even more fun when people hear about them.
