## Your Relationship Assets

**Purpose:** Map every capability in your ecosystem and who owns it.

**Asset Class:** Relationship Assets (Layer 12)

---

## RELATIONSHIP TIERS

### **TIER 1: Direct Relationships (10-20 people)**
People who answer immediately, take calls, help without hesitation.

**Value:** Warm introductions, immediate feedback, collaboration, capital access  
**Revenue:** $0 direct (enables everything else)

**Action:** List these 10-20 people by name

---

### **TIER 2: Warm Network (50-100 people)**
Previous clients, event attendees, LinkedIn connections, people you've helped.

**Value:** Deal sourcing, customer generation, partnerships, information  
**Revenue:** $5K-20K/month (brokerage + referrals + JVs)

**Action:** Identify 30-50 to nurture quarterly

---

### **TIER 3: Community/Audience (1K-10K people)**
Newsletter subscribers, TikTok followers, community members, course students.

**Value:** Customer generation, deal validation, content distribution, talent  
**Revenue:** $15K-50K/month (community + courses + consulting leads)

**Action:** Grow to 10K-50K followers by Month 6

---

### **TIER 4: Institutional Access (3-5 networks)**
Business groups, entrepreneur meetups, investor networks, associations.

**Value:** Access to many people, credibility, partnerships, speaking  
**Revenue:** $20K-50K/month (sponsorships + partnerships + speaking)

**Action:** Join 2-3 key networks, become known

---

## CAPABILITY MAPPING

### **CAPABILITY 1: Capital Access** (Current: 2/10 → Target: 8/10)

**Gap:** Need 5-10 investor relationships

**Action:**
- Ask Tier 1: "Who do you know who invests?"
- Get 3-5 case studies proving model
- Attend investor events
- Hire broker for intros

**Revenue Impact:** $1M-10M capital availability

---

### **CAPABILITY 2: Operator Network** (Current: 6/10 → Target: 9/10)

**Gap:** Need 50-100 pre-qualified operators

**Action:**
- Community: Invite operators
- Events: Host operator workshops
- Content: Target operators on LinkedIn
- Referrals: Ask for intros

**Revenue Impact:** $20K-50K/month (deal brokerage)

---

### **CAPABILITY 3: Deal Flow** (Current: 5/10 → Target: 9/10)

**Gap:** Need 5-10 consistent deal sources

**Action:**
- Build relationships with accountants/lawyers
- Offer finder fee (they bring deal, you close)
- Community sources deals
- Systematic research weekly

**Revenue Impact:** $20K-100K/month (deals + acquisitions)

---

### **CAPABILITY 4: Talent/Contractors** (Current: 6/10 → Target: 9/10)

**Gap:** Need 2-3 trusted in each discipline

**Action:**
- Use current network
- Hire from community
- Ask for referrals
- Build bench (Upwork/Toptal)

**Revenue Impact:** Faster scaling (reduces time to market)

---

### **CAPABILITY 5: Marketing/Distribution** (Current: 6/10 → Target: 8/10)

**Gap:** Need 5-10 distribution partners

**Action:**
- Build relationships with creators
- Invite podcasters to speak
- Partner via revenue share
- Recruit from community

**Revenue Impact:** 50%+ faster audience growth

---

### **CAPABILITY 6: Industry Expertise** (Current: 8/10 → Target: 9/10)

**Gap:** Need deep expertise in 5-10 sectors

**Action:**
- Hire consultants ($2K-5K/month)
- Recruit experts to community (equity/revenue share)
- Interview experts (build relationship)
- Advisory board

**Revenue Impact:** Better deals (higher success rate)

---

### **CAPABILITY 7: Legal/Compliance** (Current: 3/10 → Target: 8/10)

**Gap:** Need lawyer + accountant

**Action:**
- Get referrals from investors/founders
- Contact firms in your space
- Retain on monthly basis

**Revenue Impact:** Risk reduction (saves 10% on deals)

---

### **CAPABILITY 8: Leadership/Mentorship** (Current: 5/10 → Target: 8/10)

**Gap:** Need 1-2 experienced mentors

**Action:**
- Ask directly for mentorship
- Offer equity/revenue share
- Quarterly check-ins
- Find through community

**Revenue Impact:** Avoid costly mistakes (saves 20%)
