# CAPITAL-TARGETING-STRATEGY.md
## How Money Enters the System

**Purpose:** Map how capital flows into your venture studio across 8 revenue streams.

**Question Answered:** > Which money should we pursue first, and in what sequence?

---

## THE 8 CAPITAL SOURCES

### **Stream 1: Consulting Revenue (IMMEDIATE)**
**What:** $250/hr AI automation consulting  
**Time to Revenue:** 48 hours  
**Effort:** 10-15 hours/week capacity  
**Monthly Potential:** $10K-20K  
**Why First:** Fastest revenue, builds proof, funds everything else

---

### **Stream 2: Community Membership (FAST)**
**What:** $300-500/month membership  
**Time to Revenue:** 24 hours  
**Monthly Potential:** $15K-50K/month (50-100 members)  
**Why Second:** Recurring revenue, creates deal flow, builds network

---

### **Stream 3: Courses & Products (MEDIUM)**
**What:** Digital courses ($297-500) + playbooks  
**Time to Revenue:** 1 week  
**Monthly Potential:** $5K-15K/month  
**Why Third:** Leveraged income, scales without time

---

### **Stream 4: SOP Licensing (SCALING)**
**What:** License playbooks ($100-500/mo per license)  
**Time to Revenue:** 2 weeks  
**Monthly Potential:** $12K-24K/month per playbook (10 playbooks = $120K+)  
**Why Fourth:** Most scalable, semi-passive

---

### **Stream 5: Speaking & Sponsorships (VISIBLE)**
**What:** Speaking fees ($5K/event) + sponsorship ($2K-5K/event)  
**Time to Revenue:** 1 month  
**Monthly Potential:** $10K-20K/month (2-4 events/month)  
**Why Fifth:** Builds authority, feeds audience

---

### **Stream 6: Deal Sourcing & Brokerage (HIGH-VALUE)**
**What:** Finder fee (10-20% of deal value)  
**Time to Revenue:** 1 month  
**Monthly Potential:** $20K-50K/month (5-10 deals)  
**Why Sixth:** High leverage, creates portfolio

---

### **Stream 7: Event Sponsorships (COMMUNITY)**
**What:** Sponsor packages ($5K-25K) for events  
**Time to Revenue:** 3 weeks  
**Monthly Potential:** $20K-50K/event (12 events/year)  
**Why Seventh:** Community-driven revenue, builds network

---

### **Stream 8: Equity & Acquisition (LONG-TERM)**
**What:** 20-30% equity stakes in portfolio companies  
**Time to Revenue:** 6-12 months  
**Monthly Potential:** $100K-500K/year (portfolio exits)  
**Why Eighth:** Massive upside, requires portfolio build first

---

## CAPITAL PROGRESSION

### **Phase 0 (Week 1-2): $8-15K**
- Consulting: $5-10K
- Community: $3-5K (founding members)

### **Phase 1 (Month 2): $25-50K**
- Add: Courses + Speaking
- Consulting scaling (higher rates)

### **Phase 2 (Month 3-4): $75-150K**
- Add: SOP Licensing + Deal Brokerage
- All streams active

### **Phase 3 (Month 5-6): $150-250K**
- Add: Event Sponsorships
- Equity returns beginning

### **Phase 4 (Month 7-12): $350-500K/month**
- All 8 streams running
- SaaS launched
- Portfolio companies generating returns

**Year 1 Total: $1.5M-2.5M**

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION

### **Months 1-2:** Revenue $8-15K/month
```
70% to operations ($5.6-10.5K): Salary, tools, marketing
20% to reinvestment ($1.6-3K): Ads, content
10% contingency ($0.8-1.5K)
```

### **Months 3-6:** Revenue $75-150K/month
```
50% to scaling ($37.5-75K): Team, tools, ads, acquisitions
30% to founder ($22.5-45K): Your salary
15% to development ($11.25-22.5K): SaaS prep
5% contingency ($3.75-7.5K)
```

### **Months 7-12:** Revenue $200-500K/month
```
40% to scaling ($80-200K): Team, infrastructure, acquisitions
25% to founder ($50-125K): Your salary
20% to development ($40-100K): SaaS scaling
10% to portfolio ($20-50K): Company optimization
5% contingency ($10-25K)
```
