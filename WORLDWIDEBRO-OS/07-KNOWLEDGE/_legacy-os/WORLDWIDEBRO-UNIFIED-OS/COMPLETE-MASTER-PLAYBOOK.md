## PART 5: SELLING TO WEALTHY PEOPLE, LENDERS, INVESTORS

### Segment 1: High-Net-Worth Individuals ($1M-50M+)

**What They Want:**
- 25-35% annual returns
- Passive income
- Diversification
- Proof of concept

**Your Pitch:**
```
"We source, vet, and optimize profitable businesses
generating 25-40% annual returns through operational improvement.
Your capital: We manage. Your returns: 25-35% annually."
```

**Deal Structure:**
- You find acquisition (net $10-50K deal value)
- HNI puts up 40-60% capital
- You retain 20-40% equity
- Operate for 3-5 years
- Exit at 3-5x multiple
- HNI gets: 25-35% IRR, you get: equity growth + operations fees

**How to Reach:**
- Events (host them, get sponsor intros)
- Referrals (through operators, lawyers, accountants, brokers)
- Direct outreach (LinkedIn warm intros)
- Exclusive network (build one)

**Target:** 10-20 HNI commitments = $500K-2M capital

---

### Segment 2: Institutional Investors (VCs, PE, Angels)

**What They Want:**
- 20-30% IRR
- Portfolio approach (multiple bets)
- Founder-led execution
- Clear exit timeline

**Your Pitch:**
```
"We've built a deal sourcing and operator network
across 31 sectors. We manage a fund investing in opportunities,
targeting 25-30% annual returns."
```

**Deal Structure:**
- You raise fund ($1M-10M)
- Investors become LPs (limited partners)
- You're GP (general partner, get 2% AUM + carry)
- Deploy capital into 10-20 opportunities per year
- Exit 20% of portfolio annually
- Target 25-30% annual returns to LPs

**How to Reach:**
- Warm intros from operators (they know angels)
- LinkedIn + Twitter (build audience, show thesis)
- Pitch events (AngelList, local investor groups)
- Operator referrals (they know VCs)

**Target:** $1-5M initial fund commitments

---

### Segment 3: Lenders (Banks, Alternative, SBA)

**What They Want:**
- Predictable repayment
- Underlying assets
- Cash flow visibility
- Low default risk

**Your Pitch:**
```
"We identify pre-vetted, pre-optimized small businesses
with strong cash flow and apply our operational system.
Default rate: <3%. Average lending term: 3-5 years."
```

**Deal Structure:**
- You source opportunity worth $100K-1M
- They lend 60-70% of deal value
- You put up 20-30% equity
- Operate business
- They get: Interest (8-12%) + fees (2-3%) annually
- Repay loan in 3-5 years

**How to Reach:**
- SBA-approved lenders (their officers have deals)
- Bank relationship managers (explain thesis)
- Alternative lenders (Kabbage, OnDeck, etc)
- Factoring companies (great for service businesses)

**Target:** $2-10M annual lending access
