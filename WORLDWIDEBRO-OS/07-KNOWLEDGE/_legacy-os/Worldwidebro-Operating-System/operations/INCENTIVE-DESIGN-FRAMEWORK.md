# Incentive Design Framework — Worldwidebro Holdings

**Date:** 2026-06-16  
**Purpose:** Align team, contractor, and partner incentives with 4-layer capital system  
**Related:** 07-PARTNERSHIP-ECONOMICS.md (partnership models), UNIT-ECONOMICS-BY-VENTURE-TYPE.csv (venture profitability)

---

## PRINCIPLE

Every person on the team should have a job, a clear number, and a way to win.

---

## LAYER 1: SERVICE VENTURES ($5K-$15K/mo)

### Service Operator

**Job:** Execute contracts profitably  
**Clear Number:** $8K/mo revenue, 40-50% margin

**Compensation:**

| Element | % of Profit | Amount | Trigger |
|---------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Base | 40% | $2-3K/mo | Monthly |
| Performance | 20% | $1-2K/mo | Hit $8K revenue target |
| Equity | 10% | Ownership % | Annual (if $20K+/mo) |

**Example:** $12K monthly revenue = $6K profit → $2,400 base + $1,200 bonus = $3,600/mo

---

## LAYER 2: PRODUCT VENTURES ($20K-$30K/mo)

### Product Lead (SaaS, Templates, Courses)

**Job:** Build and scale product to $3K+ MRR  
**Clear Number:** $3K-$5K MRR, 60%+ gross margin

**Compensation:**

| Element | Structure | Trigger |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Base | $2-3K/mo | Monthly |
| Milestone | $1K | Hit $1K MRR |
| Milestone | $2K | Hit $3K MRR |
| Profit-share | 30% of net profit | Ongoing after $3K MRR |
| Equity | 20% of venture | Launch (earn to 30%) |

**Example:** Product at $8K MRR = $5K profit → $2,500 base + $1,500 profit-share = $4,000/mo + equity

### Growth Lead

**Job:** Drive user acquisition  
**Clear Number:** CAC <$50, 20% MoM growth, 70% month-3 retention

**Compensation:**

| Element | Amount | Trigger |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Base | $1.5-2K/mo | Monthly |
| User bonus | $10/user | Retention verified |
| Referral bonus | 10% of referred ARR | Ongoing |
| Equity | 5-10% | Performance milestone |

---

## LAYER 3: ACQUISITION VENTURES ($24K-$75K/mo)

### Acquisition Manager

**Job:** Scout, acquire, and optimize SMBs  
**Clear Number:** $20K-$100K deals, 34% annual ROI

**Compensation:**

| Element | Structure | Trigger |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Base | $3-5K/mo | Monthly |
| Deal bonus | 3-5% of price | Close acquisition |
| Performance | 10% of annual profit | Year-end |
| Equity | 5-10% of venture | Acquisition |

**Example:** $80K acquisition → $2,400-4,000 bonus + $2,800 year-1 performance = $5K salary + $5K+ bonus + equity

---

## LAYER 4: CAPITAL COMPOUNDING ($8K-$15K/mo)

### Portfolio Manager

**Job:** Deploy capital across 20-30 holdings, compound yield  
**Clear Number:** 15-20% annual yield, <10% quarterly drawdown

**Compensation:**

| Element | Amount | Trigger |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Base | $2K/mo | Monthly |
| Performance fee | 10% of profits | Quarterly |
| Equity stake | 5% of fund | Annual |

---

## CONTRACTOR MODELS

**Layer 1 Service:** Contractor gets 60%, Operator margin 40%  
**Layer 2 Growth:** 15-20% of new ARR brought in  
**Layer 3 Ops:** 2-3% of acquisition price  

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

1. **What success looks like** (their number)
2. **How they're tracked** (monthly/quarterly)
3. **How they win** (comp + equity + bonuses)
4. **What happens if they fail** (reset or pivot)

### Monthly Check-in

- Did you hit your number?
- Did you profit more than last month?
- What's the blocker to next milestone?

---

## SUMMARY

✅ Alignment: Everyone profits when business profits  
✅ Scaling: Comp grows with revenue, not headcount  
✅ Ownership: Equity aligns long-term success  
✅ Sustainability: Layer 1 funds growth; Layers 2+3+4 compound  
✅ Fairness: Clear numbers, clear rewards
