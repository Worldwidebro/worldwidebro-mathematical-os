## Escalation to Hermes (Holding Company)

**Finance** escalates when:
- Cash position falls below 30-day runway (Tier 1 OPCO)
- Any Tier 1 OPCO misses revenue target by >20%

**Technology** escalates when:
- Platform downtime impacts >15 minutes (Tier 1 OPCO)
- Security incident affects >1,000 users

**Operations** escalates when:
- Critical process misses SLA by >50%
- Compliance deadline at risk

**Sales** escalates when:
- Tier 1 OPCO pipeline misses target by >20% (2+ consecutive months)
- Major deal ($50K+) at risk of loss

**Marketing** escalates when:
- Tier 1 OPCO lead targets missed by >25% (2+ consecutive months)

**Product** escalates when:
- Major feature miss impacts Tier 1 OPCO by >$100K MRR

**People** escalates when:
- Turnover exceeds 20% in any quarter
- Tier 1 OPCO loses >2 key executives

**Legal** escalates when:
- Regulatory investigation initiated
- Litigation exposure >$1M (Tier 1 OPCO)

**Risk** escalates when:
- Material security breach (>1K records affected)
- Uninsured liability >$500K (Tier 1 OPCO)

**Research** escalates when:
- Data quality issues impact >5% of strategic decisions
- Competitive intelligence reveals >20% market shift

**Customer Success** escalates when:
- Tier 1 OPCO churn >5% in quarter
- >2 high-value customers at risk

**Governance** escalates when:
- Tier 1 OPCO receives qualified audit opinion
- >5 Critical findings from audit

## Appendix: Glossary

- **Liaison Agent:** Named agent responsible for OPCO-department relationship
- **SLA:** Service-level agreement (response time commitment)
- **OPCO:** Operating company (market/territory)
- **Tier:** Revenue/strategic importance classification (1-4)
- **Escalation:** Decision routing to higher authority
- **Hermes:** Holding company AI agent for enterprise-wide decisions
- **CSM:** Customer Success Manager
- **CAE:** Chief Audit Executive
- **MRR:** Monthly Recurring Revenue
- **CSAT:** Customer Satisfaction Score
- **NPS:** Net Promoter Score
