# IZA OS Operating Company Charters
## 18 Autonomous Market Territories | Governance & Decision Framework

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Authority:** Hermes (Strategic Coordinator)  
**Audience:** OPCO Presidents, Board, Capability Department Heads

---

## Executive Summary

This charter defines the autonomous governance model for 18 Operating Companies (OPCOs). Each OPCO owns a market territory—not individual ventures, but entire sectors—and operates with clear decision authority, escalation thresholds, and department relationships. OPCOs coordinate vertically through Hermes and horizontally through 12 capability departments.

**Core Principle:** Delegate aggressively to OPCO Presidents; escalate financial and strategic decisions only when necessary.

---

## Governance Framework

### Decision Authority Threshold

| Decision Type | Authority | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational** (<$100K) | OPCO President | None |
| **Capital Investment** ($100K-$1M) | OPCO President + Hermes Approval | Hermes Budget Council |
| **Strategic Initiative** (>$1M or Multi-OPCO) | Hermes + Board | Board |
| **Personnel** (Hire/Fire/Comp) | OPCO President | Hermes (if >$500K annual) |
| **Compliance/Risk** (Any severity) | OPCO President + Legal | Hermes + Risk Officer |
| **Venture Launch** (New to portfolio) | OPCO President | Hermes (architecture review) |
| **Venture Exit** (Divestiture) | OPCO President | Hermes (>$500K loss) |

### Escalation Path

```
OPCO President
    ↓ (>$100K or Strategic)
Hermes Coordinator
    ↓ (>$1M or Board-level)
Executive Board
    ↓ (Policy/Regulatory)
Legal + Risk Committees
```

## Cross-OPCO Governance

**Hermes Responsibilities:**
- Strategic alignment across OPCOs
- Capital allocation and investment decisions
- Department resource prioritization
- Conflict resolution between OPCOs
- Policy and compliance oversight
- Performance monitoring and forecasting

**Monthly Cadence:**
- Executive dashboard review (KPIs, risks, escalations)
- Department budget and resource allocation
- Cross-OPCO strategic initiatives
- Venture health scorecard review
- Risk and compliance audit

### Venture Governance Across OPCOs

**Venture Ownership:**
- Each venture belongs to exactly one OPCO
- OPCO President owns venture performance
- Hermes owns portfolio-level decisions

**Inter-OPCO Ventures:**
- Ventures serving multiple OPCOs report through primary owner
- Secondary OPCOs access through service agreements
- Revenue allocation: Primary owner retains 80%, secondary OPCOs get 20%

5. Marketing & Brand
6. Customer Success & Operations
7. Risk & Insurance Management
