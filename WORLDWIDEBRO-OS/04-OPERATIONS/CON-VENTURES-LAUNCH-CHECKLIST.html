CON Ventures — Launch Checklist to First Dollar

Winners Circle WC LLC · Charlotte, NC · 20 construction ventures · Updated 2026-06-24

How to use this: The 6-step launch checklist is identical for every CON venture. Only Step 2 (license) changes by trade — that splits the 20 ventures into 3 tiers. Start with Tier A (no license, fastest dollar), sub under GCs for Tier B, and get a license or licensed partner for Tier C.
Verify current NC thresholds/rules before relying on them — boards update periodically.

The Universal 6-Step Checklist (every venture)

  • Step 0 — Insurance: bind $1M/$2M General Liability (Next, Thimble, biBERK, Hiscox). Add Workers' Comp once you have W-2 employees; subs carry their own.
  • Step 1 — Entity ready: operate under Winners Circle WC LLC; confirm NC business/privilege registration + EIN; open business bank account.
  • Step 2 — License (TRADE-SPECIFIC → see tier tables below): obtain the NC license the trade requires, OR start by subcontracting under a licensed GC.
  • Step 3 — Offer + pricing: define scope, line up crew/subs, set your markup rate before quoting.
  • Step 4 — Subcontract pre-quals: register with NC GCs winning federal/commercial work (SYNCON, HICAPS, Group III, Riley, CMC) — then call estimating.
  • Step 5 — Federal track (parallel): APEX Accelerator (free help) → Login.gov → UEI + SAM.gov registration (honest reps & certs) → SBA certs.
  • Step 6 — Contracts in hand: Subcontractor Agreement, Client Service Agreement, Independent Contractor Agreement (see contracts section).

Recommended Launch Order

  • Week 1 (money): Pick 1 Tier A venture you can personally deliver + 1 Tier B to sub (e.g., CON-009 Roofing). Insurance + 5 GC pre-quals + first calls.
  • Week 2-4: SAM.gov registration (Step 5) in parallel. First sub task order landed.
  • Month 2-3: NCLBGC license application for Tier B priming. Reinvest first revenue.
  • Month 3+: Pursue Tier C via licensed partner or your own exam.

Contracts Each Venture Needs

Every CON venture runs on the same 3 core agreements. Draft each to attorney-review standard (clear parties, scope, payment, IP/ownership, confidentiality, warranties, indemnification, termination, dispute resolution, governing law = North Carolina):

  • Subcontractor Agreement — between Winners Circle WC LLC and the crews/subs you hire (defines scope, pay, insurance requirements, liability).
  • Client / Master Service Agreement — between you and the GC or homeowner client (scope, deliverables, payment schedule, change orders, warranty).
  • Independent Contractor Agreement — for 1099 labor (status, payment, no-benefits, IP, confidentiality).

These are reusable across all 20 ventures — draft once, swap the scope/trade per venture.
