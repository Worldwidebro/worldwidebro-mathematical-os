# Service Venture Kit — Sector-Agnostic Template

**What this is:** The reusable clone of the construction sub kit. Operationalizes **Engines A (Contract/B2G), C (Productized), B (Retainer)** — any B2B/B2G service venture that sells labor + compliance + capacity into primes, enterprises, or government.

**Proven instance:** `CON-*` files (construction). **Next targets:** Medical Courier (logistics), cleaning/inspection (productized), IT-services (retainer).

> **To clone a new venture, swap only 3 things.** Everything else carries over unchanged.

---

## THE 3 SWAPS (10-minute clone)

| Swap | Construction example | Your new venture |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| **{{SERVICE}}** — what you deliver | roofing / general labor | 🟦 _________ |
| **{{BUYERS}}** — who pays (the primes/agencies) | NC general contractors | 🟦 _________ |
| **{{NAICS}}** — industry codes | 236220 / 238190 / 238990 | 🟦 _________ |

Then pick the engine, fill the buyer-channel row, and the 4 sub-docs below auto-fit.

---

## ENGINE SELECTOR (pick the motion)

| Engine | When it fits {{SERVICE}} | Buyer channel | First-$ |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------|---------|
| **A — Contract / B2G sub** | Field labor sold into gov/prime contracts | Bid boards + prime prequal lists + diversity certs | Days |
| **C — Productized service** | Standardized, fixed-price, repeatable unit | Direct outbound + web offer ("$499 audit") | Days–weeks |
| **B — B2B retainer** | Ongoing managed service | Sales-led outbound to SMB/enterprise | Weeks |

> A is procurement-driven (forms + certs). B/C are sales-driven (no bid boards). Same kit, different buyer channel.

## THE 4 SUB-DOCS (clone these per venture)

### 1) Capability statement → `{{VENTURE}}-CAPABILITY-STATEMENT.md`
Pattern: `CON-CAPABILITY-STATEMENT.md`. Swap {{SERVICE}}/{{BUYERS}}/{{NAICS}}. Keep: company data block, M/W/SB cert angle, references-on-request, 1-page PDF export. **Only true blanks per venture: insurance + phone.**

### 2) Sales scripts → `{{VENTURE}}-SALES-SCRIPTS.md`
Pattern: `CON-SALES-SCRIPTS.md`. Cold call + voicemail + 3-touch email + appointment-set + close.
- **Engine A ask:** "Add me to your prequalified {{SERVICE}} list."
- **Engine C ask:** "Can I send you a fixed-price {{SERVICE}} quote?"
- **Engine B ask:** "Can we do 15 min on managing your {{SERVICE}} ongoing?"

### 3) Formation & credential tracker → `{{VENTURE}}-FORMATION-TRACKER.md`
Pattern: `CON-FORMATION-CREDENTIAL-TRACKER.md`. Universal spine:
- Insurance (GL) — first domino, every engine
- UEI/SAM — Engine A federal only
- Certs: NC HUB / Charlotte CBI / SBA SDB-8(a) — multiplier for A; helpful for B/C gov-adjacent buyers
- Entity = Winners Circle WC LLC (sub-first; spin dedicated LLC before physical/high-liability work)

### 4) Buyer list + channels → reuse `GOV-CONTRACT-REVENUE-ACTION-SHEET.md` pattern
Where {{SERVICE}} work lives, by engine:

| Engine | Channels to register/target |
|--------|----------------------------|
| **A** | BuildingConnected, PlanHub, Procore network · NC eVP (evp.nc.gov), NCDOT, SAM.gov · prime prequal forms |
| **C** | Direct outbound list + simple web offer + Google Business Profile |
| **B** | Targeted account list (hospitals, law firms, PE-owned SMBs, property mgmt) + LinkedIn + referrals |

---

## UNIVERSAL FIRST MOVES (any service venture)

1. Bind **General Liability insurance** (the one real cost, ~$50–150/mo)
2. Fill capability statement (insurance + phone) → 1-page PDF
3. Register on the engine's buyer channels (mostly free)
4. Run the sales scripts → book appointments → close first deal
5. Deliver clean → ask for the next job + a reference

> **Cost to be in-market (Engine A/C): ~under $500.** Free: UEI/SAM, all diversity certs, all sub bid boards.
