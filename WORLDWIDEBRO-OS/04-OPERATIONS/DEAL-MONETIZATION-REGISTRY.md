# Deal & Monetization Registry — All 18 Sectors

**How you middleman each sector and where the money comes in.** Middleman = control flow, don't produce/own. Grounded in `ventures-master.csv` (712). Flags: ✅ $0 pursue-now · 🔧 build-heavy (SaaS) · 💰 asset/capital · ⚠️ license/bond trap · 🚫 not viable.

> Monetization = how the spread is captured. 5 ways money comes in: **MARGIN** (buy-sell spread), **FEE/TAKE-RATE** (% of transaction), **REFERRAL** (finder's fee), **RETAINER** (recurring), **LICENSING** (IP/franchise).

## WHY MONETIZATION IS REAL HERE (and not in the rest)

The ✅ rows monetize because **you sit between an existing buyer and existing supply** — the demand and the labor both already exist; you just route and take the spread. The 🔧 "AI" rows (128 ventures) have to *build a product first* — there's no spread to capture until software ships. The 🚫/⚠️ rows need capital, charters, or licenses.

**So "how to bring monetization in across all ventures" honestly resolves to:** ~45 ventures can capture a spread *this week with $0*; ~128 need a build; the rest need capital or a license. The money comes in via **margin, fee, referral, retainer, or licensing** — and the fastest, proven one is construction crew margin.
