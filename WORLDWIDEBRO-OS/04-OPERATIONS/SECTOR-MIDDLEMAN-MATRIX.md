# Sector Middleman Matrix — Zero-Cost Deal Plays

**Goal:** Where you can **broker / subcontract / middleman** jobs without producing the service or owning the asset — sorted by how little it costs to enter.

**Models (per sector lock-on):** `MATCH` marketplace · `BROKER` deal originator · `ARB` arbitrage/resell · `NET` contractor network · `PLAT` platform.
**Middleman = control of flow** (people · labor · information · capital · contracts). Certs/registries are permission layers, not income — see [`GO-TO-MARKET-ENGINES.md`](../08-DATA/Influence-Venture-Business-OS/INFRASTRUCTURE_LAYERS/venture-hub/docs/GO-TO-MARKET-ENGINES.md).

| **logistics — freight brokerage** | ~15 of 30 | MC Authority + surety bond required | ~$300 reg + **$75,000 BMC-84 bond** + process agents | Broker *as an agent under* an existing licensed broker; OR stick to intrastate courier (Tier 1) |

| **education (accredited)** | subset | Accreditation = slow + costly | $$$ + years | Stay in non-accredited training/affiliate (Tier 2) |

## THE MOVE

1. **Tier 1 only, now.** ~218 ventures, $0 entry. Construction is already kitted (`CON-*`) — clone the `SERVICE-VENTURE-KIT-TEMPLATE.md` into staffing + facilities + courier-coordination next.
2. **Avoid Tier 3 traps** (freight bond, FINRA, RE license) until a specific deal pays for the barrier.
3. **Defensibility upgrade later:** once deals flow manually, build the routing system (lead → CRM → sub/crew → invoice). That's the high-level middleman — funded by Tier 1 cash, not before.

> Reality check: this matrix is the map. The first dollar is still a construction sub call (SYNCON). Middleman income starts when you route ONE real deal, not when the matrix is complete.
