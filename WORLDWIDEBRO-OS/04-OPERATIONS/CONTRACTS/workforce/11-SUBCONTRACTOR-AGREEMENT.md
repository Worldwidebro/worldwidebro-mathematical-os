## 1. SCOPE OF WORK
Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, materials, tools, and supervision to perform the work described in **Exhibit A** (the "Work") at 🟨[PROJECT SITE/ADDRESS], in a good and workmanlike manner consistent with the Prime Contract, all plans/specs, and applicable code.

## 2. TERM & SCHEDULE
Work begins 🟨[START] and shall be substantially complete by 🟨[END], time being of the essence. Subcontractor shall coordinate with Contractor's schedule and the Prime's site rules.

## 3. PRICE & PAYMENT
- **Subcontract price:** 🟨$[AMOUNT] (lump sum) OR 🟨$[RATE]/unit per Exhibit A.
- **Payment terms:** Contractor shall pay Subcontractor within 🟨[7/10] days **after Contractor receives payment from the Prime for the Work** (pay-when-paid). Receipt of payment by Contractor from the Prime is a condition precedent.
- **Retainage:** 🟨[5/10]% retained until final completion and acceptance.
- **Lien waivers:** Subcontractor shall submit a conditional/unconditional lien waiver with each payment application.

## 4. INSURANCE (condition of starting work)
Before mobilizing, Subcontractor shall provide a **Certificate of Insurance** evidencing:
- Commercial General Liability: min 🟨$1,000,000 per occurrence
- Workers' Compensation: statutory NC limits (or valid NC exemption)
- Auto liability if vehicles used on site
Subcontractor shall name **Winners Circle WC LLC and the Prime as additional insureds**, primary and non-contributory.

## 5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
Subcontractor is an independent contractor (1099), not an employee. Subcontractor controls means/methods, supplies its own crew, tools, and pays its own taxes, wages, and benefits. Nothing creates a partnership or joint venture.

## 6. COMPLIANCE & LICENSING
Subcontractor warrants it holds all required NC trade licenses for the Work, and shall comply with OSHA, the Prime's safety program, permits, and all applicable laws. Subcontractor is responsible for its own permits where its trade requires.

## 7. WARRANTY
Subcontractor warrants the Work against defects in workmanship and materials for 🟨[12] months after final completion, and shall promptly correct defective Work at its own cost.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION
To the fullest extent permitted by NC law, Subcontractor shall indemnify and hold harmless Contractor and Prime from claims, damages, and liabilities arising out of Subcontractor's Work, negligence, or breach — to the extent caused by Subcontractor's acts or omissions.

## 9. NON-CIRCUMVENTION / NON-SOLICITATION  *(protects the spread)*
During this Agreement and for 🟨[12] months after, Subcontractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, contract with, or perform work for the Prime or any client introduced by Contractor on this project, except through Contractor. Breach entitles Contractor to the fee it would have earned plus costs.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY
Subcontractor shall keep confidential all pricing, client information, and Contractor's business terms. (Incorporates NDA #3.)

## 11. TERMINATION
Contractor may terminate for cause (default uncured within 🟨[3] days of notice) or for convenience upon 🟨[7] days' notice, paying for Work properly performed to the date of termination.

## 12. CHANGE ORDERS
No change to scope or price is valid unless in a written change order signed by both parties before the changed Work proceeds.

## 13. DISPUTES & GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of North Carolina. Disputes shall first be mediated in 🟨[Mecklenburg County], NC; unresolved disputes resolved by binding arbitration / NC courts in that county.

## 14. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement + Exhibit A is the entire agreement, superseding prior discussions. Amendments must be written and signed. If any provision conflicts with the Prime Contract, the Prime Contract's stricter requirement flows down to Subcontractor.
