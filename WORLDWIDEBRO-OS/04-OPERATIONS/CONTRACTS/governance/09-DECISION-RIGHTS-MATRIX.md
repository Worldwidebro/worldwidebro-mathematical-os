# DECISION RIGHTS MATRIX
**Governance framework for WinnersCircle WC LLC**

## STRATEGIC DECISIONS (Owner Authority)

| Decision | Approve? | Notice | Details |
|----------|----------|--------|---------|
| New venture | Owner | 30 days | $50K+ capital allocation |
| Dissolve venture | Owner | 30 days | Based on tier classification |
| Change profit split | Owner | 30 days | Amendments to operating agreement |
| Raise external capital | Owner | N/A | Investment, loans, equity |
| Major acquisition | Owner | 30 days | >$500K commitment |
| Strategic pivot | Owner | 30 days | Changes to venture portfolio |

## OPERATIONAL DECISIONS (OPCO Authority)

| Decision | OPCO | Threshold | Escalate? |
|----------|------|-----------|-----------|
| Hire contractor | OPCO + Owner review | All | Yes, all |
| Spend capital | OPCO autonomous | <$50K | Yes if >$50K |
| Contracts | OPCO | <$100K | Yes if >$100K |
| Client acquisition | OPCO autonomous | All | No |
| Service delivery | OPCO autonomous | All | No |
| Pricing changes | OPCO | Standard | Owner if >10% |

## IP & DATA DECISIONS (Owner Authority)

| Decision | Who | Details |
|----------|-----|---------|
| IP assignment | Owner (final) | All contractor/employee work |
| Repo access | Owner | Approves who sees repo intelligence |
| Data retention | Owner | What data kept, deleted, archived |
| Confidentiality terms | Owner | NDA duration, scope, exceptions |
| Non-compete enforcement | Owner | Pursue violations or settle |

## FINANCIAL DECISIONS

| Decision | Authority | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Annual budget | Owner | By venture/sector |
| Spending variance | Owner | >10% of budget requires approval |
| Profit distribution | Owner | Quarterly/annually |
| Tax filings | Owner | Annual compliance |
| Audit engagement | Owner | Financial review, SOC 2, etc. |

## VENTURE TIER TRANSITIONS

| Tier | Criteria | Action | Authority |
|------|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | MRR >$10K | KEEP & SCALE | Owner/OPCO |
| 2 | MRR $3-10K | NURTURE | OPCO with Owner oversight |
| 3 | MRR <$3K | IMPROVE or CLOSE | 90-day plan, then Owner decision |
| 4 | Dead | DISSOLVE | Owner decision |

## ESCALATION RULES

**If uncertainty:** Ask owner (owner final authority on all major decisions).
