## 5) CASH-FLOW FUNDING ⭐ (the one that matters for subbing)

**The sub problem:** you pay the crew *before* the prime pays you (pay-when-paid, §3 of Subcontractor Agreement #11). That gap is the #1 reason subs run out of cash. Solutions:

| Tool | What | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| **Invoice factoring** | Sell the prime invoice for ~90–95% now | Bridges the pay-when-paid gap — construction-specific factors exist |
| **Material financing** (e.g. Billd, Knapp) | Finance materials, pay in 120 days | If a scope needs materials up front |
| **Line of credit** (Carolina Small Biz / bank) | Revolving working capital | Once you have a contract in hand |

> This is the funding you'll actually use first — not a startup loan, but **factoring the first won invoice** so you can pay the crew and keep the spread without fronting cash.
