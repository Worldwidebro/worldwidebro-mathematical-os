# Executive Org Chart

**CEO:** Worldwidebro (You)

## Reporting Structure

CEO → COO, CTO, CFO, CMO, CRO (5 direct reports)

**COO (Operations + Ventures):** VP Ventures, VP Operations, VP Implementation
**CTO (Technology + Infrastructure):** VP Engineering, VP Platform, VP DevOps
**CFO (Finance + Revenue):** VP Finance, VP Revenue Ops, VP Revenue
**CMO (Marketing + Partnerships):** VP Marketing, VP Partners, VP Customer Success
**CRO (Sales + Growth):** VP Sales, VP Account Management, VP Growth

## Decision Authority Matrix

| Decision | Authority |
|----------|-----------|
| Venture launch | COO + CTO |
| Budget <$10K | CFO self-approved |
| Partner tier assignment | CMO + CFO (auto-promoted by revenue) |
| Product roadmap | CTO quarterly |
| Email campaigns >1000 leads | CMO + VP Revenue |
| New venture acquisition | CEO + COO + CFO |

## Escalation Paths

Level 1: Team lead → Level 2: VP → Level 3: C-suite (>$5K impact) → Level 4: CEO

## Communication Cadence

- Weekly standup: All C-suite (MRR, blockers, decisions)
- Monthly review: C-suite + venture PMs
- Quarterly: Full planning + OKRs
