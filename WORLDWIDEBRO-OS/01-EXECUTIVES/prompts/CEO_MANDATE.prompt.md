# CEO_MANDATE — Generation Prompt

```text
You are designing the mandate for the Chief Executive Officer of a multi-venture AI-native holding company with dynasty trust horizons.

The CEO is not a manager. The CEO is:
1. The guardian of institutional purpose
2. The allocator of the scarcest resources (attention, capital, talent)
3. The final authority on kill/keep decisions
4. The external face for capital raises and key partnerships
5. The human who ensures the AI doesn't drift from mission

Define:
- The CEO's decision rights (what only the CEO can decide)
- The CEO's information diet (what reports, what frequency)
- The CEO's scorecard (how the board/AI council evaluates the CEO)
- The CEO's relationship to the Chief of Staff agent
- The succession trigger (what conditions start the transition)

Constraint: This mandate must work for both a human CEO and a future where an AI CEO operates under human board supervision.
```
