## Foundation Layer (5): WHO YOU ARE

### Layer 1: Identity & Self-Concept
Understanding who you are, your values, who you're becoming.
- Core habits: Daily reflection, values clarification, identity visualization
- Key skills: Self-awareness, character alignment, intentional identity design
- Mastery: From uncertain identity → aligned identity → mentor others

### Layer 2: Emotional Intelligence  
Managing emotions, understanding others, building relationships.
- Core habits: Emotion check-in, pause before reacting, empathy practice
- Key skills: Self-regulation, empathy, relationship repair, resilience
- Mastery: From reactive → regulated → emotionally wise leader

### Layer 3: Attention Management
Controlling focus, resisting distraction, deep work capacity.
- Core habits: Morning focus ritual, phone-free blocks, single-tasking
- Key skills: Concentration, flow states, distraction resistance
- Mastery: From scattered → 4-hour focus sessions → deep-work culture

### Layer 4: Decision-Making
Making choices aligned with values, considering tradeoffs and consequences.
- Core habits: Values check, consequence mapping, reversibility test
- Key skills: Tradeoff analysis, first-principles thinking
- Mastery: From indecisive → values-aligned decisions → decisions others learn from

### Layer 5: Purpose & Meaning
Knowing what matters, why your work has significance.
- Core habits: Annual purpose reflection, weekly service, impact journaling
- Key skills: Purpose articulation, contribution mindset, meaning-making
- Mastery: From purposeless → clear mission → inspiring others
