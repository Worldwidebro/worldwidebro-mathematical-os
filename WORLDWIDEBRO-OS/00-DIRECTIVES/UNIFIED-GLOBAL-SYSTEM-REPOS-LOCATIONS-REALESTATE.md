### AUCTION OPPORTUNITIES (Lisbon/Portugal)

**Where to find deals:**
1. **Portuguese Gov Auctions** (CPRA)
   - Foreclosed properties
   - Seized assets
   - Often 30-50% below market

2. **Bank Auctions** (Caixa Geral, BPI, others)
   - Foreclosures
   - Off-market deals
   - 40-60% of market value

3. **Bulk Property Sales**
   - Developers clearing inventory
   - 20-30% discounts
   - 5-10 properties at once

4. **Estate Sales / Inheritance**
   - Quick liquidations
   - Motivated sellers
   - Often below-market pricing

**Example Deal Path:**
1. Buy foreclosed apartment: €150K (vs €220K market)
2. Renovate: €10K (simple cosmetics)
3. Rent: €1,200/month = 9.6% gross yield (first year)
4. 5 years: Appreciate to €250K + collected rent
5. Sell: €100K profit + rent collected + tax benefits
