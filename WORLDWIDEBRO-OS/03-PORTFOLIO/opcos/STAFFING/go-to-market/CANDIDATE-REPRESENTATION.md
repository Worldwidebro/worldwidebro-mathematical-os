# Candidate Representation & Consent

> **Template — not legal advice.** Have a North Carolina attorney review.
> **Candidates never pay a fee** in contingency recruiting — the hiring employer
> pays. Keep this short; candidates sign it (or accept electronically) before you
> submit them to an employer.

---

**Candidate:** `[FULL NAME]`  ·  **Date:** `[DATE]`

By signing below, I authorize **Worldwidebro Staffing** ("Agency") to represent me
for employment opportunities and agree to the following:

1. **No fee to me.** I will never be charged a fee for Agency's placement services.
   The hiring employer pays Agency.

2. **Permission to submit.** Agency may share my resume, qualifications, and contact
   information with prospective employers for the purpose of considering me for roles.
   Agency will not submit me to a specific employer without my okay for that employer.

3. **Accuracy.** The information I provide (experience, skills, certifications, work
   authorization, references) is true and complete to the best of my knowledge.

4. **Background & references.** I authorize prospective employers and/or Agency to
   verify my references, employment history, and, where applicable and with separate
   consent as required by law, conduct background or drug screening.

5. **Work authorization.** I am legally authorized to work in the United States and
   will provide I-9 documentation to the hiring employer.

6. **At-will / no guarantee.** Agency does not guarantee placement, and any employment
   I accept is between me and the employer (at-will unless the employer states
   otherwise).

7. **Privacy.** I consent to Agency storing and processing my information to match me
   with opportunities. I may request deletion of my data at any time.

8. **Equal opportunity.** Agency represents candidates without regard to race, color,
   religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or other protected status.
