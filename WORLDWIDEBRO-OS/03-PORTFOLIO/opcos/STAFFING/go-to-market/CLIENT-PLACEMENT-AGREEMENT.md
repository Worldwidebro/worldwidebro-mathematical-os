# Direct-Hire / Placement Services Agreement

> **Template — not legal advice.** Have a North Carolina attorney review before
> sending to clients. Fill every `[BRACKET]`. This is a **contingency** recruiting
> agreement: the client pays **only when a referred candidate is hired**. It does
> **not** make you an employer of the candidate, so it avoids workers' comp,
> payroll, and EOR obligations.

---

## 1. Parties

This Placement Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on `[DATE]` between:

- **Agency:** `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` ("Agency"), `[ADDRESS]`, operating as **Worldwidebro Staffing**.
- **Client:** `[CLIENT COMPANY NAME]` ("Client"), `[ADDRESS]`.

## 2. Services

Agency will source, screen, and refer qualified candidates ("Candidates") to
Client for direct-hire positions ("Roles") that Client describes in a job order.
Agency performs **recruiting and referral services only**. Client retains sole
discretion over interviewing, selecting, and hiring any Candidate. Agency is **not**
the employer of any Candidate and is not responsible for the Candidate's wages,
benefits, taxes, or conduct after hire.

## 3. Placement Fee

For each Candidate that Client (or an affiliate of Client) hires, Client pays Agency
a one-time **Placement Fee** equal to:

> **`[20]`% of the Candidate's first-year base compensation.**

"First-year base compensation" means the Candidate's annualized base salary (or,
for hourly roles, hourly rate × 2,080), **excluding** bonuses, commissions, overtime,
and benefits, unless otherwise agreed in the job order.

**Minimum fee:** `[$5,000]` per placement.

## 4. Invoicing & Payment Terms

- Agency invoices Client on the Candidate's **start date**.
- Payment is due **net `[15]` days** from the invoice date.
- Late balances accrue interest at `[1.5]`% per month (or the maximum allowed by law).

## 5. Replacement Guarantee

If, within **`[90]` days** of the start date, the Candidate (a) voluntarily resigns
or (b) is terminated by Client for documented cause, Agency will recruit **one
replacement at no additional Placement Fee**, provided Client's account is current.

- The guarantee is void if the Role's duties or compensation materially change, or
  if Client's payment is past due.
- If no suitable replacement is delivered within `[60]` days of Client's written
  request, Agency will refund a prorated portion of the Placement Fee:
  `[100% if departure ≤30 days; 50% if 31–60 days; 25% if 61–90 days]`.

## 6. Fee Trigger & Referral Validity

- A Candidate is "referred" when Agency first submits the Candidate's name/resume to
  Client in writing.
- Client owes the Placement Fee if it hires a referred Candidate within **`[12]`
  months** of the referral date, whether or not for the original Role.
- If Client already has a bona fide, documented relationship with a Candidate prior
  to Agency's submission, Client must notify Agency in writing within **`[3]` business
  days** of the submission; otherwise the submission stands.

## 7. Non-Circumvention

For **`[12]` months** after a referral, Client will not refer the Candidate to a
third party (including affiliates, subsidiaries, or other employers) to avoid the
Placement Fee. Doing so makes the full Placement Fee immediately due.

## 8. Client Responsibilities

Client will: provide accurate Role descriptions and compensation ranges; review
submissions promptly; conduct its own interviews and any background/reference checks
it requires before hiring; and make all hiring decisions in compliance with
applicable equal-employment and labor laws.

## 9. Equal Opportunity & Lawful Sourcing

Agency sources Candidates without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national
origin, age, disability, or any other protected status, consistent with federal and
North Carolina law. Final hiring decisions and compliance therewith are Client's
responsibility.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information (Candidate data, Role
details, pricing) confidential and use it only to perform under this Agreement.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Agency makes no guarantee of any Candidate's performance, longevity, or fitness.
Agency's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the Placement Fee
actually paid for the relevant placement. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages.

## 12. Term & Termination

This Agreement is effective on the date above and continues until terminated by
either party with **`[15]` days** written notice. Sections 3–7, 10, and 11 survive
termination as to Candidates referred before termination.

## 13. Independent Contractors

The parties are independent contractors. Nothing here creates a partnership, joint
venture, or employment relationship between Agency and Client or between Agency and
any Candidate.

## 14. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina. Venue for any
dispute lies in `[Mecklenburg County]`, North Carolina.
