## 🏗️ Bankability Roadmap Structure

Each venture roadmap includes 6 layers over 12 weeks:

**Layer 1:** Identity (Weeks 1-2) — LLC, EIN, bank account, address, phone, email  
**Layer 2:** Credit & Leverage (Weeks 3-6) — DUNS, Net-30 accounts, business credit card, payment history  
**Layer 3:** Documentation System — Legal, financial, sales, contracts, proof assets, licensing  
**Layer 4:** Systems & Automation — Accounting, CRM, operations, communications  
**Layer 5:** Funding Readiness (Weeks 10-12) — Bank statements, revenue trend, credit score 70+, documentation  
**Layer 6:** Government Contracting — SAM.gov, UEI, NAICS, certifications, past performance, capability statement
