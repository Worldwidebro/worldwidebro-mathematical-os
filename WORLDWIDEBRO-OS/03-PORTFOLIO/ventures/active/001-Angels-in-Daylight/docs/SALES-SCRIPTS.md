# Sales Scripts — Angels In Daylight

This is a DTC streetwear apparel brand, not a services/B2G venture — the engines below replace the generic B2G template that was here before.

## Content hook (organic — Instagram/TikTok)

> "Saints don't dress like everyone else." — post the hero-reveal commercial
> (`angels-in-daylight/commercials/hero-reveal-c1/`), caption drives to bio link → storefront.

## Product page copy pattern (per SKU)

1. Lead with the visual (product shot from `product-shots/clean_transparent/`)
2. One line on the graphic story (saint/angel art, blackletter, graffiti-tag — whichever applies to that SKU)
3. Material + fit (from `AID-MASTER-SKU-LIST.csv` category/garment_type/colorways)
4. Price anchor: positioned below $150 hype-brand tier, above fast-fashion — $38-65 depending on category (tees lower, jackets higher)

## Launch sequence (first 10 SKUs)

| Step | Action | Channel |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Post hero-reveal commercial | Instagram Reels + TikTok |
| 2 | Storefront link in bio, first 10 core SKUs live | Medusa storefront |
| 3 | DM/comment reply script: "Link in bio — [SKU] drops now" | IG/TikTok comments |
| 4 | Retarget engagers with product carousel | Meta Ads (once first sales validate demand) |
| 5 | Streetwear community seeding (non-spammy, genuine posts) | Reddit r/streetwear, relevant Discords |

## Objection handling

- "Is this real/in stock?" → storefront checkout is live proof, no pre-order ambiguity
- "Why this price?" → point to material/fit details + limited catalog vs. mass-market
