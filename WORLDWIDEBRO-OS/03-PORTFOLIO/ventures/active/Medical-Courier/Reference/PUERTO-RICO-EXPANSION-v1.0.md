# Puerto Rico Expansion — Medical Courier

**Short answer: yes, it works in Puerto Rico.** PR is a US territory, so the entire federal compliance backbone (HIPAA, OSHA, DOT/PHMSA) applies exactly as in NC. You swap the *local* registration and licensing layer, account for Spanish-language operations, and you're in a market with unusually strong demand drivers.

---

## What stays the same (federal — identical to NC)
- **HIPAA** + Business Associate Agreements with every client — federal law, applies in PR
- **OSHA bloodborne pathogens (29 CFR 1910.1030)** — applies; PR operates under federal OSHA via **PR OSHA (PR Dept. of Labor / Depto. del Trabajo)**
- **DOT/PHMSA UN3373** packaging for infectious substances — federal, applies
- Commercial auto + cargo/cold-chain liability insurance
- The entire driver hiring + onboarding packet in `HIRING/` (translate to Spanish)

## What changes (local layer)
| Item | NC | Puerto Rico |
|---|---|---|
| Entity registration | NC Secretary of State | **PR Departamento de Estado** (Registro de Corporaciones) |
| Tax / merchant reg | NC Dept of Revenue | **PR Hacienda** — Merchant's Registration Certificate (Certificado de Registro de Comerciante) + IVU sales tax |
| DBA / business name | Mecklenburg Register of Deeds | PR Departamento de Estado |
| Driver records (MVR) | NCDOT DMV | **PR DTOP / DISCO** (Departamento de Transportación y Obras Públicas) |
| Insurance regulator | NC Dept of Insurance | **Oficina del Comisionado de Seguros de PR (OCS)** |
| Language | English | **Spanish primary** — all driver docs, training, client materials |

## Why PR is actually a strong market
- **High diabetes & chronic-disease rates** → heavy **dialysis** and lab-draw volume (recurring routed revenue, your best contracts)
- Dense metro (San Juan, Bayamón, Carolina, Caguas) = short routes, more stops/hour
- Aging population + home health demand
- **Federal Opportunity Zone / Act 60 tax incentives** may apply to a PR-domiciled operation — worth a CPA conversation
- Hurricane-resilience logistics is a real need (cold-chain + backup planning is a differentiator)

## Local URLs
- PR Dept. of State (business registration): https://estado.pr.gov
- PR Hacienda (treasury / merchant registration): https://hacienda.pr.gov
- PR Dept. of Labor / PR OSHA: https://www.trabajo.pr.gov
- PR DTOP (transportation): https://www.dtop.pr.gov
- PR Office of the Insurance Commissioner: https://ocs.pr.gov

## Setup deltas vs NC (the only extra work)
1. Register entity with PR Depto. de Estado + get Hacienda Merchant Certificate
2. Translate all hiring/training/client docs to **Spanish**
3. Source PR-based commercial auto + cargo insurance via an OCS-licensed agent
4. Confirm PR OSHA training provider for bloodborne pathogen certification
5. Everything else in this packet ports directly

## Strategic note
Run **Charlotte first** to prove the SOP and build the document set, then **clone into PR** — the compliance umbrella and all templates are reusable. PR is a parallel market, not a different business. Consider a PR-domiciled entity for Act 60 if you scale meaningfully there (get a PR CPA/attorney).

> Not legal/tax advice — confirm PR entity, tax, and licensing specifics with a PR-licensed attorney and CPA before launch.
