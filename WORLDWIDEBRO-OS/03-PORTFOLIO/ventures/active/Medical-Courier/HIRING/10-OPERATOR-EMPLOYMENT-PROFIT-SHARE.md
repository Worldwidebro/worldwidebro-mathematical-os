## 1. Definitions
1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information of the Company or its clients, including client lists, pricing, contracts, Business Associate Agreements, operating procedures, financial data, and Protected Health Information.
1.2 "**PHI**" means Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA (45 C.F.R. § 160.103).
1.3 "**Distributable Net Profit**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2.
1.4 "**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 14.2.
1.5 "**Restricted Period**" means the period of the Employee's employment plus twenty-four (24) months following the termination of employment for any reason.

## 2. Position, Duties, and Reporting
2.1 **Position.** The Company employs the Employee as **Operating Manager**, reporting directly to the Owner.
2.2 **Duties.** The Employee shall direct day-to-day operations, including dispatch and routing, recruitment, training, supervision, and compliance management of drivers; client service and retention; and execution of HIPAA, OSHA, and U.S. DOT/PHMSA compliance obligations. The Employee shall manage drivers using the Company's hiring and onboarding documents.
2.3 **Standard of performance.** The Employee shall devote full business time and best efforts to the Company and shall comply with all Company policies, client requirements, and applicable law.

## 3. Term and At-Will Status
3.1 **At-will employment.** Employment is **at-will**. Either Party may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without Cause, subject to the notice and final-pay provisions of Section 14.
3.2 No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create a guarantee of employment for any specific period.

## 4. Base Compensation
4.1 **Base salary.** The Company shall pay the Employee a base salary of **$________ per [year/month]**, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll schedule, subject to applicable withholdings (W-2). The base salary is guaranteed regardless of Company profitability.
4.2 **Benefits.** The Employee shall be eligible for [benefits / "such benefits as the Company may offer"], and for workers' compensation coverage as required by North Carolina law.

## 5. Profit-Share Incentive Bonus
5.1 **Bonus.** In addition to base salary, the Employee shall earn an incentive bonus equal to **thirty percent (30%) of Distributable Net Profit** (the "**Profit-Share Bonus**"); the Owner retains the remaining seventy percent (70%). The Parties acknowledge that the Profit-Share Bonus constitutes "wages" under the North Carolina Wage and Hour Act (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 95-25.1 et seq.), and the calculation, conditions, proration, and forfeiture terms of this Section are disclosed in writing in advance as required by that Act.
5.2 **Distributable Net Profit** means, for each calendar quarter, Gross Revenue actually collected, less, in the following order:
   (a) Direct costs (driver compensation, fuel, vehicle, insurance, equipment, packaging);
   (b) Operating expenses (software, administrative, licensing, overhead);
   (c) The Owner's management fee / priority return of **$________ per month**;
   (d) A retained-earnings reserve of **________%** for growth and contingencies.
The Profit-Share Bonus applies only to the amount remaining after subsections (a)–(d).
5.3 **Payment timing.** The Profit-Share Bonus shall be calculated and paid **quarterly**, within thirty (30) days after the close of each calendar quarter.
5.4 **Eligibility period.** No Profit-Share Bonus shall accrue during the first ninety (90) days of employment (the "Introductory Period"); accrual begins [on day 91 / with the next full calendar quarter].
5.5 **Proration and forfeiture.** Upon termination, the Profit-Share Bonus shall be **prorated** through the termination date and paid with the next applicable quarterly calculation. The Employee forfeits any unaccrued bonus for periods after termination. This proration and forfeiture provision is disclosed in advance pursuant to N.C. Gen. Stat. § 95-25.13.

## 6. No Equity or Ownership
6.1 This Agreement grants the Employee **no equity, membership interest, ownership, or right to acquire ownership** in the Company. The Profit-Share Bonus is incentive compensation only.
6.2 The Company, the Owner, and their successors retain **100% ownership** of the Company and all of its assets, including client contracts, Business Associate Agreements, brand, goodwill, systems, and intellectual property.

## 7. Authority and Decision Rights
7.1 The Owner retains final authority over pricing, execution and termination of client contracts, expenditures exceeding **$________**, and selection of the Employee's successor.
7.2 The Employee holds day-to-day operational discretion within Owner-approved budgets and standard operating procedures.

## 8. Financial Transparency
8.1 The Company shall provide the Employee reasonable access to the profit-and-loss statements used to calculate Distributable Net Profit, to be reviewed with the Owner each quarter.

## 9. Confidentiality and Protected Health Information
9.1 The Employee shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it solely to perform her duties, and not disclose it except as required to perform those duties or by law.
9.2 The Employee shall comply with HIPAA and all client Business Associate Agreements, and shall execute the Company's HIPAA Confidentiality Agreement. Any breach involving PHI is grounds for immediate termination for Cause and may carry civil and criminal liability.
9.3 The obligations of this Section survive termination indefinitely.

## 10. Restrictive Covenants
10.1 **Legitimate interest.** The Employee acknowledges that, as Operating Manager, she will have access to Confidential Information and substantial relationships with the Company's clients and drivers, and that the covenants below are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.
10.2 **Non-solicitation of clients.** During the Restricted Period, the Employee shall not solicit, divert, or service any client or prospective client with whom the Company did business, or which the Employee learned of, during her employment.
10.3 **Non-recruitment.** During the Restricted Period, the Employee shall not solicit, recruit, or hire any driver, employee, or contractor of the Company.
10.4 **Non-competition (divisible / step-down territory).** During the Restricted Period, the Employee shall not own, manage, or work for a competing medical-courier business within the following territories, which the Parties intend to be **separate, severable, and independently enforceable**, applied from broadest enforceable to narrowest:
   (a) the State of North Carolina;
   (b) the Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metropolitan statistical area;
   (c) Mecklenburg County and each county contiguous thereto.
If any territory in (a)–(c) is held unenforceable, the remaining territories shall remain in full force. The Parties acknowledge that North Carolina courts do not judicially revise (blue-pencil) restrictive covenants, and they have therefore drafted these provisions as distinctly divisible.
10.5 **Reasonableness; reformation limits.** If any covenant is held unreasonable as to duration, the Parties intend the longest enforceable duration to apply to the extent permitted by North Carolina law.

## 11. Company Property
11.1 Upon termination, the Employee shall immediately return all Company and client property, Confidential Information, PHI, devices, credentials, and records.

## 12. Representations and Warranties
12.1 Each Party represents that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any other agreement. The Employee represents she is not subject to any restrictive covenant that would impair her performance.

## 13. Indemnification
13.1 The Employee shall indemnify the Company against losses arising from her gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of Sections 9 or 10. The Company shall indemnify the Employee for claims arising from acts performed within the lawful scope of her duties, except to the extent caused by her gross negligence or willful misconduct. This Section survives termination.

## 14. Termination
14.1 **By either Party.** Either Party may terminate on [fourteen (14)] days' written notice, or immediately by the Company for Cause.
14.2 **Cause** means dishonesty, breach of Sections 9 or 10, violation of HIPAA/OSHA/DOT requirements, material breach of Company policy, or conduct materially harmful to the Company.
14.3 **Final pay.** The Company shall pay all earned wages, including any accrued and prorated Profit-Share Bonus, on or before the next regular payday following termination, as required by N.C. Gen. Stat. § 95-25.7.

## 15. Dispute Resolution
15.1 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation, then non-binding mediation in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina. Any unresolved dispute shall be [litigated in the state and federal courts located in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina / resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association]. The prevailing Party may recover reasonable attorneys' fees to the extent permitted by law.

## 16. Governing Law
16.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

## 17. Miscellaneous
17.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, with the HIPAA Confidentiality Agreement, is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior understandings.
17.2 **Amendment.** No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
17.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder remains in effect, consistent with Sections 10.4–10.5.
17.4 **Waiver.** No waiver of any breach operates as a waiver of any other breach.
17.5 **Assignment.** The Company may assign this Agreement to a successor; the Employee may not assign it.
17.6 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses above.
17.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronically.

## ANNEX — Panel Risk & Enforceability Notes (not part of the executed contract)
*Reviewed as: Senior Corporate Attorney · COO · Head of HR · Enterprise Risk Officer · Executive Recruiter. For your decision-making; remove before signing.*

| # | Issue | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NC no blue-pencil rule** | Court will void an overbroad covenant entirely rather than narrow it | Divisible step-down territory used (§10.4); have counsel confirm |
| 2 | **Profit-share = wages (NCWHA)** | Forfeiture/proration unenforceable if not disclosed in advance | Disclosed in §5.5; keep signed copy on file |
| 3 | **Consideration for covenants** | If signed after start date, covenants may fail for lack of new consideration | Recitals tie covenants to the job offer + incentive; sign at or before start |
| 4 | **"Distributable Net Profit" disputes** | Vague expense categories invite conflict | Define expense categories precisely with counsel/CPA; consider a capped Owner fee |
| 5 | **Misclassification** | W-2 chosen (correct given control); never run this role as 1099 | Maintain W-4/I-9, payroll, workers' comp |
| 6 | **Non-compete vs non-solicit** | Non-solicit (§10.2–10.3) is far more enforceable than non-compete (§10.4) in NC | If counsel is uneasy, rely primarily on non-solicit |
| 7 | **Indemnification of employer by employee** | Limited enforceability; courts disfavor broad employee indemnity | Keep narrow (gross negligence/willful/§9–10 breach only) — already scoped |
| 8 | **Arbitration vs court** | Choose one in §15 before signing | Decide with counsel; arbitration = privacy/speed, court = appeal rights |
