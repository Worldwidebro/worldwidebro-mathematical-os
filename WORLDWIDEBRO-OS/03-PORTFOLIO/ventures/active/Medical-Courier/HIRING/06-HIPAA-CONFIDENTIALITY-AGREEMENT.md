# HIPAA Confidentiality Agreement — Driver

> Have an attorney confirm this aligns with your clients' Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). Every driver signs before handling any PHI.

---

**Driver name:** ____________________
**Company:** [Company] / Winners Circle WC LLC

As a medical courier handling protected health information (PHI) in the course of transporting specimens, records, and pharmaceuticals, I acknowledge and agree:

1. **Definition.** "PHI" includes any individually identifiable health information — patient names, specimen labels, requisition forms, lab orders, and any documents or containers I transport.

2. **Confidentiality.** I will keep all PHI strictly confidential. I will not read, copy, photograph, share, or discuss PHI with anyone except as required to perform my delivery duties.

3. **Minimum necessary.** I will access only the information necessary to complete pickups and deliveries.

4. **Safeguards.** I will keep specimens and documents secured in the vehicle, never left unattended in public view, and protected from loss or theft.

5. **No personal use.** I will never use PHI for any personal purpose or disclose it for any reason unrelated to my duties.

6. **Incident reporting.** I will immediately report any actual or suspected breach, loss, theft, or unauthorized disclosure of PHI to my supervisor.

7. **Return of materials.** Upon end of engagement, I will return all PHI, devices, badges, and company materials.

8. **Consequences.** I understand that violating this agreement or HIPAA may result in immediate termination and civil or criminal penalties under federal law.

9. **Survival.** My confidentiality obligations continue after my engagement ends.

I have read, understand, and agree to the above.
