# Medical Courier — Venture Hub

**Type:** Layer 1 service venture (labor income → dispatch business)
**Entity:** Winners Circle WC LLC (DBA "[Company] Medical Logistics")
**Markets:** Charlotte, NC (launch) · Puerto Rico (expansion — see PUERTO-RICO-EXPANSION.md)
**Target:** $5–9K/mo solo booked → $15K/mo with 3–5 driver roster

## The model
You don't buy yourself a driving job. You build a **dispatch/logistics company** where drivers run routes and you own the **contracts + compliance umbrella**. Contracts are the moat; drivers are interchangeable.

**Arc:** Find contracts → drive them yourself to prove the SOP → hire drivers under your compliance + contracts → sell more contracts.

## Compliance framework (federal — applies in NC AND PR)
- **HIPAA** — BAA with every client; PHI in transit → hhs.gov/hipaa
- **OSHA bloodborne pathogens (1910.1030)** — driver training
- **DOT/PHMSA UN3373** — Category B infectious substance packaging
- **Commercial auto + cargo/cold-chain liability** insurance
- No statewide/territory "medical courier license" exists — compliance IS the license.
