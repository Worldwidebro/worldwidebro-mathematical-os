# Driver Agreement — 1099 & W-2 Versions

> ⚠️ **Have a licensed attorney review before use.** Misclassifying a W-2 worker as 1099 is a real audit/liability risk in medical courier (you must control HIPAA/OSHA procedures — the IRS "control test" often points to W-2). Use 1099 to test, convert to W-2 as you stabilize.

---

# VERSION A — 1099 Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is between **[Company] / Winners Circle WC LLC** ("Company") and **____________________** ("Contractor"), effective **__________**.

1. **Engagement.** Contractor provides courier/delivery services as an independent contractor, not an employee. Contractor controls the manner and means of performance, subject to client and regulatory requirements.
2. **Services.** Pickup and delivery of medical specimens, pharmaceuticals, and supplies per assigned routes/runs, following all chain-of-custody, temperature, HIPAA, OSHA, and DOT requirements.
3. **Compensation.** $______ per [route / stop / mile]. Invoiced [weekly]. Paid via [direct deposit] within [7] days. Contractor responsible for all taxes (issued IRS Form 1099-NEC).
4. **Vehicle & expenses.** Contractor provides own insured vehicle and bears own operating expenses unless otherwise agreed.
5. **Compliance.** Contractor must complete required HIPAA, bloodborne pathogen, and packaging training and follow all procedures. Contractor signs the HIPAA Confidentiality Agreement (separate doc).
6. **Confidentiality / PHI.** Contractor will not disclose any protected health information; breach is grounds for immediate termination and potential liability.
7. **Insurance.** Contractor maintains valid auto insurance; provides proof to Company.
8. **Term & termination.** At-will; either party may terminate with [3] days' notice (immediate for compliance breach).
9. **Independent status.** No benefits, no withholding; Contractor not eligible for unemployment/workers' comp through Company.
10. **Governing law.** State/territory of [NC / PR].
