## Wyoming LLC Tax Advantages

1. **No Wyoming state income tax** — keep more of every dollar
2. **Pass-through taxation** — LLC profits taxed on your personal return (Schedule C)
3. **Self-employment tax deduction** — deduct 50% of SE tax from gross income
4. **S-Corp election** — when net profit exceeds $40K/yr, elect S-Corp to save SE tax

## Sales Tax

- **Wyoming:** No personal income tax, no sales tax on digital products/services
- **Sales tax nexus:** You owe sales tax in states where you have significant sales (economic nexus: ~$100K revenue)
- **Digital products:** Most states exempt digital downloads/SaaS — verify per state
- **Physical products:** Register in states where you sell $100K+ or 200+ transactions
