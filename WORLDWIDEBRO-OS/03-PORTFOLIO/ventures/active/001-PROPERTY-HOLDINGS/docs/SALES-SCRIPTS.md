# REA Sales Scripts — Real Estate Outreach

**Use:** Acquiring property managers, short-term rental operators, and real estate developers for digital search and booking portals.
**Pairs with:** `REA-CAPABILITY-STATEMENT.md` · `REA-FORMATION-CREDENTIAL-TRACKER.md`.

---

## 1) COLD CALL / INTRO SCRIPT (property manager / leasing director)
> "Hi [Name], this is [Rep Name] with Property Holdings. I'm calling because we help property managers and leasing teams in the Charlotte region automate their viewing schedule and capture more tenant applications online. I'm not selling software today—we ran a tenant conversion check-sheet showing how local agencies lose up to 20% of applicants due to slow listing page load speeds. Can I email that speed checklist to you or your operations lead?"
