# Venture Selection Framework: Prioritizing Your 712

## The Core Problem

You have 712 ventures. **Most are at "planned" stage.**

Resources are finite. You need to pick the **10-15 ventures that will generate cash flow, case studies, and distribution** before scaling to all 712.

This framework helps you pick based on **evidence, not excitement.**

---

## The 10-Factor Scoring Model

```text
Score = Pain + Urgency + Buying Power + 
        Reachability + Fulfillment + 
        Scalability + AI Leverage + Strategic Fit +
        (Frequency + Competition)
```

| Factor | Weight | Definition | Scoring |
|--------|--------|------------|---------|
| **Pain** | 10 | Is there a painful, REAL problem? | Do customers already try to solve it? (existing solutions = validation) |
| **Frequency** | 8 | How often does the problem occur? | Daily=10, Weekly=7, Monthly=4, Yearly=2 |
| **Urgency** | 9 | How fast do they need it? | Immediate need=10, Can wait=3 |
| **Buying Power** | 10 | Can they afford it? | Enterprise/SMB=10, Individual=5, Non-profit=2 |
| **Reachability** | 8 | Can you find + reach them? | LinkedIn/industry groups=10, Scattered=4 |
| **Competition** | 7 | Can you differentiate? | Unique angle=10, Crowded=3 |
| **Fulfillment** | 9 | Can you ship THIS MONTH? | MVP ready=10, Code ready=7, Concept=2 |
| **Scalability** | 8 | Can it reach $1M+ revenue? | Recurring, multi-customer=10, One-off=3 |
| **AI Leverage** | 8 | Can automation reduce cost? | High potential=10, Manual-only=3 |
| **Strategic Fit** | 9 | Does it strengthen YOUR ecosystem? | Feeds templates/content/data=10, Standalone=3 |

**Max possible score: 86**

## Scoring Validation Checklist

Before launching each Tier 1 venture:

- [ ] Customer pain point defined + validated (interviews with 5+ prospects)
- [ ] Buying power confirmed ($500+ MRR per customer minimum)
- [ ] Reachability confirmed (know where to find first 100 customers)
- [ ] Fulfillment status: MVP-ready or code-ready (ship within 2 weeks)
- [ ] AI leverage identified (what automates this?)
- [ ] Strategic fit clear (how does this feed other ventures?)
- [ ] Repo status: >60% code, no critical blockers
- [ ] Founder/operator assigned + committed
- [ ] Revenue model clear (SaaS/service pricing defined)
- [ ] Success metrics defined (revenue, customers, case studies targets)

**If all 10 boxes checked = LAUNCH**  
**If <7 boxes checked = MOVE TO TIER 2**
