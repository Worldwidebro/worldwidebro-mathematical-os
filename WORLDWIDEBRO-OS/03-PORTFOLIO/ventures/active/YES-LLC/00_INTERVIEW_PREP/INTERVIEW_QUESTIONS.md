# Questions to Ask Wave Tomorrow

---

## Critical Questions (Ask These)

### About the Work

1. **"What are the highest-priority problems you're solving right now?"**
   - Understand what matters
   - Assess if you're right fit

2. **"What does success look like in 30, 60, and 90 days?"**
   - Get clear expectations
   - Identify timeline

3. **"What challenges have previous contractors encountered?"**
   - Reveal hidden risks
   - Learn from their experience

### About the Technical Work

4. **"What tools and systems are currently in use?"**
   - Understand their stack
   - Assess your fit

5. **"What's the current technical architecture? Are there diagrams?"**
   - Understand systems
   - Prevent surprises

6. **"What technical debt exists that affects you?"**
   - Reveals real constraints
   - Helps prioritize

### About Communication & Process

7. **"How do you track projects and communicate progress?"**
   - Learn their process
   - Know how to report

8. **"Who are the primary stakeholders for technical decisions?"**
   - Identify decision-makers
   - Know who to loop in

9. **"How do you prefer status updates?"**
   - Daily standup? Weekly email? Slack?
   - Show responsiveness

### About Scope & Timeline

10. **"Is the PDF the complete scope, or is there more?"**
    - Clarify boundaries
    - Assess commitment

11. **"What happens if I discover blockers outside my control?"**
    - Understand escalation
    - Know how to handle surprises

---

## Most Important Question

After they explain the work:

**"Before I commit, is there anything I should know that might affect whether I'm the right person for this?"**

Shows you're serious about being right fit.

---

## How to Ask

✅ Listen first, ask clarifying questions naturally  
✅ Write down answers  
✅ Reference their PDF when relevant  

❌ Don't fire off list like interrogation  
❌ Don't ask questions you should know  
❌ Don't get too deep in technical weeds

---

## Most Important

**Listen more than you talk.**

The more they talk, the better you understand what they need.
