## During the Interview

### Listen Phase (Start)
- Let them describe the opportunity
- Take notes
- Show genuine interest
- Don't interrupt

### Question Phase (Middle)
- Ask 3-4 questions from INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md
- Let them talk
- Be honest ("I don't know that, but I can learn it")

### Story Phase (When Asked)
- Tell one relevant story from INTERVIEW_STORIES.md
- Use STAR method (Situation → Task → Action → Result)
- Keep it to 2-3 minutes
- Connect it back to Wave's needs

### Close Phase (End)
- Ask: "What does success look like in 30, 60, and 90 days?"
- Ask: "Before I commit, is there anything I should know?"
- Say: "I'm excited about Wave's mission. Looking forward to learning more."

---

## What They're Evaluating

✅ Can you do the work?  
✅ Are you reliable?  
✅ Will you communicate?  
✅ Are you easy to work with?  

Your stories should answer those questions through concrete examples.

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Oversell ("I can build anything")  
❌ Talk about your tools  
❌ Dominate the conversation  
❌ Be unclear about scope/timeline  
❌ Ask for things unprompted  

---

## What TO Do

✅ Listen more than you talk  
✅ Ask intelligent questions  
✅ Tell concrete stories (not claims)  
✅ Be honest about what you know/don't know  
✅ Be professional but human
