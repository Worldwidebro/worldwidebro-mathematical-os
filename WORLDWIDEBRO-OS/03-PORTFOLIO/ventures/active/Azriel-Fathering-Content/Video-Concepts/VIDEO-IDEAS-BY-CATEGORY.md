## Series Ideas (Recurring Formats)

### Weekly Series
- **Monday:** Monster Truck Monday (stunts, tricks)
- **Wednesday:** Roblox Wednesday (building, gaming, tutorials)
- **Friday:** Fathering Friday (life lessons, character building)

### Monthly Series
- **Monthly Challenge:** Pick a new skill, 4-week progression
- **Game Night Special:** Complex game or challenge (once monthly)
- **Collab Video:** With friend, family member, or other creator

### Seasonal Ideas
- Summer: Outdoor sports focus, water activities
- Fall: Back to school skills, reading time
- Winter: Indoor activities, building projects
- Spring: New growth, trying new sports

## Content Strategy Notes

**Frequency:** 1-2 videos per week (sustainable)  
**Length:** 8-15 minutes (optimal for young audiences)  
**Mix:** 40% Entertainment, 40% Educational, 20% Behind-the-scenes  
**Engagement:** Every video includes a question or call-to-action  
**Theme:** Always about bonding and learning together
