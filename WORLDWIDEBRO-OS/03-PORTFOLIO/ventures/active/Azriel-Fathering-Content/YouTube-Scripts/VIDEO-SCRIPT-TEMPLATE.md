## Notes & Tips

- **Keep it paced for 7-year-olds:** Mix narration with action
- **Show effort:** Don't hide the attempts or failures—kids relate to struggle
- **Celebrate growth:** Praise Azriel's effort, creativity, and improvement
- **Camera tips:** Close-ups for reaction, wide shots for action, slow-mo for fun moments
- **Audio:** Keep background music upbeat but not overwhelming
