# CON Sales Scripts — Sub-to-GC Outreach

**Use:** Getting Winners Circle WC LLC added to NC general contractors' pre-qualified subcontractor lists.
**Pairs with:** `CON-CAPABILITY-STATEMENT.md` (attach/leave-behind) · `GOV-CONTRACT-REVENUE-ACTION-SHEET.md` (call list) · `CON-FORMATION-CREDENTIAL-TRACKER.md` (bid boards).
**Swap 🟦 = your trade** (roofing / electrical / painting / GC labor). **🟨 = fill before sending.**

> Goal of every first touch is NOT to win a job — it's to get **added to the prequalified sub list** and get the estimator's direct email. Low ask = high yes-rate.

---

## 1) COLD CALL SCRIPT (estimator / PM)

**Opening (15 sec):**
> "Hi, this is Antwuan with Winners Circle — a Charlotte-based 🟦 subcontractor. I'm not selling anything — I work with GCs in the region and I'd like to get on your prequalified sub list for upcoming bids. Are you the right person for subcontractor prequalification, or can you point me to them?"

**If yes / right person:**
> "Appreciate it. We're available for your NC commercial and federal projects, we carry our own insurance, and we help primes meet M/W/SB participation goals 🟨. What's the best way to get added — a form, or should I email my capability statement to you directly?"

**Get the commitment (always close for ONE of these):**
> "Great — I'll send my capability statement to [confirm email]. When's your next round of bids going out, so I make sure I'm in the system before then?"

**Gatekeeper (front desk):**
> "Hi — quick one: who handles subcontractor prequalification or estimating? I just want to get added to their bid list and send a capability statement. Can you give me their name and email?"

**Objection — "We already have our subs":**
> "Totally understand — I'm not asking to replace anyone. I'd just like to be the backup when a sub falls through or a schedule gets tight, and we help with M/W/SB goals 🟨. Can I send the capability statement so I'm on file?"

**Objection — "Send it to info@…":**
> "Will do. So it doesn't get lost — who on the estimating team should I follow up with by name next week?"

**Objection — "What have you done?":**
> "We're newer as a registered entity but the crews are experienced 🟨. I can give you trade and supplier references, and we'll prove it on a small first scope. Lowest-risk way to start — happy to bid a small package."

## 2) VOICEMAIL (≤20 sec)

> "Hi, this is Antwuan Johns with Winners Circle, a Charlotte 🟦 subcontractor. I'd like to get added to your prequalified sub list for upcoming NC projects — we're insured and help with M/W/SB goals 🟨. I'll email my capability statement; you can reach me at (704) 388-5030. Thanks — talk soon."

*(Always send the email immediately after the voicemail. Voicemail + email together ≈ 2x callback.)*

## 4) APPOINTMENT-SET CLOSE (when they're warm)

> "Rather than go back and forth by email — could we do 10 minutes 🟨 [day/time] so I understand the scopes you sub out most and what your prequal process looks like? I'll come ready with references and pricing on a sample package."

*Book it on the spot. Confirm by email same day with the capability statement re-attached.*

---

## 5) FIRST-DEAL / TEAMING CLOSE

When they invite a bid:
> "Thanks for the invite. I'll get you a clean number by 🟨 [date]. If we're aligned, I'll sign your standard subcontract/teaming agreement and we can start with this package and build from there."

Then: scope → bid → teaming agreement → first sub PO → **deliver clean → ask for the next bid + a reference.**

---

## QUICK REFERENCE (tape to monitor)

- **The ask is always:** "Add me to your prequalified sub list" (not "give me a job")
- **Always leave with:** estimator's name + direct email + next bid date
- **Always send:** capability statement immediately after every call/voicemail
- **Trade swap 🟦 · fill 🟨 before first send**
