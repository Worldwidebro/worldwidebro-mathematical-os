## Expense Tracker Ai
### Modern automated accounting, ledger audits, and real-time tax optimization.

---

### COMPANY OVERVIEW
Expense Tracker Ai provides automated bookkeeping, document extraction, and ledger compliance engines for small businesses, contractors, and startups. We eliminate manual data entry by extracting invoices with AI (OCR), reconciling ledgers instantly, and providing continuous tax liability forecasts to maximize deductions before year-end.

---

### CORE COMPETENCIES
- **AI Bookkeeping & Transaction Matching** — Daily transaction reconciliation and bank syncs
- **Automated Invoice & Receipt OCR** — Intelligent receipt scanning, categorizing, and matching
- **Deduction Auditing & Tax Strategy** — Continuous tax code compliance scans and write-off identification
- **Portfolio & Equity Rollups** — Unified equity tracking and cash flow forecasting dashboards

---

### DIFFERENTIATORS
- **Real-Time Tax Liability Mapping** — No waiting until April; track estimated tax obligations daily
- **99% OCR Accuracy** — Line-item extraction for complex invoices, matching items to custom expense rules
- **Integration Engine** — Feeds bookkeeping records directly to Twenty CRM, QuickBooks, or custom SQL databases
- **Audit-Ready Ledgers** — Automatic link of every transaction to its supporting document (PDF/Image)
