# HRMS Sales Outreach & Prospect List

## Cold Email Template (LinkedIn DM or Email)

### Subject: Quick question about payroll at [Company]

```
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] works in [construction/logistics/field services]—exactly who we're building payroll software for.

We're solving a specific problem: construction and logistics companies spend 3-5 hours per week on payroll (spreadsheets, tax calculations, direct deposit setup). Most payroll software is designed for office workers—not for weekly pay, prevailing wage, crew dynamics.

Would you be open to 20 quick questions about how your payroll works right now? We're talking to owners to validate what matters most. Takes 15-20 min, no pitch.

Free if you can chat [DAY/TIME this week]?

Thanks,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[HRMS]
```

## Multi-Channel Outreach (Beyond LinkedIn)

### Email Outreach (Parallel to LinkedIn)
- Use Apollo.io or Hunter.io to find work emails
- Email template: Same as above, slightly more formal
- Send at: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm (highest open rate)
- Personalize 2-3 details (company name, recent news)

### Phone Outreach (Warm-Up)
- After email, call the company main line
- Ask for "[Operations Manager] or whoever handles payroll"
- **Script**: "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Your Name] with HRMS. I emailed you yesterday about quick payroll questions for construction companies. Got 15 min to chat?"
- If "not interested": "Who would be best to talk to?" (referral to right person)

### Industry Events
- Chamber of Commerce meetings
- Construction association meetings (AGC, ABC)
- Logistics conferences
- Trade shows (e.g., Concrete Expo, HVAC Conference)
- **Strategy**: Hand out flier, book 1-2 meetings per event

### Referral Loop
- After every discovery call (positive or negative): "Would you know anyone else I should talk to?"
- Document referrals in spreadsheet
- Email referral: "Hey [Contact], [Name] suggested I talk to you about payroll."
- Referral close rate: 20-30% higher than cold

## Discovery Call Checklist

Before Every Call:
- [ ] Researched company (LinkedIn, website, employee count, last funding/news)
- [ ] Know: industry, main pain points (weekly pay?, prevailing wage?, crew dynamics?)
- [ ] Have script visible
- [ ] Quiet room, no distractions
- [ ] Calendar reminder (10 min before)
- [ ] Notebook or Notion doc open

During Call:
- [ ] Record sentiment (excited, skeptical, neutral)
- [ ] Note exact pain point quotes
- [ ] Ask permission to record (if recording)
- [ ] Note buy signal: 1=not interested, 5=strong interest

After Call (Within 30 min):
- [ ] Write 1-page summary (company, contact, pain points, signal, next steps)
- [ ] Add to HRMS-DISCOVERY-RESULTS.md
- [ ] Flag strong signals (4-5) for quick follow-up (same day)
- [ ] Send demo video or case study if interested

## Sales Messaging by Industry

### Construction
**Opening**: "I know construction payroll is complex—weekly pay, prevailing wage, certified payroll, seasonal workers. Most payroll software doesn't get it."

**Pain Points to Probe**:
- How do you handle prevailing wage calculations?
- Weekly or biweekly pay?
- How long does payroll take per week?
- Any tax compliance mishaps?

**Value Proposition**: "We automate prevailing wage, certified payroll, and weekly pay in one place. No more spreadsheets, no compliance risk."

---

### Logistics
**Opening**: "I know logistics payroll is different—driver pay, mileage rates, per-shipment bonuses. Generic payroll software breaks for you."

**Pain Points to Probe**:
- How do you calculate driver pay (hourly, mileage, bonus)?
- Weekly or biweekly pay?
- How long does it take to process payroll?
- Any driver complaints about delays or errors?

**Value Proposition**: "We handle driver pay structures, mileage tracking, and bonus calculations. Fast, accurate, drivers get paid on time."

---

### Field Services
**Opening**: "I know field service payroll is messy—crew leaders, techs in the field, travel time, overtime. Payroll software designed for offices doesn't work."

**Pain Points to Probe**:
- How do you track crew time and travel?
- Overtime calculation complex?
- How do you handle per-job rates vs. hourly?
- Crew leaders—do they manage their own team pay?

**Value Proposition**: "We handle crew dynamics, per-job rates, travel time, and overtime by state. One tool for your field operations payroll."
