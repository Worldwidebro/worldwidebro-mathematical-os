# Interview Question Sets by Role

## COO Interview Questions (Round 1-3)

### Round 1: Screening (30 min)

1. **Built processes that scaled 0→50 concurrent projects. What did you learn?**
2. **What does "operational excellence" mean to you?**
3. **How would you catch bad delivery before it happens?**
4. **Walk us through your last 3 months. What kept you busy?**
5. **Why this role at this stage?**

### Round 2: Deep Dive (60 min)

1. **Design our deal-flow from intake to payment. Critical checkpoints?**
2. **30% of projects failing quality checks. What's your first move?**
3. **Tell us about the worst process you inherited. How did you fix it?**
4. **How do you balance speed with quality at scale?**
5. **Management philosophy. How would you work with our Chief Operator?**

### Round 3: CEO Conversation (30 min)

1. **Revenue at $30K/month but ops breaking. First move?**
2. **How do you want to be managed?**
3. **What would make you leave this company in 18 months?**

## CFO Interview Questions (Round 1-3)

### Round 1: Screening (30 min)

1. **Walk us through the last P&L you managed. How did you identify profit/loss?**
2. **Caught a financial problem early. What happened?**
3. **Design a payment system that splits revenue 5 ways correctly, every time.**
4. **Why fractional/early-stage role?**
5. **What accounting software do you prefer and why?**

### Round 2: Deep Dive (60 min)

1. **Design financial structure for $2.5M/month marketplace taking 10%**
   - Cash flow management
   - Tax implications (1099 vs W2)
   - Payment timing
   - Chargebacks and reserves

2. **15% of deals unprofitable. Root cause analysis. What do you tell CEO?**
3. **Set up ACH payments to 100+ referrers weekly. Walk us through.**
4. **What does a monthly board report look like? What metrics matter?**
5. **How do you think about equity? Cap table management?**

### Round 3: CEO Conversation (30 min)

1. **Need to pay 100 referrers $5K each Friday but only have $300K in bank. What do you do?**
2. **Your take-home: Are we going to be profitable?**
3. **How do you want to be managed? Non-negotiables?**

## Chief Originator Interview Questions (Round 1-3)

### Round 1: Screening (30 min)

1. **Most successful referral program you built. What made it work?**
2. **Right now, how many people would return your call for an opportunity?**
3. **How would you get 100 construction referrers excited about our program?**
4. **Why this role? What gets you excited about deal sourcing?**
5. **Deal you lost to competitor. What would you do differently?**

### Round 2: Deep Dive (60 min)

1. **Design referrer program from scratch**
   - 10 types of referrers we target?
   - Incentive structure per type?
   - Onboarding and support?
   - Measure and improve quality?

2. **$10K to spend acquiring referrers this month. How do you spend it?**
3. **Referrer relationship went south. What happened? Prevention?**
4. **How do you motivate a referrer who brought 1 deal but isn't bringing more?**
5. **What does 12-month referrer recruitment plan look like?**

### Round 3: CEO Conversation (30 min)

1. **By month 6: 20 deals/month = 30-40 active referrers. How do you get there?**
2. **Biggest concern about this business model? What could go wrong?**
3. **How do you want to be managed? How often do you want feedback?**

## Scoring Rubric (1-5 Scale)

### Must-Have Competencies

| Competency | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Directly Relevant Experience** | First time | Some exposure | Done 2-3x | Deep experience | Built/scaled 2x+ |
| **Systems Thinking** | Task-focused | Some processes | Good systems | Excellent | Natural architect |
| **Communication** | Unclear | Somewhat unclear | Adequate | Clear | Excellent |
| **Ownership Mindset** | Blames others | Some accountability | Takes ownership | Strong owner | Relentless |
| **Learning Agility** | Stuck in ways | Some flexibility | Adaptable | Quick learner | Learns extremely fast |

### Decision Thresholds

- **HIRE:** Average 4.0+ across must-haves + CEO chemistry
- **MAYBE:** Average 3.5-3.9 (another round or trial)
- **PASS:** Average below 3.5

## Red Flags

**COO:**
- Can't describe a process they built
- Hasn't managed multi-party systems
- Talks "cutting costs" not "improving quality"

**CFO:**
- Uncomfortable with spreadsheets/formulas
- Hasn't processed 100+ payments/week
- Can't explain P&L in simple terms

**Chief Originator:**
- Claims big networks but can't name 10 referrers
- Never worked with commission structures
- Views referrers transactionally
