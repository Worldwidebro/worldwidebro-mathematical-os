# Worldwidebro Holdings: Parallel Hiring Package

## POSITION 1: Chief Operating Officer (COO)

**Reports to:** CEO (Antwuan Divine Johns)  
**Location:** Remote (preferred) / Flexible  
**Start Date:** July 2026  
**Equity:** 0.5-2%  
**Salary:** $80K-120K/year

### About the Role

Worldwidebro Holdings is building a multi-party incentive ecosystem that routes deals through a standardized system. We're scaling from $10K/month to $100K-150K/month in 12 months.

As our **COO**, you'll own:
- Execution quality and consistency across all deals
- Building and managing the Chief Operator role (who oversees service delivery)
- Creating standard processes that scale from 5 to 50+ concurrent projects
- Managing financial tracking, risk, and operational metrics
- Direct line to CEO for strategic decisions

### Key Responsibilities

**Month 1-2 (Launch Phase):**
- Map all deal-flow processes (intake → execution → payment → reputation)
- Create operational standards for service delivery
- Build quality checklist for operator acceptance/rejection
- Set up weekly ops sync cadence
- Define escalation procedures for issues

**Month 3+ (Scale Phase):**
- Hire and onboard Chief Operator
- Coach Chief Operator on quality standards
- Run weekly operations metrics reviews
- Identify and fix operational bottlenecks
- Scale processes to handle 20+ concurrent deals/month

### Required Experience

- 5+ years operations/program management experience
- Built or scaled operations from 0→50+ concurrent projects
- Familiar with deal flow/project intake systems
- Strong process documentation skills
- Comfortable with ambiguity + rapid iteration

### Compensation Structure

**Base:** $80K-120K/year  
**Equity:** 0.5-2% (vests over 4 years, 1-year cliff)  
**Bonus:** 2-5% of profit after $50K/month revenue (quarterly)

## POSITION 2: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

**Reports to:** CEO (Antwuan Divine Johns)  
**Location:** Remote  
**Start Date:** June/July 2026  
**Equity:** 1-3%  
**Salary/Retainer:** $3K-8K/month (fractional) OR $70K-100K/year (full-time)

### About the Role

Every deal needs financial clarity. As our **CFO**, you'll build the financial backbone:
- P&L per deal (know profit/loss on every $50K project)
- Automatic payment splits (referrer 10%, operator 35%, costs 40%, platform 10%, profit 5%)
- ACH/wire payment processing and audit trails
- Monthly investor reporting
- Cap table management + equity tracking

### Key Responsibilities

**Immediate (Weeks 1-4):**
- Audit current deal economics
- Create P&L template for standard deal types
- Set up ACH payment infrastructure
- Build payment split calculator
- Create monthly reporting dashboard

**Ongoing (Weeks 5+):**
- Process weekly payment runs (every Friday)
- Reconcile all transactions against deal contracts
- Generate monthly P&L reports
- Track cap table + equity allocations
- Prepare quarterly investor updates
- Tax planning + compliance

### Required Experience

- 5+ years financial management (startups, SaaS, or services)
- Comfortable with spreadsheets + database design
- ACH/payment processing knowledge
- P&L analysis and financial forecasting
- Tax compliance experience

### Compensation Structure

**Option 1 (Fractional):**
- $3K-8K/month (20-30 hrs/week)
- 1-2% equity (vests over 4 years, 1-year cliff)
- Performance bonus: 2-3% of collected platform fees

**Option 2 (Full-Time):**
- $70K-100K/year
- 1-3% equity

## POSITION 3: Chief Originator (VP Sourcing/Deal Flow)

**Reports to:** CEO (Antwuan Divine Johns)  
**Location:** Remote  
**Start Date:** July 2026  
**Equity:** 2-5%  
**Salary:** $60K-90K/year + **2-5% override on ALL referrer fees**

### About the Role

Our entire business depends on deal flow. As **Chief Originator**, you own:
- Building and managing the referrer network (deal finders)
- Qualifying deals before they route to operators
- Setting referrer commission rates and terms
- Tracking referrer reputation scores
- Recruitment and retention of top deal sources

### Key Responsibilities

**Month 1-2 (Network Launch):**
- Identify 50+ potential referrer sources
- Create referrer program (incentives, terms, support)
- Build referrer intake form + onboarding
- Define deal qualification criteria
- Set commission structure per deal type

**Month 3+ (Scale Phase):**
- Recruit and activate 100+ referrers (target: 20+ deals/month)
- Weekly check-ins with top referrers
- Track referrer quality scores
- Monthly payout processing ($100K+ in referrer fees)
- Identify and fix sourcing bottlenecks

### Required Experience

- 5+ years business development or sales
- Experience building referral/affiliate networks
- Relationship management at scale (100+ contacts)
- Negotiation and deal-making skills
- Comfortable with variable commission structures

### Compensation Structure

**Base:** $60K-90K/year  
**Equity:** 2-5% (vests over 4 years, 1-year cliff)  
**Override Bonus:** 2-5% of ALL referrer fees collected (recurring)

**Real Math Example:**
- You recruit Marco (referrer)
- Marco brings Ace Construction deal: $50K value
- Referrer fee: 10% of $50K = $5K
- Your override: 3% of $5K = $150
- **Scale to 100 referrers → $15K+/month from override bonus alone**
