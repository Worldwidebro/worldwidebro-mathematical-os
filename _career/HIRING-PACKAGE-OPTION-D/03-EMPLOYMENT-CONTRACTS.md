# Employment Contract Templates

## TEMPLATE 1: COO Employment Agreement

**Key Sections:**
- Position: Chief Operating Officer, reporting to CEO
- Salary: $80K-120K/year
- Equity: 0.5-2% (4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- Hours: 40-50 hrs/week, remote
- Bonus: 2-5% of profit after $50K/month revenue
- Termination: At-will, 2 weeks severance if no cause
- Non-Compete: 12 months post-departure
- Probation: 90 days

**Responsibilities in Contract:**
- Map and standardize deal-flow processes
- Build operational standards for service delivery  
- Hire and manage Chief Operator role
- Run weekly operations metrics reviews
- Scale processes from 5 to 50+ concurrent projects

## TEMPLATE 3: Chief Originator Employment Agreement

**Key Sections:**
- Position: Chief Originator (VP Sourcing), reporting to CEO
- Salary: $60K-90K/year
- Equity: 2-5% (4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- Hours: 40-50 hrs/week, remote
- **Override Bonus: 2-5% of ALL referrer fees collected (recurring)**
- Termination: At-will, 2 weeks severance
- Non-Compete: 12 months + non-solicitation of referrers for 12 months
- Probation: 90 days

**Critical Term:**
Override bonus example: If 100 referrers each bring 1-2 deals/month at $50K average, referrer fees = $50K-100K/month. Your 3% override = $1.5K-3K/month PLUS base salary.

## Contract Customization Checklist

**Before Sending to Candidates:**

- [ ] Get template reviewed by employment lawyer
- [ ] Customize salary/equity ranges per role and candidate
- [ ] Verify non-compete enforceability in your state
- [ ] Add 409A valuation for equity (get from advisor)
- [ ] Adjust severance terms (optional)
- [ ] Customize probation period length
- [ ] Add specific performance metrics if desired
- [ ] Get CEO signature authority confirmed
- [ ] Keep signed copies in secure location

**Key Salary Ranges:**
- COO: $80K-120K
- CFO: $3K-8K/month fractional OR $70K-100K full-time
- Chief Originator: $60K-90K + override commission

**Equity Terms (Standard):**
- 4-year vesting schedule
- 1-year cliff (all-or-nothing first 12 months)
- Monthly vesting after cliff (25% each year)

**Severance (Optional):**
- Termination without cause: 2 weeks pay
- Termination for cause: None
- Proration of bonus: Usually yes
- Equity acceleration: Single-trigger at change of control

## Important Notes

1. **Not legal advice.** Have employment lawyer review.
2. **State-specific.** Non-competes vary in enforceability.
3. **Tax advisor needed.** For 409A valuation + option structures.
4. **Send after offer accepted.** Not before verbal offer.
5. **Keep copies filed.** Signed contracts go in secure folder.
