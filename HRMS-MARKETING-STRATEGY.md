# HRMS Marketing & Advertising Strategy

**Goal**: Launch with focused messaging per sector, then scale through paid ads  
**Timeline**: May 13-27 (parallel to acquisition pipeline)  
**Budget**: $0 for May (launch), $5K/month scaling starting June

---

## 🎯 Brand Positioning

### Core Positioning Statement
```
HRMS: The payroll software built for 30-500 person companies
that don't want a $10K/month ADP contract.
```

### Why This Works
- **Specific**: Not generic "all-in-one HR" (crowded space)
- **Problem**: Solves the #1 pain (ADP cost + complexity)
- **Audience**: 30-500 people = sweet spot (enterprise too expensive, freelancers don't need it)
- **Differentiation**: Cheap + compliant (the core tension)

---

## 📱 Channel Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch Channels (May 13-27) — $0 Cost
1. **Cold Email** (highest ROI for B2B SaaS at launch)
2. **LinkedIn Outreach** (warm audience, decision makers active)
3. **Founder + Network Referrals** (warm intros convert 60%+)
4. **Content**: Blog posts explaining payroll compliance (SEO foundation)

### Phase 2: Paid Channels (June 1-30) — $5K/month
1. **LinkedIn Ads** ($2K/month) — Target HR/Finance managers by company size/industry
2. **Google Search Ads** ($2K/month) — "Payroll software for small business", "ADP alternative", "Multi-state payroll"
3. **Facebook Ads** ($1K/month, geo-targeted) — Construction/logistics owner networks

### Phase 3: Growth Channels (July+)
1. **Content Marketing** (blog + SEO)
2. **Partner Programs** (reseller deals with accountants, HR consultants)
3. **Webinars** (product + compliance education)

## 📝 Messaging Framework by Sector

Each sector gets 3 messages:
1. **Awareness**: Define the pain (most people don't know it's a problem)
2. **Consideration**: Show the solution (but position vs. current solutions)
3. **Decision**: Close the deal (trial → paid)
