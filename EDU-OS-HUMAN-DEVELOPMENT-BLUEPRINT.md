# EDU-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Education Operating System
**Sector Focus:** Online courses, coaching, curriculum, training
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: EDU-Specific Stressors

- Student retention pressure (high churn)
- Content quality maintenance (ever-evolving)
- Technology stack complexity
- Imposter syndrome (teaching what you know)
- Feedback criticism (student complaints)

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

- 100+ active students/alumni
- Peer educators (collaboration, referral)
- Platform partners (tech support)

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Student Asks for Refund; Work Done**
- Low: Refuse, blame them
- High: Empathize, offer support

**Test 2: Your Content Gets Outdated**
- Low: Keep selling old course
- High: Refresh regularly, update students

**Test 3: Student Struggles; Demands Coaching**
- Low: Give refund, move on
- High: Find alternative solution

**Test 4: Opportunity to Charge More Without Value**
- Low: Raise price arbitrarily
- High: Justify price with substance

**Test 5: Student Success Story Doesn't Match Claims**
- Low: Exaggerate results in marketing
- High: Tell honest story

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (transform through education)
- L2-5: Energized by teaching; handles criticism
- L6: Consistent delivery; responsive
- L7: Alumni network; peer referrals
- L8: Innovates curriculum; stays current
- L9: Honest about outcomes; ethical
- L10: Building community; lasting impact
