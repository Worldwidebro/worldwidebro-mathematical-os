# research/agentic-enterprise/01-IBM-BLUEPRINT-FOR-AGENTIC-OPERATIONS

IBM surveyed over 2,000 C-suite executives across 16 countries and 17 industries. The core finding is that **the workflow—not the department—becomes the primary unit of value creation**.

## Architectural Shift
Traditional model:
`CEO ├── Finance ├── HR ├── Operations ├── Sales └── IT`

Emerging model:
`BUSINESS OUTCOME ↓ END-TO-END WORKFLOW ↓ HUMANS + AGENTS + SYSTEMS ↓ OUTCOME`

## Key Recommendations
1. Establish a **Long-Horizon Workflow Engine** to orchestrate tasks across department boundaries.
2. Maintain strict **Policy Adherence** and safety constraints on autonomous AI judgment.
