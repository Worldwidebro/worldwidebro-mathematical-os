   - Revenue: Indirect (relationship capital)

- **WhatsApp/Group Chat:** Weekly updates on upcoming events
- **One-on-One:** Personal invites to key contacts (warm outreach)
- **Referral Incentive:** "Bring a friend, get $20 off next event"

#### Revenue Model

- Attendance target: 20-40 people
- Ticket price: $25-75 per person
- **Gross per event:** $500-3,000
