## 1. Channel Mix Architecture

Company Brain categorizes distribution channels into four structural categories (PESO Model):
1. **Paid:** Search, Social, Sponsored Newsletters, Direct Mail.
2. **Earned:** PR, Podcast Interviews, Unsolicited Practitioner Endorsements.
3. **Shared:** Technical Communities (Hacker News, Reddit r/LocalLLaMA, Twitter/X).
4. **Owned:** Direct Cold Outbound, Substack/Email Newsletters, GitHub Repositories.
