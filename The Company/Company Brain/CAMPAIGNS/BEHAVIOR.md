## 1. High-Intent Behavioral Triggers

We monitor for specific observable behaviors that signal ripe commercial opportunities:

- **Hacker News / Twitter Venting:** Technical founders posting complaints about OpenAI monthly rate limits or sudden billing spikes.
- **GitHub Dependency Shifts:** Repositories introducing token-heavy agent frameworks (`langchain`, `crewai`, `autogpt`).
- **Content Engagement:** A prospect clicking links in our technical email more than twice or forwarding the email internally.

---

## 2. Automated Behavioral Routing

When a prospect exhibits \(\ge 2\) high-intent signals within a 48-hour window, the **Outbound Strategist Agent** automatically escalates the account to Priority P0 for personalized founder InMail touch.
