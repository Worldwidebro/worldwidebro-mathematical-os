## 1. Enterprise Customer Lifecycle

```text
PROSPECT ──> QUALIFIED LEAD (MQL) ──> OPPORTUNITY (SQL) ──> AUDIT CLIENT ──> EXPANSION PARTNER
```

1. **Prospect (`STAGE-01`):** Cold account matching ICP criteria in database.
2. **Engaged Visitor / Replier (`STAGE-02`):** Responded positively to outbound touch or downloaded technical tear-down.
3. **Qualified Opportunity (`STAGE-03`):** Attended 30-min discovery call; confirmed >$5k/mo API spend; reviewed 3X ROI guarantee.
4. **Active Audit Client (`STAGE-04`):** Signed MNDA & SOW; paid 50% deposit ($3,750); provided read-only repo access.
5. **Audit Completed (`STAGE-05`):** Received 48-hour presentation; benchmark report delivered; remaining $3,750 fee settled.
6. **Retainer / Expansion Partner (`STAGE-06`):** Contracted for monthly infrastructure optimization and local mesh orchestration ($3k–$10k/mo MRR).
