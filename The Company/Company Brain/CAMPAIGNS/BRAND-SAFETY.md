## 1. Compliance Mandates

- **CAN-SPAM / CASL / GDPR Compliance:**
  - Every cold outbound email must contain a valid physical business address.
  - Every email must contain a functional, 1-click unsubscribe link.
  - Unsubscribe requests must be honored within 24 hours and added to global suppression lists.
- **Truth in Advertising:**
  - All financial savings claims must be substantiated by empirical benchmarks.
  - The 3X ROI Guarantee terms must be explicitly documented in every contract.
- **Intellectual Property Protection:**
  - Client codebase metadata collected during audits must be stored in encrypted volumes and purged upon client request.
