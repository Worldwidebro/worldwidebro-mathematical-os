## 1. Buying Catalysts

Prospects do not buy software audits randomly; they buy when a catalyst forces action:

1. **The CFO Invoice Shock:** The monthly AWS/Anthropic bill arrives and exceeds budget by >50%.
2. **The Security Audit Failure:** An upcoming SOC2 or enterprise client questionnaire flags third-party AI data leaks.
3. **The Sprint-Blocking Outage:** Claude or OpenAI goes down for 3 hours during a critical sprint deadline.
4. **The Quarterly Planning Session:** Engineering leadership is tasked with finding 20% infrastructure cost savings.
