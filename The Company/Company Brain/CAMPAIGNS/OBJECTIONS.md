## 1. Top 5 Prospect Objections & Exact Counters

| Objection | Root Skepticism | Direct Tactical Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Our engineers are too busy to support an audit."** | Fear of developer distraction. | "Zero engineering time required. We only need read-only repo access. In 48 hours, we hand your leadership team the complete benchmark and turnkey implementation scripts." |
| **"We can't share our private codebase with third parties."** | Security & IP paranoia. | "We provide a turnkey, open-source local scanner (Docker/Graft) that runs entirely on your own air-gapped machine and outputs only sanitized topological metadata." |
| **"Local open-weights models are too weak for real coding."** | Outdated perceptions of 2023-era local LLMs. | "Modern quantized reasoning models (Qwen 2.5 Coder 32B, DeepSeek R1 distilled) rival GPT-4o on real coding benchmarks and run at 45 tokens/sec on standard Apple Silicon." |
| **"$7,500 is too expensive for a diagnostic."** | Unconvinced of positive ROI. | "We contractually back this with a **3X ROI Guarantee**: If we don't identify at least $22,500 in annualized savings, we refund 100% of your fee immediately." |
| **"We can just build this ourselves internally."** | Build vs buy bias. | "Your senior engineers cost $150+/hour. Having them spend 3 weeks building custom routing and compression costs $25,000+ in opportunity cost. We give you a production roadmap in 48 hours." |
