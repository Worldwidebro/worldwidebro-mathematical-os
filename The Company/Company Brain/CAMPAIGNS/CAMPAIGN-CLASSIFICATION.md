## 1. Four-Dimensional Classification Matrix

Every campaign in Company Brain is classified across 4 orthogonal dimensions:

```text
                  DIMENSION 1: FUNNEL OBJECTIVE
       Top-of-Funnel (Awareness) ◄──► Bottom-of-Funnel (Conversion)
                                ▲
                                │
DIMENSION 2: AUDIENCE TYPE      │      DIMENSION 3: TOUCH MODEL
B2B Enterprise / Startups ◄─────┼─────► Low-Touch Automated / High-Touch Sales
                                │
                                ▼
                   DIMENSION 4: MEDIA CHANNEL
                   Outbound / Paid / Content / Organic
```

---

## 2. Classification Schema for CAM-001

| Dimension | Classification Value | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Funnel Objective** | **Bottom-of-Funnel / Demand Gen** | Primary goal is cash collection ($37,500) through closed paid audits, not passive impressions. |
| **2. Audience Type** | **B2B Technical Enterprise** | Targets VPs of Engineering and CTOs at software companies (15–60 engineers). |
| **3. Touch Model** | **High-Touch Consultative** | Outbound sequence routes to 30-min technical discovery call and custom SOW handoff. |
| **4. Media Channel** | **Direct Outbound & InMail** | Signal-based, highly personalized 1:1 email and LinkedIn distribution. |
