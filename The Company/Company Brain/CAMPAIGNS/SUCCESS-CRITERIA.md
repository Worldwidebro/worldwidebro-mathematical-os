## 1. Binary Evaluation Standard

In Company Brain, success is never graded on a curve or described with ambiguous subjective adjectives ("great traction", "promising feedback"). Success is binary: `TRUE` or `FALSE`.

---

## 2. CAM-001 Success Criteria Matrix

| Threshold Level | Minimum Condition | Result Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Terminal Failure** | < 1 Closed Audit ($0 - $7,499 Revenue) | `FAILED` — Mandatory blameless post-mortem; review ICP & offer. |
| **Minimum Acceptable** | 1–2 Closed Audits ($7,500 - $15,000 Revenue) | `MARGINAL` — Covers budget; review sales friction before scaling. |
| **Target Success** | 5 Closed Audits ($37,500 Revenue) | `SUCCESS` — Reinvest proceeds into evergreen inbound flywheel. |
| **High Stretch** | > 8 Closed Audits ($60,000+ Revenue) | `EXTRAORDINARY` — Spin out dedicated managed infrastructure OpCo. |
