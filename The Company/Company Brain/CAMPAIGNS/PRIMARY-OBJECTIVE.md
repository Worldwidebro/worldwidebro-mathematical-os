## 1. Definition & Rules of Engagement

The Primary Objective is the sole metric by which the campaign is declared a triumph or a failure upon post-mortem review.

1. **Non-Negotiability:** Cannot be modified in-flight to mask poor performance.
2. **Cash or Conversion Anchor:** Must measure real-world commercial commitment, signed contracts, or empirical data acquisition.
3. **Automated Verification:** Must be tracked in real-time via `_REGISTRIES/CAMPAIGN-RESULT-REGISTRY.json`.
