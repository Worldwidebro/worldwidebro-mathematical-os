## 1. Canonical Taxonomy Rules

All URLs deployed in campaigns must strictly adhere to the standardized schema:

`https://worldwidebro.com/audit?utm_source={source}&utm_medium={medium}&utm_campaign={campaign}&utm_content={content}&utm_term={term}`

- **`utm_source`:** `direct_outbound` | `linkedin` | `twitter` | `substack` | `github`
- **`utm_medium`:** `email` | `inmail` | `social_post` | `newsletter` | `readme`

- **`utm_content`:** `tear_down_v1` | `roi_calculator` | `case_study_mlx` | `security_airgap`
- **`utm_term`:** `repo-intelligence` | `token-compression` | `local-mlx` | `private-ai`
