## 1. Messaging Philosophy

Messaging in Company Brain is grounded in direct, technical, and commercial truth. We speak engineer-to-engineer, avoiding hand-waving buzzwords. Every message must communicate:
1. **Acute Resonance:** Acknowledging the exact financial and technical friction the prospect experiences.
2. **Structural Explanation:** Explaining the mechanical root cause (why token dumping fails).
3. **Turnkey Resolution:** Presenting an actionable, low-friction diagnostic path forward.
