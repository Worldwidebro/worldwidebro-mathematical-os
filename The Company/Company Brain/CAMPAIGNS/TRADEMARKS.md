## 1. Privacy & Data Handling Policy

1. **Zero Retention of Raw Code:** When performing AST graph extractions, raw source code strings are discarded after AST parse trees are committed to memory.
2. **Encrypted Ingest:** All telemetry and client communications use TLS 1.3 encryption.
3. **Platform Policy Adherence:** Strict compliance with LinkedIn User Agreement, Google Ads Policy, and Meta Business Terms.
4. **Accessibility:** Web assets adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines.
