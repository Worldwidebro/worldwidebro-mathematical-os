## 1. Pricing Philosophy: The No-Brainer ROI Anchor

We never price based on hours worked. We price based on the value created.

- **Anchor Value:** Prospect spends $15,000/mo in LLM API bills ($180,000/year).
- **Annual Savings Delivered:** 60% reduction \(\to\) $108,000 in annual net savings.
- **Audit Fee:** **$7,500 USD** (Fixed Fee).
- **ROI Multiple:** Prospect achieves **14.4x** first-year ROI on the diagnostic fee.
- **Payment Structure:** 50% upfront ($3,750) + 50% upon deliverable handoff ($3,750).
