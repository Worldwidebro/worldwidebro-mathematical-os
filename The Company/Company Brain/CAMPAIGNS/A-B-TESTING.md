## 1. Testing Rigor & Guardrails

1. **No Peeking / Early Stopping:** Tests must achieve pre-calculated sample sizes before declaring a winner.
2. **Minimum Sample Size:** At least 100 observations per variant for outbound email tests; at least 500 visitors per arm for web funnels.
3. **Significance Level:** \(\alpha = 0.05\) (95% confidence interval).
