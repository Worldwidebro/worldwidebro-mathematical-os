## 1. Why High-Touch Outbound First?

For an enterprise diagnostic priced at **$7,500**, broad programmatic display ads or TikTok videos produce negative ROI. CTOs and VPs of Engineering do not buy $7,500 infrastructure audits from generic banner ads.

**The Selection Thesis:**
1. **High Direct Resonance:** 1:1 signal-based cold email allows precise tailoring to the prospect's tech stack (e.g., citing their TypeScript agent repos).
2. **Zero Media Markup:** Outbound capital is spent on data enrichment and infrastructure warmup, not paying ad network margins.
3. **Immediate Feedback:** Objections and replies arrive within 24 hours, directly informing the product and offer roadmap.
