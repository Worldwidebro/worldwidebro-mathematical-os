## 1. Overview
The **Control Matrix** specifies the operational and governance checkpoints across all 7 planes of the WorldwideBro / Company Brain architecture. It ties high-level constitutional mandates directly to machine-verifiable operational tools (Neo4j, Qdrant, Graft, Fractal, and OmniRoute).

## 3. The 7 Operational Planes Summary

1. **Plane 1: Company Brain** — Constitutional intelligence, identity, decisions, policies (`00-CONSTITUTION`, `01-IDENTITY`, `20-DECISIONS`, `21-POLICY`).
2. **Plane 2: Engineering Brain** — Code intelligence, repositories, skills, CI/CD (`13-REPOSITORIES`, `15-SKILLS`, `56-ENGINEERING`, `57-CODE-INTELLIGENCE`).
3. **Plane 3: Operations Brain** — Execution loops, tools, runbooks, workflows (`18-TOOLS`, `22-EXECUTION`, `29-OPERATIONS`, `55-LOOP-ENGINEERING`).
4. **Plane 4: Financial Brain** — FinOps, venture accounting, budgets, monetization (`24-FINANCE`, `56-ENGINEERING/INFRASTRUCTURE/12_COST-GOVERNANCE`).
5. **Plane 5: Customer/Market Brain** — Products, sales, marketing, partnerships (`25-SALES`, `26-MARKETING`, `28-PRODUCT`, `36-PARTNERS`).
6. **Plane 6: Autonomous Agent Brain** — Autonomous agent trees, memory, inference routing (`10-MEMORY`, `16-AGENTS`, `17-MODELS`, `19-ORCHESTRATION`).
7. **Plane 7: Meta/Evolution Brain** — Telemetry, reflection, evolution, metrics (`40-METRICS`, `41-OBSERVABILITY`, `44-LEARNING`, `45-EVOLUTION`).
