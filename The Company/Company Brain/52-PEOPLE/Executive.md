# Executive Role Archetype

> **Domain:** [[52-PEOPLE]]  
> **Authority:** Sovereign Operator & Executive Governance  

## Definition
An **Executive** in the Company Brain ecosystem holds sovereign decision-making authority, budgetary sign-off gates, and fiduciary accountability over one or more of the 35 sectors, 7 core OpCos, or autonomous AI agent fleets.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Truth Ledger Integrity**: Uphold [[REALITY]] against placeholder architectures or false completion claims.
2. **Capital Allocation**: Authorize infrastructure budgets and monitor FinOps through [[ECONOMIC-REALITY]].
3. **Agent Approval Authority**: Serve as the human-in-the-loop sign-off gate for high-autonomy agent workflows in [[16-AGENTS]].
4. **Strategic Direction**: Enforce priorities defined in [[PRIORITIES]] and eliminate non-essential work via [[KILL-LIST]].
