# Cold-Call Playbook — OPS-001 Staffing

**Purpose:** Script and objection handlers for outbound recruitment calls to hiring managers  
**Target:** 74 qualified prospects (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, construction trades)  
**Success Metric:** 8–12% of calls convert to job order (6–9 job orders per 75 calls)  
**Prep Time:** 10–15 min per call batch (review prospect name, company, role, pain point)

---

## OPENING SCRIPT (45 seconds)

**When hiring manager answers:**

> "Hi [name], this is [your name] with Worldwidebro Staffing. I'm calling because I saw [company name] is hiring for [trade/role], and we just placed two [trade]s with [similar company type] last month. They said finding reliable skilled workers is their biggest challenge right now—is that something you're dealing with too?"

**Wait for response.** If YES → proceed to [Value Pitch](#value-pitch). If NO or HESITANT → [Objection #1](#objection-1-not-urgent).

### Value Pitch (20 seconds)

> "What we do is simple: you tell us the role, pay, and must-haves. Within 24 hours, we send you 3–5 pre-qualified candidates who've already passed background and skills verification. You interview, you pick, they start. No recruiter fees, no long contracts—just one placement at a time. Are you actively hiring right now?"

**If YES:** → Move to [Capture Job Order](#capture-job-order-details)  
**If NO but interested:** → [Follow-Up Sequence](#follow-up-sequence---no-job-order-today)  
**If NO and not interested:** → [Closing](#closing)

---

## CAPTURE JOB ORDER DETAILS (3–4 min)

**Once you have YES, pull up the Zapier form or notebook.** Ask these in order:

| Field | Example | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Role Title** | "Journeyman Electrician" | Confirm actual title, not "Electrician" (journeyman vs. apprentice matters) |
| **Pay Range** | "$28–$35/hr" or "$65K–$75K/yr" | If unsure, ask "What's your budget for this role?" |
| **Work Type** | W2 / 1099 / Contract | Required—affects candidate pool |
| **Must-Haves** | "NC license, 5+ yrs exp, own tools, valid DL" | List ALL deal-breakers. If no answer: "What would make someone fail the first day?" |
| **Start Date** | "Sept 15" or "ASAP" | Affects urgency and availability search |
| **Hiring Manager** | "[name], [title], [phone], [email]" | Always get direct contact—don't rely on HR alone |
| **Interview Process** | "1 in-person interview, $50 tool allowance test, 1-week trial" | Understand their timeline (affects our candidate pipeline) |
| **Company Name** | From intro | Verify spelling |
| **Industry/Context** | New build, service, emergency response, etc. | Context for matching |

**Restate the order before hanging up:**  
> "So I have: [role], [pay], [must-haves], start [date]. I'll send you 3–5 candidates by [tomorrow, 2pm]. Cool?"

## COMMON OBJECTIONS & REBUTTALS

### Objection #1: "We're not actively hiring right now"

**Rebuttal (20 seconds):**  
> "I hear that. But you've got projects coming up, right? What I'd like to do is just get on your radar so when you do need someone—and it's always last-minute in construction—you can ping us. Takes 30 seconds. Do you have 30 seconds?"

**If they say YES:**  
- Get their email and phone
- "What's the role you usually hire for?" (capture for future outreach)
- Add to [Follow-Up Sequence](#follow-up-sequence---no-job-order-today)
- Log in Zapier form (see [Logging](#logging-to-zapier))

**If NO:** → [Closing](#closing)

---

### Objection #2: "We always use [recruiter/staff agency/internal referrals]"

**Rebuttal (25 seconds):**  
> "Totally makes sense. Here's the difference: those guys take 20–30% commission, or they tie you into a contract. We do one placement at a time, no commission—you just pay if you hire. And since we already vet skills and background, your team just interviews. Can I send you our process so you see how it works?"

**If they say YES:**
- Email 1-page process overview (see [Template Email](#template-email-after-job-order)) 
- Follow-up call in 3 days
- Log in Zapier: "Sent process overview"

**If NO:** → [Closing](#closing)

---

### Objection #3: "What if they don't work out? What's your guarantee?"

**Rebuttal (30 seconds):**  
> "Good question. Here's what we guarantee: if in the first 48 hours they're not a fit, we send you a replacement candidate at no extra cost. But here's the real thing—we don't send candidates we haven't vetted. Last month 9 out of 10 placements stuck. We're picky on our end so you don't have to be on yours."

**If they ask for details:**  
- "What does 'work out' mean to you?" (listen for pain point—attitude, skills, reliability, cultural fit)
- Match that requirement to [Must-Haves](#capture-job-order-details) when we search
- Proceed to job order if satisfied

**If still skeptical:** → [Closing](#closing)

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE — No Job Order Today

**Scenario:** Hiring manager interested but no active opening yet.

### Day 0: Same-Call Follow-Up
- "Can I shoot you an email tomorrow with 2–3 candidates we think would be great if you DO hire?"
- If YES: Capture email + phone + expected role
- If NO: "No problem. I'll reach out in a month to see if anything's changed. Fair?"

### Day 1 (tomorrow): EMAIL — "Here's who I'm thinking of"
```
Subject: Two [trade] candidates for [company name]

Hi [name],

Even though you're not hiring yet, I found two candidates who match what you 
described: [5+ yrs exp, NC license, own tools].

[CANDIDATE 1] – [Yrs exp], [Key skill/cert]  
[CANDIDATE 2] – [Yrs exp], [Key skill/cert]

Keep them in your back pocket. If one of them fits when you do hire, let me know 
and they can start within 48 hours.

Otherwise, I'll check back in a month to see if priorities have shifted.

— [Your name]  
Worldwidebro Staffing
```

### Day 3: IF NO RESPONSE — Voicemail Script (leave on first try if no answer)
> "Hi [name], it's [your name] from Worldwidebro. Sent you an email yesterday with two candidates we think are solid for your next [role]. If either one clicks, just shoot me a text and we'll get rolling. [Your phone]. Talk soon."

### Day 7: IF STILL NO RESPONSE — Follow-up email (softer)
```
Subject: Just checking in

Hi [name],

Didn't hear back on the candidates email. No pressure—I know you're busy. 
Just want to stay on your radar for when the time is right.

I'll reach out again at the end of the month. If something changes before then, 
you know where to find me.

— [Your name]  
Worldwidebro Staffing  
[Your phone]
```

### Day 30: IF NOT YET CONVERTED — Callback
- Call same hiring manager again
- "I sent over some candidates a month ago. Wanted to see if anything's changed on hiring."
- If YES: proceed to [Capture Job Order](#capture-job-order-details)
- If NO: offer quarterly check-in email, close call politely

## CLOSING (if not converting)

**Do NOT be pushy.** End on good terms—they may need you in 3 months.

**If they say NO:**
> "No worries. If anything changes or you hear of a friend who's hiring, think of us. I'm [your name], [phone]. Talk soon."

**If they seem annoyed:**
> "I appreciate your time. Good luck with your hiring."

**Then log in Zapier: Status = "Not Interested"**

## DAILY BATCH PROCESS

**Before Starting Call Block:**
1. Pull up 10–15 prospect names from [OPS-001 Prospect List]
2. Jot down: company name, industry, estimated size (1-person vs. 50+)
3. Skim 30-second web search: any recent growth/hiring news?
4. Open Zapier form in adjacent tab

**During Calls:**
- Keep notes in Zapier form (don't submit until after each call)
- Aim for 8–12 calls per 60-min block (5–7 min per call average)

**After Call Block:**
1. Review Zapier entries for completeness
2. Submit form
3. Calculate: X calls → Y job orders = Z conversion rate
4. Update [Daily Log Sheet](./daily-activity-log.md)
5. Flag any "Callback Scheduled" for next day

**Target Week 1:** 50 calls → 4–6 job orders (8–12% conversion)

## SUCCESS METRICS

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Calls per day** | 8–12 | ~6–8 hours work after breaks |
| **Conversion rate** | 8–12% | 1 job order per 8–12 calls |
| **Avg call duration** | 5–7 min | Includes objection handling + job order capture |
| **Job orders per week** | 4–6 (from 50 calls) | Week 1 goal |
| **Follow-up re-engagement** | 15–20% | "Interested, no opening" who become job orders within 30 days |
| **Voicemail-to-callback** | 5–10% | Voicemail callbacks that turn into conversations |

**Weekly Check-In (Friday):**  
Review Zapier log: Total calls → Total job orders → Conversion %. If <8%, analyze objections and refine rebuttal script.
