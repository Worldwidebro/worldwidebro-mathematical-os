> **Authority:** Master Control Plane ([[00-CONSTITUTION/control-planes/CP-050|CP-050]])

> **Scope:** 34 Control Planes across 50 Domains, 35 Sectors, and 7 Planes

## 1. Executive Summary
The **Control Planes** are the authoritative governing nodes of WorldwideBro / Company Brain.
Each Control Plane oversees specific operational domains, coordinates with commercial sectors, and enforces dedicated Control Points.
