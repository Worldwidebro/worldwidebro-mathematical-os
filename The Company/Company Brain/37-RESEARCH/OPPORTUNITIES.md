## 2. Quantitative Opportunity Scoring Formula

Every surfaced opportunity is scored out of 100 before receiving engineering resources:

$$\text{Opportunity Score} = (0.30 \times WTP) + (0.25 \times TF) + (0.20 \times RM) + (0.15 \times DL) + (0.10 \times CS)$$

Where:
- **WTP (Willingness to Pay, 0-100):** Proven existing budget or acute cost of inaction ($/year).
- **TF (Technical Feasibility, 0-100):** Ability to implement using our 177 code-backed repos + starred open-source assets without research risk.
- **RM (Regulatory & Data Moat, 0-100):** Compliance lock-in (HIPAA, DOT, SEC) or proprietary graph data.
- **DL (Distribution Leverage, 0-100):** Existing customer access, partner networks, or automated acquisition loops.
- **CS (Capital Synergy, 0-100):** Eligibility for non-dilutive SBIR grants or federal procurement set-asides.
