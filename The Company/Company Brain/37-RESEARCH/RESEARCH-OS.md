## 2. Core Principles of Research-OS

1. **Evidence Outranks Opinion:** Never record a market thesis or customer assumption without verifiable provenance and citation in [`EVIDENCE.md`](file:///Users/acebless/Documents/The%20Company/Company%20Brain/37-RESEARCH/EVIDENCE.md).
2. **Preprint vs. Peer-Reviewed Rigor:** Distinguish raw claims from verified science. Mark research clearly: `PREPRINT`, `PEER_REVIEWED`, `PUBLISHED`, `REPLICATED`, `RETRACTED`, or `UNVERIFIED`.
3. **Macro to Micro Funnel:** Connect macroeconomic government datasets (Census, BLS, FRED) directly to venture unit economics and customer demand.
4. **Technology to Opportunity Signal:** A cluster of patents or academic breakthroughs is meaningless unless it solves an acute commercial problem with proven willingness to pay.
5. **Continuous Pipeline:** Research feeds the Neo4j Knowledge Graph continuously via automated collectors and APIs, rather than manual browsing.
