# PATENT-INTELLIGENCE.md — Patent Analytics & IP Whitespace Discovery

> **Authority:** Legal & Technology Control Plane (CP-027 / CP-031)  
> **Source:** USPTO Open Data APIs, PatentsView, Google Patents  
> **Purpose:** Identifying commercial whitespace, emerging technology clusters, expired patent exploitation opportunities, and freedom-to-operate verification.

---

## 1. The Patent Opportunity Loop

```text
  USPTO / GLOBAL IP FILINGS
             │
             ▼
  PATENT CLUSTER DETECTION (CPC / IPC classifications)
             │
             ▼
  ASSIGNOR / ASSIGNEE MAPPING (Who is funding/filing?)
             │
             ▼
  CITATION VELOCITY (Which technologies are being built on?)
             │
             ▼
  EXPIRATION AUDIT (Which valuable patents are entering public domain?)
             │
             ▼
  WHITESPACE IDENTIFICATION (Where is demand high but patent density low?)
             │
             ▼
  VENTURE PROPOSITION / DEFENSIVE FILING
```

## 3. The Expired Patent Exploitation Strategy

- **Public Domain Capitalization:** Patents in the US expire 20 years from their earliest filing date.
- **Protocol:** Systematically scan expired patents in logistics optimization, supply chain algorithms, and material estimation. When a foundational patent expires, its proprietary methods can be legally converted into open-source capabilities or commercial venture features without licensing fees.
