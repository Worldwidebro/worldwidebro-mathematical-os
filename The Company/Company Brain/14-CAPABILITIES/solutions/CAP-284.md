## 1. Overview & Scope
Maintain formal risk registers evaluating likelihood, financial impact, and mitigation controls across operations.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-284` typically encounter:
- Leadership blind to critical single-points-of-failure until catastrophic events occur
- No systematic framework to prioritize engineering security investments
- Inability to satisfy enterprise insurance underwriting risk evaluations
