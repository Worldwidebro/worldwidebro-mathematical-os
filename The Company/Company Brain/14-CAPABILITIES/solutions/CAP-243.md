# CAP-243: Medical Imaging Processing & DICOM Protocols

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology|SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology: Healthcare & Biotechnology]]

## 1. Overview & Scope
Ingest, render, and analyze DICOM radiology images (MRI, CT, X-Ray) with web-native zero-footprint viewers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-243` typically encounter:
- Radiologists unable to securely view medical scans remotely over standard web browsers
- Slow medical image load times choking hospital network bandwidth
- Lack of automated machine learning pre-screening for urgent brain bleeds

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Cornerstone.js**
- **Orthanc DICOM Server**
- **OHIF Viewer**
- **SimpleITK**
- **Monai (PyTorch)**
