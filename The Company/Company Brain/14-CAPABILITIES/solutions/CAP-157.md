# CAP-157: Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) & Cert Automation

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 5: Cybersecurity & Zero Trust Architecture

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Issue, renew, and revoke private and public TLS/x509 certificates automatically.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-157` typically encounter:
- Outages caused by forgotten TLS certificate expirations bringing down public websites
- Manual labor generating CSRs and installing certificates
- Inability to maintain internal corporate private root Certificate Authorities

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Let's Encrypt / Certbot**
- **Cert-Manager**
- **Smallstep CA**
- **HashiCorp Vault PKI**
- **OpenSSL**
