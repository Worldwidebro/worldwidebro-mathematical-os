## 1. Overview & Scope
Track trademark filings, patent portfolios, provisional applications, and IP licensing contracts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-282` typically encounter:
- Losing trademark rights to trademark squatters due to missed international filing deadlines
- Failure to enforce patent infringement claims before statutory limitations expire
- Accidental assignment of core company patents to third-party consulting clients
