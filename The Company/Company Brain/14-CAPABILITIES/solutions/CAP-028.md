## 1. Overview & Scope
Enforce token bucket, leaky bucket, and fixed window rate limits across authenticated users and IP addresses.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-028` typically encounter:
- API abuse and scraping exhausting backend capacity
- Noisy neighbor problems in multi-tenant SaaS environments
- Runaway cloud billing from unbounded automated client loops
