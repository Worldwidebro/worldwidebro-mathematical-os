# CAP-242: Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS)

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology|SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology: Healthcare & Biotechnology]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Analyze patient symptoms and drug regimens against medical knowledge graphs to alert on adverse interactions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-242` typically encounter:
- Preventable medical errors from adverse drug-to-drug cross interactions
- Physician burnout diagnosing complex, rare symptomatology
- Malpractice liability from missed clinical guideline recommendations

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **UMLS Metathesaurus**
- **DrugBank API**
- **BioPortal**
- **ClinicalTrials.gov API**
