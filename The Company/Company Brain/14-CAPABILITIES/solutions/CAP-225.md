## 1. Overview & Scope
Analyze machinery sensor vibrations and operating temperatures to predict failures before they occur.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-225` typically encounter:
- Catastrophic factory equipment breakdowns causing millions in halted production
- Performing expensive unnecessary scheduled maintenance on healthy machines
- Worker safety hazards from sudden mechanical failures
