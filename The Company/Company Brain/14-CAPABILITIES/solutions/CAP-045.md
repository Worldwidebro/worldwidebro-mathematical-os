## 1. Overview & Scope
Formalize hierarchical OWL/RDF ontologies and controlled vocabularies across corporate sectors.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-045` typically encounter:
- Inconsistent vocabulary causing semantic ambiguity between teams
- Inability to infer logical relationships automatically
- Brittle integration interfaces due to lack of shared semantics

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Protégé**
- **RDFlib**
- **SHACL**
- **schema.org**
- **Wikidata SPARQL**
