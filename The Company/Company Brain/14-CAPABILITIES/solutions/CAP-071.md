## 1. Overview & Scope
Coordinate specialized autonomous agents communicating through structured message protocols to achieve shared objectives.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-071` typically encounter:
- Single LLM agents becoming overwhelmed on complex multi-step tasks
- Uncontrolled communication loops burning excessive API tokens
- Lack of clear role separation and authority boundaries among agents
