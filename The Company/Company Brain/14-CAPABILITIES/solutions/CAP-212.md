## 1. Overview & Scope
Generate optimal multi-stop driver itineraries respecting delivery time windows and cargo capacities.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-212` typically encounter:
- Drivers making backtracking trips wasting hours and gallons of fuel
- Violations of customer-scheduled delivery appointments
- Overloading delivery vans beyond legal gross vehicle weight limits
