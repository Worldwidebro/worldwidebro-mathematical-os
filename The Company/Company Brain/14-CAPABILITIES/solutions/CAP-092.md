## 1. Overview & Scope

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-092` typically encounter:
- Models generating verbose, unhelpful corporate fluff instead of concise answers
- Difficulty enforcing strict negative behavioral constraints through prompting alone
- Drift toward sycophantic behavior rather than rigorous truth-telling
