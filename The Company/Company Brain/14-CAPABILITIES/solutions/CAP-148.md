# CAP-148: FIDO2 & Passwordless WebAuthn Authentication

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 5: Cybersecurity & Zero Trust Architecture

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Replace phishable passwords with hardware-backed public key cryptography and biometric passkeys.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-148` typically encounter:
- Credential harvesting phishing attacks tricking employees into surrendering passwords and SMS OTPs
- High user friction and password reset support tickets
- Account takeover risks from leaked third-party password dumps

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **WebAuthn API**
- **SimpleWebAuthn**
- **YubiKey**
- **Apple Passkeys**
- **Corbado**
