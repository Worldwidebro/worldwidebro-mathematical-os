## 1. Overview & Scope

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-037` typically encounter:
- Slow multi-table relational JOIN operations crippling query throughput
- Inability to perform multi-hop graph path analysis efficiently
- Fragmented entity views across disconnected enterprise silos
