# CAP-218: Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) & Catalogs

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering|SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering: Manufacturing & Engineering]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Centralize vendor performance scorecards, contractual pricing, and electronic parts catalogs.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-218` typically encounter:
- Purchasing agents buying identical supplies from different vendors at widely varying prices
- Unreliable suppliers repeatedly delivering late without accountability
- Lost supplier discounts due to fragmented corporate purchasing volume
