# CAP-232: Industrial IoT (IIoT) Protocol Gateways

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering|SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering: Manufacturing & Engineering]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Bridge legacy industrial protocols (Modbus, OPC UA, CAN bus) to modern cloud MQTT message brokers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-232` typically encounter:
- Inability to extract data from decades-old factory PLC controllers
- Fragile custom serial cable connections dropping sensor packets
- Security vulnerabilities exposing factory control networks to unauthorized remote access

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Eclipse Kura**
- **ThingsBoard**
