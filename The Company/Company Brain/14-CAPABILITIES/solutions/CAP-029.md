# CAP-029: Distributed Consensus & Coordination

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Maintain distributed leader election, configuration leasing, and consensus across cluster members.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-029` typically encounter:
- Split-brain scenarios causing irreversible data divergence
- Concurrent execution of singleton batch jobs corrupting state
- Cluster partition failures during node drops

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **etcd**
- **Consul**
- **ZooKeeper**
- **Chubby**
- **Raft protocol**
