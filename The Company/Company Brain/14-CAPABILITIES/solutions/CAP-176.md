# CAP-176: Payment Gateway Integration & Processing

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-030-fintech-payments|SEC-030-fintech-payments: Fintech & Payments]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Integrate credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized payment rails via developer APIs.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-176` typically encounter:
- Failed checkout conversions due to missing regional payment methods
- High merchant fees from un-optimized payment gateway routing
- Non-compliance with complex credit card network regulations

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Stripe API**
- **Adyen**
- **Braintree / PayPal**
- **Square**
- **Checkout.com**
