# CAP-113: Server-Side Rendering (SSR) & Hybrid Hydration

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Render dynamic web pages on the server with progressive hydration for fast first paint and real-time state.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-113` typically encounter:
- Blank white screens on slow mobile connections during massive JavaScript bundle downloads
- Poor social media link previews and open-graph card generation
- SEO crawlers failing to index dynamic client-rendered content

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Next.js**
- **Remix / React Router**
- **Nuxt.js**
- **SvelteKit**
- **Qwik**
