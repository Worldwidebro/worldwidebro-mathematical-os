## 1. Overview & Scope
Coordinate autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and automated sorting conveyors in fulfillment centers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-228` typically encounter:
- Human traffic jams and picker collisions in high-density warehouse aisles
- Throughput bottlenecks during holiday sales surges
- High worker injury rates from heavy repetitive manual lifting

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **ROS (Robot Operating System)**
- **NVIDIA Isaac Sim**
- **Open-RMF**
- **AWS IoT RoboRunner**
- **Kiva Protocols**
