> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-014-human-resources-staffing|SEC-014-human-resources-staffing: Human Resources & Staffing]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Extract structured candidate work histories, skills, and match qualifications against job descriptions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-263` typically encounter:
- Recruiters spending 10+ hours a week manually opening and screening thousands of PDF resumes
- High-potential talent overlooked due to unstructured resume formats
- Disjointed candidate communication leading to accepted offers elsewhere

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Greenhouse API**
- **Lever API**
- **Affinda Resume Parser**
- **OpenCATS**
- **Workable API**
