## 1. Overview & Scope
Index and search data asset metadata, schemas, descriptions, and ownership across the organization.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-047` typically encounter:
- Engineers wasting hours searching for existing datasets
- Undocumented tables leading to duplicated analytical pipelines
- Lack of clear data ownership causing abandoned datasets

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Apache Atlas**
- **OpenMetadata**
