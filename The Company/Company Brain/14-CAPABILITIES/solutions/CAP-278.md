## 1. Overview & Scope
Codify clear decision rights, RACI matrixes, and formal control plane delegation across operations.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-278` typically encounter:
- Paralysis caused by unclear ownership of major technical or financial decisions
- Lower-tier agents making unauthorized high-consequence system alterations
- Bottlenecks waiting for CEO sign-off on trivial day-to-day operational tasks
