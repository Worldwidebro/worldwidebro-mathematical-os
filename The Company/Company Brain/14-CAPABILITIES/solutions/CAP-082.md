# CAP-082: Real-Time Voice Synthesis & Speech Generation

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-004-content-media|SEC-004-content-media: Content & Media]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 3: Autonomous Agents & AI Reasoning

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Synthesize expressive, low-latency conversational speech with dynamic emotion and inflection.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-082` typically encounter:
- Robotic, unnatural text-to-speech eroding user engagement in voice applications
- High round-trip audio latency breaking conversational turn-taking
- Inability to clone specific brand voice personas accurately

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **ElevenLabs**
- **Kokoro TTS**
- **ChatTTS**
- **WhisperSpeech**
- **F5-TTS**
