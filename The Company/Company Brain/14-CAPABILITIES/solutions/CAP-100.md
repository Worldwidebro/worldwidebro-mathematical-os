# CAP-100: Dependency Graph Analysis & Vulnerability Mapping

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

## 1. Overview & Scope
Construct and query full transitive package and module dependency graphs to catch security and circular risks.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-100` typically encounter:
- Circular dependency deadlocks during module initialization
- Hidden critical CVEs inherited through deeply nested transitive packages
- Bloated artifact bundles caused by duplicated or obsolete dependencies
