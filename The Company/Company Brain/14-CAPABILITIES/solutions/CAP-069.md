# CAP-069: High-Volume Batch Processing & MapReduce

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure|SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure: Utilities & Infrastructure]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Distribute large-scale analytical and transformation batch jobs across compute clusters.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-069` typically encounter:
- Single-node batch jobs running for days and failing at the final step
- Inability to process terabyte-scale log archives cost-effectively
- Lack of checkpointing requiring full job restarts upon failure

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Apache Spark**
- **Ray**
- **Dask**
- **DuckDB Batch**
- **AWS Batch**
