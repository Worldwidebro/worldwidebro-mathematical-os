## 1. Overview & Scope
Proactively search internal systems for Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) using global IOC feeds.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-152` typically encounter:
- Attackers dwelling inside corporate infrastructure for an average of 200+ days undetected
- Lack of context regarding whether an observed probe is part of a targeted campaign
- Inability to ingest and act upon government and industry threat intelligence bulletins
