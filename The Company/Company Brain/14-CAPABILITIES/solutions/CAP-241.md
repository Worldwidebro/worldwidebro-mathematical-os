# CAP-241: Electronic Health Records (EHR) & FHIR APIs

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology|SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology: Healthcare & Biotechnology]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 8: Sector Verticals & Specialized Domains

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Integrate clinical patient histories and lab results securely via HL7 Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-241` typically encounter:
- Siloed medical records preventing doctors from accessing critical patient drug allergies
- Massive legal penalties under HIPAA for insecure health data handling
- Inability to integrate healthtech apps with Epic, Cerner, and hospital systems

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **HL7 FHIR API**
- **Medplum (Open Source EHR)**
- **HAPI FHIR**
- **Metriport**
- **Canvas Medical**
