# CAP-181: Automated Invoicing & Accounts Receivable (AR)

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-030-fintech-payments|SEC-030-fintech-payments: Fintech & Payments]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 6: FinTech, Commerce & Monetization

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Generate, dispatch, and track structured electronic invoices with automated payment reminders.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-181` typically encounter:
- Cash flow crunches caused by 60+ day overdue customer invoices
- Manual accounting hours spent drafting and emailing PDF invoices
- Mismatched invoice payments creating unallocated cash reserves

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Stripe Invoicing**
- **FreshBooks API**
- **QuickBooks Invoicing**
- **Bill.com**
- **Anvil PDF**
