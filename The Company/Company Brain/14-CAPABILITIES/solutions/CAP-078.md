## 1. Overview & Scope
Run persistent Sense-Plan-Act-Verify loops that iterate until executable proof of completion is reached.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-078` typically encounter:
- Agents halting prematurely upon generating text without verifying execution
- Unchecked error cascades spinning out of control without convergence checks
- Lack of bounded iteration limits leading to infinite billing loops
