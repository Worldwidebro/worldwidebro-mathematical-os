# CAP-269: Video Transcoding & HLS/DASH Streaming Ladders

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-004-content-media|SEC-004-content-media: Content & Media]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 8: Sector Verticals & Specialized Domains

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Transcode raw high-definition video into adaptive bitrate HLS/DASH streaming segments for seamless playback.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-269` typically encounter:
- Buffering and playback failures for mobile viewers on slow cellular networks
- Prohibitive cloud video transcoding bills from un-optimized CPU encoders
- Inability to protect premium video content with DRM encryption

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **FFmpeg**
- **Shaka Player**
- **AWS Elemental MediaConvert**
- **HLS.js**
- **Video.js**
