## 1. Overview & Scope
Monitor multi-bank cash positions, automate sweeps, and optimize yield on operational balances.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-193` typically encounter:
- Cash sitting in zero-interest accounts losing purchasing power to inflation
- Overdraft fees caused by unmonitored cash draws across subsidiary accounts
- Counterparty risk exposure during regional banking crises
