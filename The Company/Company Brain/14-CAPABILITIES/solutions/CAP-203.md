## 1. Overview & Scope
Tokenize real-world assets, real estate equity, and receivables into compliant on-chain instruments.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-203` typically encounter:
- High legal and administrative friction syndicating ownership in real estate and debt
- Lack of 24/7 global trading liquidity for physical infrastructure assets
- Fragile manual record-keeping for asset dividend distributions
