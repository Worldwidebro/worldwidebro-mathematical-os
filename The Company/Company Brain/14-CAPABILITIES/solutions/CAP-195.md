## 1. Overview & Scope
Issue white-label FDIC-insured bank accounts, routing numbers, and payment capabilities inside apps.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-195` typically encounter:
- High regulatory and capital hurdles to establishing a licensed de-novo bank
- Customers leaving product ecosystem to conduct banking transactions elsewhere
- Missing out on lucrative interchange fee revenue sharing
