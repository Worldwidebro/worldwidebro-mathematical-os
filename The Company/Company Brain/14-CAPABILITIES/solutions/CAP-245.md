> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Scrub 18 HIPAA identifiers from clinical notes and health records to create HIPAA Safe Harbor research datasets.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-245` typically encounter:
- Inability to train healthcare AI models legally without risking patient identity exposure
- Hefty criminal penalties for unauthorized disclosure of protected health information (PHI)
- High manual consulting fees to de-identify clinical datasets for medical research
