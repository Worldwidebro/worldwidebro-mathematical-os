## 1. Overview & Scope
Advance capital against outstanding verified customer invoices via automated underwriting.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-210` typically encounter:
- Rapidly growing ventures starved of cash while waiting for 90-day enterprise customer payments
- Missing supplier bulk discounts due to trapped accounts receivable
- Predatory high-interest traditional merchant cash advance loans
