# CAP-230: Vendor Service Level Agreement (SLA) Auditing

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-023-professional-services|SEC-023-professional-services: Professional Services]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously track vendor delivery times, quality scores, and contract compliance against agreed SLAs.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-230` typically encounter:
- Vendors failing delivery deadlines without triggering contractual rebate penalties
- Lack of historical data during annual contract renegotiations
- Inability to compare vendor performance objectively across geographical divisions

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **SLA Auditor Engine**
- **Vendor Scorecard**
- **Grafana SLA dashboards**
- **Service Registry**
- **Tableau Vendor Ops**
