# CAP-257: Restaurant Kitchen Display (KDS) & Table Management

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-010-food-service-restaurants|SEC-010-food-service-restaurants: Food Service & Restaurants]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Route table orders, manage kitchen cooking ticket pacing, and accept mobile QR code dining payments.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-257` typically encounter:
- Kitchen tickets lost during busy Friday dinner rushes causing 45-minute meal delays
- Servers taking wrong orders or failing to communicate food allergies to the line cooks
- Slow table turnover times reducing restaurant weekend revenue
