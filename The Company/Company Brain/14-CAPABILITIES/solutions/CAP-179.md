# CAP-179: Multi-Currency Exchange & FX Settlement

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Support dynamic real-time currency conversion, hedging, and localized foreign exchange settlement.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-179` typically encounter:
- Shocking customers with surprise international conversion bank fees
- Margin erosion caused by unhedged currency volatility across international ventures
- Complex multi-currency accounting reconciliation overhead

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Wise API**
- **Fixer.io**
- **Open Exchange Rates**
- **Stripe Multi-Currency**
- **Currencylayer**
