## 1. Overview & Scope
Dynamically adjust lesson difficulty, explanations, and practice quizzes to individual student skill levels.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-259` typically encounter:
- Fast learners becoming bored while struggling students fall permanently behind
- High cost of private one-on-one human subject tutoring
- Generic textbook explanations failing to match student learning styles
