## 1. Overview & Scope
Match government/foundation grant opportunities, draft technical proposals, and track submissions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-292` typically encounter:
- Missing out on millions in non-dilutive government R&D grant funding
- Small teams unable to compete with massive defense contractors on 200-page RFP bids
- Disqualification from grant programs due to missing administrative checklist items

- **SAM.gov API**

- **Proposal Synthesizer**
