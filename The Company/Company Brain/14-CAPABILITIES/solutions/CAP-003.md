# CAP-003: Authorization & Fine-Grained Access Control

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 1: Core Architecture & Platform Engineering

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Enforce declarative Role-Based (RBAC) and Attribute-Based (ABAC) access control across services and data resources.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-003` typically encounter:
- Privilege escalation vulnerabilities and unauthorized data access
- Hardcoded authorization logic distributed across codebases
- Audit failures due to inability to prove access segregation

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Open Policy Agent (OPA)**
- **Casbin**
- **Ory Keto**
- **Permit.io**
- **AWS IAM**
