# CAP-030: High-Availability Disaster Recovery & Failover

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure|SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure: Utilities & Infrastructure]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate multi-region cross-cloud failover, automated data replication, and Recovery Time Objective (RTO) enforcement.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-030` typically encounter:
- Prolonged business outage during cloud provider availability zone failures
- Unacceptable Recovery Point Objective (RPO) data loss
- Failed manual disaster recovery procedures during high-stress crises

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Velero**
- **Patroni**
- **Keepalived**
- **Cloudflare DNS Failover**
- **Syncthing**
