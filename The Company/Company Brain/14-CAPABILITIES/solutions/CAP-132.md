# CAP-132: Headless CMS & Content Mesh Architecture

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-004-content-media|SEC-004-content-media: Content & Media]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Decouple editorial content management from presentation frontends with structured API-driven schemas.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-132` typically encounter:
- Non-technical content creators requiring developer deployments to change marketing copy
- Monolithic WordPress sites exposing PHP and SQL security vulnerabilities
- Inability to distribute marketing content seamlessly across web and mobile apps

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Strapi**
- **Directus**
- **Payload CMS**
- **Sanity.io**
- **Ghost**

1. **Assess**: Evaluate current venture state, data contracts, and technical debt against `CAP-132` requirements.
