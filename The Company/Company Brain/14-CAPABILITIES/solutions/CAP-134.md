# CAP-134: Design System Governance & Token Synchronization

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-019-marketing-advertising|SEC-019-marketing-advertising: Marketing & Advertising]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Synchronize design tokens (colors, typography, spacing) bidirectionally between Figma and Git codebases.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-134` typically encounter:
- Designers adjusting hex colors in Figma while production code uses obsolete values
- Lengthy manual design review cycles pointing out padding and border-radius inconsistencies
- Fragmented dark-mode styling across product views

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Tokens Studio**
- **Style Dictionary**
- **Figma REST API**
- **Storybook Tokens**
- **Tailwind Theme Tokens**
