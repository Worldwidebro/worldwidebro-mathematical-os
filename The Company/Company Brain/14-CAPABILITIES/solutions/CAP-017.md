## 1. Overview & Scope
Solve complex Vehicle Routing Problems (VRP) and multi-depot traveling salesperson optimizations under dynamic constraints.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-017` typically encounter:
- Excessive fuel expenditure and vehicle wear from un-optimized dispatch paths
- Missed customer delivery SLA windows
- Inability to adapt routes dynamically to real-time traffic incidents
