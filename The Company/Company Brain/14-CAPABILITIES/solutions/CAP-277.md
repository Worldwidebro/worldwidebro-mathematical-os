## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate stage-gate venture validation from market opportunity discovery to functional MVP release.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-277` typically encounter:
- Founders wasting 6 months building products that nobody wants to buy
- Lack of disciplined validation gates before committing engineering capital
- Inability to spin up dozens of experimental market tests concurrently
