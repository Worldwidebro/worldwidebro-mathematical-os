# CAP-165: Data Loss Prevention (DLP) & Exfiltration Blocking

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Scan and block unauthorized transfers of sensitive corporate data, code, and customer records.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-165` typically encounter:
- Disgruntled employees copying customer databases to personal USB drives or cloud drives
- Pasting sensitive private customer PII into public third-party AI chatbots
- Accidental public emailing of internal financial models and spreadsheets
