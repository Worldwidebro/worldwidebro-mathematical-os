## 1. Overview & Scope
Synthesize operational, financial, and engineering telemetry into a unified real-time executive cockpit.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-298` typically encounter:
- Executives relying on stale weekly email reports to make fast decisions
- Disconnected operating silos preventing leadership from correlating marketing spend with server load
- Information overload burying critical operational anomalies
