## 1. Overview & Scope
Deploy, scale, and manage containerized service workloads across distributed compute clusters.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-009` typically encounter:
- Server resource fragmentation and low hardware utilization
- Manual intervention required for failed process restarts
- Complex multi-service networking and port allocation conflicts

---

- **Kubernetes (K8s)**
- **Docker Swarm**
- **HashiCorp Nomad**
- **k3s**
- **Amazon ECS**
