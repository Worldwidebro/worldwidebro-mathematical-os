## 1. Overview & Scope
Ingest telemetry, score risk parameters, and automate instant claim adjudication and payouts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-206` typically encounter:
- Slow, paper-heavy insurance claims processing taking weeks to pay out policyholders
- High loss ratios from inaccurate manual actuarial risk pricing
- Fraudulent claims slipping through human manual review
