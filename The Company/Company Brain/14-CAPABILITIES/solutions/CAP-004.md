# CAP-004: Data Modeling & Schema Architecture

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 1: Core Architecture & Platform Engineering

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Design scalable relational, document, and graph data models optimized for transactional integrity and analytical throughput.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-004` typically encounter:
- Data redundancy, corruption, and orphan records across services
- Costly query bottlenecks caused by non-normalized or un-indexed schemas
- Inflexible schemas preventing feature evolution

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **PostgreSQL**
- **Prisma ORM**
- **Drizzle ORM**
- **Liquibase**
- **Flyway**
