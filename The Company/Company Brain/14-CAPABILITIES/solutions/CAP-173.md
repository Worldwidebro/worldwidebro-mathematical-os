# CAP-173: Secure Remote Access & Private Mesh Tunnels

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-022-telecommunications-connectivity|SEC-022-telecommunications-connectivity: Telecommunications & Connectivity]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 5: Cybersecurity & Zero Trust Architecture

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Connect remote engineer machines and localized servers into an encrypted, identity-aware wireguard mesh.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-173` typically encounter:
- Opening SSH ports (`22`) to the public internet exposing servers to brute-force scans
- Fragmented, unreliable corporate VPNs dropping connections during large file transfers
- Lack of centralized multi-factor authentication for server terminal sessions
