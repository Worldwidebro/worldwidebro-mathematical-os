# CAP-067: Distributed Locking & Lease Management

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure|SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure: Utilities & Infrastructure]]

## 1. Overview & Scope
Acquire, maintain, and safely release distributed mutex locks across clustered microservices.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-067` typically encounter:
- Race conditions during concurrent balance deductions or seat reservations
- Deadlocks leaving background processing queues permanently frozen
- Stale locks persisting indefinitely after worker process crashes

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Redlock (Redis)**
- **etcd Distributed Lock**
- **Consul Sessions**
- **ZooKeeper Recipes**
- **ShedLock**
