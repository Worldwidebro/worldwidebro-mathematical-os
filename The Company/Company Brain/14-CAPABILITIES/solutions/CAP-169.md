# CAP-169: Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Preserve memory snapshots, analyze disk images, and reconstruct attack kill chains during breaches.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-169` typically encounter:
- Contamination of legal evidence by improperly investigating compromised servers
- Inability to determine the exact initial entry vector of an intrusion
- Prolonged dwell time while responders blindly guess attacker movements

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Velociraptor**
- **Volatility Framework**
- **Autopsy**
- **Plaso (log2timeline)**
- **GRR Rapid Response**
