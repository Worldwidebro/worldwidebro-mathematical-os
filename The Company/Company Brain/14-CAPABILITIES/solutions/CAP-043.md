# CAP-043: Entity Resolution & De-duplication

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Identify and merge identical real-world entities across disparate corporate databases.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-043` typically encounter:
- Duplicate customer profiles skewing CRM metrics and billing records
- Inability to link related records across acquired entities
- Fragmented user histories causing poor personalized experiences

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Zingg**
- **Splink**
- **Dedupe (Python)**
- **Neo4j Graph Data Science**
- **OpenSearch Fuzzy Match**
