# CAP-046: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Architecture

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Ground LLM responses with real-time vector search, re-ranking, and dynamic context injection.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-046` typically encounter:
- Hallucinations in language model outputs degrading user trust
- Language models answering from outdated pre-training knowledge
- Leakage of sensitive private data without tenant context filtering

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **LlamaIndex**
- **LangChain**
- **Haystack**
- **RAGatouille (ColBERT)**
- **FlashRank**
