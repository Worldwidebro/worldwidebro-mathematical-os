> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Generate statistically representative synthetic datasets using generative models and copulas.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-060` typically encounter:
- Sparse training data in edge-case or high-consequence scenarios
- Legal prohibitions preventing real data utilization in external AI models
- Imbalanced class distributions skewing model predictions
