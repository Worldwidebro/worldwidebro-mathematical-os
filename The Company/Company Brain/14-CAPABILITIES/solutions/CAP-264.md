## 1. Overview & Scope
Gather peer feedback, track OKR progress, and detect team burnout signals across departments.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-264` typically encounter:
- Subjective, biased annual performance reviews based on recent recency bias
- High-performing employees quitting unexpectedly without management noticing discontent
- Misalignment between individual day-to-day engineering tasks and corporate OKRs
