# CAP-059: Data Anonymization & Differential Privacy

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Generate structurally valid, anonymized datasets for safe testing, partner sharing, and research.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-059` typically encounter:
- Inability to safely test production bugs in staging without risking customer privacy
- Vulnerability to re-identification attacks on pseudo-anonymized datasets
- Delays in external partnership research due to data sharing liabilities

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **ARX Data Anonymizer**
- **Faker (Python)**
- **Diffprivlib**
- **OpenDP**
- **Presidio (Microsoft)**
