## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously collect configuration evidence and policy attestations to prove compliance automatically.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-171` typically encounter:
- Scrambling for weeks before annual audits to manually screenshot cloud consoles
- Lapse of security controls between audit windows leading to undetected vulnerabilities
- High audit fees caused by manual CPA evidence verification
