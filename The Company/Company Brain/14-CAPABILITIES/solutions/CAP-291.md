## 1. Overview & Scope
Deploy multivariate A/B tests, measure statistical significance, and optimize acquisition funnels.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-291` typically encounter:
- Marketing teams rolling out UI changes that inadvertently crush conversion rates
- Declaring A/B test winners prematurely due to lack of statistical power
- Inability to attribute conversion lifts to specific experiment variations
