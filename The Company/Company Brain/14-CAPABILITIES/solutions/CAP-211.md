# CAP-211: Fleet Telematics & Real-Time Vehicle Tracking

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-017-logistics-transportation|SEC-017-logistics-transportation: Logistics & Transportation]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Stream, parse, and map high-frequency GPS, OBD-II diagnostic codes, and driver telemetry.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-211` typically encounter:
- Vehicle breakdowns occurring without advance warning from engine diagnostics
- Inefficient driver speeding and idling driving up fuel and maintenance bills
- Inability to provide customers with accurate real-time delivery vehicle locations

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Traccar**
- **Samsara API**
- **Geotab SDK**
- **Telematics Data Protocol**
- **H3 Spatial Mesh**
