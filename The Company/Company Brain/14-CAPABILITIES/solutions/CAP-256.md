## 1. Overview & Scope
Synchronize room rates and real-time inventory across Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb, and direct hotel portals.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-256` typically encounter:
- Accidental room double-bookings during holiday surges causing angry stranded guests
- Selling rooms below market rates due to lack of real-time competitor rate updates
- High 20%+ OTA commission fees on direct hotel guest bookings
