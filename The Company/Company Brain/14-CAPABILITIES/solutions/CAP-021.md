# CAP-021: Edge Computing & Local Compute Mesh

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure|SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure: Utilities & Infrastructure]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 1: Core Architecture & Platform Engineering

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Orchestrate edge runtimes and distributed local compute nodes across private network topologies (e.g. Mac Studio + Tailscale).

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-021` typically encounter:
- High cloud egress bills and latency penalties for regional users
- Vulnerability to central cloud outages crippling edge operations
- Under-utilization of powerful on-premise hardware

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Tailscale**
- **K3s**
- **Wasmtime**
- **Cloudflare Workers**
- **Fly.io**
