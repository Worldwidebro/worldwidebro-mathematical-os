## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously test datasets for null counts, uniqueness, distribution shifts, and outliers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-051` typically encounter:
- Bad data silently polluting machine learning models and executive reports
- Broken pipelines going unnoticed until critical business failures occur
- Lack of quantifiable data freshness and completeness SLAs
