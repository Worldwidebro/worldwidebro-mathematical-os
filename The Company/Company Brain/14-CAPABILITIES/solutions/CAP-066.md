# CAP-066: Distributed Cache Invalidation & Event Broadcast

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Broadcast fine-grained cache eviction signals across multiple regional nodes over Pub/Sub.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-066` typically encounter:
- Users seeing inconsistent stale data depending on which server answers the request
- Full cache flushes causing stampedes and database outages
- Complex custom eviction logic scattered throughout backend code

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Redis Pub/Sub**
- **RabbitMQ Fanout**
- **NATS**
- **Infinispan**
- **Hazelcast**
