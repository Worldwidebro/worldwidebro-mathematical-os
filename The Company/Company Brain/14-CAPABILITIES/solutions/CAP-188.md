# CAP-188: Dispute & Chargeback Defense Management

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate the collection of delivery proof and submit evidence to dispute illegitimate chargebacks.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-188` typically encounter:
- Revenue loss from fraudulent 'friendly fraud' chargebacks
- Merchant accounts getting terminated by Visa/Mastercard for exceeding 1% dispute rates
- Hours wasted compiling shipping tracking numbers and user logs for bank responses

---

- **Chargeflow**

- **Disputeifier**
- **Signifyd**
