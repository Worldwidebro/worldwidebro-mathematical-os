## 1. Overview & Scope
Deploy and tune dense vector indices for cosine similarity search and nearest-neighbor lookups.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-036` typically encounter:
- High search latency as vector embeddings scale into millions
- Vector memory exhaustion without quantized index compression
- Lack of metadata filtering alongside vector similarity

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
