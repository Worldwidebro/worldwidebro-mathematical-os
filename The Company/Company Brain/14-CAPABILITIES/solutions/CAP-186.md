# CAP-186: Global Sales Tax & VAT Calculation Engine

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-008-financial-services|SEC-008-financial-services: Financial Services]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Calculate real-time sales tax, VAT, and GST rates across jurisdictions and generate filing reports.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-186` typically encounter:
- Severe tax penalties and audits from selling across state and international borders without tax collection
- Inability to maintain thousands of changing municipal tax rate rules manually
- Overcharging customers on tax-exempt products harming sales conversions

---

- **Quaderno**
