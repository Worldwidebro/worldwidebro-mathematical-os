## 1. Overview & Scope
Match merchant bank deposits against payment gateway gross, net, fee, and refund line items.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-194` typically encounter:
- Unexplained variances between credit card settlements and actual bank deposits
- Undetected billing fee increases from payment processors eating margins
- Difficulty separating revenue from customer sales taxes and processing fees
