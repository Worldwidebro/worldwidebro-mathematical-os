## 1. Overview & Scope
Index entire git repositories into relational and vector graphs for instantaneous symbol and logic lookup.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-099` typically encounter:
- Engineers spending hours searching for where specific business logic is implemented
- Duplicate functions written because existing internal utilities were not discovered
- Difficulty onboarding new developers or AI agents onto massive legacy repos
