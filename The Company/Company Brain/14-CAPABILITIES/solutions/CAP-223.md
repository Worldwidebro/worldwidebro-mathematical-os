## 1. Overview & Scope
Calculate mathematically optimal 3D pallet and shipping container packing layouts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-223` typically encounter:
- Shipping half-empty containers driving up ocean and air freight expenditure
- Cargo damage in transit caused by unstable or poorly weighted pallet packing
- Hours spent by dock workers manually figuring out how to fit odd-sized cargo
