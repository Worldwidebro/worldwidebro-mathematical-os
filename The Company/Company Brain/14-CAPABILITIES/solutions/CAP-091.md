# CAP-091: Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA)

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 3: Autonomous Agents & AI Reasoning

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Adapt base models to domain-specific syntax and vocabulary with lightweight low-rank adapters.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-091` typically encounter:
- Generic models failing to output company-specific DSLs or OrgScript grammar
- High GPU memory requirements for full model parameter retraining
- Inability to maintain and swap multiple customer-specific adapters on a single base model
