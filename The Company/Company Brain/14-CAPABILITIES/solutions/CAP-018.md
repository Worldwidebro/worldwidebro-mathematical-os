## 1. Overview & Scope
Deploy edge and cloud vision pipelines for object detection, image segmentation, and visual quality inspection.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-018` typically encounter:
- High manual labor costs in visual inspection workflows
- Inability to extract structured tabular data from images and documents
- High latency and inference costs on un-optimized vision models

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Ultralytics YOLOv8**
- **OpenCV**
- **MediaPipe**
- **Tesseract OCR**
- **TorchVision**
