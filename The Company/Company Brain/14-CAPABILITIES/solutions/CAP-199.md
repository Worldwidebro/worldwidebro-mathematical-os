# CAP-199: Usage-Based Metering & Consumption Billing

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-028-b2b-enterprise-software|SEC-028-b2b-enterprise-software: B2B Enterprise Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 6: FinTech, Commerce & Monetization

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Aggregate, meter, and bill high-frequency API calls, storage gigabytes, and compute seconds.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-199` typically encounter:
- Inability to monetize AI products whose costs scale with token and GPU consumption
- Billing disputes caused by opaque, un-auditable usage metrics
- Lag in usage aggregation delaying end-of-month invoice generation
