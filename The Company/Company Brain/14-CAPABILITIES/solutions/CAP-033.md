> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Decouple code deployment from feature release with real-time kill switches and audience percentage rollouts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-033` typically encounter:
- Engineering blockages waiting for marketing or sales launch dates
- Need to execute full rollbacks for a single broken minor feature
- Inability to run beta experiments on targeted user cohorts
