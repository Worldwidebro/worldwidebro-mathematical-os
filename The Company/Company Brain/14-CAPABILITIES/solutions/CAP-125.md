> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Generate automated CycloneDX and SPDX inventory manifests of all open-source dependencies and licenses.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-125` typically encounter:
- Accidental inclusion of viral GPLv3 licenses in proprietary commercial codebases
- Inability to answer enterprise client inquiries regarding exposure to zero-day CVEs
- Non-compliance with US Federal Executive Order cybersecurity requirements
