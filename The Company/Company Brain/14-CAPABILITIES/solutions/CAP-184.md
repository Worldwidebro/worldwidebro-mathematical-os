## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously reconcile bank statement line items against internal application transaction logs.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-184` typically encounter:
- Accountants spending weeks at quarter-end hunting for missing pennies
- Undetected payment gateway settlement slippage draining corporate margins
- Audit failures due to lack of verifiable reconciliation proof
