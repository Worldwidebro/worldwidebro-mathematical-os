## 1. Overview & Scope
Optimize inventory distribution across regional fulfillment hubs to minimize total delivery times.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-227` typically encounter:
- Stock shortages on West Coast while East Coast warehouses sit on stagnant excess inventory
- Costly cross-country shipping fees to fulfill orders from distant warehouses
- Inability to decide mathematically when to transfer stock between regional depots
