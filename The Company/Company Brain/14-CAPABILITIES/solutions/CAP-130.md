# CAP-130: GraphQL Federation & Unified Schema Graphs

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Combine independent subgraph schemas into a single unified GraphQL gateway for frontend clients.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-130` typically encounter:
- Frontend teams having to query multiple disparate REST endpoints to populate a single dashboard
- Schema conflicts and uncoordinated breaking changes between product domains
- N+1 query performance disasters when aggregating multi-service data

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Apollo Federation**
- **Cosmo (WunderGraph)**
- **GraphQL Mesh**
- **Hasura**
- **Stitching**
