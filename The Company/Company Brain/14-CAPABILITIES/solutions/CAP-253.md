## 1. Overview & Scope
Analyze multi-spectral Sentinel-2 and Landsat satellite imagery to monitor crop health and vegetation indices (NDVI).

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-253` typically encounter:
- Over-application of expensive fertilizer and water destroying agricultural margins
- Crop disease outbreaks spreading across fields before farmers notice on the ground
- Unpredictable harvest yields disrupting food processing supply contracts
