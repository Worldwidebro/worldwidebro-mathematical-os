# CAP-221: Freight Brokerage & Load Matching Engines

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-017-logistics-transportation|SEC-017-logistics-transportation: Logistics & Transportation]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Match commercial freight shippers with available carrier truckload capacities programmatically.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-221` typically encounter:
- Trucks driving empty backhaul miles wasting fuel and driver hours
- Shippers paying exorbitant spot freight rates during capacity crunches
- High manual brokerage phone call overhead booking simple freight lanes

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Convoy API**
- **DAT One API**
- **Truckstop.com API**
- **Freightos**
- **LoadMatch Engine**
