## 1. Overview & Scope
Verify cryptographic claims and transactions without revealing the underlying private data.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-164` typically encounter:
- Need to expose full user income or identity documents just to verify eligibility
- Public blockchain transactions exposing proprietary company financial flows
- Inability to audit enterprise systems without viewing trade secrets
