# CAP-161: Immutable Audit Logging & Tamper Proofing

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-016-legal-compliance|SEC-016-legal-compliance: Legal & Compliance]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Ship audit logs to append-only, cryptographically sealed storage resistant to attacker modification.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-161` typically encounter:
- Attackers clearing `/var/log` and cloud logs to conceal their intrusion footprint
- Inability to prove compliance with financial audit trail regulations
- Disputed actions where employees claim logs were altered retroactively

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **AWS S3 Object Lock (WORM)**
- **Sigstore Rekor**
- **Fluency Logs**
- **Vector / Logstash**
- **Qdrant Audit Trails**
