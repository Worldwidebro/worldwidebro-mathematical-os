## 1. Overview & Scope
Optimize digital ventures for Chinese search engines, ICP licenses, and WeChat Mini-Programs.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-275` typically encounter:
- Western web apps being completely blocked by the Great Firewall of China
- Slow multi-second load times due to lack of domestic Chinese CDN nodes
- Missing out on 1.4B consumers who exclusively use WeChat Pay and Baidu
