# CAP-061: Graph Neural Networks (GNN) & Relational Learning

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]

## 1. Overview & Scope
Train deep learning models directly on graph structures for link prediction and node classification.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-061` typically encounter:
- Traditional ML failing to capture complex relational network topologies
- Inability to detect sophisticated fraud rings and money laundering syndicates
- Poor recommendation relevance for highly connected social or commerce graphs

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **PyTorch Geometric (PyG)**
- **DGL (Deep Graph Library)**
- **Neo4j GDS**
- **Spektral**
- **GraphSAGE**
