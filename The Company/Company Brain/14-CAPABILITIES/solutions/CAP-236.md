## 1. Overview & Scope
Calculate Scope 1, 2, and 3 transportation emissions and optimize routes for lowest carbon impact.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-236` typically encounter:
- Enterprise clients refusing to do business with vendors that cannot report logistics emissions
- Inability to verify environmental ESG sustainability metrics for institutional investors
- Missing out on carbon reduction tax incentives and green freight subsidies
