# CAP-144: Web Application Firewall (WAF) & L7 Defense

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Inspect and filter incoming HTTP traffic to block SQLi, XSS, automated scraping, and known exploit payloads.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-144` typically encounter:
- Zero-day web vulnerabilities exploited before developers can ship patches
- Malicious botnets scraping proprietary pricing and content catalogs
- Brute-force credential stuffing overwhelming authentication endpoints

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Cloudflare WAF**
- **AWS WAF**
- **ModSecurity / Coraza**
- **Fastly Next-Gen WAF**
- **Wallarm**
