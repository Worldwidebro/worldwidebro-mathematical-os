## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously scrape federal and municipal regulatory registers to alert on emerging legal mandates.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-262` typically encounter:
- Companies hit with massive regulatory fines for failing to update policies to new laws
- Legal teams blind to regulatory changes in foreign export jurisdictions
- Weeks wasted manually scanning thousands of pages of legislative dockets
