## 1. Overview & Scope
Break down high-level corporate goals into executable, dependency-mapped work packages.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-077` typically encounter:
- Agents attempting to execute ambiguous high-level goals in a single chaotic step
- Missing prerequisites causing cascading task failures midway through execution
- Inability to parallelize independent sub-tasks effectively
