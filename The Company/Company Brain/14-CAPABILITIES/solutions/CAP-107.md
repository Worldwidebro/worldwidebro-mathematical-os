> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Implement modern CSS grid, flexbox, and container queries ensuring flawless multi-screen rendering.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-107` typically encounter:
- Layout overflow bugs and clipped content on mobile viewports
- Broken navigation drawers and modals on tablet devices
- Excessive mobile bounce rates due to clunky unresponsive UI

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **CSS Container Queries**
- **PostCSS**
- **Modern Web Guidance**
- **Autoprefixer**
