# CAP-118: Micro-Frontend Architecture & Module Federation

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Split monolithic frontend applications into independently deployable micro-apps unified at runtime.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-118` typically encounter:
- Massive frontend deployment queues with multiple teams blocking release trains
- Dependency version conflicts when teams require different library versions
- Sluggish development server reload times on million-line frontend codebases
