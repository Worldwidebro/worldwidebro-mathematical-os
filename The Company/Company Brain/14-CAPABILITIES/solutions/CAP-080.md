# CAP-080: Context Window Management & Token Compression

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Condense lengthy documents and interaction histories into compact, information-dense context budgets.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-080` typically encounter:
- Context window overflow errors crashing multi-turn agent sessions
- Degraded reasoning quality ('lost in the middle') when contexts exceed 100K tokens
- Astronomical token expenditure on redundant context re-submission

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **RTK (Rerank-Trim-Keywords)**
- **Caveman**
- **LLMLingua**
- **Summarization Cascades**
- **KV Cache Pruning**
