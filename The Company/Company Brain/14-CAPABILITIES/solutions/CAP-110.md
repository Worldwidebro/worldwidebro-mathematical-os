# CAP-110: Real-Time WebSocket & Push Protocols

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Deploy bidirectional, low-latency WebSocket and Server-Sent Events (SSE) servers for live collaboration.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-110` typically encounter:
- Clunky HTTP polling exhausting server connections and battery life
- Delayed chat messages and multiplayer interaction updates
- Broken reconnection logic during mobile cellular handoffs

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Socket.io**
- **Gorilla WebSocket**
- **Centrifugo**
- **Pusher**
- **Server-Sent Events (SSE)**

---
