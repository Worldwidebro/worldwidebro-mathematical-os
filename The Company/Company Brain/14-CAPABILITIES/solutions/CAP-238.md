# CAP-238: Yard Management Systems (YMS) & Gate Automation

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-017-logistics-transportation|SEC-017-logistics-transportation: Logistics & Transportation]]

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate trailer parking spot assignments, dock door scheduling, and automated gate camera check-ins.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-238` typically encounter:
- Truck drivers wandering logistics yards searching for specific drop trailers
- Congested yard gates backing trucks up onto municipal public roads
- Detention fees paid to trucking companies while drivers wait hours for an open dock door

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **YardView**
- **Kaleris YMS**
- **Odoo Gate**
- **Plate Recognizer (ALPR)**
- **DockMaster API**
