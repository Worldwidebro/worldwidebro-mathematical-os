## 1. Overview & Scope
Settle international payments in seconds using fiat-backed stablecoins and local off-ramps.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-205` typically encounter:
- Losing 3-5% in banking wire fees and foreign exchange markups on overseas payroll
- International payments getting held in correspondent banking queues for days
- Difficulties paying remote international contractors in volatile local currencies
