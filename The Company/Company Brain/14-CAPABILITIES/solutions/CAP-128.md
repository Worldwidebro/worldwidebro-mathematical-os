# CAP-128: Automated Semantic Versioning & Changelogs

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Derive semantic version increments and generate categorized changelogs from conventional commits.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-128` typically encounter:
- Confusion over breaking API changes due to inconsistent manual version numbering
- Uninformative 'bug fixes and improvements' changelogs alienating enterprise users
- Human errors during production package publishing workflows

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Changesets**
- **Release Please (Google)**
- **Git-Cliff**
