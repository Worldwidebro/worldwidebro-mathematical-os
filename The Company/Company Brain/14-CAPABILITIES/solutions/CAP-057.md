## 1. Overview & Scope
Sync analyzed records, customer scores, and predictive metrics back into operational SaaS tools.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-057` typically encounter:
- Valuable business intelligence trapped in data warehouses without operational impact
- Fragile custom cron scripts syncing data to CRM tools
- Delayed sales lead scoring caused by manual CSV exports
