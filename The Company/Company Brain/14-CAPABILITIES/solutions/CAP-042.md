# CAP-042: Automated Knowledge Extraction from Unstructured Data

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Extract structured entities, relationships, and key facts from PDFs, emails, and documentation.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-042` typically encounter:
- Valuable organizational intelligence trapped in static PDFs and docs
- Manual data entry bottlenecks and high transcription error rates
- Loss of document layout context during text extraction

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Unstructured.io**
- **Docling**
- **Marker**
- **PaddleOCR**
- **LangChain Document Loaders**
