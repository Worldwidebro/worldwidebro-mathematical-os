> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Ingest and normalize real-time property listings, historical tax data, and comps via RESO Web API.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-248` typically encounter:
- Stale real estate listings showing homes for sale that were already sold days ago
- Inconsistent field naming across hundreds of regional MLS boards
- Inability to syndicate property listings to portals compliant with MLS display rules
