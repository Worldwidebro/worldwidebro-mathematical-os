## 1. Overview & Scope
Estimate residential and commercial real estate market values using predictive regression on spatial comps.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-249` typically encounter:
- Inaccurate home valuations causing investors to overpay for real estate assets
- Slow manual real estate appraisal processes delaying mortgage approvals for weeks
- Inability to adjust valuation models for hyper-local neighborhood price premiums
