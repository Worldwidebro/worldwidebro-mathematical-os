# CAP-266: Real-Time Ad Bidding & DSP/SSP Advertising Engines

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-019-marketing-advertising|SEC-019-marketing-advertising: Marketing & Advertising]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Evaluate impression bids within 50 milliseconds in OpenRTB ad exchange auctions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-266` typically encounter:
- Ad network timeouts dropping bid opportunities and forfeiting millions in ad revenue
- Overpaying for low-converting ad inventory due to lack of real-time valuation
- Displaying scam ads that damage publisher brand credibility

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Prebid.js**
- **OpenRTB (IAB)**
- **Apache Flink DSP**
- **Google Ad Manager API**
- **Magnite SDK**
