# CAP-197: Digital Wallet & Contactless Token Integration

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-030-fintech-payments|SEC-030-fintech-payments: Fintech & Payments]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 6: FinTech, Commerce & Monetization

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Integrate Apple Pay, Google Wallet, and peer-to-peer tokenized pass engines into mobile apps.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-197` typically encounter:
- High checkout abandonment on mobile devices when users must type 16-digit card numbers
- Inability to issue digital loyalty cards, boarding passes, and event tickets
- Security risks associated with storing credit cards on client devices

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Apple Pay Web / PassKit**
- **Google Wallet API**
- **Stripe Apple Pay**
- **PassNinja**
- **Contactless SDK**
