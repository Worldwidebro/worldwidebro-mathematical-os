# CAP-012: Database Administration & Storage Ops

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate database provisioning, point-in-time recovery, replication, vacuuming, and health monitoring.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-012` typically encounter:
- Catastrophic data loss during hardware failures without verified backup restores
- Degraded query performance from index bloat and un-analyzed tables
- Unplanned replication lag leading to stale read replicas

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **pgBackRest**
- **Patroni**
- **Barman**
- **Percona Toolkit**
- **pgAdmin**
