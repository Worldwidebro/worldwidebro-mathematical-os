## 1. Overview & Scope
Handle sensitive credit card numbers exclusively through tokenized iframe fields to bypass PCI scope.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-187` typically encounter:
- Massive annual compliance costs and security liabilities of storing raw PAN card numbers
- Severe catastrophic liability in the event of database exfiltration
- Inability to accept card payments legally without Level 1 certification
