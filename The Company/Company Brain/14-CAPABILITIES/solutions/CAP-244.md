# CAP-244: HIPAA-Compliant Telemedicine & Encrypted Video

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology|SEC-012-healthcare-biotechnology: Healthcare & Biotechnology]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 8: Sector Verticals & Specialized Domains

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Deliver end-to-end encrypted, low-latency video consultations with integrated patient charting.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-244` typically encounter:
- Security breaches exposing sensitive doctor-patient therapy sessions to eavesdropping
- Telehealth app crashes and lag frustrating elderly or non-technical patients
- Inability to sign required HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with generic video providers

---

- **Daily.co Telehealth**
- **LiveKit HIPAA**
- **Twilio Video Health**

- **Agora Health**
