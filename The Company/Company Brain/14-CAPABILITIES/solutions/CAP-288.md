## 1. Overview & Scope

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-288` typically encounter:
- Losing top executive talent to competitors due to below-market compensation packages
- Over-diluting company equity by granting uncalibrated stock option grants
- Shareholder lawsuits over unaligned executive golden parachute severance terms
