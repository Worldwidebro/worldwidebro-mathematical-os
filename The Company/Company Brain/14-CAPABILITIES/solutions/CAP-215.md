# CAP-215: Cold Chain Temperature & IoT Sensor Monitoring

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-017-logistics-transportation|SEC-017-logistics-transportation: Logistics & Transportation]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Continuously monitor and alert on refrigerated cargo temperature and humidity during transit.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-215` typically encounter:
- Spoilage of temperature-sensitive vaccines and fresh food during transport
- Inability to prove regulatory FDA compliance for perishable goods
- Costly insurance disputes over where in transit cargo spoiled

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **ThingsBoard**
- **Node-RED**
- **Sensirion IoT**
- **AWS IoT Core**
- **BLE Beacon Loggers**
