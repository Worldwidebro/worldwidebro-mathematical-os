# CAP-237: Automated Cross-Docking Coordination

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-017-logistics-transportation|SEC-017-logistics-transportation: Logistics & Transportation]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Transfer arriving incoming freight directly to outbound transport vehicles with zero warehouse dwell time.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-237` typically encounter:
- Warehouse floor congestion caused by staging freight that is leaving the same afternoon
- Multiple handling cycles increasing worker labor costs and cargo damage risks
- Missed same-day distribution delivery schedules

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **CrossDock Manager**
- **Odoo Cross-Dock**
- **JDA Logistics**
- **HighJump WMS**
- **Dock Scheduling API**
