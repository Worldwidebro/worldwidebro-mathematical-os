# CAP-050: Change Data Capture (CDC) Architecture

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Capture low-level database write-ahead log (WAL) mutations and stream them as event notifications.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-050` typically encounter:
- High polling overhead stressing production transactional databases
- Out-of-sync read replicas and secondary search indices
- Lost event state during dual-write architectural bugs

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Debezium**
- **Kafka Connect**
- **PostgreSQL Logical Replication**
- **Benthos**
- **Estuary**
