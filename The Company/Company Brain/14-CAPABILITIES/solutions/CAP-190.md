## 1. Overview & Scope
Adjust prices dynamically based on real-time demand, competitor pricing, inventory levels, and churn risk.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-190` typically encounter:
- Leaving substantial revenue on the table during periods of peak customer demand
- Accumulating excess perishable or seasonal inventory due to rigid pricing
- Losing price-sensitive customers to agile dynamic-pricing competitors
