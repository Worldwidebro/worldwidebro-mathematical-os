# CAP-189: Marketplace Escrow & Milestone Hold Engine

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-029-marketplace-platform|SEC-029-marketplace-platform: Marketplace & Platform]]

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Hold funds securely in escrow until buyer milestone approvals or physical shipment confirmations.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-189` typically encounter:
- Buyer fraud claiming non-delivery while merchant already shipped valuable goods
- Merchant insolvency before fulfilling high-value custom client orders
- Legal issues operating unauthorized holding escrow services
