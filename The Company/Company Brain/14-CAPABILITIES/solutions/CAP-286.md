## 1. Overview & Scope
Maintain an interconnected, self-healing corporate knowledge graph with bidirectional wikilinks.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-286` typically encounter:
- Critical institutional knowledge walking out the door when senior engineers quit
- Teams wasting hours solving problems that were already solved in another division
- Obsolete documentation giving misleading instructions to new hires and AI agents

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Obsidian**
- **Company Brain Graphify**
- **Notion**
- **GitBook**
- **Docusaurus**
