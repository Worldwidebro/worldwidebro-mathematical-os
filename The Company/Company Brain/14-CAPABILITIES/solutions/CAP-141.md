# CAP-141: Enterprise Identity & Access Management (IAM)

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy|SEC-033-cybersecurity-privacy: Cybersecurity & Privacy]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 5: Cybersecurity & Zero Trust Architecture

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Centralize user directories, single sign-on (SSO), and role-based directory permissions across enterprise services.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-141` typically encounter:
- Employees retaining access to internal tools after offboarding
- Fragmented credentials across dozens of unmanaged SaaS tools
- Failure in enterprise customer security evaluations requiring SAML/SCIM

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Okta**
- **Keycloak**
- **Google Workspace IAM**
- **Authentik**
- **Ory Kratos**
