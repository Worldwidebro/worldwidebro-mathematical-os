# CAP-246: Genomic Data Sequencing & Variant Analysis

## 1. Overview & Scope
Process raw DNA/RNA sequencing data (FASTQ, BAM, VCF) to detect genetic disease variants and biomarkers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-246` typically encounter:
- Compute bottlenecks running massive bioinformatic pipelines on single servers
- Inability to match identified genetic variants to published biomedical literature
- Fragmented clinical genetics reports preventing personalized oncology therapies

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **GATK (Broad Institute)**
- **Nextflow**
- **Biopython**
- **Ensembl API**
- **ClinVar API**
