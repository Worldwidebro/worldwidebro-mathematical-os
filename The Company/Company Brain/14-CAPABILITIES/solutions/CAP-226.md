## 1. Overview & Scope
Track physical enterprise hardware, laptops, and machinery from procurement to retirement.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-226` typically encounter:
- Lost or stolen corporate laptops remaining un-accounted for on corporate asset books
- Inaccurate corporate asset valuation and tax deduction calculations
- Equipment operating past its economic life incurring massive maintenance overhead
