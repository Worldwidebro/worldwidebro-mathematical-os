# CAP-183: Double-Entry Bookkeeping & Financial Ledgers

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-008-financial-services|SEC-008-financial-services: Financial Services]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 6: FinTech, Commerce & Monetization

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Maintain immutable, balanced debit-and-credit ledgers for all monetary transactions and internal accounts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-183` typically encounter:
- Unbalanced financial books making accurate auditing impossible
- Inability to reconstruct historical account balances at specific points in time
- Financial discrepancies between application balances and external bank statements

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Beancount**
- **Modern Treasury Ledgers**
