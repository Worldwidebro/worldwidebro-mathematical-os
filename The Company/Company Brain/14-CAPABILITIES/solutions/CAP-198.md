## 1. Overview & Scope
Track referral links, coupon attributions, multi-tier partner commissions, and payout schedules.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-198` typically encounter:
- Inaccurate referral attribution rewarding fraudulent or self-referred clicks
- High administrative overhead calculating and disbursing monthly affiliate commissions
- Unhappy marketing partners due to lack of real-time conversion dashboards
