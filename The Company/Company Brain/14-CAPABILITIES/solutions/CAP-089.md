## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate standardized regression benchmarking across complex reasoning and tool-calling trajectories.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-089` typically encounter:
- No quantifiable metric to determine if prompt tweaks improved or degraded agent performance
- Silent regressions in tool-calling reliability across model version updates
- Inability to select the most cost-effective model for a specific task
