## 1. Overview & Scope
Implement durable background task queuing and point-to-point asynchronous messaging systems.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-007` typically encounter:
- HTTP request timeouts during heavy computational jobs
- Lost jobs upon service crash or deployment restarts
- Uncontrolled consumer failure cascades without dead-letter queues

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **RabbitMQ**
- **Celery**
- **BullMQ**
- **Redis Streams**
- **AWS SQS**
