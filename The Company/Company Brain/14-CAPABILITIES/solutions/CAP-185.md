## 1. Overview & Scope
Evaluate borrower creditworthiness, cash flow health, and default probabilities using automated models.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-185` typically encounter:
- High default rates on merchant cash advances or deferred customer loans
- Manual underwriting processes taking days to approve simple business lines of credit
- Algorithmic bias violating fair lending regulations
