# CAP-289: Meeting Intelligence & Action Item Extraction

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Transcribe executive and client calls, extract decisions, and sync action items to project issue trackers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-289` typically encounter:
- Decisions made during executive meetings forgotten or lost with no written record
- Hours wasted hand-typing meeting minutes and emailing summaries
- Action items slipping through the cracks without assigned owners or deadlines
