## 1. Overview & Scope
Map physical building architectural geometry, HVAC telemetry, and energy consumption into 3D digital twins.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-250` typically encounter:
- Excessive commercial building energy costs from heating empty office wings
- Inability to visualize equipment failure locations within complex skyscrapers
- Reactive facility management fixing broken elevators only after tenant complaints

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling

- **Autodesk Tandem**
- **Azure Digital Twins**
- **BIMserver**
- **Matterport API**
- **Cesium 3D**
