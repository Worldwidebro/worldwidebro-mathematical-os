## 1. Overview & Scope
Accelerate LLM token generation using lightweight draft models paired with large verifier models.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-085` typically encounter:
- Sluggish 15 tok/sec generation speeds creating user-facing lag
- Under-utilization of GPU memory bandwidth during auto-regressive generation
- High cost per generated token on large reasoning models
