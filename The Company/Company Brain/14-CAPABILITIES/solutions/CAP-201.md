## 1. Overview & Scope
Model multi-round dilution, stock option pools, SAFE notes, and liquidation waterfall scenarios.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-201` typically encounter:
- Founders losing company control due to unanticipated financing round dilution
- Mismatched shareholder records causing disputes during acquisition exits
- Errors calculating option vesting and exercise tax liabilities
