# CAP-182: Merchant Settlement & Split Payouts

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Route customer payments and split disbursements automatically to multi-party marketplace sellers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-182` typically encounter:
- Manual spreadsheet calculations to disburse weekly vendor earnings
- Legal liabilities holding customer funds without required money transmitter licenses
- Errors in calculating marketplace platform commission cuts
