> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)

## 1. Overview & Scope
Fuse lexical BM25 scores with dense vector similarities via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-064` typically encounter:
- Pure vector search failing on exact product SKUs, code identifiers, or acronyms
- Pure lexical search failing on abstract conceptual queries
- Suboptimal ranking when combining text and visual metadata
