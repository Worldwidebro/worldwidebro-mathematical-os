## 1. Overview & Scope
Execute trustless on-chain milestone escrow contracts with multi-sig and oracle resolution.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-204` typically encounter:
- Counterparty default risk in cross-border trade without access to international courts
- High banking fees on traditional international letters of credit
- Slow multi-day bank settlement times freezing operational liquidity
