# CAP-020: Financial Statement Automation & GAAP Engine

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-008-financial-services|SEC-008-financial-services: Financial Services]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 1: Core Architecture & Platform Engineering

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate multi-entity balance sheets, P&L generation, and cash flow reconciliation compliant with GAAP/IFRS.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-020` typically encounter:
- Delayed month-end close preventing timely executive decision making
- Manual spreadsheet calculation errors skewing burn rate forecasts
- Discrepancies between payment gateway logs and accounting ledgers

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Ledger CLI**
- **Beancount**
- **Modern Treasury**
- **QuickBooks API**
- **Xero API**

---

## 4. Implementation Path
1. **Assess**: Evaluate current venture state, data contracts, and technical debt against `CAP-020` requirements.
2. **Select**: Choose appropriate tooling and integration patterns from the verified capability stack.
3. **Implement**: Deploy runtime primitives, wire configuration, and establish operational boundaries.
4. **Test**: Execute automated test harness, mutation checks, and security validation.
5. **Deploy**: Release to staging and production through standard CI/CD deployment pipelines.
6. **Observe**: Monitor telemetry, latency, error budgets, and venture business outcome metrics.
