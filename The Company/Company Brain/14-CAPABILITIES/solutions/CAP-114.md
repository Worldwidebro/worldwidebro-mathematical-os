# CAP-114: Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline-First

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Deliver installable, offline-capable web apps utilizing Service Workers and Cache API.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-114` typically encounter:
- Web apps becoming completely useless during intermittent subway or airplane connectivity
- Loss of unsubmitted user work when connections drop
- Inability to engage users via native desktop and mobile push notifications

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Workbox**
- **IndexedDB / Dexie.js**
- **Web Push API**
- **PWA Builder**
- **ServiceWorker API**
