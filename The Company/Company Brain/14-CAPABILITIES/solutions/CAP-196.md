# CAP-196: Point of Sale (POS) Hardware & Reader Integration

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-021-retail-e-commerce|SEC-021-retail-e-commerce: Retail & E-commerce]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 6: FinTech, Commerce & Monetization

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Connect physical card readers, barcode scanners, and receipt printers to web and mobile apps.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-196` typically encounter:
- Inability to unify in-person retail sales with online e-commerce inventories
- Clunky manual payment entry on standalone card terminals causing clerk mistakes
- Hardware connectivity drops over Bluetooth and USB losing sales

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Stripe Terminal**
- **Square Reader SDK**
- **Adyen POS**
- **Zebra Scanner SDK**
- **ESC/POS printer drivers**
