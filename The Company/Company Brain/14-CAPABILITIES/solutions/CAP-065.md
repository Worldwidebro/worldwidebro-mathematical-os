# CAP-065: Semantic Cache & Embedding Query Deduplication

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml|SEC-032-artificial-intelligence-ml: Artificial Intelligence & ML]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Cache identical or semantically equivalent LLM queries using vector distance thresholds.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-065` typically encounter:
- Paying repeated inference costs for recurring user questions
- High latency on popular inquiries that have already been computed
- Unnecessary GPU load during spike traffic events

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **GPTCache**
- **Redis LangChain Cache**
- **Semantic Router**
- **Momento**
- **LiteLLM Cache**
