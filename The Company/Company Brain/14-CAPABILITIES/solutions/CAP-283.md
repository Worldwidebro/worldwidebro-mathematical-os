## 1. Overview & Scope
Author, distribute, and enforce employee acknowledgments of security, ethics, and remote work policies.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-283` typically encounter:
- Employees claiming ignorance of corporate acceptable-use policies during security breaches
- Inability to prove to SOC 2 auditors that 100% of staff signed code-of-conduct agreements
- Outdated corporate policies contradicting modern labor laws
