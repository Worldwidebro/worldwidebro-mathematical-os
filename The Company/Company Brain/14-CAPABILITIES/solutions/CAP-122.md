# CAP-122: Code Coverage Auditing & Test Quality Gates

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 4: Software Engineering & Delivery

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Track line, branch, and condition test coverage with PR blocking rules on regressions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-122` typically encounter:
- Gradual decay of test coverage across quarters as deadlines rush features
- Untested code merged into master branches without review awareness
- Lack of visibility into which areas of legacy code are dangerous to refactor

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Codecov**
- **Coveralls**
- **Istanbul / NYC**
- **pytest-cov**
- **Tarpaulin (Rust)**
