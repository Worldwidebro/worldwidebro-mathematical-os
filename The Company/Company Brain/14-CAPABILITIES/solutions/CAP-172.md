## 1. Overview & Scope
Express and enforce corporate security policies across Kubernetes, Terraform, and APIs declaratively.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-172` typically encounter:
- Engineers deploying unapproved open security groups or non-compliant containers
- Manual security reviews slowing down delivery pipelines
- Inconsistent policy enforcement across cloud, on-prem, and container stacks
