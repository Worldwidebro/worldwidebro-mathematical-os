# CAP-219: Automated Procurement & Purchase Order Workflows

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering|SEC-018-manufacturing-engineering: Manufacturing & Engineering]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 7: Operations, Supply Chain & Logistics

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Automate reorder point triggering, purchase order generation, and approval routing.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-219` typically encounter:
- Production lines halted because critical raw materials were not reordered in time
- Unauthorized rogue spending outside corporate purchasing channels
- Hours wasted hand-typing purchase orders from inventory sheets

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **Coupa**
- **Precoro**
- **Procurify API**
- **Odoo Purchase**
- **Zapier / n8n B2B**
