# CAP-055: Time Series Database & Metric Aggregation

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure|SEC-026-utilities-infrastructure: Utilities & Infrastructure]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 2: Data Engineering & Knowledge Systems

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Ingest and compress timestamped metrics, sensor readings, and tick data at high ingestion rates.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-055` typically encounter:
- Relational database indexing collapse under high-frequency metric inserts
- Inability to downsample or roll up historical metrics efficiently
- Slow sliding-window aggregations across time intervals

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **TimescaleDB**
- **VictoriaMetrics**
- **InfluxDB**
- **QuestDB**
- **Prometheus TSDB**
