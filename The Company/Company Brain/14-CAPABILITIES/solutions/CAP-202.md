## 1. Overview & Scope
Facilitate compliant transfers, secondary tender offers, and liquidity programs for private equity.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-202` typically encounter:
- Key early employees locked into illiquid paper wealth unable to buy homes or pay taxes
- Uncontrolled private share transfers to hostile third-party buyers
- Complex legal paperwork and transfer approvals delaying secondary transactions
