# CAP-010: Microservices & Service Decomposition

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-024-technology-software|SEC-024-technology-software: Technology & Software]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 1: Core Architecture & Platform Engineering

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Architect decoupled microservices and event-driven domains bounded by explicit domain contracts.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-010` typically encounter:
- Monolithic deployment bottlenecks preventing independent domain updates
- Tight coupling leading to cascading runtime failures
- Bloated development teams conflicting on shared monorepos

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **gRPC**
- **Dapr**
- **Kong Gateway**
- **Traefik**
- **Envoy**

## 4. Implementation Path
1. **Assess**: Evaluate current venture state, data contracts, and technical debt against `CAP-010` requirements.
2. **Select**: Choose appropriate tooling and integration patterns from the verified capability stack.

4. **Test**: Execute automated test harness, mutation checks, and security validation.
5. **Deploy**: Release to staging and production through standard CI/CD deployment pipelines.
