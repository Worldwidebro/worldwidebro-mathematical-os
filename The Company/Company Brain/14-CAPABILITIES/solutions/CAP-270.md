## 1. Overview & Scope
Index, tag, version, and search gigabytes of production media assets, logos, and raw video footage.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-270` typically encounter:
- Creative teams wasting hours searching through disorganized Google Drive folders for marketing assets
- Using outdated or unapproved logos in high-stakes public marketing collateral
- Accidental deletion of master video project files
