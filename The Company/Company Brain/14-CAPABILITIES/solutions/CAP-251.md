# CAP-251: 3D Architectural CAD & BIM Modeling Engines

> **Authority:** System Architecture & Capability Control Plane (CP-027)  
> **Sector:** [[SECTORS/SEC-002-construction-infrastructure|SEC-002-construction-infrastructure: Construction & Infrastructure]]  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Category:** Tier 8: Sector Verticals & Specialized Domains

---

## 1. Overview & Scope
Process and render Building Information Modeling (BIM) files (IFC, Revit) inside interactive web browsers.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Ventures operating without `CAP-251` typically encounter:
- Contractors working from out-of-date 2D paper blueprints causing expensive rework
- Inability for non-technical clients to visualize architectural designs before construction
- Clashes between plumbing, electrical, and structural beams discovered late on job sites

---

## 3. Solution Space & Reference Tooling
Recommended tooling from internal platforms and the 904 Starred External Capability Universe:
- **three.js**
- **IFC.js (That Open Engine)**
- **Autodesk Platform Services (Forge)**
- **BIMcollab**
- **Revit API**
