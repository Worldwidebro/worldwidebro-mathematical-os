### Post 5: Tip/Insight (Week 3, Tue)
**Title:** "How to Spot a Bad Staffing Agency (3-Minute Checklist)"

Not all staffing agencies are equal. Here's how to spot the ones that waste your time:

1. **Do they screen candidates before sending you?** (Bad agencies just send everyone)
2. **Can they show retention data?** (Bad agencies = high turnover, they profit from churn)
3. **Do they understand your specific role?** (Bad agencies: one-size-fits-all generic candidates)
4. **What's their markup?** (Anything over 35% is inflated; under 25% suggests they're cutting corners)
5. **Can they fill a role in 2-3 weeks?** (If not, they don't have a real pipeline)
