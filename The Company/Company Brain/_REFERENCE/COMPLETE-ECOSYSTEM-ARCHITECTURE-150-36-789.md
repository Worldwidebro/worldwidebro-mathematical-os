## LAYER 1: LEGAL/FINANCIAL STRUCTURE (150 Entities)

### The Family Office Foundation

```
PRINCIPAL (Individual / Family)
    ↓
FAMILY TRUST
    └─ Holds 100% ownership
    └─ Directs capital allocation
    └─ Appoints trustees/board
    ↓
TRUST ENTITIES (5-10 specialized trusts)
    ├─ REVOCABLE TRUST (flexible, adjustable)
    ├─ IRREVOCABLE TRUST (tax protection)
    ├─ QUALIFIED PERSONAL RESIDENCE TRUST (real estate)
    ├─ GRANTOR RETAINED ANNUITY TRUST (wealth transfer)
    └─ Asset Protection Trusts (state-specific)
    ↓
HOLDING COMPANY STRUCTURE
    ├─ ASSET LLC (IP ownership)
    │   └─ Owns: patents, trademarks, software, brands
    │
    ├─ REAL ESTATE LLC (property)
    │   └─ Owns: warehouses, offices, land, development rights
    │
    └─ ADMIN LLC (operations)
        └─ Owns: operational infrastructure, services
    ↓
OPERATING C-CORPORATION (Tax efficient)
    └─ Holds all 36 OpCos
    └─ Single tax filer (check-the-box)
    └─ Eliminates double taxation
    ↓
36 OPERATING COMPANIES (OpCos)
    ├─ OpCo-001 (Agriculture/Forestry)
    ├─ OpCo-002 (Construction)
    ├─ ...
    ├─ OpCo-035 (Platforms)
    └─ OpCo-036 (Other/Emerging)
    ↓
~150 TOTAL LEGAL ENTITIES
    └─ 1 Family Trust
    └─ 5-10 Specialized Trusts
    └─ 3 Holding Company LLCs (Asset, Real Estate, Admin)
    └─ 1 Operating C-Corp
    └─ 36 Operating Companies (OpCos)
    └─ ~95-100 Other entities (JVs, SPVs, foreign entities, subsidiaries)
```

|------|-------|------|---------|---|

| **Trust Layer** | 1-10 | Trusts | Wealth protection, dynasty, succession | Pass-through |
| **Holding Layer** | 3 | LLCs | Asset separation, IP/RE/Ops | Check-the-box |

```

  ↓ (invests capital)

  ↓ (holds all)

Revenue
  ↓ (flows through)
OpCo → C-Corp → Trust → Distributions to Principal
```
