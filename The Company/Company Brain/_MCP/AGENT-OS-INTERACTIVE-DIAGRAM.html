Decision Type Agent Can Decide? Escalates To Reason
Prospect priority (cold calls) ✅ YES (Revenue Agent) — Operational detail, no financial impact
Email content ✅ YES (Sales Agent) — Marketing decision, brand aligned
Pricing discount < $500 ✅ YES (Revenue Agent) — Small discount, revenue impact acceptable
Pricing discount > $5K ❌ NO CFO Approval Material financial impact, must verify budget
Hiring decision ❌ NO CEO + HR Agent Strategic headcount, culture fit decision
New product line ❌ NO CEO + Board Strategic venture decision, capital allocation
Venture capital raise ❌ NO CEO + CFO + Board Dilution, cap table, strategic ownership
Partnership agreement ❌ NO CEO + Legal Long-term commitment, legal obligations

class AgentOrchestrator:
  def __init__(self, neo4j_uri, qdrant_url, registry_path):
    self.neo4j = GraphDatabase.driver(neo4j_uri)
    self.qdrant = qdrant_client.QdrantClient(qdrant_url)
  
  def run(self, objective):
    tasks = self.plan(objective) # Break into tasks
    agents = self.select_agents(tasks) # Query Neo4j
    results = await self.execute_parallel(agents, tasks)
    evaluation = self.evaluate(results)
    self.record_execution(Neo4j) # Store outcome
