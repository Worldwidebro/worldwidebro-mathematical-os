### Post 5: Tip/Insight
**Title:** "The 3 Metrics That Actually Matter in Fleet Routing"

Not all routing software measures the same things. Here's what actually matters:

1. **Cost per mile** (fuel + labor + vehicle wear)
2. **On-time delivery rate** (customer satisfaction + retention)
3. **Driver retention** (lower turnover = lower recruiting costs)

Bad routing software optimizes for #1 only (fuel). Modern routing optimizes for all three simultaneously.

**The gap:** Focusing on fuel alone saves 5-10%. Optimizing all three saves 15-25% + improves retention.

### Post 6: Problem/Solution

Fleet ops worry about driver retention. They raise wages. Retention stays flat.

**Why?** Bad routing = longer hours = burnout = quit.

**What drivers actually want:**

**Solution:** Modern routing = 15-20% shorter average driver day = better work-life balance = retention improves without raising wages.
