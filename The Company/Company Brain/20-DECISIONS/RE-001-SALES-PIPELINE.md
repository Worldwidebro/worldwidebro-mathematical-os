# RE-001 Sales Pipeline — WorldwideBro Holdings Real Estate Syndication

**Venture:** RE-001 (WorldwideBro Holdings) — Real Estate Syndication & Investment  
**Product:** Commercial real estate syndication deals with investor participation ($5K-25K+ per investor)  
**Revenue Model:** Syndication fees (typically 2-5% of raised capital + performance fees)  
**Pipeline Velocity:** 60-90 days (prospect to term sheet), 90-120 days (term sheet to capital deployed)  
**Priority:** 🔴 STRATEGIC (highest deal value, longer cycle, premium customers, recurring fee potential)

---

## PIPELINE STAGES

### Stage 1: PROSPECT
**Definition:** Accredited investor contact identified  
**Action:** Outbound to accredited investor networks, angel syndicates, family offices  
**Qualification:**
- Accredited investor ($200K+ income or $1M+ net worth)
- Prior real estate or syndication experience (easier conversation)
- Active investor mindset (not passive wealth)
- Portfolio in commercial/multifamily

**Move to Next Stage:** Call made, prospect engaged

**Win Rate to Stage 2:** 80% (8 of 10 calls connect)

---

### Stage 2: CALL CONNECTED
**Definition:** Investor answered, heard deal flow value prop, interested  
**Value Prop:** "Professional real estate syndications with 8%+ annual returns and sponsor track record"  
**Win Rate to Stage 3:** 15% (1-2 of 8 connected interested in receiving deal flow)

**Move to Next Stage:** "Yes, send me deal memos" or "I want to see opportunities"

---

### Stage 3: PITCH MATERIALS SENT
**Definition:** Investor receives deal package (summary, sponsor background, track record)  
**Action:**
- Send one-pager on deal (property, market, financial projections)
- Sponsor track record + bios
- Sample investor agreement (non-binding)
- Schedule follow-up call for "questions"

**Win Rate to Stage 4:** 50% (1 of 2 investors interested in full underwriting)

**Move to Next Stage:** Investor wants full business plan + offering doc

---

### Stage 4: KYC & ACCREDITATION APPROVED
**Definition:** Investor accreditation verified, documentation collected  
**Action:**
- Request tax return copy (verification of accreditation)
- Collect W-9 (tax ID)
- Banking info for wire transfer
- Investor accreditation letter (if available)

**Win Rate to Stage 5:** 90% (most accredited investors complete KYC)

**Move to Next Stage:** Investor approved for investment

---

### Stage 5: TERM SHEET SIGNED
**Definition:** Investor agrees to invest in specific deal, terms agreed  
**Revenue Recognition:** Syndication fee calculated on committed capital  
**Fee Structure Examples:**
- $500K investment × 2% fee = $10K
- $1M investment × 2.5% fee = $25K
- Plus performance fee on exits (typically 20% of profits over hurdle)

**Action:**
- Finalize investment amount
- Sign investor agreement
- Collect signed accreditation documentation
- Confirm banking details for wire

**Win Rate to Stage 6:** 95% (after KYC + term sheet, capital typically deploys)

**Move to Next Stage:** Capital deployed to deal

---

### Stage 6: CAPITAL DEPLOYED
**Definition:** Investor wire received, capital deployed to deal  
**Revenue:** Syndication fee + ongoing asset management fee (1% annually)  
**LTV:** $10K-25K upfront + $5K-10K annual ongoing (multiple years)

## CONVERSION MATH

```
10 calls → 8 connected → 1-2 interested → 1 pitch sent → 0.5 KYC → 0.5 term sheet → 0.5 deployed
= 1 new investor per 20-40 calls = $5K-25K per close

Average: $10K syndication fee per investor
```

### Revenue Timeline
- **Months 1-3:** Deal evaluation + investor education (no revenue)
- **Months 3-4:** KYC + term sheet (fees recognized when term sheet signed)
- **Months 4+:** Asset management fees ($5K-10K annually per investor)

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### MUST HAVE:
- ✅ Accredited investor (verified income/net worth)
- ✅ Prior investment experience (simplifies education)
- ✅ Capital available for investment ($100K-1M+)
- ✅ Real estate investor interest (not stocks-only)

### STRONG INTEREST SIGNALS:
- "Yes, I invest in commercial real estate"
- "Send me deal flow, I review all syndications"
- "What's your sponsor track record?" (due diligence question = serious buyer)
- Prior syndication experience
- Angel/VC background

### DEAL KILLERS:
- Not accredited
- "I'll think about it" (low conviction)
- Only interested in T-Bills (risk-averse)
- No real estate experience (education burden)
- "I'm only looking at single-family" (asset class mismatch)
