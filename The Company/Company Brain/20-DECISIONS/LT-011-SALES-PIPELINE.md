# LT-011 Sales Pipeline — CarrierDispatch TMS


**Venture:** LT-011 (CarrierDispatch) — Transportation Management System (SaaS)  
**Product:** Route optimization + fleet TMS ($500-2K per month per fleet)  
**Revenue per Deal:** $500-2K MRR (recurring SaaS)  
**Pipeline Velocity:** 14-30 days (prospect to pilot), 30-60 days (pilot to contract)  
**Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM (high LTV SaaS, longer cycle, strategic customers)  
**SPECIAL:** Carolina Logistics likely evaluating TMS vendors → fast-track opportunity (14 days to contract)

---

## PIPELINE STAGES

### Stage 1: PROSPECT
**Definition:** Freight ops/fleet manager contact identified  
**Action:** Cold call to logistics companies, freight carriers, 3PLs, fleet managers  
**Qualification:**
- 10+ trucks/fleet (minimum fleet size for ROI)
- Current TMS pain points (manual routing, inefficient dispatch, no real-time tracking)
- Budget for software ($500-2K/month)

**Move to Next Stage:** Call made, prospect listened

**Win Rate to Stage 2:** 80% (8 of 10 calls connect)

---

### Stage 2: CALL CONNECTED
**Definition:** Fleet manager answered, heard value prop, didn't hang up  
**Value Prop:** "Route optimization software that cuts fuel costs 15% and improves on-time delivery"  
**Win Rate to Stage 3:** 20% (1-2 of 8 connected interested in demo)

**Move to Next Stage:** "Tell me more" or "I want to see a demo"

---

### Stage 3: DEMO SCHEDULED
**Definition:** Demo video or call scheduled, baseline metrics collected  
**Action:**
- Collect baseline: current fleet size, avg routes/day, fuel cost/mo, on-time %
- Show 5-min product demo or video walkthrough
- Share case study (similar fleet type)
- Ask: "Does this solve your routing problem?"

**Win Rate to Stage 4:** 50% (1 of 2 interested → pilot approved)

**Move to Next Stage:** Prospect says "let's try it"

---

### Stage 4: PILOT (30 DAYS)
**Definition:** Free 1-month pilot, TMS wired to fleet operations  
**Action:**
- Integrate their vehicle data + routes (API setup)
- Run optimization algo for 1-2 weeks
- Measure: fuel savings, route efficiency, on-time improvements
- Weekly check-ins

**Win Rate to Stage 5:** 50% (1 of 2 pilots shows clear ROI)

**Move to Next Stage:** Pilot results justify subscription

---

### Stage 5: CONTRACT SIGNED
**Definition:** 12-month SaaS contract signed  
**Revenue:** $500-2K/month MRR (based on fleet size)  
**LTV:** $6K-24K (12-month contract, potential 2-3 year relationship)

**Renewal Rate:** Target 80% (customers often add more users/fleets)

---

```
10 calls → 8 connected → 1-2 interested → 1 demo → 0.5 pilots → 0.25 closes
= 1 new customer per 20-30 calls = $500-2K/month recurring
```

- Year 1: $6K-24K (depending on churn and upsells)

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### MUST HAVE:
- ✅ 10+ truck fleet (minimum for ROI)
- ✅ Current TMS pain points (inefficiency, no tracking, etc.)
- ✅ Budget allocated for software

### STRONG INTEREST SIGNALS:
- "Our current TMS sucks" (pain clear)
- "We're shopping for a replacement" (buying signal)
- "Can you show us a demo?" (engagement)
- Specific routing/fuel pain mentioned

### DEAL KILLERS:
- <10 trucks (too small)
- "Happy with current TMS" (no motivation)
- "No budget this year" (no deal)
- Manual routing only (not aware they need software)
