# LT-005 Sales Pipeline — HealthRoute Medical Courier

**Venture:** LT-005 (HealthRoute) — HIPAA-Compliant Medical Specimen Logistics  
**Product:** Recurring medical courier service ($2K-5K per month per client)  
**Revenue per Deal:** $2K-5K MRR (recurring)  
**Pipeline Velocity:** 14-21 days (prospect to contract signed)  
**Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM (recurring revenue, longer cycle, higher LTV)

---

## PIPELINE STAGES

### Stage 1: PROSPECT
**Definition:** Lab director/procurement contact identified  
**Action:** Cold call to medical labs, pathology centers, diagnostic facilities  
**Qualification:** 
- 50+ specimen pickups per week (volume requirement)
- Current courier has pain points (cost, reliability, compliance)
- HIPAA-aware (familiar with regulated logistics)

**Move to Next Stage:** Call made, prospect expressed interest

**Win Rate to Stage 2:** 80% (8 of 10 calls connect)

---

### Stage 2: CALL CONNECTED
**Definition:** Lab director answered, heard pitch, didn't hang up  
**Value Prop:** "HIPAA-compliant, same-day specimen pickup with real-time tracking"  
**Win Rate to Stage 3:** 20% (1-2 of 8 connected interested in discussing volume)

**Move to Next Stage:** "Tell me more" or "I want to discuss"

---

### Stage 3: NEEDS ANALYSIS
**Definition:** You asked about specimen volume, current courier, pain points  
**Action:** Discovery call (30 min) covering:
- Current monthly specimen volume
- Pickup frequency (daily, 2x/day, etc.)
- Geographic service area (radius)
- Current courier costs
- Pain points (delays, compliance issues, reliability)
- Who approves budget?

**Win Rate to Stage 4:** 50% (1 of 2 prospects wants formal proposal)

**Move to Next Stage:** Prospect says "send me a proposal"

---

### Stage 4: PROPOSAL SENT
**Definition:** Custom proposal sent based on volume + service area  
**Action:** 
- Calculate pricing based on pickup frequency + geography
- Include compliance details (HIPAA BAA, training, insurance)
- Offer 30-day pilot at 50% discount or free (reduces perceived risk)
- Include SLAs (guarantee 95% on-time pickup)

**Win Rate to Stage 5:** 30% (1 of 3-4 proposals → pilot)

**Move to Next Stage:** Prospect agrees to pilot

---

### Stage 5: PILOT (30 DAYS FREE or DISCOUNTED)
**Definition:** 30-day trial, prospect evaluates service quality  
**Action:**
- Assign dedicated account manager
- Daily check-ins first week
- Weekly check-ins weeks 2-4
- Measure: on-time performance, specimen tracking, compliance

**Win Rate to Stage 6:** 50% (1 of 2 pilots converts to contract)

**Move to Next Stage:** Pilot results justify contract

---

### Stage 6: CONTRACT SIGNED
**Definition:** 12-month service contract signed, recurring revenue starts  
**Revenue:** $2K-5K/month MRR (depending on volume)  
**LTV:** $24K-60K (2-5 year relationship)

---

## CONVERSION MATH

```
10 calls → 8 connected → 1-2 interested → 1 proposal → 0.3 pilots → 0.15 closes
= 1 new customer per 30-40 calls = $2K-5K/month recurring
```

### Revenue Timeline
- Month 1-3: Acquisition cost absorbed
- Month 4+: Recurring $2K-5K/month (cumulative)
- Year 1: $2K-5K/month × 8-12 months = $16K-60K depending on close timing

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### MUST HAVE:
- ✅ 50+ pickups/week minimum (volume to support service)
- ✅ HIPAA-aware (familiar with compliance requirements)
- ✅ Budget to evaluate new vendor

### STRONG INTEREST SIGNALS:
- "Yes, our current courier is expensive/unreliable"
- "We need better tracking"
- Budget approved for vendor evaluation
- Specific pain points mentioned

### DEAL KILLERS:
- <50 pickups/week (too small, not profitable for us)
- Long-term contract with current courier (loyalty)
- No HIPAA knowledge (education burden)
- "We'll think about it" (low priority)
