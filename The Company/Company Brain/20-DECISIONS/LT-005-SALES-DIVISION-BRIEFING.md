## ⚠️ OBJECTION HANDLING — THE FIVE YOU'LL HEAR

### **Objection #1: "We use UPS / generic courier"** (Frequency: 60%)

**Root Fear:** Already have solution, switching costs, unknown risk

**Your Response:**
> "Many labs do. The issue: generic couriers don't understand specimen handling. They delay, they don't maintain temp control, and they can't prioritize STAT orders. HealthRoute specializes in medical specimens. How often do you have delayed results with your current courier?"

**If They Push Back:**
> "Have you ever had a specimen compromised or delayed because the courier didn't maintain proper temperature? That's exactly the gap we fill."

**Win Condition:** They admit to at least 1-2 delayed specimens/month

---

### **Objection #2: "HIPAA compliance is too much hassle"** (Frequency: 40%)

**Root Fear:** Regulatory burden, compliance risk, IT complexity

**Your Response:**
> "That's exactly why we exist. We handle all HIPAA documentation, security training, audit trails—you just use the app. Our carriers are HIPAA-certified and insured. You're more protected with us than DIY."

**If They Push Back:**
> "Would you rather manage HIPAA compliance in-house and hope you're doing it right, or let specialists handle it? We absorb the liability and compliance risk."

**Win Condition:** They agree HIPAA is a real concern + our solution reduces risk

---

### **Objection #3: "Cost is a concern"** (Frequency: 50%)

**Root Fear:** Budget pressure, ROI unclear, price seems high

**Your Response:**
> "I get it. But think about it this way: one lost patient due to delayed results costs you $5K in referral value. One HIPAA violation can cost $100K+. Our service is insurance against that. What's your typical specimen volume?"

**If They Push Back:**
> "How much would you lose if a HIPAA audit found your courier wasn't compliant? Our service includes that protection."

**Reframe:** Cost → Risk protection + Revenue opportunity (faster = more referrals)

**Win Condition:** They admit cost is manageable vs. risk + referral value

---

### **Objection #4: "We don't have that many specimens"** (Frequency: 30%)

**Root Fear:** Volume too low, cost not justified for their size

**Your Response:**
> "Even small labs benefit—we handle fractional service. You only pay for what you use. Plus, you can scale up as you grow. Let me send a quote based on your actual volume."

**If They Push Back:**
> "How many specimens per day does it take before delivery speed starts affecting patient care? At that threshold, HealthRoute makes sense financially."

**Win Condition:** They acknowledge speed matters + agree to see pricing

---

### **Objection #5: "Let me check with the lab director"** (Frequency: 70%)

**Root Fear:** Not the decision maker, need internal alignment

**Your Response:**
> "Perfect. Can you tell me your typical volume and who the director is? I'll send a one-page overview to both of you. That way when you talk, you'll both have the same info."

**If They Push Back:**
> "What would help the lab director get comfortable with this? I can prepare specific data they need to see."

**Win Condition:** Get name + email of decision maker + permission to send materials
