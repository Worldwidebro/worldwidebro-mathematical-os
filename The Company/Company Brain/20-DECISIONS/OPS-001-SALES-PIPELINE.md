# OPS-001 Sales Pipeline — CareerOps Staffing

**Venture:** OPS-001 (CareerOps) — High-Velocity Staffing Placements  
**Product:** Warehouse & logistics staff placement with placement fee model  
**Revenue per Deal:** $2,500 (one-time placement fee)  
**Pipeline Velocity:** 3-7 days (prospect to close)  
**Priority:** 🔴 HIGHEST (fastest ROI, lowest sales cycle, highest connect rate)

---

## PIPELINE STAGES

### Stage 1: PROSPECT
**Definition:** Contact identified and call scheduled  
**Action:** Outbound cold call to HR managers at logistics/retail/manufacturing facilities  
**Move to Next Stage:** Call made and connected OR callback scheduled

**Win Rate to Stage 2:** 80% (8 of 10 calls connect)

---

### Stage 2: CALL CONNECTED
**Definition:** HR manager answered, heard pitch, didn't hang up  
**Win Rate to Stage 3:** 25% (2 of 8 connected = interested)

**Move to Next Stage:** Prospect says "yes we have open roles" or "send me candidates"

---

### Stage 3: INTERESTED
**Definition:** "Yes, we have open roles and want to see profiles"  
**Action:** Send 2-3 matching profiles within 4 hours  
**Win Rate to Stage 4:** 50% (1 of 2 interested = agreed to hire)

**Move to Next Stage:** Interview scheduled with candidate

---

### Stage 4: PROFILE SENT & AGREED
**Definition:** Profiles sent, interview scheduled, candidate hired  
**Action:** Confirm interview, verify candidate transport  
**Win Rate to Stage 5:** 95% (payment collection)

**Move to Next Stage:** Candidate worked 3+ days

---

### Stage 5: CLOSED WON
**Definition:** Payment received, placement confirmed  
**Revenue:** $2,500 per placement  
**Collection Rate:** 95%

---

## CONVERSION MATH

```
10 calls → 8 connected → 2 interested → 1 placement = $2,500
20 calls → 16 connected → 4 interested → 2 placements = $5,000
50 calls → 40 connected → 10 interested → 5 placements = $12,500
100 calls → 80 connected → 20 interested → 10 placements = $25,000
```

**Overall Win Rate:** 10% of calls → placement

---

## TIMELINE

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Prospecting | 0-1 days | 10 calls made, 8 connected |
| Discovery | 1-2 days | 2 interested, profiles requested |
| Placement | 2-5 days | Interview, candidate hired |
| Guarantee | 5-90 days | 90-day guarantee active |
| Collection | 5-10 days after hire | Payment received |

**Total Sales Cycle:** 3-7 days

---

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### MUST HAVE:
- ✅ Open warehouse/logistics roles (verified)
- ✅ Hiring manager or HR decision-maker
- ✅ Willing to interview this week

### STRONG INTEREST SIGNALS:
- "We need staff right now"
- "Send profiles today"
- Specific role + wage range
- Sense of urgency

### DEAL KILLERS:
- "We don't pay placement fees"
- Requires exclusive contract
- Staffing agency competitor
- Budget frozen

- Status: Prospect / Connected / Interested / Agreed / Closed Won

- Phone (text)
- Roles Open (text)
- Num Positions (number)
