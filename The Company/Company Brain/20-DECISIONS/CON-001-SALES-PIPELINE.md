# CON-001 Sales Pipeline — ACE Construction Field OS

**Venture:** CON-001 (ACE Construction) — Field Operations & Consultation  
**Product:** On-site construction consultation ($299 upfront via Stripe)  
**Revenue per Deal:** $299 (consultation fee, paid upfront)  
**Pipeline Velocity:** 2-5 days (prospect to payment)  
**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH (quick close, lower close rate, premium value)

---

## PIPELINE STAGES

### Stage 1: PROSPECT
**Definition:** GC contact identified  
**Action:** Cold call to general contractors, site managers, operations directors  
**Qualification:** General contractors with 10+ active projects or recurring renovation work  
**Move to Next Stage:** Call made, connected

**Win Rate to Stage 2:** 80% (8 of 10 calls connect)

---

### Stage 2: CALL CONNECTED
**Definition:** GC/owner answered, heard value prop  
**Value Prop:** "Free on-site consultation to identify construction efficiency gains and compliance issues"  
**Win Rate to Stage 3:** 20% (1-2 of 8 connected say "yes, I'm interested")

**Move to Next Stage:** "I want a consultation" or "send me more info"

---

### Stage 3: CONSULTATION SCHEDULED
**Definition:** Prospect agreed to consultation, date/time set  
**Action:** Confirm appointment date/time, send email reminder day before  
**Win Rate to Stage 4:** 80% (most scheduled consultations happen)

**Move to Next Stage:** Consultation completed, GC ready to pay

---

### Stage 4: PAYMENT PROCESSED
**Definition:** $299 paid via Stripe (upfront, checkout form)  
**Action:** Send Stripe payment link, follow up if not paid within 24 hours  
**Win Rate to Stage 5:** 95% (most pay after scheduling)

**Move to Next Stage:** Payment confirmed in Stripe

---

### Stage 5: CLOSED WON
**Definition:** Consultation delivered, relationship established  
**Revenue:** $299 per consultation  
**Next Step:** Upsell to implementation project ($2K-5K per project)

---

## CONVERSION MATH

```
10 calls → 8 connected → 1-2 interested → 1 scheduled → 0.8 paid = 1 close = $299
20 calls → 16 connected → 3-4 interested → 2-3 scheduled → 2 paid = $598
50 calls → 40 connected → 8 interested → 6-7 scheduled → 5-6 paid = $1,495-1,794
100 calls → 80 connected → 16 interested → 12-13 scheduled → 11-12 paid = $3,289-3,588
```

**Overall Win Rate:** 10% of calls → consultation payment

## TIMELINE

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Prospecting | 0-1 days | 10 calls made, 8 connected |
| Discovery | 0-1 days | 1-2 interested, consultation date set |
| Consultation | 1-3 days | On-site visit, findings documented |
| Payment | 0-1 day (usually same day) | $299 charged via Stripe |

**Total Sales Cycle:** 2-5 days

---

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### MUST HAVE:
- ✅ Active construction projects (verified via LinkedIn, Crunchbase, permit records)
- ✅ Decision-maker (GC owner, project manager, operations director)
- ✅ Has construction budget available

### STRONG INTEREST SIGNALS:
- "I want a consultation"
- "Yes, we need help with efficiency"
- Specific project mentioned
- Complaints about current workflow

### DEAL KILLERS:
- "I'll think about it" (low intent)
- Company closing/bankruptcy
- No active projects
