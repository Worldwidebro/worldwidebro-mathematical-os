# HealthRoute Courier — Driver Onboarding Package

**Prepared for:** Anissa Valentin, Operations Director  
**Organization:** HealthRoute Courier LLC  
**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Location:** Charlotte, NC Metro  

---

## Executive Summary

All HealthRoute drivers transporting medical specimens must complete HIPAA awareness training and OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens (BBP) certification before first pickup. This document outlines the complete onboarding workflow, timeline, costs, and compliance requirements.

**Estimated onboarding time:** 5-7 business days from application to first route  
**Estimated cost per driver:** $150-250 (including certifications, background check, drug screen)

---

## Pre-Hire Phase (Days 1-3)

### 1. Application & Resume Review
- Standard courier/delivery driver application
- Resume review (focus: delivery experience, clean driving record)
- Phone screening: 30 minutes
  - Ask: "Why interested in medical logistics?"
  - Confirm: Legal right to work in US, valid driver's license, transportation
  - Explain: HIPAA/BBP requirements, on-time attendance critical for healthcare

### 2. Background Check (FCRA Compliant)
- **Provider:** Checkr, GoodHire, or Instant Checkmate
- **Cost:** $40-60/candidate
- **Scope:**
  - Criminal history (7-year lookback)
  - Sex offender registry (national)
  - Address verification
  - Employment history verification (optional)
- **Timeline:** 24-48 hours
- **Pass criteria:** No felony convictions, no sexual offenses, clean record for past 3 years minimum

### 3. Driving Record Check (Motor Vehicle Report / MVR)
- **Provider:** Courts.com, LexisNexis, or state DMV
- **Cost:** $10-25
- **Scope:**
  - License status (valid, non-suspended)
  - Violations (last 3-5 years)
  - Accidents (last 3 years)
  - DUI/DWI history
- **Pass criteria:** 
  - No DUI/DWI in past 7 years
  - Maximum 1 major violation (reckless, speeding >15mph over, at-fault accident) in past 3 years
  - No license suspensions

### 4. Reference Checks
- Minimum 2 professional references (prior employers preferred)
- **Questions:**
  - Reliability and punctuality
  - Customer service capability
  - Vehicle maintenance responsibility
  - Any safety concerns

### 5. Conditional Offer
- Offer letter issued contingent on:
  - Drug screen clearance
  - Medical evaluation clearance
  - Completion of HIPAA + BBP training

---

## Hire Phase (Days 3-5)

### 6. Drug Screen (DOT-Compliant)
- **Provider:** LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics, or local urgent care
- **Cost:** $40-80
- **Test type:** 5-panel standard (cocaine, marijuana, amphetamines, opioids, phencyclidine)
- **Timeline:** Same-day results
- **Pass criteria:** Negative on all panels
- **Note:** Specimen handling role requires DOT compliance

### 7. Medical Evaluation (DOT-Compliant)
- **Provider:** DOT-certified medical examiner (state-required for commercial drivers)
- **Cost:** $100-150
- **Scope:**
  - Vision screening (corrected 20/40 or better)
  - Hearing test
  - Blood pressure (<160/100 for certification)
  - General physical exam
- **Timeline:** Same-day to 48 hours
- **Valid for:** 2 years

### 8. Legal / Tax Setup
- **I-9 Verification** (employment eligibility)
  - Government-issued ID (driver's license)
  - Social Security card or passport
- **W-4 Form** (federal tax withholding)
- **State tax forms** (NC state withholding if NC resident)
- **Direct deposit setup** (ACH authorization)
- **Signed acknowledgment** of HealthRoute Employee Handbook

### 9. Background Check Results Review
- Once results returned, confirm pass/fail
- If failed: Do not proceed to onboarding
- If passed: Advance to training phase

---

## Training Phase (Days 5-7)

### 10. HIPAA Awareness Training
- **Provider Options:**
  - HIPAA.com - https://www.hipaa.com/training/ ($30-50)
  - Compliance.com - https://www.compliance.com/hipaa-training
  - HHS Resources - https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours (online, self-paced)
- **Content:**
  - What is PHI (Protected Health Information)
  - HIPAA privacy rules
  - Security safeguards for patient data
  - Breach reporting procedures
  - Consequences of non-compliance
- **Completion:** Certificate of completion required
- **Valid for:** Annual refresher required

### 11. BBP/OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Certification
- **Provider Options:**
  - American Red Cross - https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/bloodborne-pathogens
  - OSHA Online Training - https://www.osha.gov/bloodborne-pathogens
  - CPR.com - https://www.cpr.com/bloodborne-pathogens-certification
  - National Safety Council - https://www.nsc.org/training/bloodborne-pathogens
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours (online or in-person)
- **Cost:** $30-75
- **Content:**
  - Sharps safety and needle stick prevention
  - Specimen container handling
  - Spill cleanup procedures
  - PPE requirements (gloves, hand sanitizer)
  - Exposure incident reporting
  - Bloodborne pathogen routes of transmission
- **Completion:** Certificate of completion required
- **Valid for:** 1 year (annual refresher)

### 12. HealthRoute Onboarding Training
- **Vehicle familiarization:**
  - Vehicle inspection checklist (fuel, lights, tire pressure, cleanliness)
  - GPS/routing system (Google Maps integration)
  - Radio/communication system
  - Maintenance reporting
- **Specimen handling SOP:**
  - Chain of custody documentation
  - Temperature monitoring (cooler calibration)
  - Secure handoff procedures (photo proof, signature)
  - Incident reporting (spill, damage, delay)
- **Customer service training:**
  - Professional appearance and communication
  - Phone courtesy during dispatch
  - HIPAA-compliant information handling
  - De-escalation if customer complaint occurs
- **Route training:**
  - First route shadowed with supervisor
  - Radio check-in procedures
  - Delivery address verification
  - Payment/billing documentation (if applicable)
- **Duration:** 1 full day (8 hours)

## Pre-Go-Live (Day 7)

### 13. Verification & Equipment Issuance
- ✓ Driver's license copy (upload to driver file)
- ✓ Insurance verification (if required by fleet)
- ✓ Uniform fitting (HealthRoute polo, ID badge)
- ✓ Equipment issuance:
  - Company phone (with dispatch app pre-loaded)
  - GPS/scanner device
  - Handheld thermometer for temperature verification
  - Cooler(s) and insulated carriers
  - Latex gloves and hand sanitizer
  - Route manifest binder
- ✓ Officer sign-off (supervisor: "Driver ready for assignment")

### 14. First Supervised Route
- **Duration:** 4-6 hours
- **Route:** Live pickup/delivery (not simulated)
- **Supervisor:** Present in vehicle or shadowing via GPS
- **Checkpoints:**
  - Pre-route vehicle inspection
  - Communication clarity (radio/dispatch)
  - Pickup protocol (ID verification, chain of custody)
  - Temperature maintenance
  - Delivery protocol (proof of delivery, customer interaction)
  - Post-route vehicle cleanup
  - Documentation accuracy (manifest, times, signatures)
- **Sign-off:** Supervisor certifies driver ready for independent assignment
- **Post-route:** 30-minute debrief (feedback, questions, clarifications)

---

## Go-Live (Day 8+)

### 15. Independent Assignment
- Driver assigned to regular routes or on-call dispatcher
- Daily vehicle pre-trip inspection
- Real-time GPS monitoring
- Weekly check-in with supervisor (safety, customer feedback)
- Monthly performance review (on-time %, incident-free, customer satisfaction)

## Critical Compliance Notes

### HIPAA Requirement
- All drivers transporting medical specimens must acknowledge HIPAA Privacy Notice
- Annual refresher training required (schedule for September annually)
- Any breach of PHI must be reported to Anissa within 24 hours

### OSHA BBP Requirement
- Federal mandate for anyone handling bloodborne pathogen specimens
- Certificate must be on file before first shift
- Annual refresher required (schedule for September annually)
- Exposure incident log maintained by HR

### DOT Compliance (if applicable)
- If transporting federally regulated hazardous materials (Category B specimens qualify), driver must have DOT medical certification
- Medical card must be carried in vehicle
- Renewal every 2 years

## Recommended Policies

### Driver Agreement Acknowledgment
Each driver should sign:
- HIPAA Privacy Notice acknowledgment
- Specimen Handling Safety Agreement
- Vehicle Maintenance Responsibility
- Incident Reporting Requirements
- Termination of Employment clause (including confidentiality)

### Monthly Compliance Audit
- Verify all drivers maintain current certifications
- Spot-check drug screening compliance (random annual screens recommended)
- Review incident reports (spills, delays, customer complaints)
- Update medical certification expiration calendar

### Annual Refresher Schedule
- **September 15:** HIPAA + BBP refresher training begins
- **October 15:** All drivers must complete and provide certificates
- **Tracking:** HR maintains master compliance spreadsheet (names, certification dates, expiration)
