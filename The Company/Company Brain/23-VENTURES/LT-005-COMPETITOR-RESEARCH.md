# Competitive Courier Platforms Research
## Driver Acquisition, Work Models & Partnership Opportunities

**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Focus:** 8 Competitor/Alternative Platforms for Driver Pipeline  
**Objective:** Identify driver recruitment channels, commission structures, partnership opportunities

---

## PLATFORM COMPARISON MATRIX

| Platform | Driver Pay Model | Commission Rate | Work Discovery | Key Opportunity |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **CBDriver** | Job Board | Variable (better MVR = better pay) | Browse & Apply | Partner for B2B job listings |
| **USPack (gouspack.com)** | Hourly + Routes | $26.34/hr avg ($15-$31 range) | Direct recruiting | Medical/retail delivery routes |
| **Couriergigs** | Commission | $15-$28/hr range | Job board + employer network | B2B courier company partnerships |
| **Roadie (UPS owned)** | Per-delivery + routed gigs | $117-$200/day | App-based gig selection | Crowdsourced delivery network |
| **Dropoff** | Per-delivery | $28.73/hr avg | Application + onboarding | Recurring route partnerships |
| **MedSpeed** | W-2 Employment | $16-$21/hr or $37K/yr | Direct hiring | Direct competitor (medical focus) |
| **Stat Experts** | B2B Service | Unknown | B2B logistics | Medical logistics partnership |
| **Priority Dispatch** | Commission | $0.45-$0.75/mile | Direct recruiting | Independent contractor network |

## DRIVER PAYMENT MODELS SUMMARY

**Hourly/W-2 Models:**
- MedSpeed: $16-$21/hr (W-2 employment)
- USPack: $26.34/hr avg (routed deliveries)
- Dropoff: $28.73/hr avg (per-delivery)

**Commission Models:**
- Priority Dispatch: $0.45-$0.75/mile
- CBDriver: Variable (based on driver quality)
- Couriergigs: $15-$28/hr range

**Gig/Per-Delivery Models:**
- Roadie: $117-$200/day per gig
- Dropoff: Pay-per-delivery (premium for recurring routes)

## HEALTHROUTE COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE

✅ **24/7 STAT dispatch** — Most platforms don't offer true 24/7  
✅ **Medical specialization** — Only MedSpeed + Stat Experts compete here  
✅ **Charlotte local focus** — National platforms can't compete on speed  
✅ **Transparent per-delivery rates** — Drivers know exact earnings  
✅ **W-2 + Independent contractor options** — Most platforms single model  
✅ **Recurring routes + on-demand flexibility** — Unique combination
