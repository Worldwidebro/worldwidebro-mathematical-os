# Executive Infrastructure Accountability

> **Authority:** CP-001 & CP-027  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

## 1. Accountability Charter
The Sovereign Operator and Executive Leadership maintain strict fiduciary accountability for infrastructure uptime, data sovereignty, and financial efficiency. No automated agent may deploy billable cloud resources without human-in-the-loop authorization.
