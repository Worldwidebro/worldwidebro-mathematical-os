# Incident Response & Escalation Framework

> **Authority:** CP-027  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

## 1. Incident Severity Levels
- **SEV-1 (Critical Outage):** Knowledge graph or inference gateway down blocking venture operations.
- **SEV-2 (Degraded Performance):** Local models offline, system operating on cloud fallback.
- **SEV-3 (Minor Defect):** Observability tracing delayed.
