# FinOps Lifecycle (Inform, Optimize, Operate)

> **Authority:** CP-020 (Financial) & CP-027 (Infrastructure)  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06  
> **Framework:** Cloud FinOps Foundation Framework

## 1. Overview & Principles
FinOps in Company Brain is the operational discipline of continuous, real-time cloud and local infrastructure cost management. Rather than treating infrastructure as fixed overhead, FinOps aligns every dollar of hardware amortization, electricity, API tokens, and SaaS services directly against venture business outcomes.

## 2. Three Phases of Company Brain FinOps
1. **Inform (Visibility & Allocation):**
   - Track every token and compute cycle through [[56-ENGINEERING/INFRASTRUCTURE/12_COST-GOVERNANCE/COST-ALLOCATION|COST-ALLOCATION.md]] and [[56-ENGINEERING/INFRASTRUCTURE/12_COST-GOVERNANCE/UNIT-COST|UNIT-COST.md]].
   - Maintain machine-readable cost records in [[_REGISTRIES/infrastructure_cost_registry.json|infrastructure_cost_registry.json]].
2. **Optimize (Rate & Usage Reduction):**
   - Execute [[56-ENGINEERING/INFRASTRUCTURE/12_COST-GOVERNANCE/COST-OPTIMIZATION|COST-OPTIMIZATION.md]] playbooks (quantize local models to 5-bit, enforce token compression via RTK/Caveman, trim container resource reservations).
   - Enforce the zero-placeholder policy from [[ANTIGRAVITY]] to prevent paying for idle services.
3. **Operate (Continuous Governance):**
   - Align daily spending against [[56-ENGINEERING/INFRASTRUCTURE/12_COST-GOVERNANCE/INFRASTRUCTURE-BUDGET|INFRASTRUCTURE-BUDGET.md]] ($309/mo baseline).
   - Review executive unit economics in [[ECONOMIC-REALITY]].
