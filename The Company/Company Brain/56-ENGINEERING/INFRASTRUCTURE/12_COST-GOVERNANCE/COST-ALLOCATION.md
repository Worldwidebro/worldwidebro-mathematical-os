# Cost Allocation by Venture & Capability

> **Authority:** CP-020 & CP-027  
> **Status:** ACTIVE — Audited 2026-09-06

## 1. Cost Allocation Model
All shared physical infrastructure costs (Mac Studio, drives, fiber network) are pooled and allocated proportionally across:
- **7 Core Operating Ventures:** Allocated 70% of base capacity (see [[23-VENTURES]]).
- **R&D & Knowledge Core:** Allocated 20% of base capacity (see [[AI-BRAIN]]).
- **Shared Corporate Operations:** Allocated 10% (see [[46-GOVERNANCE]]).
