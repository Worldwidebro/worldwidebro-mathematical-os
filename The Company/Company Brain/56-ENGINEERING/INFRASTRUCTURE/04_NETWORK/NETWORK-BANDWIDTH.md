# Network Bandwidth Capacities & Constraints

> **Authority:** CP-027 | **Status:** AUDITED

- Local LAN: 1000 Mbps Full Duplex.
- Tailscale Peer-to-Peer: Up to 850 Mbps direct throughput.
- DERP Relay: 10-25 Mbps (used only when direct UDP fails).
