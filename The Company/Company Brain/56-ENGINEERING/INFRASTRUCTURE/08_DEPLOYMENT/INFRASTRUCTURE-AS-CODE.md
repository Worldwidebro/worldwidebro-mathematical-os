# Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) Framework

> **Authority:** CP-027 | **Standard:** NIST SP 800-204C | **Status:** ACTIVE

## NIST SP 800-204C Distinction
NIST explicitly treats **Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC)**, **Policy-as-Code (PaC)**, and **Observability-as-Code (OaC)** as distinct infrastructure concerns.
- **IaC Role:** Declaratively provisions compute instances, networks, storage volumes, and container definitions without embedding policy or dashboard logic.
