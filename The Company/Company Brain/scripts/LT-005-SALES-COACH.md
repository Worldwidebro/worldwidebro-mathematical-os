# LT-005 HealthRoute Medical Courier — Sales Coach

**Product**: HIPAA-compliant specimen delivery logistics (B2B service contracts)  
**ICP Pain**: Specimen delays, compliance risk, no dedicated courier, losing patients to faster competitors  
**Differentiation**: HIPAA-certified, real-time tracking, insurance-backed liability, 24/7 coverage  
**Price Point**: $2K-5K/month service contract

---

## OPENER (15 sec)

"Hi [name], this is [you] with HealthRoute Medical Courier. I'm calling because we help medical labs move specimens fast and safely—HIPAA-compliant, real-time tracking, insurance-backed. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**Key Points:**
- Address the core need (fast, safe specimen delivery)
- Name compliance (HIPAA credibility)
- Ask for permission (2 minutes)

---

## VALUE PROP (30 sec)

"Most labs use generic couriers or share rides that delay results. We specialize in specimen logistics: HIPAA-compliant, real-time GPS tracking, climate-controlled transport, and full liability insurance. Faster turnaround = happier patients = more referrals."

**Key Points:**
- Show the gap (generic couriers cause delays)
- Quantify outcome (faster = more referrals)
- Lead with compliance (HIPAA matters)

---

## CALL TO ACTION (15 sec)

"Let me ask you a few quick questions about your current volume and geography. Then I can send you a customized quote. How many specimens daily on average?"

**Key Points:**
- Start with discovery (volume assessment)
- Promise customized pricing (not generic)
- Low friction (quick questions)

## TOP 5 OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "We use UPS / generic courier"

**Root Fear:** "Already have a solution" / Switching costs

**Handler:**
"Many labs do. The issue: generic couriers don't understand specimen handling. They delay, they don't maintain temp control, and they can't prioritize STAT orders. HealthRoute specializes in medical specimens. How often do you have delayed results with your current courier?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Have you ever had a specimen compromised or delayed because the courier didn't maintain proper temperature? [Listen] That's exactly the gap we fill."

---

### Objection 2: "HIPAA compliance is too much hassle"

**Root Fear:** "Regulatory burden" / Compliance risk

**Handler:**
"That's exactly why we exist. We handle all HIPAA documentation, security training, audit trails—you just use the app. Our carriers are HIPAA-certified and insured. You're more protected with us than DIY."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Would you rather manage HIPAA compliance in-house and hope you're doing it right, or let specialists handle it? We absorb the liability and compliance risk."

---

### Objection 3: "Cost is a concern"

**Root Fear:** Budget pressure, ROI unclear

**Handler:**
"I get it. But think about it this way: one lost patient due to delayed results costs you $5K in referral value. One HIPAA violation can cost $100K+. Our service is insurance against that. What's your typical specimen volume?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"How much would you lose if a HIPAA audit found your courier wasn't compliant? [Listen] Our service includes that protection."

---

### Objection 4: "We don't have that many specimens"

**Root Fear:** "Volume too low" / Cost not justified

**Handler:**
"Even small labs benefit—we handle fractional service. You only pay for what you use. Plus, you can scale up as you grow. Let me send a quote based on your actual volume."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"How many specimens per day does it take before delivery speed starts affecting patient care? [Listen] At that threshold, HealthRoute makes sense financially."

---

### Objection 5: "Let me check with the lab director"

**Root Fear:** Not the decision maker, need internal buy-in

**Handler:**
"Perfect. Can you tell me your typical volume and who the director is? I'll send a one-page overview to both of you. That way when you talk, you'll both have the same info."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What would help the lab director get comfortable with this? [Listen] I can prepare specific data they need to see."

---

## CLOSING TIPS

✅ **Do:**
- Lead with compliance (HIPAA is the trust driver)
- Quantify the risk (HIPAA violation cost + lost referrals)
- Offer flexible volume (fractional service)
- Emphasize speed (faster turnaround = competitive advantage)

❌ **Don't:**
- Oversell technology (logistics is the service, not the app)
- Push on price if volume is low (fractional models exist)
- Ignore compliance concerns (compliance is the deal)
- Make it about cost (make it about risk + referral quality)

---

## NEXT STEPS AFTER CALL

1. **Send customized quote** — Within 24 hours based on volume/geography data
2. **Lab visit (optional)** — Visit facility to understand current workflow, pain points
3. **Pilot period** — Often 1-month pilot at discounted rate ($1K/month) to prove value
4. **Service agreement** — $2-5K/month ongoing based on volume + geography
5. **Compliance certification** — Provide HIPAA training + audit documentation
6. **Onboarding** — Driver assignments, pickup/dropoff points, emergency protocols

3. "Are you currently using a generic courier or managing delivery in-house?"
4. "How often do you experience delays?"
5. "Have you had any compliance audits recently?"
