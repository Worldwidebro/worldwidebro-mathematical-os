# OPS-001 CareerOps Staffing — Sales Coach

**Product**: Place vetted warehouse workers (permanent, 12+ months)  
**ICP Pain**: High turnover, seasonal hiring, temp agency costs 40-60% markup  
**Differentiation**: Pre-screened, 12-month guarantee, 6-month replacement promise  
**Price Point**: $2,500 placement fee (one-time)

---

## OPENER (15 sec)

"Hi [name], this is [you] with CareerOps Staffing. I'm calling because we place vetted warehouse workers who stay 12+ months—not temp workers who leave in 90 days. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**Key Points:**
- Name the problem (temp churn)
- Establish credibility (12+ month retention)
- Ask for permission (2 minutes)

---

## VALUE PROP (30 sec)

"Most staffing agencies charge 40-60% markup and their workers leave after 90 days. We charge $2,500 flat, our workers are pre-screened and background-checked, and they stay 12+ months. If they leave in 6 months, we replace them free."

**Key Points:**
- Show the gap (40-60% vs. $2,500 flat)
- Quantify the benefit (12+ months retention)
- Add safety net (6-month replacement promise)

---

## CALL TO ACTION (15 sec)

"I have 3 workers ready this week. Can I send you their profiles and wage expectations? You can review today and let me know if they're a fit?"

**Key Points:**
- Specific ask (send profiles)
- Create urgency (3 workers ready this week)
- Low friction (review and respond)

---

## TOP 5 OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "We use a temp agency"

**Root Fear:** Switching costs, breaking existing relationship, uncertainty about change

**Handler:**
"I get it. The problem with most temp agencies is the 40-60% markup and 90-day churn. Our guarantee: if our worker leaves in 6 months, we replace free. Let me just send three profiles—no obligation. You can compare side-by-side."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"How much are you spending annually with your current temp agency? [Listen] Most GCs find our model saves 30-40% while keeping better quality workers."

---

### Objection 2: "$2,500 is expensive"

**Root Fear:** Budget concern, ROI unclear, questioning value

**Handler:**
"What does it cost you right now to recruit and train one warehouse worker? Most companies say $3-5K. We're $2,500 flat, and they're already trained and vetted. Plus our 6-month replacement promise saves you time."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Think about it this way: one bad hire that leaves after 60 days costs you $5K in hiring and training waste. We're insurance against that."

---

### Objection 3: "We're not hiring right now"

**Root Fear:** No immediate need, timing misalignment

**Handler:**
"Totally understood. When do you typically see your next hiring surge? [Listen] Perfect. Let me follow up then—I'll have fresh profiles ready. When should I check back?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Even if you're not hiring this month, most warehouses have unexpected turnover. Can I save your number so I can reach you when the need hits?"

---

### Objection 4: "Your workers aren't reliable"

**Root Fear:** Quality concern, prior bad experience with staffing, skepticism

**Handler:**
"That's exactly what we solve. Our screening includes background checks, reference calls, and reliability history. Our 12-month retention rate proves it works. How about we try one worker risk-free for the first month?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What does reliability mean to you? [Listen] I can show you our screening process and connect you with three clients for references."

---

### Objection 5: "Let me check with management"

**Root Fear:** Not the decision maker, unsure how to pitch internally

**Handler:**
"Perfect—that's smart. Let me send you three worker profiles first so you can review. Then when you talk to your manager, you'll have real names and qualifications to discuss. Can you look at them today?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Who's the final decision maker? [Listen] Let me send profiles to both of you so you're aligned before the conversation."

---

## CLOSING TIPS

✅ **Do:**
- Emphasize the guarantee (6-month replacement free)
- Lead with specific workers ready to place
- Ask permission before sending anything
- Listen for the real objection (often not what's said first)

❌ **Don't:**
- Oversell the price (it's fair vs. temp agencies)
- Promise instant results (screening takes 3-5 days)
- Push hard on initial call (your job is to get permission to send profiles)

---

## NEXT STEPS AFTER CALL

1. Send 3 worker profiles within 2 hours
2. Follow up in 24 hours: "Did you get the profiles? Any questions?"
3. If interest: Schedule 15-min call to discuss wage, start date, onboarding
4. If placement: Create ClickUp task, set placement fee collection deadline (30 days)
