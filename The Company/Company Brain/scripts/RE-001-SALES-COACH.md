# RE-001 WorldwideBro Real Estate — Sales Coach

**Product**: Real estate investment syndication (sourcing + investor placement)  
**ICP Pain**: Lack of deal flow, no trusted sponsor, high fees, opaque returns  
**Differentiation**: Direct sponsor relationships, vetted deals, transparent returns, track record  
**Price Point**: $5K-25K+ syndication fees (% of deal)

---

**Key Points:**
- Address core need (deal flow for accredited investors)
- Quantify benefit (12-15% returns, passive)
- Establish credibility (institutional quality)

"Most RE syndications charge 2-3% fees and lack transparency. We source deals, vet sponsors, and show you exactly where your money goes. Average investor makes 12-15% passive returns with full transparency. We've closed [X] deals totaling [Y] capital."

**Key Points:**
- Show the gap (high fees + opacity in other syndicators)

**Key Points:**

## TOP 5 OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "I don't know your track record"

**Root Fear:** "Unproven sponsor" / Capital loss risk / Credibility gap

**Handler:**
"Fair point. Let me send you our deal history, investor testimonials, and audited returns. Our sponsors have 15+ years of experience. You can verify everything independently. Look it over and let's talk."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Would it help if I connected you with 2-3 existing investors who've already made returns with us? They can answer your questions directly."

---

### Objection 2: "Real estate deals are risky"

**Root Fear:** "Capital loss risk" / Uncertainty about downside protection

**Handler:**
"True, any investment has risk. That's why we specialize in vetting—we only syndicate deals with experienced sponsors, strong downside protection, and clear exit strategies. Would you be interested in a lower-risk deal structure?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What level of downside protection would make you comfortable? [Listen] We have stabilized income-producing assets (less risk) and development deals (higher return/risk) in our pipeline."

---

### Objection 3: "I have a real estate advisor already"

**Root Fear:** "Existing relationship" / Loyalty, not looking for new advisor

**Handler:**
"Great—advisors often recommend our deals to clients. We're not asking you to drop your advisor; we're offering a curated opportunity. Let me send the deal memo and if it fits your criteria, your advisor can review it too."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What type of real estate does your advisor typically recommend? [Listen] Our current deals might complement that perfectly—your advisor can review and approve."

---

### Objection 4: "The minimum is too high"

**Root Fear:** "Capital commitment too large" / Liquidity concern

**Handler:**
"What's your typical investment size? [Listen] We have deals starting at [lower amount]. And some syndications have co-investment opportunities with lower minimums. Let me match you with appropriate deals."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"If we had a deal at [investor's comfort level], would you be interested in learning more?"

---

### Objection 5: "I want to think about it"

**Root Fear:** "Not convinced yet" / Uncertainty, needs time

**Handler:**
"Smart—real estate decisions take time. Here's what I'll do: send you the last 3 deal memos and our 3-year performance summary. Review for a week and let's schedule a 15-min call to answer questions. Sound good?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What specific questions do you want answered before we chat? [Listen] I'll make sure to address those in the materials I send."

---

## CLOSING TIPS

✅ **Do:**
- Lead with track record (social proof is powerful in real estate)
- Emphasize transparency (opposite of typical RE syndications)
- Offer multiple risk profiles (some investors want stability, others want growth)
- Respect the process (real estate decisions aren't rushed)

❌ **Don't:**
- Pressure on timeline (real estate is a long-term decision)
- Oversell returns (12-15% is realistic, not guaranteed)
- Skip the advisor (many accredited investors have fiduciaries)
- Minimize risk (always be honest about downside)

---

## NEXT STEPS AFTER CALL

1. **Send materials** — Deal memos (last 3), track record, investor testimonials, audited returns (within 24 hours)
2. **Initial review** — Investor reviews materials for 3-7 days
3. **Discovery call** — 15-min call to discuss investment criteria, risk tolerance, liquidity needs
4. **Deal recommendation** — Based on profile, recommend 1-3 deals from current pipeline
5. **Due diligence** — Investor requests sponsor financials, phase of development, exit timeline
6. **Investment decision** — Investor commits capital or passes
7. **Ongoing updates** — Quarterly investor updates, annual meetings, exit communication

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Ask during the initial call:

1. "What's your typical investment size?"
2. "Are you looking for income (cash flow) or appreciation (growth)?"
3. "What's your hold timeline?"
4. "Do you prefer stabilized assets or development opportunities?"
5. "Have you syndicated real estate before?"
6. "What returns are you targeting?"
7. "What's most important to you: transparency, track record, or deal type?"

---

## DEAL MEMO STRUCTURE (Send with every inquiry)

Each deal memo should include:

- **Executive Summary:** Asset type, location, sponsor, target returns, timeline
- **Investment Details:** Minimum investment, capital structure, return expectations
- **Sponsor Profile:** Experience, previous deals, track record
- **Asset Overview:** Property type, location, tenant profile, financials
- **Risk Factors:** Market risk, operator risk, financing risk, exit risk
- **Use of Funds:** How capital is deployed
- **Investor Timeline:** When capital is called, when distributions begin, exit date
- **Comparison Chart:** This deal vs. typical RE syndications (fees, transparency, returns)
