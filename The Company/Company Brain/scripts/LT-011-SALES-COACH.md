# LT-011 CarrierDispatch TMS — Sales Coach


**Product**: SaaS transportation management system for freight companies  
**ICP Pain**: Route inefficiency, driver accountability, no visibility into cost per mile, manual load planning  
**Differentiation**: Real-time routing, driver safety, cost-per-mile analytics, easy integration  
**Price Point**: $500-2K/month SaaS (1 month free pilot)

---

## OPENER (15 sec)

"Hi [name], this is [you] with CarrierDispatch. I'm calling because most freight companies lose 15-25% to routing inefficiency and poor driver accountability. We have a TMS that cuts that in half. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**Key Points:**
- Quantify the problem (15-25% waste)
- Show the benefit (cut in half = huge ROI)
- Ask for permission (2 minutes)

---

## VALUE PROP (30 sec)

"Most freight ops still use spreadsheets or basic systems. We give you real-time route optimization, live driver tracking, cost-per-mile analytics, and compliance reporting. Average client saves 10 hours/week in planning and 12% on fuel costs."

**Key Points:**
- Show the gap (spreadsheets = inefficiency)
- Quantify benefit (10 hrs + 12% fuel savings weekly)
- Emphasize analytics (cost-per-mile matters)

---

## CALL TO ACTION (15 sec)

"We offer a 1-month free pilot with your fleet. No setup cost—we import your routes, show you the savings, and you decide. Can we schedule a 20-minute call to review your current operation?"

**Key Points:**
- Remove friction (free pilot, no setup cost)
- Show confidence (import existing routes)
- Low time commitment (20-min call)

## TOP 5 OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "Our drivers won't accept GPS tracking"

**Root Fear:** "Driver revolt" / Labor relations concern

**Handler:**
"Understandable concern. Most drivers actually prefer it—they know their safety is monitored. Plus, it protects them (liability). And our system shows they're not the problem; it's usually routing. Can I send you a 2-min driver intro video?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What if we frame it as a safety feature, not surveillance? Most drivers buy in when they understand it protects them. Would a 5-min all-hands presentation help?"

---

### Objection 2: "We already have a TMS"

**Root Fear:** "Switching costs" / Investment already made

**Handler:**
"What system are you using? [Listen] The issue with [system] is [pain point from research]. Ours is built for modern fleets. And the free pilot means you can compare side-by-side. No risk."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"How much are you spending on [system] annually? [Listen] If we can prove 12% fuel savings alone, the new system pays for itself."

---

### Objection 3: "Implementation is too complicated"

**Root Fear:** "IT burden, downtime" / Operational disruption

**Handler:**
"It's actually plug-and-play. We import your routes in 1 day, wire to your telematics, and you're live. Most fleets go live in under a week. No downtime—you keep running the old system while we migrate."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What's your biggest concern about implementation? [Listen] We have a step-by-step migration plan—you're never without a system."

---

### Objection 4: "$500-2K/month is expensive"

**Root Fear:** Budget pressure / ROI unclear

**Handler:**
"Average client saves $3-5K/month in fuel + labor. So it pays for itself in 2 months. Plus the free pilot lets you see the math. How many trucks are you running?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What's the cost of one 15% inefficient route per truck per month? [Listen] Multiply that by your fleet size—that's what we save."

---

### Objection 5: "Let me talk to my fleet manager"

**Root Fear:** Not the sole decision maker / Need internal alignment

**Handler:**
"Perfect—that's who should see this. Let me send you a 5-min walkthrough video and a ROI calculator. You two can review together and we'll set up a demo. Sound good?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What would help your fleet manager get comfortable with this? [Listen] I can prepare specific metrics they care about (cost per mile, driver utilization, etc.)."

---

## CLOSING TIPS

✅ **Do:**
- Lead with ROI (free pilot removes decision risk)
- Quantify savings (fuel + labor + hours saved)
- Emphasize driver safety (reframe tracking positively)
- Show existing fleet data (import routes, run analysis)

❌ **Don't:**
- Oversell features (TMS is about ROI, not features)
- Push on price during free pilot (let results speak)
- Ignore the fleet manager (they're the key stakeholder)
- Promise instant results (some optimization takes 2-3 weeks)

---

## NEXT STEPS AFTER CALL

1. **Schedule 20-min discovery call** — Understand current fleet size, routes, pain points
2. **Free pilot setup** — 1 week to import routes + integrate telematics
3. **Real-time optimization** — Run live routing during Week 2-4 (no change to actual ops)
4. **Pilot debrief** — Present fuel savings data, driver utilization, cost-per-mile improvements
5. **Pricing presentation** — Show monthly cost vs. projected savings
6. **Onboarding** — Driver training (30 min), dispatch training (1 day), compliance setup

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Ask during the initial call:

1. "How many trucks are you running?"
2. "What's your average daily routes per truck?"
3. "What visibility do you currently have into cost-per-mile?"
4. "How much time per day does your dispatch team spend planning routes?"
5. "What telematics system are you using (if any)?"
6. "What's been your biggest fleet operations headache in the last 6 months?"
