# CON-001 ACE Construction — Sales Coach

**Product**: Field operations SaaS + $299 consultation fee  
**ICP Pain**: Manual tracking, no visibility into jobs, lost revenue from inefficiency  
**Differentiation**: Field-real (real-time vs spreadsheets), compliance-ready, ROI calculator included  
**Price Point**: $299 upfront consultation (immediate close)

---

## OPENER (15 sec)

"Hi [name], this is [you] with ACE Construction. I'm calling because most GCs lose 10-15% revenue to field inefficiency—missing time entries, duplicate work, no visibility. We do free on-site consultations to show you where your blind spots are. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**Key Points:**
- Quantify the problem (10-15% revenue loss)
- Offer free value first (consultation)
- Ask for permission (2 minutes)

---

## VALUE PROP (30 sec)

"We specialize in helping GCs see exactly where they're losing time and money in the field. In a 1-hour on-site consultation, we walk your crew, identify the problems, and show you the fix. Most GCs find $50-100K in annual waste they didn't know about."

**Key Points:**
- Show the value (identify waste + solution)
- Quantify benefit ($50-100K/year typical)
- Low time commitment (1-hour consultation)

---

## CALL TO ACTION (15 sec)

"I can come out this week for a free consultation. It's $299 if you want the written report, but you get the findings on-site that day. What day works for you?"

**Key Points:**
- Remove friction (free initial assessment)
- Create urgency (this week)
- Offer paid option (report for $299)

---

## TOP 5 OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "We use spreadsheets/pen and paper"

**Root Fear:** "That's how we've always done it" / Change is risky / Fear of disruption

**Handler:**
"Totally get it—lots of GCs do. The issue is spreadsheets don't catch field reality. You find out two weeks later that a crew double-entered hours or a job took 3x longer than expected. That's money gone. Let me just come out and show you what you're missing."

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What's the most expensive mistake you've made in the last year due to missing data? [Listen] That's exactly what we prevent. The free consultation will show you."

---

### Objection 2: "$299 is too much for a consultation"

**Root Fear:** Budget pressure, unclear ROI

**Handler:**
"Most GCs who get the report find $2-5K in inefficiencies. So the $299 pays for itself in a month. Plus, if you don't find value, I won't charge you. How's that?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"How much do you estimate you're losing per month to field coordination issues? [Listen] The consultation usually pays for itself in the first week once you see the gaps."

---

### Objection 3: "We don't have time for this"

**Root Fear:** Disruption, busy schedule, time is money

**Handler:**
"It's 1 hour on-site, and we don't need to interrupt your crews much. Just walk the job, talk to a foreman or two, and I'll show you the findings same day. When's your lightest day this week?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"I can work around your schedule—early morning, lunch break, or end of day. What works best?"

---

### Objection 4: "We're happy with how things are running"

**Root Fear:** "Change is risky" / Confidence in current operations / Not seeing the gap

**Handler:**
"I hear that a lot. The thing is, most GCs who say that find $50-100K in waste once we audit. You don't know what you don't see. Let me just come do a free look—if I don't find anything, no report, no charge. Deal?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"Can I ask: when was the last time you actually audited your field operations? [Listen] Most GCs haven't—that's where the gaps hide."

---

### Objection 5: "Let me think about it"

**Root Fear:** Low commitment, stalling, uncertainty

**Handler:**
"Smart. Here's what I'll do: let me schedule a time slot this week, and you can confirm or cancel by tomorrow. That way you're not juggling multiple conversations. How about Thursday afternoon?"

**Follow-Up If Resistance Continues:**
"What would help you decide? Is it the cost, the time, or are there other concerns? [Listen] Let me address that directly so you can move forward."

---

## CLOSING TIPS

✅ **Do:**
- Lead with the free consultation (lowers barrier)
- Mention specific waste numbers ($50-100K/year)
- Emphasize on-site findings (same-day value)
- Offer "no charge if we don't find value" guarantee

❌ **Don't:**
- Push hard (GCs are skeptical of outsiders)
- Promise overnight transformation (field ops take time)
- Make it about the software (it's about the business problem first)
- Oversell the $299 report upfront

---

## NEXT STEPS AFTER CALL

1. **Schedule on-site consultation** — Book specific day/time within 1 week
2. **Pre-site call** — 10 min the day before to understand operation size, crew count, typical jobs
3. **On-site audit** — Walk the job, talk to foreman/crew leads, observe processes
4. **Same-day debrief** — Present 3-5 key findings + improvement priorities
5. **Written report** — $299 for detailed report (if value is clear, close this immediately)
6. **Follow-up** — Send report within 48 hours, schedule 30-min strategy call within 1 week
