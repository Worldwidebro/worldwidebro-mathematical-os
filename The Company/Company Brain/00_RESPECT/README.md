## 1. Executive Summary

The **`00_RESPECT`** subsystem establishes the inviolable boundaries, ethical rules, agency constraints, and human-agent partnership principles of Company Brain. It guarantees that autonomous agents, background daemons, and human operators act with mutual respect, absolute truthfulness, and strict adherence to sovereign authority.
