## 1. Respecting Context

Acting without awareness is inherently disrespectful to the stability of the system.
1. **Survey the Terrain First**: Before running a command, check active processes, open ports, and git branches.
2. **Understand the User's Real Goal**: Do not jump to blind mechanical fixes. Clarify ambiguous requirements when intent is underspecified.
3. **Respect Operational Mood & Mode**:
   - In planning/discovery mode: Do not mutate code files prematurely.
   - In production debugging mode: Minimize downtime and prioritize service stability over aesthetic refactoring.
