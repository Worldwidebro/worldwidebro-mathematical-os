## 2. Executive Summary & Problem Hook

### The Hook (The Problem)
The American labor market faces a deep paradox: millions of living-wage roles in logistics, construction technology, specialty healthcare, and technical services remain unfilled for months, while millions of hardworking jobseekers are trapped in stagnant minimum-wage retail or service positions. Traditional staffing agencies operate on high markups (40–60%) and rely on manual, biased screening that takes 14–21 days per hire. Non-degree candidates who possess 80%+ of required competencies are systematically rejected by automated keyword filters because they lack arbitrary credentials.

### The Solution
WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC has built **CareerOps**, an AI-augmented talent operations platform grounded in a formal **12-Layer Labor Market Ontology**:
1. **Granular Skill Decomposition:** Deconstructs resumes and informal work histories into verified competency vectors rather than job title labels;
2. **Automated Skill Scoring:** Screens 500 candidate profiles in under 5 minutes with zero pedigree or zip-code bias;
3. **Turnkey Workforce Operations:** Incorporates automated onboarding compliance, statutory I-9/W-4 processing, and dynamic rate cards ensuring a minimum 35% margin for operators while guaranteeing living wages ($22–$38/hr) for placed workers.

### The Funding Request
We are requesting **\$350,000** over 18 months to deploy the CareerOps Workforce Engine across an underserved pilot cohort of **500 non-degree jobseekers** in partnership with local American Job Centers (AJCs) and vocational training organizations, aiming for a **≥75% living-wage placement rate within 30 days** at an 80% reduction in time-to-hire.

### Section 2: SMART Project Milestones
- **Milestone 1 (Months 1–3): Ontology & AJC System Integration.** Wire the CareerOps intake portal with local American Job Center databases and state workforce job banks. Calibrate algorithmic scoring to prevent racial, age, and gender bias.
- **Milestone 2 (Months 4–9): Candidate Cohort 1 Intake & Placement (250 Workers).** Screen and profile 250 displaced workers. Conduct structured interviews and placement into local logistics (`LT-011`), healthcare transport (`LT-005`), and construction field coordination (`CON-001`) roles.
- **Milestone 3 (Months 10–15): Candidate Cohort 2 Intake & Advanced Placement (250 Workers).** Expand screening to advanced manufacturing, IT support, and dispatch operations. Track wage gains and employer satisfaction.
- **Milestone 4 (Months 16–18): Longitudinal Retention Analysis & Playbook Publication.** Audit 6-month and 12-month job retention, finalize economic impact metrics, and deliver the open-source WIOA Workforce AI Playbook to the USDOL.

## 5. Evaluation Plan & Post-Award Metrics

Every dollar granted will be measured against USDOL Common Performance Measures:
1. **Employment Rate (Q2 Post-Exit):** Target ≥75% employed in living-wage jobs 90 days after program exit.
2. **Employment Rate (Q4 Post-Exit):** Target ≥70% continuous retention after 1 year.
3. **Median Earnings:** Target median hourly wage of \$24.50/hr (vs. \$15.00/hr baseline).
4. **Credential Attainment:** Target 80% completing structured skills verification and certification.
5. **Measurable Skill Gains:** Target 90% demonstrating measurable advancement on the 12-Layer Skill Graph.
