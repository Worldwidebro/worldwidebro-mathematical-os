## 2. Institutional Standard & Compliance Rules

All grant dossiers strictly adhere to the 10 Critical Rules of the Grant Writer operating standard:
1. **Verifiable Truth (`REALITY.md` oath):** Every claim is grounded in verified repository code, live URLs, and actual operating capabilities. Zero fabrication.
2. **Guidelines First:** All submissions map directly to agency NOFO guidelines, CFDA numbers, and scoring rubrics.
3. **Funder Priorities First:** The funder's stated mission is the primary problem statement; our technology is the proven intervention.
4. **Narrative-to-Budget Reconciliation:** Every dollar in the budget narrative maps 1-to-1 with personnel, fringe, supplies, subcontracts, and indirect costs.
5. **No Boilerplate:** Each proposal is custom-tailored to the specific agency culture (e.g., NIH Specific Aims vs. NSF Project Pitch vs. DOL WIOA workforce metrics).
6. **2 CFR Part 200 Compliance:** Strict adherence to federal cost principles, allowable expenses, and de minimis 10% MTDC indirect calculations.
7. **Post-Award Integrity:** Each pack establishes the exact reporting milestones and outcome metrics required upon award.
