## 1. Executive Credit Memorandum & Loan Purpose

### Business Description
ACE Construction & Contracting LLC operates a dual-engine construction model combining **commercial and residential general contracting/trade subcontracting** with proprietary field execution software (**ACE Field OS**). The company specializes in deep energy efficiency retrofits, building envelope sealing, commercial tenant improvements, and specialty sub-trades. By equipping crews with mobile-first punch lists, photo-verified quality logs, and OSHA compliance checklists, ACE eliminates the standard 8%–12% margin leakage typical in independent construction.

### Credit Request & Structure
1. **SBA 7(a) Small Loan (\$350,000):**
   - *Term:* 10 Years fully amortizing (zero balloon).
   - *Interest Rate:* Prime + 2.50% (variable quarterly).
   - *Use of Proceeds:* Field equipment acquisition, specialized energy auditing tools, working capital reserves for commercial project bonding, and hiring 2 lead project superintendents.
2. **SBA Surety Bond Guarantee (SBG) Agreement:**
   - Pre-qualifies ACE Construction for bid, performance, and payment bonds up to **\$9,000,000 for single contracts** (and up to \$14M aggregate), backed by an 80%–90% SBA federal guarantee to surety companies. This enables ACE to bid on lucrative public school, municipal, and military base construction projects.
3. **Progress Draw / Mobilization Revolving Line (\$150,000):**
   - Prime + 3.0%, interest-only monthly revolving line with 12-month review, advancing 80% against certified architect pay applications (AIA G702/G703) to bridge 30–60 day payment cycles.
