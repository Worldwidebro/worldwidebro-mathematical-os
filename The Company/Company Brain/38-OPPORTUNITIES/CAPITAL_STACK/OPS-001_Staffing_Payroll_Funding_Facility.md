## 1. Executive Credit Memorandum & The Staffing Cash Flow Paradox

### Business Description
WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC operates **CareerOps** (`https://ops-staff-001-staffing-worldwidebros-projects.vercel.app`), an AI-powered talent operations and contingent staffing firm. Grounded in a formal **12-Layer Labor Market Ontology** (`_ONTOLOGY/LABOR_MARKET_ONTOLOGY.md`), CareerOps matches non-degree, skilled hourly workers into high-margin placements across trade construction (`CON-001`), medical logistics (`LT-005`), and commercial freight dispatch (`LT-011`).

### The Cash Flow Paradox & Payroll Funding Solution
Staffing firms face a unique structural working capital requirement:
- **Weekly Cash Outflow:** Contingent workers are statutory W-2 or contract employees who **must be paid every Friday**, alongside mandatory state/federal employer payroll taxes (FICA, Medicare, FUTA, SUTA) and workers' compensation premiums.
- **Delayed Cash Inflow:** Corporate clients pay on **Net-30, Net-45, or Net-60 day terms**.
- **The Solution (Staffing Payroll Funding Line):** A dedicated payroll funding line advances **90%–95% of approved weekly timesheet billings within 24 hours** directly into the payroll account, completely insulating operations from client payment delays. As clients pay their invoices into a dedicated lockbox, the facility sweeps the balance, deducts its 1.5%–2.5% fee, and remits the 35%–37% profit margin back to CareerOps.
