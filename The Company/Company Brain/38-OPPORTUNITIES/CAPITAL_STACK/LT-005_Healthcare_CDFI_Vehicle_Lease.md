## 1. Executive Credit Memorandum & Mission Alignment

### Business Description
HealthRoute Logistics LLC operates a dedicated, HIPAA-compliant medical courier dispatch platform (`https://healthroute-courier.vercel.app`) designed to eliminate pre-analytical laboratory diagnostic errors. HealthRoute specializes in transporting sensitive clinical specimens (biopsies, blood cultures, infectious disease panels, pharmaceutical compounds) between outpatient clinics, rural Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs), and regional reference laboratories.

### CDFI Mission Fit
Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs) are federally certified mission-driven lenders that provide patient, below-market capital to businesses expanding essential healthcare access in Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSAs) and Medically Underserved Areas (MUAs). 
- **The Problem Solved:** Rural clinics and community health centers often experience 48–72 hour diagnostic delays because large commercial couriers refuse to service low-density routes. HealthRoute's software aggregates independent medical couriers with continuous IoT temperature logging (-20°C, 2°–8°C, ambient), restoring diagnostic turnaround times to <4 hours.

### Structure of the Financing
1. **Healthcare CDFI Term Loan (\$250,000):**
   - *Term:* 7 Years fully amortizing.
   - *Interest Rate:* **4.25% fixed** (subsidized through CDFI Fund healthcare allocations).
   - *Use of Proceeds:* HIPAA security infrastructure hardening, 24/7 STAT medical dispatch operations, laboratory LIMS integration software, and working capital.
2. **Dual-Zone Medical Fleet Lease (\$120,000):**
   - *Structure:* 48-Month Fair Market Value (FMV) commercial equipment lease for two (2) Ford Transit 250 cargo vans equipped with Thermo King V-200 dual-temperature refrigeration units and integrated telematics.
