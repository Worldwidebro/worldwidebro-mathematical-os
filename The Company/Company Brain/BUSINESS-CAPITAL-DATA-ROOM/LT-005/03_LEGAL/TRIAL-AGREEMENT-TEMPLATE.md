# HealthRoute Medical Courier - Trial Agreement

**This Trial Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE]**

**BETWEEN:** HealthRoute Medical Courier, Inc., a [STATE] corporation ("Provider")  
**AND:** [PROSPECT COMPANY NAME], a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] ("Customer")

---

## 1. Trial Service

Provider agrees to provide medical specimen courier services to Customer on a trial basis for a period of **five (5) business days** from the date of this Agreement ("Trial Period").

**Trial Scope:**
- Maximum: 5 specimen deliveries
- Geographic area: [SPECIFY: NC Triangle, SC Metro, etc.]
- Hours: 7am-6pm Monday-Friday
- Temperature-controlled transport: Yes
- Real-time tracking: Yes

---

## 2. Trial Terms

**2.1 Cost:** Trial services are provided **at no cost** to Customer.

**2.2 Performance Expectations:**
- On-time delivery: 99% (STAT orders prioritized)
- Temperature compliance: ±2°C maintained
- Proof of delivery: Electronic signature + photo
- Response time: <30 minutes to pickup request

**2.3 Customer Responsibilities:**
- Provide accurate pickup/delivery addresses
- Ensure specimens are properly labeled and packaged per CAP/CLIA standards
- Notify Provider of any special handling requirements
- Designate authorized contact person for coordination

---

## 3. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to:
- Protect all PHI (Protected Health Information) according to HIPAA Privacy Rule
- Not disclose trial performance metrics publicly
- Treat all communications as confidential

---

## 4. Limitations

**4.1 No Warranties:** Trial services are provided "AS-IS" without warranties of any kind.

**4.2 Liability Cap:** Provider's liability for trial services is limited to direct, documented costs incurred by Customer (not to exceed $500).

**4.3 Insurance:** Provider maintains $1,000,000 general liability and $500,000 medical malpractice coverage.

---

## 5. Data & Compliance

Customer acknowledges:
- Specimens may be transported with other customers' specimens in same vehicle
- Delivery routes may be optimized for efficiency
- Performance metrics (delivery time, temperature) will be recorded for service evaluation

---

## 6. Post-Trial Commitment

Upon successful trial completion, Customer may convert to recurring service by signing standard Master Service Agreement with:
- Pricing: $[AMOUNT] per delivery (negotiable based on volume)
- Minimum: [X] deliveries per week
- Contract term: 12 months, auto-renew

No commitment required for trial period.

---

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Trial Agreement with **24 hours notice** during the Trial Period.

---

## 8. Entire Agreement

This Trial Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and understandings.
