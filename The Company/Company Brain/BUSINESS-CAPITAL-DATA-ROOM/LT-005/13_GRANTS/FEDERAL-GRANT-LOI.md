# Letter of Inquiry to the National Institutes of Health (NIH)

**Date:** September 19, 2026
**To:** Program Officer, National Institutes of Health
**From:** HealthRoute Logistics
**Subject:** Letter of Inquiry: Eliminating Specimen Degradation in Rural Healthcare

Each year in rural and underserved communities, patients face diagnostic delays and errors due to the critical failure of specimen degradation during transit. Legacy medical courier operators suffer from systemic inefficiencies, manual pen-and-paper tracking, and lack of climate control verification, resulting in compromised samples, delayed treatments, and severe operational friction for rural healthcare providers.

HealthRoute Logistics addresses this gap by combining advanced IoT temperature probes with deterministic 13-stage HIPAA state machines. Unlike traditional couriers, our platform maintains an unbroken, cryptographically verified chain of custody and climate control for every specimen. This approach, grounded in precision logistics, entirely eliminates diagnostic loss due to transit degradation.

Our organization has built a highly sustainable and verifiable operational model to support this mission. We project Year 1 gross billings of $683,500 with a strong 1.74x DSCR. Our team brings deep expertise in both medical logistics and health data compliance, ensuring that every specimen arrives intact and fully documented.

We are requesting $450,000 over 12 months to deploy our IoT temperature tracking and HIPAA state machine infrastructure across 50 rural clinic routes. This investment will enable us to secure the diagnostic pipeline for over 100,000 patient specimens, ensuring equitable access to accurate laboratory diagnostics for rural populations.

Given the NIH's commitment to health equity and the improvement of diagnostic accuracy in underserved areas, we believe there is strong alignment with our work. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how this partnership might advance our shared goals.
