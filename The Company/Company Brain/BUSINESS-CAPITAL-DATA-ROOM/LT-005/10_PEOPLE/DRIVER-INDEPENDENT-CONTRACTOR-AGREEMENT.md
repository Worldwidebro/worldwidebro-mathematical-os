# HealthRoute Medical Courier - Independent Contractor Agreement

**This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE]**

**BETWEEN:** HealthRoute Medical Courier, Inc., a [STATE] corporation ("Company")  
**AND:** [DRIVER NAME], an individual ("Contractor")

---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

**1.1 Services:** Contractor agrees to provide medical specimen courier delivery services for Company on an independent contractor basis.

**1.2 Term:** This Agreement shall commence on [DATE] and continue until terminated by either party with [30 DAYS] written notice.

**1.3 Compensation:**
- **Per Delivery:** $[25-35] per completed delivery
- **STAT Deliveries:** $[35-50] (priority orders)
- **Payment Terms:** Weekly via [ACH/Check]
- **No Benefits:** Contractor is responsible for own health insurance, taxes, retirement

---

## 2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

**2.1 Relationship:** Contractor is an independent contractor, NOT an employee. Contractor:
- Sets own work schedule (minimum [X] deliveries/week)
- Uses own vehicle OR Company-provided vehicle (see Section 3)
- Is responsible for own insurance (see Section 4)
- Is NOT entitled to employee benefits
- Must carry own business licenses/permits

**2.2 Taxes & Withholding:** Contractor is solely responsible for:
- Federal income tax
- Self-employment tax
- State/local taxes
- Quarterly estimated tax payments
- Company will issue Form 1099-NEC for tax year

**2.3 No Exclusivity:** Contractor may work for other courier/logistics companies.

---

## 3. VEHICLE REQUIREMENTS

**Contractor Must Provide:**

**3.1 Vehicle Specifications:**
- [ ] Owned/financed by Contractor
- [ ] Current registration and inspection
- [ ] Clean interior (temperature controlled)
- [ ] GPS/tracking capability (Company-provided device)
- [ ] Refrigerated compartment capable of maintaining 2-8°C
- [ ] Backup vehicle plan if primary unavailable

**3.2 Maintenance:** Contractor responsible for all maintenance, repairs, fuel, insurance.

**3.3 Company-Provided Vehicle (If Applicable):**
- Company provides insured vehicle
- Contractor responsible for fuel
- Mileage tracked for tax purposes
- Vehicle may not be used for personal errands
- Return condition must be maintained

---

## 4. INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

Contractor must maintain at minimum:

**4.1 Personal Auto Insurance:**
- Minimum $1,000,000 liability coverage
- Medical malpractice carrier: $500,000
- Certificate of Insurance provided to Company

**4.2 Proof:** Contractor shall provide proof of insurance before first delivery and annually thereafter.

**4.3 Coverage for Specimens:** Contractor's insurance covers damage/loss to specimens during transport.

---

## 5. COMPLIANCE & TRAINING

**5.1 Training:** Contractor must complete Company training covering:
- Specimen handling per CAP/CLIA standards
- Temperature control procedures
- HIPAA privacy requirements
- Chain of custody documentation
- Incident reporting procedures

**5.2 Certifications:**
- Valid driver's license (Class D minimum)
- Clean driving record (< 2 violations in 3 years)
- Background check passed
- HIPAA training completed

**5.3 Responsibilities:**
- Maintain specimens per temperature requirements
- Provide proof of delivery (signature + photo)
- Report temperature excursions immediately
- Report incidents/damage within 2 hours
- Maintain confidentiality of patient information (PHI)
- Follow all delivery instructions precisely

---

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Confidentiality:** Contractor shall:
- Treat all patient information as confidential
- Not disclose customer names or facility data
- Not discuss delivery routes or customer locations publicly
- Sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (separate)

**6.2 Intellectual Property:** All processes, routes, and procedures are Company property.

---

## 7. LIABILITY & INSURANCE

**7.1 Indemnification:** Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Company from:
- Any damages caused by Contractor's negligence
- Any HIPAA violations by Contractor
- Any incidents involving Contractor's vehicle
- Any claims arising from Contractor's service delivery

**7.2 Coverage:** Contractor's insurance is primary; Company's insurance is secondary.

**7.3 Incident Reporting:** Contractor must report any:
- Vehicle damage
- Specimen damage/loss
- Temperature excursions
- Delivery delays
- Customer complaints
Within 2 hours of occurrence

---

## 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination Without Cause:** Either party may terminate with [30 DAYS] written notice.

**8.2 Termination For Cause:** Company may terminate immediately for:
- HIPAA violation
- Falsifying delivery records
- Reckless driving
- Customer complaints about professionalism
- Failure to maintain required insurance
- Failure to complete training requirements

**8.3 Final Payment:** Unpaid deliveries will be settled within [7 DAYS] of termination.

---

## 9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**9.1 Disputes:** Any disputes shall be resolved through:
1. Good-faith negotiation (14 days)
2. Mediation (if unresolved)
3. Binding arbitration (per [STATE] law)

---

## 10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations.
