# LT-005 Financial Model Master

## 1. Monetization Map
LT-005 acts as the vertical operations company (Medical Courier), acting as the first best customer of LT-011 infrastructure.

*   **Standard Courier:** Route-based delivery fees.
*   **STAT/Emergency:** Premium transaction fees.
*   **Recurring:** Scheduled facility routes (Hospitals, Labs, Pharmacies).

## 2. Unit Economics & Intelligence
*   **Equation:** Jobs/day × Average Ticket × Operating Days
*   **Capacity constraint:** How many medical deliveries/routes can be serviced profitably given the current driver/vehicle pool?
*   **Target Metrics:** Revenue per route, Revenue per vehicle, Revenue per driver, Gross Margin per job.

## 3. Financial Flow
**Gross Revenue → COGS → Gross Profit → OpEx → EBITDA → Cash Flow**
*   **COGS:** Driver payments, Fuel, Vehicle costs, Insurance, LT-011 Software Licensing/Transaction fees.
*   **OpEx:** Local market sales, Compliance/Regulatory, Dispatch labor (if internal), Management.
