## 1. Credit Overview for Underwriters
ACE Construction & Contracting LLC is applying for an integrated credit facility to expand its commercial contracting and energy decarbonization retrofit operations:
* **Primary Facility:** \$350,000 SBA 7(a) 10-year term loan at Prime + 2.50% (monthly debt service: \$4,366).
* **Secondary Facility:** \$150,000 Contractor Progress Draw Revolving Line at Prime + 3.00% (interest-only monthly).
* **Bonding Program:** Pre-approval for SBA Surety Bond Guarantee allocation covering single projects up to **\$9,000,000**.

---

## 2. Debt Service Coverage & Cash Flow Capacity

$$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Operating Income (EBITDA)}}{\text{Annual Principal + Interest}} = \frac{\$182,600}{\$62,900} = \mathbf{2.90\times} \quad (\text{Benchmark} \ge 1.25\times)$$

* **Year 1 Cash Flow Cushion:** Net cash flow after complete debt service is **\$119,700**.
* **Year 2 Projections:** EBITDA rises to \$538,800 against \$64,400 debt service ($\mathbf{8.36\times}$ coverage).
* **Stress Test (25% Revenue Decline):** At \$1,119,000 gross revenue (a severe 25% drop), EBITDA remains \$89,000, still maintaining a **1.41x DSCR**, ensuring uninterrupted loan repayment.
