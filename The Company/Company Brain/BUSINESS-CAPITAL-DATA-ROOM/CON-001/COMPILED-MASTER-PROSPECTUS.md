### Company Identity

ACE Construction & Contracting LLC is a North Carolina-formed construction company operating under the brand "ACE Construction" with an integrated field operations platform called ACE Field OS. We are organized as a limited liability company (LLC) in both North Carolina and Delaware, with NAICS codes 236220 (Commercial Construction) and 238990 (Specialized Trade Contracting). Our live commercial portal operates at `https://ace-construction.vercel.app` with verified production code at commit `67e7b82`.

We operate in the commercial construction and energy efficiency retrofit space, specifically focusing on work that qualifies for Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) rebates and 179D tax incentives. Our dual-revenue model combines hands-on general contracting services with software-as-a-service licensing for field operations mobile applications.

### Problem & Opportunity

We are solving three distinct but interconnected problems in the construction services market. First, 85% of trade contractors still track jobs on paper and text messages, losing 8–12% per job in margin leakage from disputed change orders, delayed payroll reconciliation, and rework. Second, IRA rebates and 179D tax incentives require rigorous photo documentation of every air seal and insulation cavity—unverified work fails inspection and the contractor forfeits the rebate entirely. Third, traditional sureties will not bond small contractors without $1M+ in liquid net worth, effectively locking them out of municipal and school construction contracts—precisely the most profitable work available.

The opportunity is substantial: $1.8 trillion in total U.S. construction spending exists, with our serviceable market at $85 billion in commercial tenant improvement and residential energy retrofit work across the Southeast.

### Product & Service

We offer a three-tier service model. First, ACE Construction provides direct commercial general contracting on cost-plus (18–25%) or fixed-bid milestone-draw contracts, with a target scope of $15K–$500K per project. Second, we offer site walk feasibility consulting at $299 per study, delivered via direct Stripe checkout on our commercial portal. Third, we license ACE Field OS—our mobile application—at $79/month per crew, solving the verification and audit trail problem through geotagged photographs, timestamped checklists aligned to ASHRAE 90.1 and ENERGY STAR standards, and automated inspection pass-rate optimization.

The Field OS app works by having crews photograph every job milestone with geotagged timestamps before walls are drywalled, linking photos to standardized checklists, and creating audit trails that satisfy federal rebate documentation requirements and support SBA surety bonding qualification.

### Market & Competition

We operate in a fragmented, underserved market. There are 25,000+ general contractors in the U.S., most with fewer than 50 employees and minimal software adoption. Competition is primarily indirect—our real competitors are paper-based job tracking, unreliable spreadsheets, and the status quo of margin leakage. A small number of larger HVAC-focused field-management apps exist (e.g., ServiceTitan, Fieldwire), but none specifically address IRA rebate verification, bonded contractor qualification, or the combined hands-on contracting + software licensing model that we offer.

Our positioning is built on verified compliance with federal rebate documentation standards and the demonstrated ability to qualify for SBA surety bonding—assets that traditional field-management software companies do not emphasize.

## The Problem

85% of trade contractors track jobs on paper and text messages. This costs them 8–12% per job in margin loss: disputed change orders, delayed payroll, rework.

There's a secondary problem: IRA energy rebates and 179D tax incentives require photo documentation of every air seal and insulation cavity. Unverified work fails inspection and the contractor loses the rebate.

A third problem: traditional sureties won't bond small contractors unless they have $1M+ liquid net worth. This locks contractors out of municipal and school construction contracts—the most profitable work.

## What We Do

We operate ACE Construction (hands-on contracting) and ACE Field OS (a mobile app). The app works like this:

1. Crew photographs every job milestone with geotagged timestamps
2. Photos get linked to standardized checklists (ASHRAE 90.1, ENERGY STAR standards)
3. This creates an audit trail good enough for federal rebates and SBA surety bonding

Result: We eliminate the 8–12% margin leakage and unlock access to $9M in bonded public works contracts.

We offer three services:
- Commercial construction work ($15K–$500K+ per project)
- ACE Field OS subscription ($79/month per crew)
- Site feasibility consulting ($299 per study)
