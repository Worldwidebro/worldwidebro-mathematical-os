# Tax Credit Strategy Memo

**Venture:** ACE Construction & Contracting LLC
**Date:** September 21, 2026

## 1. Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) Strategy
For ACE Construction (NAICS: 236220 / 238990), staffing field crews and trade contractors provides an excellent opportunity to leverage WOTC.

**Implementation Steps:**
- **Onboarding Integration:** Incorporate IRS Form 8850 and ETA Form 9061 into the new hire packet for all trade workers and laborers.
- **Targeted Hiring:** Focus hiring efforts on WOTC-eligible individuals, such as qualified veterans and residents of empowerment zones, specifically for field labor and diagnostic equipment operation.
- **Filing Deadline:** Submit Form 8850 to the state workforce agency within 28 days of the worker's start date.
- **Financial Impact:** Claim tax credits ranging from $2,400 to $9,600 per eligible hire, directly offsetting the labor costs associated with our field crews and improving our Year 1 Gross Profit ($447,600).

## 2. Research & Development (R&D) Tax Credit Strategy
The creation of the **ACE Field OS**, our proprietary mobile verification platform, constitutes qualified research.

**Implementation Steps:**
- **Qualified Research Activities (QRAs):** Track developer hours dedicated to "Software Platform Scaling", specifically the mobile app offline sync, camera AI integration, and the energy-efficiency audit trails.
- **Four-Part Test Compliance:** Maintain technical records demonstrating the algorithmic challenges of geotagged daily logs and offline synchronization in environments with poor connectivity.
- **Payroll Tax Offset:** Apply the federal R&D tax credit against employer payroll taxes, conserving the $140,000 working capital & payroll reserve for our lead superintendents.
- **Documentation:** Use Git repositories (verified commit 67e7b82) and technical specifications (e.g., threat models, architecture reviews) as primary substantiation for the IRS.
