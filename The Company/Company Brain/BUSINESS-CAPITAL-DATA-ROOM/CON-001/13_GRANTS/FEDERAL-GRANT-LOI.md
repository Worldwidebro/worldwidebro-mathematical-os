# Letter of Inquiry to the Department of Energy (DOE)

**Date:** September 19, 2026
**To:** Program Officer, Department of Energy
**From:** ACE Construction
**Subject:** Letter of Inquiry: ACE Field OS Energy-Efficiency Retrofit Scaling

Each year, the commercial construction sector struggles with systemic inefficiencies and manual pen-and-paper tracking that obscure critical energy performance data. Legacy operators in this space face 8-12% billing disputes and struggle to provide accurate energy-efficiency audit trails, leaving significant energy waste unaddressed in commercial buildings and creating severe working capital drag that stifles green modernization.

ACE Construction addresses this gap through ACE Field OS, our proprietary mobile verification platform. Unlike legacy operators, our system enforces geotagged daily logs and robust energy-efficiency audit trails in real-time. This approach eliminates 8-12% margin leakage and ensures absolute compliance with green building standards, unlocking access to bonded public works and verifiable energy retrofits.

Over the past year, ACE Construction has demonstrated exceptional operational and financial capability. Operating at the intersection of commercial tenant contracting and software verification, we project Year 1 gross billings of $1.49 million and a debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) of 2.90x. Our team successfully executes complex commercial builds while maintaining the stringent data trails required for modern energy compliance.

We are requesting $500,000 over 12 months to accelerate the deployment of ACE Field OS across our regional commercial project portfolio. This investment will enable us to standardize energy-efficiency audit trails for over 50 commercial retrofits, directly measuring and verifying emissions reductions and energy savings for our commercial clients.

Given the DOE's commitment to verifiable energy efficiency in the commercial sector and the modernization of building infrastructure, we believe there is strong alignment with our work. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how this partnership might advance our shared goals of sustainable commercial development.
