## 1. Project Summary & Societal Impact
Commercial and residential buildings generate 39% of U.S. greenhouse gas emissions. While high-efficiency HVAC heat pumps, air barriers, and R-value insulation receive billions in federal tax credits (IRA Section 25C and 179D), an estimated 25%–40% of intended energy efficiency is lost in the field due to unverified subcontractor installations. 

ACE Construction is developing the **ACE Decarbonization Verification Module**, an offline-first mobile inspection engine that guides independent trade contractors through mandatory ASHRAE/BPI air-sealing quality standards, automatically capturing geotagged photographic proof before walls are closed.

---

## 2. Specific Project Goals & Milestones
* **Goal 1:** Codify DOE Zero Energy Ready Home (ZERH) standards into digital, one-click mobile punch lists.
* **Goal 2:** Deploy the system across 25 independent regional contracting crews executing residential retrofits.
* **Goal 3:** Measure a verified $\ge 90\%$ first-pass inspection rate and a 22% reduction in envelope air leakage across 150 building units.
