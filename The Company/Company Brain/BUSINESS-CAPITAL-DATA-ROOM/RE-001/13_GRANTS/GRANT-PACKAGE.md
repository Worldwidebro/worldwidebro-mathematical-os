## 1. 1-Page Specific Aims & Statement of Need
Over the last decade, institutional private equity buyers have systematically acquired hundreds of thousands of single-family starter homes in working-class neighborhoods, inflating market rents by 15%–25% annually and permanently locking out low-to-moderate income (LMI) families. While local non-profit Community Development Corporations (CDCs) and community land trusts have mission mandates and access to subsidized capital to preserve affordable housing, they lack algorithmic data tools to identify distressed or tax-delinquent homes before institutional cash investors buy them at auction.

WorldwideBro Holdings LLC bridges this technology gap through the **Affordable Housing Deal Engine**, providing non-profit housing entities with real-time intelligence to acquire and preserve naturally occurring affordable housing (NOAH).

### Specific Project Objectives:
* **Objective 1: Multi-County Ingestion Spiders (Months 1–3):** Build automated scraping and ETL data connectors to county GIS portals, property tax delinquency registries, code enforcement citations, and estate probate records across 3 pilot jurisdictions in North Carolina.
* **Objective 2: Section 4 Underwriting & Rehab Engine (Months 4–6):** Integrate HUD Fair Market Rent (FMR) guidelines, local construction labor indices, and automated scope-of-work generators to calculate post-rehab after-repair values (ARV) meeting long-term affordability standards.
* **Objective 3: Non-Profit CDC Pilot Onboarding (Months 7–10):** Onboard 5 non-profit CDCs onto the Holdings Deal Room platform, delivering automated daily off-market feeds and supporting the pre-foreclosure acquisition of $\ge 50$ single-family affordable homes.
* **Objective 4: Policy Playbook & Compliance Dissemination (Months 11–12):** Validate acquisition veracity, evaluate rehab cost accuracy against affiliate `CON-001` trade benchmarks, and publish the open-access *Community Housing Intelligence Playbook*.
