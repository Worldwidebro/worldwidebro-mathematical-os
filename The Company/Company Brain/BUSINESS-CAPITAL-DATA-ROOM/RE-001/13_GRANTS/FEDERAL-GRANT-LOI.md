# Letter of Inquiry to the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)

**Date:** September 19, 2026
**To:** Program Officer, Department of Housing and Urban Development
**From:** WorldwideBro Holdings / Real Estate Deal Engine
**Subject:** Letter of Inquiry: Revitalizing Distressed Properties for Section 8 Housing

Each year, municipalities struggle with blighted and distressed properties while low-income families face a severe shortage of affordable housing. Legacy operators in residential leasing suffer from systemic inefficiencies, manual tracking, and high administrative overhead, limiting their ability to rapidly convert distressed inventory into safe, compliant, and affordable housing stock.

WorldwideBro Holdings addresses this gap by combining public tax and lien distress data crawling with our in-house trade renovation capabilities. Unlike traditional landlords, we programmatically identify distressed assets, rapidly renovate them using our construction arm, and lease them exclusively to guaranteed Section 8 voucher tenants. This approach ensures high-quality living standards for vulnerable populations while revitalizing neighborhoods.

Our organization operates with strong financial discipline and operational capacity. Our seed portfolio produces a 1.49x property-level DSCR and a 1.99x aggregate Year 1 DSCR, with projected Year 1 gross billings of $278,880. Our vertically integrated approach allows us to deliver affordable housing units 30% faster than traditional developers.

We are requesting $750,000 over 24 months to accelerate the acquisition and renovation of 20 severely distressed properties. This investment will enable us to convert these blighted units into permanently affordable, energy-efficient housing for 20 Section 8 eligible families, directly addressing the affordable housing crisis in our target markets.

Given HUD's commitment to expanding access to affordable housing and eliminating community blight, we believe there is strong alignment with our work. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how this partnership might advance our shared goals of community revitalization and housing equity.
