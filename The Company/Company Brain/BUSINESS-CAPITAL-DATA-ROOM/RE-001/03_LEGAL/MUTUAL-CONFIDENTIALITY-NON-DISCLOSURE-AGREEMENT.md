### PREAMBLE & RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Company has developed, owns, operates, and maintains a proprietary commercial real estate operating system and asset management platform, including but not limited to the **RE-001 Real Estate Deal Engine, Autonomous 11-Agent Fleet, Automated 10-Year Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Underwriting Engine, Federal Reserve Macroeconomic Rate Stress-Testing Models, GIS Parcel Intelligence & Zoning Systems, and SEC Rule 506(c) Syndication Deal Room** (collectively, the **"Platform"**, **"Demonstration"**, and **"Proprietary Materials"**); and

**WHEREAS**, Recipient desires to access, review, evaluate, and inspect a confidential demonstration of the Platform, including proprietary source code, operational workflows, off-market deal pipeline, underwriting models, syndication structures, and customer/vendor data, solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential investment, co-general partner (Co-GP) participation, capital allocation, debt financing facility, or strategic business relationship with Company (the **"Permitted Purpose"**); and

**WHEREAS**, Company is willing to disclose such proprietary information and grant access to the Demonstration only upon the express condition that Recipient agrees to protect the confidentiality, intellectual property, and commercial integrity of the Platform and enters into binding non-circumvention covenants as set forth herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

### ARTICLE 1: DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1. **Scope of Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by Company to Recipient, whether orally, visually, electronically, or in writing, or made accessible through the Platform demonstration (`https://re-001-worldwidebro-holdings.vercel.app`), operator console (`/console/*`), investor deal room (`/investor/deal-room`), password-protected staging portals, electronic data rooms, or code repositories, including but not limited to:
- **(a) Software & Technical Assets:** Proprietary source code, software architecture, UI/UX workflows, database schemas, prompt engineering sequences, autonomous agent execution runtimes, API proxy designs, algorithms, trade secrets, and technical documentation.
- **(b) Real Estate Pipeline & Deal Flow:** Off-market property listings, letters of intent (LOIs), purchase and sale agreements, seller identities, motivated seller distress metrics, contract pricing, parcel GIS overlays, zoning analyses, and contractor rehab scopes.
- **(c) Financial Models & Underwriting Intelligence:** 10-year discounted cash flow (DCF) pro formas, debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) calculations, Federal Reserve FRED macroeconomic stress models, loan amortization schedules, equity waterfall structures, preferred return hurdle models, cap tables, distribution ledgers, and revenue fee schedules.
- **(d) Operational & Network Data:** Prospective investor registers, accredited limited partner (LP) allocations, commercial lender facilities, property management ledgers, tenant payment histories, and contractor/vendor networks.

1.2. **Marking Not Required.** All information disclosed during the Demonstration or contained within the Platform, Console, or Data Room shall be deemed Confidential Information regardless of whether it is explicitly marked with a "CONFIDENTIAL" or "PROPRIETARY" stamp.

---

### ARTICLE 2: NON-DISCLOSURE AND RESTRICTIONS ON USE

2.1. **Standard of Care.** Recipient agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict trust and confidence, exercising at least the same degree of care as it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

2.2. **Permitted Purpose Only.** Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely and exclusively for the Permitted Purpose. Recipient shall not utilize Company’s Confidential Information, algorithms, or deal pipeline to develop, build, license, commercialize, or assist any third party in developing any competing software, platform, underwriting service, or real estate fund.

2.3. **No Reverse Engineering or Replication.** Recipient shall not reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, screen-record, scrape, copy, reproduce, mirror, or capture source code, API responses, or architectural workflows from the Platform or Demonstration.

2.4. **Authorized Disclosures.** Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except strictly to its bona fide officers, directors, investment committee members, legal counsel, and certified public accountants (collectively, **"Representatives"**) who: (i) have a direct "need-to-know" for the Permitted Purpose, (ii) are informed of the confidential nature of the information, and (iii) are bound in writing by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement. Recipient remains fully liable for any breach by its Representatives.

---

### ARTICLE 3: NON-CIRCUMVENTION (REAL ESTATE & DEAL PROTECTION)

3.1. **Non-Circumvention Covenant.** Recipient expressly recognizes that Company invests substantial time, proprietary capital, algorithmic modeling, and relationship capital to source, analyze, negotiate, and structure real estate acquisitions, syndications, and debt facilities. Accordingly, Recipient agrees that for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** following the Effective Date, Recipient and its affiliates shall NOT, directly or indirectly:
- **(a) Direct Deal Sourcing / Bypassing:** Contact, solicit, negotiate with, or enter into any purchase agreement, option, lease, joint venture, or financing arrangement with any property owner, seller, broker, tenant, or lender whose property, identity, or opportunity was disclosed by Company during the Demonstration or within the Data Room, without Company's prior written consent.
- **(b) Commercial Circumvention:** Interfere with, circumvent, avoid, or bypass Company’s interest in any property, deal, syndication, assignment fee, or sponsor promote presented during the evaluation.
- **(c) Personnel Solicitation:** Solicit, recruit, or hire any officer, employee, software engineer, or key contractor of Company who was introduced to Recipient during the evaluation.

3.2. **Liquidated / Remedial Measure.** In the event Recipient breaches Section 3.1 and closes or finances a real estate transaction involving any property or counterparty disclosed by Company without Company's participation, Recipient shall immediately pay to Company a transaction origination and break-up fee equal to **the greater of: (i) 3.0% of the gross transaction purchase price, or (ii) \$25,000.00**, plus any profits realized by Recipient, without prejudice to any other legal or equitable remedies available to Company.

---

### ARTICLE 4: EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1. **Safe Harbor Exclusions.** The obligations of non-disclosure shall not apply to information that Recipient can definitively demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:
- **(a)** is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a wrongful act or breach of this Agreement by Recipient or its Representatives;
- **(b)** was already lawfully in Recipient's possession prior to disclosure by Company without duty of confidentiality;
- **(c)** is lawfully received from an independent third party having the legal right to disclose such information without restriction; or
- **(d)** is independently developed by Recipient without access to, reference to, or reliance upon Company's Confidential Information.

4.2. **Legally Compelled Disclosure.** If Recipient is required by valid subpoena, court order, or regulatory authority to disclose any Confidential Information, Recipient shall provide Company with prompt written notice (where legally permissible) to afford Company the opportunity to seek a protective order or appropriate remedy.

---

### ARTICLE 5: OWNERSHIP OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & NO LICENSE

5.1. **Exclusive Property Rights.** All Confidential Information, source code, agent architectures, DCF models, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and patentable inventions embodied within the Platform and Demonstration are and shall remain the sole and exclusive property of WorldwideBro Holdings LLC.

5.2. **No License or Transaction Obligation.** Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Recipient any license, patent right, copyright, or ownership interest in the Platform. Neither party is obligated under this Agreement to enter into any investment, underwriting agreement, term sheet, or definitive transaction.

---

### ARTICLE 6: TERM AND SURVIVAL

6.1. **Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and shall govern all disclosures made in connection with the Demonstration and ongoing evaluation.

6.2. **Survival.** 
- **(a)** The confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations in Article 2 shall survive for a period of **three (3) years** following the date of disclosure; provided, however, that all **Trade Secrets, proprietary source code, algorithms, and prompt architecture** shall remain confidential **in perpetuity** for so long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.
- **(b)** The non-circumvention obligations set forth in Article 3 shall survive for **twenty-four (24) months** following the Effective Date.

---

### ARTICLE 7: EQUITABLE RELIEF AND REMEDIES

7.1. **Injunctive Relief.** Recipient acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement would cause immediate, severe, and irreparable injury to Company for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Therefore, in addition to all other remedies available at law, Company shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief, specific performance, and temporary restraining orders in any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting bond or other security.

7.2. **Attorneys' Fees.** If any legal action, arbitration, or proceeding is brought for the enforcement of this Agreement or because of an alleged dispute or breach, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees, court costs, and expert witness expenses incurred therein.

---

### ARTICLE 8: GOVERNING LAW, JURISDICTION, AND MISCELLANEOUS

8.1. **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and governed in accordance with the substantive laws of the **State of North Carolina**, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

8.2. **Exclusive Jurisdiction & Venue.** The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive personal jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in **Mecklenburg County, North Carolina (Charlotte)** for any litigation or dispute arising hereunder.

8.3. **Electronic Signatures & Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in two or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one binding instrument. Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic mail (including PDF, DocuSign, Adobe Sign, or other compliant electronic signature) shall be fully effective and legally binding under the **U.S. Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7001 et seq.)** and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA).

8.4. **Severability & Entire Agreement.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be severed or modified to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. This Agreement constitutes the complete and final understanding of the parties regarding the subject matter hereof.
