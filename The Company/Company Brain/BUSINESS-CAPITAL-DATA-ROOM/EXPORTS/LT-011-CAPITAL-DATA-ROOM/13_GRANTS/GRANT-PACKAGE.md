## 1. Executive Summary & Societal Need
Long-haul heavy-duty freight trucks produce over 23% of total U.S. transportation emissions. Over **20.8% of long-haul dry-van miles are driven completely empty**, generating 34 million metric tons of avoidable CO2 emissions annually. 

WorldwideBro Fleet OS LLC is developing an automated **Corridor Load-Chaining & Deadhead Minimization Engine** embedded into CarrierDispatch. By dynamically solving the multi-constraint Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP) across independent owner-operators, this project aims to cut deadhead empty-miles by $\ge 18\%$ along critical interstate freight corridors.
