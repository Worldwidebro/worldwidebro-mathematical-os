# Financial Ecosystem Mapping for Venture OS

**How 5 Ventures Map to Complete Financial Services Ecosystem**

Date: 2026-09-08  
Authority: CP-032 (Business Operations) + CP-020 (Financial Control Plane)

---

## The Insight

The **Business Artifact Ontology** we just built tracks *legal documents* for deal lifecycles.

But underneath every deal is a **financial ecosystem** that determines what capital sources, products, and terms are available.

**Missing piece:** We need to map each venture to:
1. **Financial ecosystem layers** they operate in
2. **Capital products** they can access
3. **Financial documents** required (separate from legal docs)
4. **Regulatory/compliance** frameworks
5. **Infrastructure providers** (KYC, payments, underwriting, etc.)

This document connects those dots.

---

```
FINANCIAL OS
├─ 1. Banking (17 types: Commercial, Investment, Digital, Credit Unions, etc.)

├─ 8. Capital Markets (Exchanges, Bonds, Derivatives, etc.)

├─ 11. Financial Infrastructure (KYC, AML, APIs, Underwriting, etc.)
└─ 12. Financial Marketplaces (Loan, Credit, Insurance, Investment, etc.)
```

## The Complete Picture: Documents × Capital × Regulatory

Now we can see how the **Business Artifact Ontology** (legal documents for deal structure) layers on top of the **Financial Ecosystem** (capital products + terms):

```
VENTURE DEAL LIFECYCLE

Stage 1: Discovery (Identify capital need)
  ↓
  Financial Ecosystem Analysis: What products can this venture access?
  - CON-001 → SBA lending + government grants + equipment finance + surety bonds
  - OPS-001 → Venture capital + venture debt + payment processing
  - LT-005 → Commercial credit facilities + equipment finance + acquisition lending
  - LT-011 → Strategic investment + revenue-based financing + cyber insurance
  - RE-001 → Construction loans + permanent financing + title insurance + appraisal
  ↓
Stage 2: Qualification (Get preapproval)
  ↓
  Financial Documents Prepared:
  - Tax returns, financial statements, business plan
  - Proof of funds, customer contracts, financial models
  - Insurance certificates, compliance documentation
  ↓
Stage 3: Term Sheet (Agree to terms)
  ↓
  Business Artifact Ontology Activated: Legal documents needed
  - NDA, Term Sheet, Purchase Agreement (for equity)
  - Loan Agreement (for debt)
  - SPA, Closing Docs, Officer Certificates
  ↓
Stage 4: Due Diligence (Validate business)
  ↓
  Financial Infrastructure Providers:
  - KYC/AML providers (verify identities)
  - Fraud detection (verify data integrity)
  - Credit bureaus (business credit check)
  - Underwriting engines (risk assessment)
  - Appraisers/valuators (asset assessment)
  ↓
Stage 5: Definitive Docs (Finalize legally)
  ↓
  Regulatory/Compliance Framework:
  - SBA requirements (personal guarantee, UCC searches)
  - HIPAA compliance (healthcare ventures)
  - SOC 2 audit (SaaS ventures)
  - Environmental assessment (real estate)
  - Securities filing (if equity round)
  ↓
Stage 6: Closing (Wire funds)
  ↓
  Financial Infrastructure Execution:
  - ACH setup, wire routing numbers
  - Payment processor configuration
  - Treasury account setup
  - First draw/disbursement
  ↓
Stage 7: Post-Close (Compliance ongoing)
  ↓
  Financial Reporting:
  - Monthly financial statements
  - Regulatory filings (tax, compliance)
  - Covenant monitoring (loan maintenance)
  - Insurance renewals
  - Credit rating updates
```
