## PRESENT STATE: Where Are We Today?

### Company Identity

WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC is a North Carolina-formed staffing and talent operations company operating under the brand "CareerOps." We are organized as a limited liability company in both North Carolina and Delaware, with NAICS codes 561320 (Temporary Help Services) and 561311 (Employment Placement Agencies). Our live platform operates at `https://ops-staff-001-staffing-worldwidebros-projects.vercel.app` with verified production code spanning repositories `Worldwidebro/ops-staff-001-staffing` and `Worldwidebro/career-ops`.

Our mission is to solve the skilled labor shortage by matching non-degree, hourly jobseekers into living-wage roles in construction, healthcare logistics, and technical dispatch. We operate on 35%–37% institutional margins while guaranteeing weekly pay to workers through payroll factoring partnerships.

### Founder & Ownership

Our ownership structure remains in formation. The legal entity has been established with proper Delaware formation, but complete capitalization documentation and shareholder documentation are in progress. This is documented as a material blocker in our capital readiness assessment (see [[OPERATIONAL-REALITY]]). We are working with business counsel to finalize cap table and ownership documentation.

### Problem & Opportunity

We are solving a critical labor market bottleneck: millions of living-wage roles in trade construction, healthcare logistics, and technical dispatch go unfilled for months because legacy staffing agencies rely on manual, biased resume screening and credential-based filtering. Traditional agencies charge 40%–60% markups and take 14–21 days per placement. Non-degree candidates with 80%+ of required job competencies are filtered out by applicant tracking systems that prioritize college degrees over demonstrated capabilities.

The opportunity is substantial—there are hundreds of thousands of unfilled roles annually, each taking weeks to fill through conventional channels, while millions of skilled workers remain unemployed or underemployed.

### Product & Service

CareerOps is a two-sided staffing platform with three core service offerings. First, we operate a candidate evaluation and matching engine built on our proprietary 12-Layer Labor Market Ontology, which scores demonstrated capabilities rather than arbitrary credentials. Second, we provide institutional staffing services to employers (logistics companies, healthcare transport, construction firms) with Net-30 payment terms and 1.5% late interest, operating at 35%–37% gross margins. Third, we guarantee weekly pay to workers through dedicated payroll factoring lines, eliminating the traditional 14-21 day payment cycle.

Our tech stack includes automated candidate file ingestion (500 candidate files in under 5 minutes), 6-dimensional computable fit scoring, and a turnkey contract suite (Master Services Agreement, Worker Contractor Agreement, Rate Schedule Addendum, W-4/I-9 compliance packets).

### Customers & Market

We currently have zero paying customers and zero placements executed. This is our most critical operational blocker: we have 74 high-priority prospects identified in our call list, but zero have been contacted to date. We are pre-revenue and operating at the prospect-qualification stage. Our business model depends on executing our first 3–5 successful placements to demonstrate placement velocity and worker retention, which will then unlock institutional buying from logistics operators and healthcare transport companies.

Our ideal customer profile spans general contractors (construction), logistics operators (warehousing, last-mile delivery), healthcare transport companies (ambulette services), and specialized trade firms requiring hourly skilled labor.

### Market & Competition

We operate in a $150+ billion U.S. staffing market fragmented across thousands of regional agencies. The traditional staffing model—40%–60% markups, 14–21 day payment cycles, credential-biased screening—has remained largely unchanged for decades. Our direct competitors are regional staffing agencies, but our real competitive advantage is our technology-first matching (competency graphs vs. resume keyword matching) and our worker-friendly payment guarantee model (weekly vs. standard 14-21 day cycles).

Our positioning is built on speed (5-minute candidate ingestion vs. days of manual screening) and placement quality (competency-based matching vs. credential filtering).
