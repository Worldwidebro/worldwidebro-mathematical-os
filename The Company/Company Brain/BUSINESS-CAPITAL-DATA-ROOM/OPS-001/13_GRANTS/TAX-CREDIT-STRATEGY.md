# Tax Credit Strategy Memo

**Venture:** WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC (CareerOps)
**Date:** September 21, 2026

## 1. Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) Strategy
As a temporary help services and staffing firm (NAICS: 561320), CareerOps is uniquely positioned to claim the WOTC. 

**Implementation Steps:**
- **Pre-Screening:** Integrate IRS Form 8850 (Pre-Screening Notice and Certification Request) and ETA Form 9061 directly into the automated onboarding flow of the CareerOps platform.
- **Target Groups:** Actively focus our 12-layer labor ontology to match workers from WOTC target groups (e.g., qualified IV-A recipients, qualified veterans, ex-felons, vocational rehabilitation referrals).
- **Filing:** Ensure all Form 8850s are submitted to the North Carolina state workforce agency within 28 days of the employee's start date.
- **Financial Impact:** Claim up to $9,600 per qualified veteran and between $2,400 to $4,000 for other qualified non-degree workers, drastically reducing our effective payroll burden and increasing the 35% markup spread.

## 2. Research & Development (R&D) Tax Credit Strategy
The development of our proprietary software, the 12-layer Labor Market Ontology, qualifies for the federal and North Carolina R&D Tax Credit.

**Implementation Steps:**
- **Qualified Research Activities (QRAs):** Document the engineering hours spent on the "Workforce Sourcing & Ontology AI" and the "Compliance & Verification Gateway".
- **Four-Part Test:** Ensure documentation proves the software development is technological in nature, intended to create new or improved functionality, involves elimination of uncertainty, and relies on a process of experimentation.
- **Payroll Tax Offset:** Given our operating status, utilize the federal R&D credit to offset up to $500,000 of the employer portion of payroll taxes under the PATH Act.
- **Documentation:** Maintain Git commit logs (e.g., verified commit c4d32f1), Jira tickets, and architectural diagrams to substantiate the claim.
