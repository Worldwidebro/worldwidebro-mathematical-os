# Letter of Inquiry to the Department of Labor (DOL)

**Date:** September 19, 2026
**To:** Program Officer, Department of Labor
**From:** WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC (CareerOps)
**Subject:** Letter of Inquiry: Scaling the Labor Market Ontology for Industrial Workforce Placement

Each year, the industrial staffing sector faces a mismatch between worker skills and employer needs. Legacy operators rely on systemic inefficiencies and manual pen-and-paper tracking, creating 8-12% billing disputes and leaving many qualified workers underemployed or subjected to severe wage delays due to working capital drag within staffing agencies.

CareerOps addresses this gap by deploying an automated 12-layer Labor Market Ontology. Unlike traditional temporary help services, our system algorithmically matches worker capabilities to precise industrial requirements while ensuring immediate payroll execution through our embedded factoring systems. This approach eliminates placement friction and guarantees equitable, on-time compensation for industrial workers.

Over our operating history, WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC has achieved significant scale and reliability. Supported by a committed $350,000 payroll factoring facility, we capture high operational margins and project Year 1 gross billings of $1.42 million with a robust 2.95x DSCR. We have successfully placed hundreds of workers while maintaining perfect payroll compliance.

We are requesting $600,000 over 24 months to expand our Labor Market Ontology platform to upskill and place 1,500 displaced or underemployed workers in high-demand industrial roles. This investment will enable us to provide targeted micro-credentials and secure stable, high-wage employment for these individuals.

Given the DOL's commitment to workforce innovation and expanding access to quality industrial jobs, we believe there is strong alignment with our work. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how this partnership might advance our shared goals of economic mobility and labor market efficiency.
