## 1. 1-Page Specific Aims & Program Thesis
Over 62% of U.S. working-age adults lack a 4-year degree and are systematically excluded from living-wage careers by automated keyword screening in legacy Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), even when possessing 80%+ of requisite technical competencies. Simultaneously, high-growth industrial employers face crippling staffing shortages in logistics, technical dispatch, construction project coordination, and clinical support.

WorldwideBro Staffing Ops LLC addresses this systemic inefficiency by deploying **CareerOps**, an algorithmic competency evaluation and workforce operations platform powered by an institutional **12-Layer Labor Market Ontology**.

### Program Objectives & Specific Aims:
* **Aim 1: Algorithmic Competency Extraction & Bias Mitigation (Months 1–3):** Integrate the CareerOps intake portal with local American Job Center (AJC) data feeds. Ingest and profile 500 displaced and hourly workers, translating fragmented work histories into objective, verified competency vectors with zero demographic bias.
* **Aim 2: Micro-Skilling Bridge & Employer Matching (Months 4–12):** Match candidates to living-wage job openings ($22–$38/hr) across regional logistics, healthcare supply chain, and advanced contracting sectors, closing discrete skill gaps with targeted 4-hour micro-skilling modules.
* **Aim 3: Accelerated Placement & Longitudinal Retention Verification (Months 13–18):** Achieve a $\ge 75\%$ job placement rate within 30 days of profile completion (375+ total placements), reducing average hiring cycle time from 21 days to under 4 days, while verifying a $\ge 85\%$ 6-month retention rate.
