# ADR-001: Separation of Living Source Truth from Compiled Deliverables

**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Date:** 2026-09-07

**Context:** In accordance with gstack documentation philosophy, PDFs must never be the single source of truth.

**Decision:** Maintain all source truth in Markdown, YAML, JSON, and OpenXML Excel. Generate PDFs strictly via the Venture Document OS compilation engine.
