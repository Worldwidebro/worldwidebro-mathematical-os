# Letter of Inquiry: NC IDEA SEED Grant

**Date:** September 21, 2026
**To:** NC IDEA Foundation / North Carolina Department of Commerce
**From:** WorldwideBro Fleet OS LLC (CarrierDispatch)
**Re:** Grant Funding for Proprietary Logistics Software Development

## Executive Summary
WorldwideBro Fleet OS LLC (dba CarrierDispatch) respectfully submits this Letter of Inquiry for the NC IDEA SEED grant to support the commercialization and scaling of our proprietary transportation management system. Our software eliminates empty return miles (deadhead) for independent truckload owner-operators, significantly enhancing supply chain efficiency in North Carolina's logistics sector.

## Mission Alignment
Our mission is to "Deliver algorithmic dispatch orchestration and deadhead mileage reduction for independent truckload owner-operators." This aligns directly with NC IDEA's mandate to foster economic development and technological innovation within the state.

## The Technology: DispatchOS
CarrierDispatch integrates real-time load matching with non-recourse embedded factoring. The requested grant funds will be utilized directly for:
- Dispatch Automation & Telematics Integration
- ELD telematics data ingestion
- Automated route optimization algorithms
