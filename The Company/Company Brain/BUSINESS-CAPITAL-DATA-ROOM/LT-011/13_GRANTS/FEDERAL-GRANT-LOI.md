# Letter of Inquiry to the Department of Transportation (DOT)

**Date:** September 19, 2026
**To:** Program Officer, Department of Transportation
**From:** WorldwideBro Fleet OS LLC (CarrierDispatch)
**Subject:** Letter of Inquiry: Eliminating Empty Miles via DispatchOS

Each year, the U.S. freight transportation sector suffers from systemic inefficiencies, generating millions of empty return miles that contribute to unnecessary carbon emissions and highway congestion. Legacy operators rely on manual pen-and-paper tracking, resulting in 8-12% billing disputes and severe working capital drag that prevents small carriers from upgrading to cleaner technologies.

CarrierDispatch addresses this gap by deploying our DispatchOS platform to eliminate empty return miles. Unlike existing freight brokerages, we integrate real-time load matching with non-recourse embedded factoring. This approach ensures carriers are constantly loaded and immediately paid, maximizing fleet utilization and dramatically reducing the carbon footprint of regional freight operations.

Over the past year, WorldwideBro Fleet OS LLC has proven the viability of this model. Our operations currently project Year 1 gross billings of $540,000 with an extraordinary 3.73x DSCR. By solving the working capital and utilization equations simultaneously, we empower owner-operators to run highly efficient, low-emission routes.

We are requesting $500,000 over 18 months to scale the DispatchOS platform across a network of 100 independent regional carriers. This investment will enable us to track, measure, and eliminate an estimated 2 million empty miles, directly reducing greenhouse gas emissions and improving supply chain resilience.

Given the DOT's commitment to optimizing freight networks and reducing transportation-related emissions, we believe there is strong alignment with our work. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how this partnership might advance our shared goals for a sustainable and efficient supply chain.
