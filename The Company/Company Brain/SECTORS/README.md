## 1. Executive Summary

Company Brain organizes the global commercial economy into **35 canonical business sectors** (`SEC-001` through `SEC-035`). Each sector file in this directory establishes:
1. **Industry Scope & Market Dynamics**: Real-world sector classification and macroeconomic drivers.
2. **Venture Pipeline**: Active OpCos and speculative ventures mapped to the sector.
3. **Capabilities & Supply Chain**: Internal capabilities (`CAP-001` to `CAP-300`) and external open-source dependencies.
4. **Control Planes & Automation**: Operational regulatory constraints and dedicated agent swarms.
