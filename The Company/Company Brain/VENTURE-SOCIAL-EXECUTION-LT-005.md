### Post 5: Tip/Insight
**Title:** "The 4 Metrics That Define HIPAA-Compliant Medical Delivery"

Not all couriers meet the same compliance standards. Here's what hospitals actually need:

1. **Chain of custody** (100% documented, auditable, automated)
2. **Temperature integrity** (+2-8°C maintained, logged continuously for biologics)
3. **On-time emergency response** (45-min max for critical lab, blood bank, pathology)
4. **HIPAA audit readiness** (real-time tracking, automated alerts, compliance reports)

Generic couriers fail on all four. Medical-grade couriers are built for all four.

**The gap:** Generic courier + some training = still exposed. Medical-grade courier + built-in compliance = audit-proof.
