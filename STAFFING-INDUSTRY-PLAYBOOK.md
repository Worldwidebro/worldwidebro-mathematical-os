## Business Model

### The Arbitrage

```
STA pays contractor:     $30/hour
STA overhead:            $5/hour
STA cost:                $35/hour

STA sells to CON at:     $40/hour
STA margin:              $5/hour (12.5%)

CON bills client at:     $60/hour
CON margin:              $20/hour (33%)

Total system arbitrage: $30/hour captured across chain
```

### Revenue Model

**Per placement:**
- 1 contractor × 8 hours/day × 22 days = 176 hours/month
- 176 hours × $5 margin = $880/month per contractor

**Profitability:**
```
20 contractors × $880 = $17,600 revenue
Opex: $3,800 (recruiting, tech, admin)
Profit: $13,800 (78% margin)

Breakeven: 5-10 contractors (Month 1-2)
Scale target: 30+ contractors = $26.4K/month (Month 6)
Growth rate: +10 contractors/month
```

## Contractor Funnel

### Stage 1: Recruitment
**Sources:** Indeed ($200), LinkedIn ($300), Referrals ($100/hire), Nextdoor (free), Trade schools

**Target:** 50+ applications by Month 3

### Stage 2: Vetting
**Checks:** Background (Checkr), References (2), Availability, Skills, Reliability
**Pass rate:** 50-70% of applicants

### Stage 3: Matching
**Algorithm:** Skill + Availability + Distance + Rate + Reliability score
**Match success:** 85%+ (contractor accepts offer)

### Stage 4: Execution
**Workflow:** Confirm 24h before → Contractor clocks in → CON supervises → Clock out → Rate & pay

### Stage 5: Retention
**Bonuses:** $100 (10 jobs), $200 (25 jobs), $500 (50 jobs)
**Referral bonus:** $50 per new contractor

## Delegation Network Integration

### How STA Powers CON

```
STA finds contractors (labor supply)
  ↓
CON executes projects (capital deployment)
  ↓
RE manages properties (asset creation)
  ↓
FIN finances it all (margin capture)

Example:
1. CON needs 10 electricians for $5K total labor
2. STA matches 10 contractors, sells to CON at $40/hr (vs $30 cost)
3. CON bills client at $60/hr, captures $20/hr margin
4. Project completes → property handed to RE
5. RE manages property at $2K/month
6. FIN refinances property
7. STA margin: $1K | CON margin: $2K | RE margin: $2K/mo | FIN margin: captured
8. Total system value from $5K labor: $50K+ over 12 months
```

**Result:** More jobs → More margin → More capital → More ventures
