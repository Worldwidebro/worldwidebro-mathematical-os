# Ace Construction: Discord Community Guidelines

**Purpose:** Private owner community ($97+/mo subscribers)  
**Culture:** Owner-to-owner, problem-solving, systemization-focused

---

## CHANNEL STRUCTURE

### #introductions
Intro + one challenge you face  
*"Hi I'm Mike, $1.8M GC, my PM drowns in estimates I should delegate"*

### #estimating-and-pricing
How you estimate, your system, your pricing logic  
Pinned: Owner workflows + examples

### #systemization-wins
"I automated X. My team now does it. Here's how."  
Pinned: Real case studies

### #hiring-and-delegation
Hiring people who think like you (impossible)  
Honesty: can't hire your replacement, have to build a system

### #growth-and-scaling
What happens after you're not the bottleneck  
Revenue, next problems, what's next

### #jobs-and-opportunities
Contractors hiring, owners looking for work

### #off-topic (Tavern)
Construction memes, random thoughts, life

---

## 5 RULES

1. **No sales pitches.** Peer community, not vendor floor.
2. **Specificity wins.** "I have a problem" = ignored. "Estimating takes 4 hours, bottlenecks my PM" = answers.
3. **Honesty first.** "This is hard and I don't know" is welcome. "Just delegate" isn't.
4. **Respect owners.** You built something real. So did they.
5. **Share freely.** What worked for you might work for them (or just the principle will).

## MODERATION

**Actions:**
- Spam/sales → remove + warning
- Vague question → "specific example?"
- Confrontation → "DMs please"
- Repeated negativity → "you doing okay?" check-in

**Tone:** Helpful, not authoritarian.
