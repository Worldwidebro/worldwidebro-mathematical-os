## Channel Breakdown (30-Day Distribution)

### LinkedIn
- **Effort**: 60% of daily outreach
- **Expected**: 150+ messages → 50+ responses → 15-20 calls → 8-12 conversions
- **Best for**: Getting in front of decision makers, building credibility

### Email
- **Effort**: 20% of outreach
- **Expected**: 50 emails → 5-10 responses
- **Best for**: Follow-up to LinkedIn (warm), referrals

### Phone
- **Effort**: 15% (ramps Week 3-5)
- **Expected**: 50 calls → 10-15 conversations → 5-8 conversions
- **Best for**: Warm leads, closing signals

### In-Person / Events
- **Effort**: 5% (Week 4+)
- **Expected**: 1-2 events → 3-5 business cards → 1-2 conversions
- **Best for**: Building relationships, credibility

## Go / No-Go Decision Point (Jun 28)

### Go Criteria (✅ = LAUNCH TO PAID CUSTOMERS)
- ✅ 20+ discovery calls completed
- ✅ 2+ strong signals (4-5 rating) per 4 calls
- ✅ $199/month pricing validated (no major objections)
- ✅ 10+ beta customer commitments
- ✅ $2K+ MRR
- ✅ Product-market fit confidence: HIGH

### No-Go Scenarios (⚠️ = ITERATE)
- ❌ <1 strong signal per 4 calls → Refine messaging
- ❌ Price resistance at $199 → Drop to $149, verify demand
- ❌ <5 beta commitments → Extend discovery calls 2 more weeks
- ❌ <$1K MRR → Scale outreach harder (phone + events)

**If No-Go**: Extend by 2-4 weeks, don't scale paid ads until PMF clear.
