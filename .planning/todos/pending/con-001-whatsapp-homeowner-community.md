# Pilot $50/mo WhatsApp community for con-001 (Ace Construction)

Test the Course+Community stage (from `[[holding-company-100m-roadmap]]`'s 6-stage model) on one real venture before templatizing it across the portfolio.

## What it is

A $50/mo paid WhatsApp community for homeowners, teaching them how to plan, vet, and manage a renovation or new build. Customer-facing (not a meta-venture teaching other contractors) — the goal is trust-building: by the time a member is ready to hire a GC, Ace Construction is the obvious choice.

## Why con-001, why WhatsApp

- con-001 is the closest venture to real (live Supabase, deploy in progress per `[[con-001-income-prep-and-observability]]`) — best candidate to pilot on rather than a purely planned venture
- WhatsApp chosen over Discord/Telegram: broadest reach across homeowner demographics, already the informal channel homeowners use to text contractors, lowest join friction

## Relationship to venture-completion-definition

This is explicitly a go-to-market move, not a substitute for `[[venture-completion-definition]]`. con-001 still needs to independently prove nonzero repeat revenue from its core GC business — the community is a funnel toward that, not a replacement for it.

## Open items before building

- Paid-access mechanism: Stripe checkout → invite link (WhatsApp doesn't have native paid-role tooling like Discord)
- Content: who writes/hosts the "how to plan a renovation" material — con-001 itself, or licensed/templated content reusable across other service ventures later
- con-001's Resend/Cloudflare blocker (per `[[con-001-income-prep-and-observability]]`) is unrelated but still open — doesn't block this pilot since WhatsApp doesn't need that infra
