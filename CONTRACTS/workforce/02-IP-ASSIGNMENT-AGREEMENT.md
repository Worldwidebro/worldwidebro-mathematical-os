## 1. ASSIGNMENT OF IP
Contractor assigns ALL intellectual property created during engagement to Winners Circle WC LLC:

**Includes:**

- Documentation, designs, specifications
- Workflows, processes, systems
- AI prompts, models, training data

- Inventions, discoveries, improvements

**Excludes:**

- Work created outside engagement scope on contractor's time

## 2. WORK-MADE-FOR-HIRE
All work created is "work made for hire" and company owns copyright automatically.

## 3. MORAL RIGHTS WAIVER
Contractor waives all moral rights (attribution, integrity) in assigned IP.

## 4. COOPERATION
Contractor agrees to:
- Sign additional documents to perfect ownership
- Assist in patent/trademark applications
- Testify if needed in disputes
- Not claim ownership in future

## 5. COMPENSATION
IP assignment is consideration for payment. No additional payment for IP.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Contractor keeps all IP and company info confidential during and after engagement.

## 7. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon engagement end, contractor returns all company property, data, equipment.
