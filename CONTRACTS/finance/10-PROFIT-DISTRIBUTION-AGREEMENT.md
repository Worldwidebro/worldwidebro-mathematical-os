# PROFIT DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**Entity:** Winners Circle WC LLC  
**Period:** Annual (Calendar year)  
**Owner:** Antwuan Divine Johns  
**Effective:** [DATE]

## 1. PROFIT DEFINITION
Annual profit = Total revenue - Total expenses
- Revenue: All cash from all ventures
- Expenses: Operating costs, contractor payments, capital allocation to new ventures, taxes

## 2. OWNER DISTRIBUTION
100% of net profit distributed to owner in two forms:

### Option A: Distributions
- Paid quarterly or annually
- Method: ACH transfer, check
- Timing: Within [30] days of quarter/year end

### Option B: Reinvestment
- Owner may elect to reinvest instead of distribute
- Reinvestment capital available for new ventures immediately
- Recorded as owner's additional contribution

## 3. CALCULATION EXAMPLE
```
Total Revenue (all ventures):        $500,000
Less: Operating expenses            -$100,000
Less: Contractor payments           -$150,000
Less: New venture capital allocation -$50,000
Less: Taxes (estimated)              -$50,000
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Net Profit:                         $150,000

Owner share (100%):                 $150,000
Distribute or reinvest:             ✓ $150,000
```

## 4. REINVESTMENT POLICY
If owner reinvests profits:
- Funds available immediately for new ventures
- Tracked as capital contribution
- Increases owner's basis
- Not subject to distribution timing

## 5. LOSS ALLOCATION
All losses absorbed by company. May be:
- Offset against owner's capital contributions
- Carried forward to offset future profits

## 6. TAX IMPLICATIONS
- Owner responsible for estimated quarterly taxes
- Company provides Schedule K-1 annually
- Owner consults tax advisor

## 7. FREQUENCY
- Calculation: Quarterly (at minimum)
- Distribution: Quarterly or annually
- Notice: Company notifies owner of amount due by [10th of month]

## 8. CHANGES
Profit distribution formula may be amended:
- Written amendment signed by owner
- Prospective (applies to future periods only)
- Retroactive only if explicitly stated
