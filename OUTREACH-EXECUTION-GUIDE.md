# Outreach Execution Guide: Top 3 Sectors

**Objective:** Generate 50 qualified leads, close 1-5 deals month 1  
**Timeline:** Starting 2026-05-10  
**Target:** 5 calls/day, 2 demos/week, 1 deal/sector month 1

---

## SECTOR 1: E-COMMERCE (110 ventures available)

### Target Profile
- E-commerce business owners (1-50 people stores)
- Pain point: Multi-channel inventory chaos (Shopify/Amazon/eBay oversells)
- ACV: $2.5K-$5K/month
- Sales cycle: 2-3 weeks

### Venture Matching
- ECOM-001: Multi-Channel Inventory Platform
- ECOM-020: Order Fulfillment Automation  
- ECOM-050: Analytics & Customer Data
- ECOM-075: Marketing Automation

### Week 1 Execution (May 10-16)
```
Day 1-2: Identify 25 E-commerce prospects
  → Search LinkedIn: "ecommerce + owner" OR "shopify + manager"
  → Check target: 10-50 person company, selling on 2+ channels
  
Day 3-5: Cold call script (SECTOR-SPECIFIC-MESSAGING.md)
  "Hi [Name], quick one: How are you managing inventory across Shopify, 
   Amazon, and your website right now?"
  → Document response
  → If pain resonates: "Perfect. Let's do a quick demo this week?"
  → If no: "Who handles operations on your team?" [Warm intro request]
  
Day 6-7: Follow up with 5 demo bookings
  → Email: "ECOM-001: Inventory sync solved [Store name]"
  → Book 15-30 min demo for following week
```

### Success Metric Week 1
- 25 calls attempted
- 3-5 demos booked
- 1 deal moved to negotiation

## SECTOR 2: TECHNOLOGY (90 ventures available)

### Target Profile
- Startup founders, CTOs, tech team leads (2-200 person teams)
- Pain point: Dev velocity (deploys take 30 min, want 3 min)
- ACV: $3K-$8K/month
- Sales cycle: 3-4 weeks

### Venture Matching
- TECH-001: CI/CD Automation Platform
- TECH-030: Development Monitoring
- TECH-050: Infrastructure Management
- SOFT-001: API/Microservices Framework

### Week 1 Execution (May 10-16)
```
Day 1-2: Identify 15 Tech prospects
  → Search LinkedIn: "founder" OR "CTO" + "saas" + location
  → Check target: YC-backed, pre-Series B, in growth mode
  
Day 3-5: Cold call script
  "Hi [Name], I see you're building [product]. Quick question—are you 
   spending more time on deploys than actually shipping features?"
  → If struggling: "That's what we solve. Cut deploy time from 30 min to 3."
  → If not: "Fair. Who's your CTO?" [Warm intro]
  
Day 6-7: Email follow-up to interested prospects
  → Subject: "TECH-001: Deployments automated in 48 hours"
  → Demo video link or calendar
```

### Success Metric Week 1
- 15 calls attempted
- 2-3 demos booked
- 1 deal in discovery

## SECTOR 3: BEAUTY & WELLNESS (41 ventures available, FASTEST CYCLE)

### Target Profile
- Salon owners, spa owners, beauty influencers (1-100 person staff)
- Pain point: No-shows killing revenue (15% loss), manual rescheduling
- ACV: $2K-$4K/month
- Sales cycle: 1-2 weeks (FASTEST)

### Venture Matching
- BW-001: Beauty Booking Platform
- BW-015: Salon Inventory Management
- BW-025: Payment Processing
- BW-035: Client Loyalty Program

### Week 1 Execution (May 10-16)
```
Day 1-2: Identify 15 Beauty prospects
  → Search Instagram/Facebook: "salon owner" OR "spa owner" + local
  → Check: Active social, booking challenges visible in comments
  
Day 3-5: Warm call script
  "Hi [Name], quick question—how much revenue are you leaving on 
   the table because of no-shows?"
  → If YES: "15% is normal. We built a platform that eliminates that."
  → Demo offer: "How's tomorrow for 20-min demo?"
  
Day 6-7: Immediate follow-up
  → SMS to interested: "Here's your booking platform demo link"
  → Frame: "Never manual-book again"
```

### Success Metric Week 1
- 15 calls attempted
- 4-5 demos booked (highest conversion sector)
- 1-2 deals won (fastest cycle)

## ClickUp Pipeline Tracking

**For Each Call:**
1. Log in ClickUp Leads—[Sector] Tier 1 list
2. Fields:
   - Task title: "[Company] — [Sector] — [Venture]"
   - Custom field: venture_matched = BW-001 (or ECOM-020, etc.)
   - warmth_score = 1-10 (10 = hot, answered with pain point)
   - notes = Call outcome, objections, next step
   - due_date = Follow-up date (1 week, then 2 weeks, then 3 weeks)

**Status Progression:**
```
Leads—[Sector] Tier 1
  → Lead calls + initial interest
  → Move to Negotiations—Active Deals (if they want demo or proposal)
  → Move to Closed Deals—Revenue (if they sign contract)
  → Track MRR in metadata field
```

## Daily Execution Checklist

### Morning (30 min)
- [ ] Review ClickUp leads added yesterday
- [ ] Identify 10 new prospects for today (LinkedIn/Facebook search)
- [ ] Add to ClickUp as tasks
- [ ] Schedule follow-up calls for demo-bookers

### Day (4 hours)
- [ ] Make 5 cold calls (60-90 sec each)
- [ ] Document responses in ClickUp (warmth score, next step)
- [ ] Send demo emails to interested (3-5)
- [ ] Hold 2 demo calls (15-30 min each)

### Evening (30 min)
- [ ] Update ClickUp with call results
- [ ] Move deals to Negotiations if needed
- [ ] Schedule tomorrow's calls
- [ ] Note deal probability % for each prospect

## Objection Handling (From SECTOR-SPECIFIC-MESSAGING.md)

**"It's too expensive"**
→ "You're spending [time] doing this manually right now. This cuts that by 50-70%, pays for itself in 2-4 weeks. Can we do a 2-week trial?"

**"We're already using [competitor]"**
→ "Most people using them find they're missing [key feature]. We're built specifically for your use case. Worth 20 minutes to see the difference?"

**"We need to think about it"**
→ "Fair. I'll send you a 5-min demo video. Forward to your [finance] person. Let's reconnect Thursday at 2pm?"

## Deal Close Framework

When they're interested:

```
"Let me confirm I understand:

1. Your main challenge is [pain point], costing you [$ or time]?
2. If we solve that in [timeline], you're interested?
3. Who besides you needs to approve?

[Get answers]

Here's my proposal:
✓ [Feature set specific to their need]
✓ Price: $[monthly cost]
✓ Timeline: [2 weeks to 2 months]
✓ ROI: [specific math from their situation]

Ready to move forward?"
```
