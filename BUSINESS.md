## The Delegation Network

**Core insight:** Ventures don't exist in isolation. They power each other.

```
STAFFING (STA)
    │ (finds contractors, places labor)
    ↓
CONSTRUCTION (CON)
    │ (executes projects, creates job data)
    ↓
REAL ESTATE (RE)
    │ (manages properties, tracks assets)
    ↓
FINANCE (FIN)
    │ (deploys capital, funds ventures)
    ↓
TECHNOLOGY (TECH)
    │ (automates all above)
    ↓
(back to STA)
```

**Each venture generates work for the next.** Margins are captured at the handoffs.

## Business Model: 4 Capital Layers

Revenue compounds through 4 layers:

### Layer 1: Labor Income ($5K-$15K/month)
**How:** Staffing, Construction, Services ventures
- Place contractors → Get margin
- Execute projects → Get fee
- Provide services → Get hourly rate

**Ventures:** STA-001-100, CON-001-050, OPS-001-030

**Timeline:** Month 1-3 (bootstrap)

---

### Layer 2: Skill Monetization ($20K-$30K/month)
**How:** Digital products, SaaS, courses, IP
- Build product once → Sell many times
- Content → Multiple channels
- Templates → License to other OPCOs

**Ventures:** EDU-001-100 (courses), TECH-001-050 (SaaS), IP libraries

**Timeline:** Month 2-6 (parallel with Layer 1)

---

### Layer 3: Asset Acquisition ($24K-$75K/month)
**How:** Buy SMBs, optimize, hold for cash flow
- Find distressed businesses ($50K-$500K EBITDA)
- Improve operations via our OS + automation
- Hold for 34% ROI (passive income)

**Ventures:** Acquisition fund + 10-20 acquired companies

**Timeline:** Month 4-12 (once Layers 1-2 generate capital)

---

### Layer 4: Capital Compounding ($8K-$15K/month)
**How:** Systematic investing, trading, treasury optimization
- Deploy capital from Layers 1-3 into public markets
- Systematic risk/return strategies
- Hedge currency/inflation

**Ventures:** Investment fund, trading desk

**Timeline:** Month 9-12 (once $100K+ capital accumulated)

## How Money Flows

```
Customer Payment
    ↓
Venture Revenue (Layer X)
    ↓
Operating Costs (40% of revenue)
    ├─ Salaries
    ├─ Infrastructure
    ├─ Capital allocation
    └─ Reinvestment
    ↓
Gross Profit (60%)
    ├─ 20% → Reinvest in venture growth
    ├─ 20% → Fund new ventures (Layer 1-2)
    ├─ 10% → Acquisition fund (Layer 3)
    ├─ 5% → Investment capital (Layer 4)
    └─ 5% → Founder / contingency
```

**Rule:** No venture profits leave the system; all capital stays invested.

## Decision Principles

### When to Build vs. Buy
- **Build:** Unique competitive advantage, core differentiator
- **Buy:** Standard SaaS, third-party tools, existing IP
- **Acquire:** Distressed businesses with good unit economics

### When to Delegate
- **Delegate:** Routine tasks, known processes, high volume
- **Don't Delegate:** New decisions, edge cases, customer-facing apologies

### Capital Allocation
- **Never borrow** at >15% APR
- **Always reinvest** 60% of profits
- **Hold cash** only 3 months (rest deployed)
- **Target ROI:** 12-month payback per venture

### Venture Viability
- **Launch if:** Can achieve $1K MRR in 30 days
- **Pause if:** Can't prove $3K MRR in 90 days
- **Acquire instead:** Better to buy $100K revenue business than build it
