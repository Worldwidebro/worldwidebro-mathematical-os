### SBA Microloan — Your #1 Funding Priority

The SBA Microloan is the fastest, most reliable path to capital for where you are right now:

| Detail | What It Means for Up Next |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **Amount** | Up to $50K; average ~$13K — enough for remaining build + 6 months ops |
| **Interest Rate** | 7-13% — cheaper than credit cards, obtainable for startups |
| **Repayment Term** | Up to 6 years — ~$220-400/mo on a $13K loan |
| **Eligibility** | Startups with limited credit history CAN qualify |
| **Decision Time** | 2-6 weeks from application |
| **Target Borrowers** | Women, minorities, veterans, low-income entrepreneurs — higher approval odds |

**What you can use it for:**
- Working capital (pay yourself/contractors to finish build) ✅
- Equipment (laptop, phone for business operations) ✅
- Marketing & stylist outreach ✅
- Legal (LLC docs, terms, privacy) ✅
- Inventory/supplies (business cards, branding) ✅

**What you CAN'T use it for:**
- Purchasing real estate ❌
- Paying off existing debt ❌

**Realistic ask: $13,000-25,000** covering:

| Use | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Complete remaining development | $5,000-8,000 |
| 6 months hosting & services | $1,000-1,800 |
| Marketing & stylist outreach | $2,000-3,000 |
| Legal (LLC docs, terms, privacy) | $500-1,000 |
| Working capital buffer | $3,000-5,000 |

**What you'll need to apply:**
1. Business plan — you already have this (roadmap + pitch docs)
2. Cash flow projections — the revenue targets above cover this
3. Personal tax returns — standard requirement
4. Business license — Winners Circle WC LLC
5. Collateral/personal guarantee — lighter requirements than traditional loans

| **Technical bugs in production** | Medium | Medium | Sentry monitoring, daily error checks, keep it simple — don't add features until core flow is rock-solid |
| **Cash flow negative too long** | Medium | High | Keep day job / other income, apply for grants immediately, cut costs to minimum |
| **Burnout (solo operator)** | High | High | Set realistic weekly hours, hire help in Month 3, automate everything possible |
