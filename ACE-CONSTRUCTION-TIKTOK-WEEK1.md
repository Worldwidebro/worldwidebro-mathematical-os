# Ace Construction: TikTok Scripts (Week 1)

**Goal:** Awareness (burnout angle, not features)  
**Audience:** Construction owners, 35–55  
**Length:** 60 sec vertical  
**Upload:** Mon–Fri 9am ET

---

## MONDAY: "The Trap"

**0–3s:** "I scaled to $2M. Can't leave."

**3–45s:**
- Every estimate needs my approval
- Nobody prices like me
- I can't take vacation
- Built a business that needs ME

**45–60s:** "That's not a business. It's a job."

**CTA:** Link in bio → Dependency Assessment

---

## TUESDAY: "The Paradox"

**0–3s:** "Better business = harder to replace."

**3–45s:**
- Hired good people
- Can't estimate like me
- Can't solve like me
- Still the bottleneck
- Success trapped me

**45–60s:** "Your strength is your ceiling."

**CTA:** Check your Dependency Score

---

## WEDNESDAY: "The Math"

**0–3s:** "You're not working 40. You're working 70."

**3–45s:**
- 40 hours: running business
- 30 hours: only-you decisions
- Estimates, approvals, problem-solving
- Stuck there

**45–60s:** "What's your score?"

**CTA:** Free assessment

---

## THURSDAY: "Not Hiring"

**0–3s:** "Hiring more people won't fix this."

**3–45s:**
- Hiring scales the problem
- They still can't think like you
- Document your process instead
- Turn it into a system
- Your team executes

**45–60s:** "System beats hiring."

**CTA:** See how

---

## FRIDAY: "The Win"

**0–3s:** "One owner took 30 days off. Business didn't miss her."

**3–45s:**
- Estimates still went out
- Proposals still sent
- Revenue stayed same
- First time in 10 years not bottleneck

**45–60s:** "Your business CAN run without you."

**CTA:** See your score

---

**Visual:** Owner to camera, raw audio, text overlay, minimal music

**Metrics:** Views, clicks to assessment, completions, $97 sign-ups
