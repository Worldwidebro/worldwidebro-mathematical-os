[Rep Name]

**Volume**: 20 outreaches/day/rep × 4 sales reps = 80/day

**Volume**: 20 emails/day/rep = 80/day across all campaigns

**Expected Bookings**: 8-16 discovery calls/week

## 📞 Discovery Call Process (Days 1-14: May 13-26)

### Call 1: Task 8.5.A Blocker 2 (May 13-16, Thu)
**Built-in to blocker execution**  
**Targets**: 4 pre-vetted companies  
**Goal**: Validate PMF + document pain points

**Script**:
```
1. Introduction (1 min)
   "Hi [Name], thanks for taking time. We built HRMS specifically for 
   [construction/logistics]. 2 questions: (1) what's your biggest payroll 
   pain right now? (2) what are you currently using?"

2. Current Pain (3 mins)
   Listen: What's broken about their current solution?
   Dig: Monthly spend? Hours spent? Compliance concerns?

3. Solution Fit (3 mins)
   "Here's how we work... [demo HRMS demo]"
   "Does this solve your problem?"

4. Pricing (2 mins)
   "Most companies in your space use our $199/mo Starter or $499/mo Pro."
   "How does that compare to what you're spending now?"

5. Trial Close (2 mins)
   "Want to try it free for 14 days? Takes 15 mins to get your first payroll running."
```

**Call Outcomes**:
- ✅ Wants trial → Book onboarding call (same day)
- ⏳ "Interested, call back in 2 weeks" → Calendar reminder
- ❌ Not interested → Log reason, maybe referral

**Expected Result**: 2-3 trial signups from these 4 calls

## 🎯 Trial-to-Paid Conversion (Days 5-14)

### Trial Structure
```
Day 0: Signup + Payroll Setup (15 min call with CS rep)
  → Customer can run first payroll within 5 mins
  
Day 3: First Check-In
  → "How was payroll setup? Any issues?"
  → Feature deep-dive if interested (direct deposit, time tracking)
  
Day 7: Mid-Trial Follow-Up
  → "What would make you switch from [Current Solution]?"
  → Identify missing features, address objections
  
Day 10: Upsell + Close
  → "Ready to go paid? Here's what $199/mo gets you..."
  → Credit card capture
```
