# LOG-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Logistics Operating System
**Sector Focus:** Transportation, delivery, warehousing, supply chain
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: LOG-Specific Stressors

- Route optimization pressure (time + cost)
- Equipment/vehicle breakdowns (unpredictability)
- Driver management (retention, safety)
- Fuel price volatility (margin impact)
- Regulatory compliance (DOT, insurance)

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

- 10+ active customer accounts
- Driver/operator team (reliable, safe)
- 2-3 peer logistics founders

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Driver Gets Minor Accident**
- Low: Hide it from insurer
- High: Report immediately

**Test 2: Customer Pressure to Over-Load Vehicle**
- Low: Load anyway
- High: Refuse, explain safety limits

**Test 3: Fuel Cost Spike; Temptation to Cut Corners**
- Low: Skip oil changes
- High: Keep schedule, absorb cost

**Test 4: Opportunity to Undercharge Client**
- Low: Send wrong (lower) invoice
- High: Bill accurately

**Test 5: Driver Fatigue; Pressure to Meet Deadline**
- Low: Tell driver to push through
- High: Pause delivery, don't risk safety

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (move goods safely, on time)
- L2-5: Calm under pressure; prioritizes safety
- L6: Reliable scheduling; consistent service
- L7: Deep customer relationships; driver retention
- L8: Innovates routes; improves efficiency
- L9: Safety-first even when costly; honest
- L10: Building safe model; mentoring drivers
