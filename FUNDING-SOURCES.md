# FUNDING SOURCES

How Worldwidebro Holdings bootstraps and funds 712 ventures across 4 capital layers.

---

## Core Funding Strategy

**Rule:** Never borrow at >15% APR. Always reinvest 60% of profits. Bootstrap with Layer 1 (labor).

---

## Layer 1: Bootstrap (Month 1-3)

**Goal:** Generate $5-15K/month from labor arbitrage

**Method:** Revenue (No External Capital)
- Month 1: Place 10 contractors @ $100 margin each = $1K revenue, $500 costs, $500 profit
- Month 2: Reinvest $500 → 20 placements = $2K revenue, $1K costs, $1K profit
- Month 3: Reinvest $1K → Hire recruiter → 40 placements = $4K revenue, $2K costs, $2K profit
- **Target:** $15K/month by Month 3

**Ventures:** STA-001, CON-001, OPS-001

---

## Layer 2: Products (Month 2-6)

**Goal:** $20-30K/month from digital products

**Method:** Layer 1 profits + revenue
- Months 3-4: Build 5 courses, 2 SaaS, 3 templates (invest $5K from Layer 1)
- Months 4-6: Launch & scale (revenue: $50K/mo courses + $5K/mo SaaS + $30K/mo templates)
- **Target:** $20-30K/month by Month 6

**Ventures:** EDU-001-100, TECH-001-050, OPS-003-005

---

## Layer 3: Acquisitions (Month 4-12)

**Goal:** $24-75K/month from SMB acquisitions

**Method:** Accumulated capital pool
- Months 4-8: Accumulate $155K from Layer 1-2
- Months 8-12: Acquire 1-2 SMBs ($100K EBITDA each, buy at 50% = $250K-500K)
- **ROI:** 34% cash-on-cash ($2.8K/mo per acquisition)
- **Target:** $24-75K/month by Month 12

**Ventures:** Acquisition fund + 10-20 acquired companies

---

## Layer 4: Investing (Month 9-12)

**Goal:** $8-15K/month from systematic investing

**Method:** Capital deployment
- $200K+ capital at Month 9
- 60% stocks ($120K @ 8-10% return)
- 30% private credit ($60K @ 12-15% yield)
- 10% alternatives ($20K hedge)
- **Target:** $8-15K/month by Month 12

**Ventures:** Investment fund, trading desk

---

## Per-Venture Funding

**Launch Capital:** $1K-$10K from Layer 1-2 pool
**Structure:** 70% founder equity, 30% company retained (or 20% revenue-share)
**Proof:** Hit $1K MRR in 60 days
**Scale:** Hit $3K MRR at 90 days or pivot
**Success:** $10K MRR by 12 months = $4K/mo profit

Reinvest 60% of profits → fund next ventures

---

## Reinvestment Waterfall (60% of Revenue - Costs)

```
Net Profit (60%)
├─ 20% → Grow this venture
├─ 20% → Fund new Layer 1 ventures
├─ 10% → Acquisition fund
├─ 5% → Investment capital
└─ 5% → Founder + contingency
```

---

## External Capital (Avoid Until Month 12+)

**When to use:**
- Acquisition financing (leverage deal)
- >25% ROI opportunities (rare)
- Strategic partnerships

**When NOT to use:**
- Operating shortfalls
- High rates (>15% APR)
- Dilutes >30%

**Sources (if needed):**
- SBA Loans: 6-8% APR, 5-10 years (Month 6+)
- Venture Debt: 10-12% APR (acquisition only)
- Grants: 0% (non-dilutive, quarterly check)
- Avoid: Credit cards (18-25%), high-rate lenders
