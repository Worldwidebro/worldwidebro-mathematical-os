# DispatchOS Sales Leads & Outreach Guide

This document registers the target prospects, cold call scripts, and outbound call schedules to market and onboard companies onto DispatchOS (LT-011).

---

## 1. Target Customer Profile (SaaS & Brokerage)

1.  **LTL & TL Trucking Companies (20-100 trucks):** High routing complexity, driver retention struggles, dispatcher fatigue.
2.  **Regional 3PL / Freight Brokerages:** Needs fast tender matches, automatic rate confirmation OCR extraction, and real-time tracking integration to protect margins.
3.  **Local Last-Mile / Courier Services:** Relies on manual dispatching and high-frequency phone checks.

## 3. Call Scripts (Outbound Outreach)

### Script A: Target is a Trucking Fleet (Selling SaaS)
*   **Objective:** Book a 15-minute demo of the Dispatch Console and Driver App.

> **Opening (30 sec):**  
> *"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with DispatchOS. We help LTL fleet owners in the Carolinas eliminate manual scheduling and cut fuel overhead using agentic route optimization. Are you still using spreadsheets or legacy boards to coordinate your drivers' daily runs?"*
>
> **Value Prop (60 sec):**  
> *"Our software handles the heavy lifting: it calculates routes automatically, updates ETAs via live GPS, and lets drivers submit digital PODs right from their phones. Most fleets we work with save up to 2 hours per dispatcher every day and increase driver utilization by 15%. Plus, it tracks your profit margins per run automatically."*
>
> **Close (30 sec):**  
> *"I'd like to show you a quick 10-minute walkthrough of the dispatcher console and the mobile driver view this Tuesday at 2 PM. Does that work for you?"*

### Script B: Target is a Freight Brokerage (Selling automation & integrations)
*   **Objective:** Onboard them to utilize the Load Board API.

> **Opening (30 sec):**  
> *"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from DispatchOS. We help regional freight brokers parse rate confirmations instantly and match loads to qualified carriers automatically. Do you handle freight bidding manually at your brokerage?"*
>
> **Value Prop (60 sec):**  
> *"We connect directly to your email flows. When a broker receives a rate confirmation, our system extracts stops, weights, and fuel surcharges, drafts the load, and tenders it to your vetted carrier list in under 60 seconds. It also triggers geofence logs to track driver dwell times, protecting you from manual detention disputes."*
>
> **Close (30 sec):**  
> *"I can set you up with a free trial to parse and tender your first 5 loads this week. Can we schedule a screen-share tomorrow morning at 10 AM to set it up?"*
