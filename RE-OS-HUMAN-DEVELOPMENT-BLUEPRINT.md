# RE-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Real Estate Operating System
**Sector Focus:** Residential/commercial sales, property management, investment
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: RE-Specific Stressors

- Market timing pressure (buy/sell windows)
- Client emotional attachment (high stakes)
- Regulatory complexity (lending, disclosure, zoning)
- Boom/bust cycles (income volatility)
- Relationship management (multiple parties)

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

- 30+ active buyer/seller clients
- Lender/banker relationships
- 2-3 peer RE brokers
- Contractor/inspector network

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Pressure to Hide Property Defect**
- Low: Don't mention it, close deal
- High: Disclose fully

**Test 2: Client Pressure to Misrepresent Appraisal**
- Low: Help them negotiate down
- High: Use fair market value

**Test 3: Multiple Offers; Client Gets Greedy**
- Low: Encourage unreasonable terms
- High: Advise realistic, protect relationship

**Test 4: Market Downturn; Your Commission Drops**
- Low: Discourage selling, keep inventory
- High: Advise clients' best timing

**Test 5: Opportunity to Flip for Personal Profit**
- Low: Don't disclose conflict
- High: Tell client, let them participate

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (steward real estate transitions)
- L2-5: Calm through cycles; sound judgment
- L6: Quick communication; reliable
- L7: Deep network; lenders/brokers call
- L8: Innovates service (virtual tours, tech)
- L9: Transparent about conflicts
- L10: Building client wealth; mentoring agents
