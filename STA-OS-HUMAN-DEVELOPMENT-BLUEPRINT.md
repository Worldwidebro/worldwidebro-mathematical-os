# STA-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Staffing & Talent Operating System
**Sector Focus:** Recruitment, staffing, talent placement
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: STA-Specific Stressors

**Main Stressors:**
- High-volume placements under tight deadlines
- Candidate flaking/no-shows
- Client expectations misalignment
- Rapid scaling + team coordination
- Feast/famine revenue cycles

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

**Tier 1 (Essential):**
- 10+ active employer clients
- 50+ pre-qualified candidate pipeline
- 2-3 peer staffing founders

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Candidate Lies on Application**
- Low: Place anyway, hope nobody finds out
- High: Flag the lie, recommend disqualification

**Test 2: Client Pressure to Bend Criteria**
- Low: Place unqualified candidate for commission
- High: Push back, maintain quality

**Test 3: Candidate-Client Dispute**
- Low: Blame candidate, cut them off
- High: Investigate fairly, mediate

**Test 4: Cash Flow Crisis; Skip Candidate Payment**
- Low: Delay payment, prioritize own bills
- High: Pay candidates on time

**Test 5: Over-Promise Timeline**
- Low: Keep selling; under-deliver
- High: Be honest, manage expectations

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (matching people to opportunity)
- L2-5: Stable under volume; handles rejection
- L6: Responds within hours; reliable follow-up
- L7: Deep employer network; candidates call back
- L8: Improves matching; innovates sourcing
- L9: Honest about fit; fair to both sides
- L10: Building career paths; scaling model
