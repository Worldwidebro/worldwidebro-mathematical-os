# 712-Venture Workforce Planning Analysis
## Complete Staffing Blueprint - What We Need vs. What's Available

**Date:** June 6, 2026  
**Scope:** All 712 ventures across 31 sectors  
**Purpose:** Identify staffing needs, market availability, funding gaps, and role design specs  
**Outcome:** Build a staffing strategy to operationalize all 712 ventures

---

## 📊 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### The Challenge
- **712 ventures** exist in system, but most are **not yet staffed**
- Each venture **needs 3-20 people** to operate (depending on sector)
- **Total headcount needed:** ~3,500-4,500 people
- **Total payroll needed:** $280M-$350M/year
- **Market available:** ~347 people (from our job market research)
- **GAP:** 3,150+ unfilled positions

### The Opportunity
This gap represents a **$280M+ staffing agency market opportunity** within our own ecosystem.

### The Solution
1. **Identify all 712 venture staffing needs** (what we need)
2. **Map to Charlotte/Arizona job market** (what's available)
3. **Fund the gap through grants + staffing agency** (how to fill)
4. **Create the roles we need** (design specs)

## 🎯 SECTION 3: STAFFING AGENCY VENTURE (The Capital Engine)

### Why It's Critical

**The Reality:**
- Grants fund 50-100 people/year maximum
- We need 3,163 people
- At grant-only pace: 32+ years to full staffing
- **We can't wait 32 years**

**The Solution:**
Build a self-sustaining staffing agency that:
1. Generates profit from placements
2. Uses profit to fund hiring beyond grants
3. Compounds over time
4. Reaches full staffing in 3-4 years instead of 32

### Business Model: Revenue → Funding Loop

```
YEAR 1:
- 100 placements @ 20% commission = $1.6M revenue
- Less costs = $1.05M profit
- Use $1.05M to fund 20-30 additional hires beyond grants
- Total headcount placed: 135-150

YEAR 2:
- 300 placements = $4.92M revenue
- Less costs = $3.37M profit
- Use $3.37M to fund 70-75 additional hires
- Total headcount placed: 285-310 (beyond grants)
- Cumulative: 420-460 total placed

YEAR 3:
- 500 placements = $8.5M revenue
- Less costs = $4.7M profit
- Use $4.7M to fund 90-100 additional hires
- Total headcount placed: 335-345 (beyond grants)
- Cumulative: 755-805 total placed
```

### The Compounding Effect

**Staffing Agency Profit Funds Growth:**
- Year 1 profit ($1.05M) funds 20-30 extra hires
- Year 2 profit ($3.37M) funds 70-75 extra hires
- Year 3 profit ($4.7M) funds 90-100 extra hires
- **Total extra people funded by agency profit: 180-205**

Without staffing agency (grants only): 345 people by Year 3  
With staffing agency: 525-630 people by Year 3  
**Difference: 180-285 more people (52-83% faster)**
