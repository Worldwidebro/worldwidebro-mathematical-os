## LIFECYCLE STAGE DEFINITIONS

**Stage 00 — DISCOVERY:** Opportunity identified, market signals validated  
**Stage 01 — RESEARCH:** Evidence gathered, problem confirmed, customer interviewed  
**Stage 02 — VALIDATION:** Prototype tested, pricing tested, MVA confirmed  
**Stage 03 — BUSINESS-DESIGN:** Business model documented, revenue model designed  
**Stage 04 — ENTITY-FORMATION:** Legal entity formed, banking set up, contracts signed  
**Stage 05 — CAPITALIZATION:** Capital raised, deployed to venture  
**Stage 06 — BUILD:** Product/service built, infrastructure deployed, agents wired  
**Stage 07 — ORGANIZATION:** Team hired/agents deployed, roles defined, workflows live  
**Stage 08 — LAUNCH:** Go-live gates passed, system live, first customer onboarded  
**Stage 09 — CUSTOMER-ACQUISITION:** Lead funnel running, sales pipeline active  
**Stage 10 — SALES:** Contracts signed, revenue recognized  
**Stage 11 — FULFILLMENT:** Orders delivered, customer satisfaction measured  
**Stage 12 — BILLING:** Invoices issued, payments collected  
**Stage 13 — CUSTOMER-SUCCESS:** Support active, retention > 90%, NPS measured  
**Stage 14 — OPERATIONS:** Processes standardized, capacity meets demand, QA gates pass  
**Stage 15 — FINANCIAL-MANAGEMENT:** P&L tracked, margins calculated, cash flow forecasted  
**Stage 16 — OBSERVABILITY:** Events logged, metrics collected, dashboards live  
**Stage 17 — OPTIMIZATION:** Bottlenecks identified, improvements deployed, velocity improved  
**Stage 18 — SKEPTIC-RED-TEAM:** Decisions challenged, assumptions tested, risks identified  
**Stage 19 — SCALING:** Capacity expanded, new markets entered, multi-channel  
**Stage 20 — PORTFOLIO-MANAGEMENT:** Venture re-evaluated vs. portfolio, capital optimization
