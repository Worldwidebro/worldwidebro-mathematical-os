# Ace Construction: Brand Voice & Positioning

**Positioning:** Stop estimating. Start systemizing.

From: "Software that estimates faster"  
To: "The AI that turns your dependency-on-you into a business that runs without you"

---

## Who We Talk To

**Primary:** Construction business owners (GCs, electricians, HVAC, plumbing)  
Age: 35–55, Revenue: $500K–$5M/year  
**Pain:** Burnout. Can't scale because they can't delegate. Every project needs them.  
**Goal:** Sell the business, not work in it.

---

## Core Belief

You're not running a construction company. You ARE the construction company. That's not a business—it's a job with benefits.

Ace systemizes your knowledge so the business runs without you.

---

## Tone

**Honest.** Real problems, real solutions. No startup speak.  
**Direct.** Construction language. Respect the grind.  
**Confident.** We know your business.  
**Ownership-focused.** Built-by-someone-who-builds.

---

## What NOT to Say

❌ "Streamline workflows"  
❌ "Increase efficiency"  
❌ "Cloud-based solutions"  
❌ "Synergize your team"

## What to Say

✅ "Stop being the bottleneck"  
✅ "Your knowledge doesn't leave when you do"  
✅ "Hire for execution, not judgment"  
✅ "Run it from a beach"  
✅ "Sell the business, not yourself"
