## Market Overview

### Market Size & Opportunity
- **Total addressable market:** $2T+ (global construction)
- **US market:** $2.3T annually
- **Growth rate:** 3-5% annually
- **Margin potential:** 20-35% (project markup on labor + materials)

### Who Are the Customers?
1. **Property owners** (residential, small commercial) — Want renovations, repairs
2. **General contractors** (larger GCs) — Subcontract work out
3. **Property developers** (RE ventures) — Build projects
4. **Facilities managers** (OPS) — Maintenance + upgrades
5. **Commercial chains** (hospitality, retail) — Multiple locations needing work

## Business Model

### The Project Markup

```
Labor cost (via STA-001):     $3,000 (10 electricians × $30/hr × 8 hrs × 2 days)
Material cost (from suppliers): $2,000
Operating cost (15%):         $750
Total cost:                   $5,750

Client billing:               $8,000
Gross profit:                 $2,250 (28% margin)
  └─ STA margin: $1,000 (labor arbitrage)
  └─ CON margin: $1,250 (project execution)
```

### Revenue Model

**Per project:**
- Small (1-2 days): $2K-5K revenue, $1K-2K margin
- Medium (3-5 days): $5K-15K revenue, $1.5K-4K margin
- Large (1-4 weeks): $15K-50K revenue, $4K-15K margin

**Scale to profitability:**
```
4 projects/month × $2K margin = $8,000 revenue
Opex (crew leads, permits, tools): $2,300/month
Net profit: $5,700 (71%)

Breakeven: 2 projects/month (Month 1)
Target: 6-8 projects/month (Month 6) = $12K-16K revenue
Growth: +1-2 projects/month
```

## Sales Process

### Lead Sources
1. **Direct outreach** (20%) — Walk neighborhoods, property managers
2. **Job boards** (30%) — Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Facebook groups
3. **Referrals** (50%) — Past customers, word of mouth

### Sales Funnel
```
Month 1: 20 leads → 2 projects (10%)
Month 3: 60 leads → 6 projects (10%)
Month 6: 100+ leads → 8 projects (8%) — referral driven
```

### Qualification
Only pursue if:
- >$2K project scope
- Ready to start <30 days
- Clear decision maker

### Bidding Process
1. Site visit (photos, measurements)
2. Labor quote from STA-001
3. Material quotes from suppliers
4. Add 25-35% markup for profit
5. Send formal bid

**Example bid: $7,500 total ($2K materials + $4K labor + $1.5K markup)**

## Project Execution

### Stage 1: Crew Assembly (via STA-001)
1. CON requests: "Need 5 electricians for 10 days"
2. STA matches contractors from pool
3. CON approves crew (ratings, experience)
4. Crew shows up Monday 8am

**Cost:** $4,000 (STA charges $40/hr)  
**STA margin:** $1,000 (labor arbitrage)

### Stage 2: Daily Execution
- Crew logs time (app or text)
- CON supervisor checks quality
- Daily progress photos
- Weekly payment to STA

### Stage 3: Quality Checks
- Work matches bid scope
- Materials specs correct
- OSHA safety compliance
- Daily cleanup

### Stage 4: Completion
- Final inspection passed
- Customer sign-off
- Invoice sent
- STA paid
- Profit calculated

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Project overruns | Accurate scoping, timeline buffer, contingency fund |
| Crew no-shows | STA backup, daily check-ins |
| Material delays | Pre-order, supplier relationships |
| Safety incidents | Insurance, OSHA training, daily briefing |
| Customer disputes | Clear scope, progress photos, sign-offs |
| Unpaid invoices | Upfront deposit, progress payments, net 15 |
