# CON-OS Human Development Blueprint

**OPCO:** Construction Operating System
**Sector Focus:** General contracting, subcontracting, trade services
**Avg Founder Tier:** 70-79% MONITORED

## Layers 2-5: CON-Specific Stressors

**Main Stressors:**
- Tight deadlines + weather delays → rushing → mistakes
- Cash flow: paid on completion, not progress
- Supplier shortages + cost volatility
- Scope creep + client indecision
- Team management under pressure

## Layer 7: Critical Relationships

**Tier 1 (Essential):**
- 3-5 reliable subcontractors (trades)
- Material suppliers
- 2-3 peer GCs (referral network)

## Layer 9: Character Tests

**Test 1: Structural Issue Found**
- Low: Hide it, fix quietly
- High: Tell client immediately, take responsibility

**Test 2: Customer Doesn't Pay**
- Low: Escalate to attorney
- High: Negotiate, preserve relationship

**Test 3: Crew Safety Corner**
- Low: Look the other way
- High: Stop work, retrain, hold line

**Test 4: Substandard Material**
- Low: Use it, hope nobody notices
- High: Reject it, maintain quality standard

**Test 5: Underbid Job Realizes Loss**
- Low: Skip work, do bare minimum
- High: Eat the loss, finish right

## Success Profile

- L1: Clear mission (craft + business)
- L2-5: Stable under pressure; safety calls
- L6: Shows up; pays on time; consistent quality
- L7: Subs want to work for him; peers call for advice
- L8: Improves process; shares learning
- L9: Tells truth; holds safety line
- L10: Building quality legacy; mentoring subs
