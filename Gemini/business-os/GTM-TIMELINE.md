# Go-To-Market (GTM) Timeline

This document maps the phased outreach and fundraising timeline for the holdings launch.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & Soft Launch (Weeks 1-3)
*   **Goal**: Establish a credible web presence and launch local B2B lead generation.
*   **Actions**:
    *   Deploy the 30-page website (Next.js/Tailwind).
    *   Configure custom domain mailboxes (`name@worldwidebro.com`).
    *   **Partnership Outreach**: Initiate email sequences targeting local Charlotte general contractors and property managers.
*   **KPIs**: 50 meetings booked; 10 contractor agreements signed.

---

## Phase 2: Proof of Concept & Case Studies (Weeks 4-6)
*   **Goal**: Document and publish empirical validation of the delegation network.
*   **Actions**:
    *   Run the live `CON-001` → `STA-001` → `RE-001` loop.
    *   Reconcile real-time revenues in Supabase; capture sanitized screenshot mockups.
    *   Publish case studies showing transaction velocity.
*   **KPIs**: 3 completed delegation runs; $15K+ in captured margin; 1 published case study.

---

## Phase 3: Investor Pitching (Weeks 7-9)
*   **Goal**: Secure pre-seed capital and strategic joint venture LOIs.
*   **Actions**:
    *   Incorporate real traction numbers into the 10-Slide Pitch Deck.
    *   Initiate warm intros to Prop-Tech VCs, AI-infrastructure funds, and Charlotte angel networks (e.g. Queen City Angels).
*   **KPIs**: 15 investor pitches; 3 term sheets / LOIs.

---

## Phase 4: National Scale (Months 3-6)
*   **Goal**: Expand to Tier 2/3 sectors and scale national operations.
*   **Actions**:
    *   Launch Commerce and Media sector pages.
    *   Run targeted ads on LinkedIn/Meta to GCs and developers.
    *   Deploy the automated "Partner Portal" for self-serve vendor onboarding.
*   **KPIs**: 50+ active ventures; $100K+ MRR; weekly delegation velocity > 50.
