## 2. Human Operator Onboarding Protocol

When hiring a Virtual Assistant (VA) or Operations Contractor, provide them with this sequence to assume control of the daily loops. All operators must be put through the master [Shared Employee Onboarding Checklist](file:///Users/acebless/Documents/Gemini/business-os/STANDARDIZED-OPERATIONAL-FRAMEWORK.md#1-shared-employee-onboarding-all-ventures) and their industry's respective onboarding checklist before starting live work.

### Phase 1: Environment Setup
1. **Access Portals**: Grant view/edit access to the VEX dashboard, Supabase portal, and Neo4j Browser.
2. **Setup Communications**: Add the operator to the email channel (`winnerscirclewcllc@gmail.com`) and communications hub.
3. **Repository Map**: Instruct the operator to review [CODE-ORGANIZATION-MAP.md](file:///Users/acebless/Documents/Gemini/CODE-ORGANIZATION-MAP.md) to locate active codebase directories.

### Phase 2: Daily Operations Check (08:00 - 10:00 EST)
1. **Review Delegation Queue**: Check the VEX portal at `/network/delegation/queue` for any Level 2 (Human-in-the-Loop) approval requests.
2. **Review Compliance Flags**: Verify background checks and licensing statuses for matched contractors on [vetting-agent.md](file:///Users/acebless/Documents/Gemini/agents/staffing/vetting-agent.md).
3. **Process Invoices**: Check accounting dashboard for outstanding AR/AP approvals.

## 3. Human Handoff Runbook (SOP for First Construction Deal)

For the active pilot venture (`Winners Circle WC LLC` in Charlotte, NC), the operator executes the following checklist:

### Step 1: Binder Insurance
- Quote, bind, and download a General Liability certificate of insurance ($1M occurrence limit) via **NEXT Insurance** or **Thimble**.

### Step 2: Capability Statement
- Fill out the phone and insurance fields on the template, then compile to `WinnersCircleWC-Capability-Statement.pdf`.

### Step 3: Portal Registrations
- Register the company as a subcontractor on the free databases: **SYNCON**, **BuildingConnected**, and **PlanHub**.

### Step 4: Outbound Calls
- Call prime estimating teams (e.g. HICAPS at `800-498-9577`) requesting prequalification. Submit the capability PDF immediately after.

### Step 5: Escalate
- Escalate to the human principal when a bid invitation (ITB) arrives, or when signatures and cash transfers are required.
