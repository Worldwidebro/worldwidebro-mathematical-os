# Capital Compounding Engine

This document defines the reinvestment and capital allocation algorithms used by the holding company to scale from $1M to $100M ARR.

---

## 1. Reinvestment Hierarchy

Cash flows generated by active ventures are swept weekly and allocated based on ROI priorities:

```
                  Consolidated Capital Pool (WinnersCircleWCLLC)
                                         │
             ┌───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┐
             ▼                           ▼                           ▼
    [ Priority 1: High ROI ]     [ Priority 2: Growth ]      [ Priority 3: Seed ]
     ROI > 30% (alloc: 60%)       ROI 15-30% (alloc: 25%)     New projects (alloc: 10%)
```

- **Emergency Reserves**: 5% of swept capital is retained in cash reserves.

---

## 2. Compounding Engines

### The M&A Acquisition Engine
At Stage 3 ($10M+ ARR), buying cash-flowing brick-and-mortar firms becomes faster than building. The acquisition sequence:
1. Identify target service businesses (accounting, staffing, logistics) in Charlotte, NC.
2. Value targets at 3-5x revenue.
3. Fund acquisitions using internal capital (via `fund-001`).
4. Integrate the acquired firm's back-office and technology into `OPS` and `TECH` sectors (instantly cutting overhead by 20-30%).

### The RBF Underwriting Engine
Deploy capital to e-commerce and retail ventures (`COMMERCE` sector) via Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) at 6-10% interest rates, securing cheap capital for ventures and high-yield returns for the treasury.
