# Standardized Operational Framework
*Worldwidebro Holdings AI Operating System*

This document defines the repeatable operational structures, onboarding checklists, customer acquisition campaigns, and AI agent matrices for the core operating companies under Worldwidebro Holdings.

---

## 1. Shared Employee Onboarding (All Ventures)
This pipeline is identical across every operating company under the portfolio to ensure a consistent corporate culture, operational standard, and tool usage.

### Day 1: Welcome & Admin Setup
*   [ ] **Welcome Meeting**: Align on mission, values, and department structure.
*   [ ] **Payroll & HR Setup**: Fill I-9/W-4, direct deposit details, and sign employment contract.
*   [ ] **Handbook & Policies**: Sign employee handbook, NDA, and confidentiality agreements.
*   [ ] **Accounts Provisioning**: Issue company email and primary login credentials.

### Day 2: Tooling & SOPs Orientation
*   [ ] **Communication Standards**: Set up Slack/Teams, review channel etiquette, and response SLAs.
*   [ ] **CRM Training**: Initialize CRM workspace and walkthrough logging interactions.
*   [ ] **Calendar & Scheduling**: Sync calendar settings and schedule weekly team catch-ups.
*   [ ] **SOP Walkthrough**: Read core playbooks and operational matrix guidelines.

### Day 3: Shadowing & Practical Exercises
*   [ ] **Shadow Session**: Active observation of an experienced team member or simulated run.
*   [ ] **Practical Exercises**: Complete role-specific mock operations (e.g. entry, tracking, or support).
*   [ ] **Quality Standards**: Review key performance indicators (KPIs) and compliance criteria.

### Week 1: Vetting & Certification
*   [ ] **Role Proficiency Check**: Run through the sector-specific checklist under supervision.
*   [ ] **Self-Guided Work**: Take on initial low-risk operational items (Level 1 Authority).
*   [ ] **Manager Approval**: Receive sign-off from operations lead.

### First 30 Days: Integration & Feedback
*   [ ] **Weekly Coaching**: Scheduled meetings to review progress and answer questions.
*   [ ] **KPI Reviews**: Verify candidate/lead metrics are on track.
*   [ ] **Feedback Loops**: Collect operator suggestions to optimize workflows.

## 5. Recommended HR OS Tool Stack

For near-term execution, we utilize a highly automated SaaS tool stack. All workflows are integrated using **n8n** and logged in **Supabase**:

*   **Forms & Client Intake**: **Jotform** (supports e-signatures, conditional routing, PDF generation, and HIPAA-compliant data fields).
*   **SOPs & Knowledge Base**: **Notion** / **Obsidian** (accessible locally for agents, synched via markdown).
*   **Signatures & Contracting**: **Jotform Sign** (direct integration with intake flows).
*   **Applicant Tracking (ATS)**: **Workable** or **Ashby** (for recruiters and staffing agencies).
*   **Training & LMS**: **Trainual** or **TalentLMS**.
*   **Payroll & HR Administration**: **Gusto** (for employees and contractors).
*   **Background & Driving Screening**: **Checkr** (for courier DMV MVR checks and staffing background reviews).
*   **Orchestration & Database**: **n8n** + **Supabase** + **Neo4j**.

## 5. Implementation Rules
1. **Zero-Cost Priority**: All tools must use open-source or free-tier APIs where available to avoid operational overhead.
2. **Authority Limits**:
   * **Level 1 (Autonomous)**: Executing routine outreach, scraping, scheduling, and standard contract generation.
   * **Level 2 (Human-in-Loop)**: Client proposals >$5k, final hire approvals, issuing custom invoices.
   * **Level 3 (Strategic)**: Pivot decisions, budget allocation >$50k, launching new territory nodes.
