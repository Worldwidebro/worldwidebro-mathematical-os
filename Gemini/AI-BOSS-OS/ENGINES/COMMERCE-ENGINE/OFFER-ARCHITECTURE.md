# Offer Architecture: Product Bundling & Pricing Models

This document outlines how digital products, templates, SaaS, and services are structured to maximize customer lifetime value (LTV) and average order value (AOV).

---

## 1. Offer Category Frameworks

We structure products under four strategic monetization tiers:

### A. Tier 1: Free Lead Magnets (The Hook)
*Goal: Acquire email addresses at scale.*
- **Examples**: Free Prompt Packs (10 AI Prompts to run a company), Obsidian Vault Config markdown files, custom n8n JSON workflows.
- **Conversion Goal**: > 50% landing page conversion.

### B. Tier 2: Low-Ticket Core Offers ($29 - $49)
*Goal: Convert attention into paying customers and offset acquisition overhead.*
- **Examples**: "AI Business Operating System Notion Dashboard", "Obsidian Workspace Template", "SOP Playbook Generator Templates".
- **Conversion Goal**: > 2% from email signup to purchase.

### C. Tier 3: Medium-Ticket Systems ($199 - $299)
*Goal: Provide complete infrastructure assets.*
- **Examples**: "AI Venture Spawner Blueprint & Code Templates", "Complete Financial Control Dashboard Database", "Developer Operations System OS".
- **Conversion Goal**: > 10% upsell from Tier 2 buyers.

### D. Tier 4: High-Ticket Enterprise Services ($2,000+)
*Goal: Custom implementations and systems orchestration.*
- **Examples**: Custom local model (Ollama) infrastructure setups, Tailwind-free custom dashboard systems, private Tailscale routing clusters.
- **Conversion Goal**: Enterprise lead capture routed directly to CEO Brain.

---

## 2. Bundling Playbook

To increase average order value, checkout flows dynamically bundle complementary products:
- **Core Product**: Notion AI OS Template ($29).
- **Order Bump**: 1,500 Agent Skills Prompt Library (Add $15).
- **Post-Purchase Upsell**: Venture Spawner Setup video course (Add $99).
- **Resulting AOV**: Projected increase from $29 to $62.
