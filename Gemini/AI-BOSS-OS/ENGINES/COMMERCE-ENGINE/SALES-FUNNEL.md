## 2. Automated Welcome & Nurture Sequences

Once a lead registers for a free asset, our `Sales Agent` triggers the following automated email campaign over 5 days:

- **Day 1: Deliver & Delight**
  - *Content*: Send direct link to download the free asset. Do not sell anything yet. Build trust.
- **Day 2: Behind-the-Scenes Build**
  - *Content*: Show how the asset fits into the broader AI Boss OS. Include a screen recording showing real results.
- **Day 3: The Pain & The Opportunity**
  - *Content*: Outline the friction of building manually vs buying our validated template. Present a 48-hour discount code (e.g. 20% off) for the Tier 2 template.
- **Day 4: Case Study & Proof**
  - *Content*: Showcase another builder/venture using the template to generate revenue.
- **Day 5: Final Call**
  - *Content*: Urgency email. Expiring coupon code. Redirect to affiliate SaaS if they decide not to buy.
