# Customer Journey: The Attention-to-Revenue Lifecycle

This document defines the phases a user experiences as they interact with our media and commerce engine, moving from a passive viewer to a long-term enterprise client.

---

## 1. Journey Stage Definitions

Our customer lifecycle is mapped into 7 distinct progressive steps:

```text
Attention ➔ Trust ➔ Community ➔ Leads ➔ Customers ➔ Revenue ➔ Data
```

### Stage 1: Attention (Top of Funnel)
- *User Behavior*: Scrolls TikTok/YouTube, stops at a 3-second visual pattern interrupt showing our Mac Studio Node or n8n workflow graph.
- *Goal*: Stop-scroll, watch past 15 seconds, and click the creator's profile page.

### Stage 2: Trust (Value Proof)
- *User Behavior*: Views profile, notes consistent "Building in Public" documentation, and downloads a free prompt pack.
- *Goal*: Build credit through clean, high-value code, templates, or workflows that work on first run.

### Stage 3: Community (Engagement Space)
- *User Behavior*: Joins our Discord or reads the weekly newsletter.
- *Goal*: Interactive discussions, resolving setup issues, and matching them to target templates.

### Stage 4: Leads (Sensing Profile)
- *User Behavior*: Opts-in for advanced templates, updates profile data, and logs their sector interests (e.g. Finance, Technology).
- *Goal*: Capture detailed demographic and utility metrics.

### Stage 5: Customers (Initial Transaction)
- *User Behavior*: Purchases a $29 Notion AI OS template.
- *Goal*: Smooth digital delivery, excellent onboarding documentation, and prompt follow-up.

### Stage 6: Revenue (LTV Compounding)
- *User Behavior*: Upgrades to SaaS platform subscription or purchases the Venture Spawner.
- *Goal*: High retention, low churn, and automated affiliate payout tracking.

### Stage 7: Data Feedback Loop
- *User Behavior*: Submits questions, reviews, or requests custom features in comment sections or community channels.
- *Goal*: Capture signals to feed back into `PRODUCT-DISCOVERY` to build better content and spin up the next set of ventures.
