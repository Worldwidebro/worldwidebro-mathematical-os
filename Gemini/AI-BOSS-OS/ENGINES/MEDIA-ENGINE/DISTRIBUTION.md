# Distribution: Omnichannel Repurposing & Routing

This document outlines the protocols for taking a single proven piece of organic media and distributing it across the entire Worldwidebro brand footprint to maximize organic reach and compound traffic.

---

## 1. Repurposing Matrix

Every video generated by our TikTok Engine is logged and analyzed. Once a video passes the **"Viral Threshold"** (>100k views, >10% engagement), it is automatically scheduled for omnichannel distribution.

```text
TikTok Organic (Source of Truth)
   │
   ├──➔ YouTube Shorts (Re-title for Search Optimization)
   ├──➔ Instagram Reels (Tailor hashtags and description format)
   └──➔ Newsletter Editorial (Convert script to deep-dive text article)
```

---

## 2. Platform Customization Guides

### A. YouTube Shorts
- **Optimization Strategy**: Focus on search intent. YouTube is a search engine; title must use searchable search queries.
- **Conversion Mechanism**: Pin a comment with the exact redirect URL to the lead magnet. Use the "Related Video" link to route viewers to long-form video content.
- **Hashtags**: Limit to 3 tags maximum.

### B. Instagram Reels
- **Optimization Strategy**: Focus on shares and saves. Reel captions can be longer than TikTok. Write 3-4 paragraphs of breakdown context.
- **Conversion Mechanism**: Use "Comment 'OS'" automations (e.g. ManyChat integration) to send the direct link to the user's DMs.
- **Hashtags**: Use 5-8 niche tags.

### C. Newsletter Editorial
- **Optimization Strategy**: Deep-dive value. Take the core insight of the 60-second video and expand it into a 500-word newsletter issue.
- **Conversion Mechanism**: Embedded product links, captable updates, and community invitations.
- **Distribution**: Emailed to our list using the Newsletter Engine.
