# Content Calendar: Weekly Editorial Calendar & Schedule

This document outlines the standard batching structure, publishing schedule, and posting times for the Worldwidebro creator network.

---

## 1. Weekly Batching Cycle

To maximize output without creative burnout, our agents run on a 7-day batching cadence:

| Day | Phase | Active Agent | Deliverable |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Monday** | Trend Scan & Selection | `Trend Hunter Agent` | Compiled list of 15 trending topics and angles. |
| **Tuesday** | Script Writing | `Script Agent` & `Hook Agent` | 10 structured scripts with 3 hook variations each. |
| **Wednesday** | Media Generation | `Video Agent` & `Voice Agent` | Audio generation, B-Roll assets, and initial renders. |
| **Thursday** | Editing & Assembly | `Editing Agent` | Final edited videos with burned captions and audio tracks. |
| **Friday** | Quality Assurance | `Compliance Agent` | QA checklist verified; metadata, tags, and titles ready. |
| **Saturday** | Batch Uploads | `Publishing Agent` | Videos scheduled in scheduling queue. |
| **Sunday** | Analytics Review | `Analytics Agent` | Weekly metrics scorecard compiled and routed to CEO brain. |

---

## 2. Daily Posting Cadence (EST Times)

Our historical audience analytics indicate the following peak engagement windows. Publishing agents schedule uploads to hit these slots:

- **Slot 1: Morning Coffee (08:30 AM)**
  - *Format*: Industry news, quick productivity hacks, lifestyle entrepreneur motivation.
  - *Goal*: High hook rate, saves, and quick shares.
- **Slot 2: Lunch Break (12:15 PM)**
  - *Format*: Behind-the-scenes tool setup, coding tips, workflow showcases.
  - *Goal*: High retention and comments.
- **Slot 3: Evening Scroll (06:00 PM)**
  - *Format*: Heavy business lessons, long-form narratives, big "Building in Public" reveals.
  - *Goal*: Profile clicks, lead magnet conversions, and community joins.

---

## 3. Dynamic Calendar Framework

Our dashboard connects to this calendar via a REST API to showcase scheduled scripts and publish status. In-progress items must always specify their assigned pillar, target product, and video URL.
