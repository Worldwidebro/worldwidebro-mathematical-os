# Brand Voice Guidelines

Primary Brand Voice: **Confident, Visionary, Human-Centric**

## Core Tone Dimensions

### 1. Confident (But Not Arrogant)
- Speak with authority and clarity.
- Avoid wishy-washy language (e.g., "we try to", "we hope to"). Use active, decisive verbs.
- **Example:** "We build scalable operations," instead of "We hope to help you build some scale."

### 2. Visionary (But Grounded)
- Paint a picture of the future. Focus on long-term value, venture builders, and operating efficiency.
- Ground the vision in concrete, actionable details.
- **Example:** "Turning licenses into enterprise-grade career paths through structured relationships and knowledge databases."

### 3. Human-Centric (Authentic & Direct)
- Use standard words that people actually use. Avoid excessive corporate jargon or overly stiff vocabulary.
- Build relationships, build trust, and demonstrate persistence.
- Write as a partner, not a cold institution.

## Style Matrix
- **Sentence Length:** Varied. Mix short, punchy statements with longer explanatory sentences to create natural rhythm.
- **Formatting:** Use list formatting and bold headers for quick scanning.
- **Emojis:** Use selectively (maximum 1-2 per piece of short content) to emphasize key concepts (e.g. 🚀, 🎉, 🔧).
