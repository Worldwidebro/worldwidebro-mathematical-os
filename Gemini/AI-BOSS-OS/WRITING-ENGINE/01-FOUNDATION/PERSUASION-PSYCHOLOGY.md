# Persuasion Psychology Frameworks

This guide defines core psychological levers to apply to copy, pitches, and communications.

## 1. Cialdini's 6 Principles of Influence
- **Authority:** Establish credentials, expertise, and research benchmarks early.
- **Reciprocity:** Offer free guides, valuable audits, or initial insights first.
- **Social Proof:** Highlight client milestones, case studies, and venture portfolio size.
- **Scarcity/Urgency:** Limit availability of direct partnership slots or initial beta cohorts.
- **Consistency:** Encourage small, low-friction initial agreements (e.g. subscribing to newsletter).
- **Liking:** Build human-centric rapport through direct, honest building-in-public narratives.

## 2. Emotional Hooks & Triggers
- **Growth & Ambition:** Focus on expansion, building equity, and scaling operations.
- **Security & Trust:** Emphasize risk mitigation, standard operations (SOPs), and compliance checks.
- **Belonging:** Frame interactions as entering the "WinnersCircle" network.
