# Style Guide

This style guide establishes grammatical, punctuation, and structural standards for all output generated by the Writing Engine.

## Grammatical Standards

1. **Active Voice:** Always prefer active voice over passive voice.
   - *Active:* "The AI Boss OS launched the venture."
   - *Passive:* "The venture was launched by the AI Boss OS."
2. **First Person Plural:** Use "we/our" when writing from the company's perspective, and "you/your" when addressing partners or customers.
3. **Serial Comma (Oxford Comma):** Always use the Oxford comma in lists of three or more items.
   - *Correct:* "knowledge, trust, and persistence."
   - *Incorrect:* "knowledge, trust and persistence."

## Formatting Guidelines

- **Headings:** Use `#` for page title, `##` for major sections, and `###` for sub-sections.
- **Lists:** Bullet points are preferred for readability. Keep list items short and consistent in grammar (e.g. all starting with verbs).
- **Highlights:** Use **bold** for key words or metrics, and `code blocks` for system files, command flags, or metrics keys.
