# Email Copywriting Sequences

Use these standard frameworks and formats when drafting email flows.

## 1. PAS Framework (Problem, Agitate, Solve)
Use for cold outreach or direct conversion.
- **Problem:** State a clear problem the recipient faces.
- **Agitate:** Discuss why it is painful, what happens if it's left unresolved, or highlight the cost of inaction.
- **Solve:** Present our venture/product as the clean, systemized solution.

## 2. AIDA Framework (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
Use for nurture emails, newsletters, or announcements.
- **Attention:** Hook the reader immediately (first sentence / subject line).
- **Interest:** Deliver fresh insight, data, or storytelling to keep them reading.
- **Desire:** Show the benefits and transformation they will experience (social proof, milestones).
- **Action:** Clear, singular Call to Action (e.g. click link, reply).
