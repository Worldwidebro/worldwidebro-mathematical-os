## 2. Operating Company (OPCO) Guidelines
Every OPCO functions as a sector-specific subdivision of the ecosystem.
- **Independence**: OPCOs must separate data and execution. Capabilities must be registered within `sector_registry.yaml` and loaded via IZA OS.
- **Reporting**: OPCOs must expose automated KPI endpoints reporting YTD revenue, token expenditure, and active agent statuses weekly to the AI Boss Command Center.

---

## 3. Compliance & Regulatory Auditing
- **Auditing Gate**: All campaign scripts, promotional emails, and web assets must pass regulatory compliance checks using the `/api/audit` compliance scraper.
- **Fiduciary Audits**: Capital expenditures, ledger balance updates, and tax calculations are reconciled nightly by the Treasury and Accountant agents under the supervision of the Finance Director agent.
