# MEDIA (MED) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Deliver automated marketing campaign trackers, Lightroom presets, and high-performance video assets (FFmpeg/HyperFrames) to drive customer traffic to all ventures.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **HOSPITALITY**: Provides event spaces, press conferences, and shoot locations.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: Provides video analytics, data scraping APIs, and CDNs.
- **STAFFING**: Provides models, camera operators, editors, and creators.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **ALL 13 SECTORS**: We run lead generation campaigns, film promotional assets, and distribute newsletters.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Campaign**: Venture requests marketing launch → Route to Sourcing (SLA < 24h).
2. **Execute Deliverables**: Edit video assets via automated FFmpeg scripts.
3. **Margin Capture**: 25% service markup on paid ad management and content retainers.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: FFmpeg · HyperFrames · Canva API · Mailchimp
- **Capabilities**: video-rendering, analytics, newsletters, campaign-mgmt

## KPI Targets
- Video Rendering Time: < 30s (average)
- Lead Click-Through: > 4.5%
- Content Output: 50+ assets/day (automated)
