# HEALTHCARE (HEALTH) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Deliver secure patient intake forms, clinic operational audits, and automated scheduling calendars to optimize local healthcare clinics and practices.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **STAFFING**: We rely on STA for certified nursing assistants, medical aides, and clinic staff.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: We need EHR database integrations, HIPAA compliance filters, and system APIs.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides medical-compliant building leases.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **HOSPITALITY**: We provide wellness program oversight and medical catering clearances.
- **OPERATIONS**: We provide corporate health and OSHA safety audits.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Intake Flow**: Patient logs in → Auto-fill intake form → Route to clinic database (SLA < 10 mins).
2. **Staff Clinics**: Delegate nurse schedules and shifts to STA (SLA < 24h).
3. **Margin Capture**: Average gross margin target of 40% on diagnostic workflows.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: PostgreSQL · Stirling-PDF · Next.js · Twilio
- **Capabilities**: compliance, forms, scheduling, data-security

## KPI Targets
- Intake Verification: < 10 mins
- HIPAA Auditing Pass Rate: 100%
- Shift Fill Rate: > 95%
