# FUNDING (FUN) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Source, structure, and deploy capital across the entire network. Acts as the internal bank, VC, and grant writer for all 712 ventures.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **REAL ESTATE**: Deal flow (properties needing refinance/acquisition).
- **CONSTRUCTION**: Project financing opportunities (milestone-based payouts).
- **COMMERCE**: Revenue data for Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) underwriting.
- **COMMUNITY / NON-PROFIT**: Grant opportunities and tax credit pipelines.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: Risk analytics and data infrastructure.
- **OPERATIONS**: SPV filings, legal structuring, and compliance.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **INVESTMENT**: Structured, underwritten deals ready for capital deployment.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Acquisition capital and refinancing loans.
- **CONSTRUCTION**: Project and milestone funding.
- **COMMERCE**: Inventory financing and growth capital.
- **COMMUNITY / NON-PROFIT**: Grant administration and impact capital.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Underwrite**: Complete automated risk assessments in < 72 hours.
2. **Structure**: Formulate SPV/RBF models.
3. **Margin Capture**: 1-2% advisory fee + success fees + exit carry.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: Neo4j · Supabase · Python (Pandas) · Stripe API
- **Capabilities**: debt-equity, underwriting, risk-modeling, spvs

## KPI Targets
- Underwrite Time: < 72 hours
- Capital Deployment: < 14 days
- Advisory Margin: > 1.5% average
- Portfolio IRR: > 20%
