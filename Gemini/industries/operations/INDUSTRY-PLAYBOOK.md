# OPERATIONS (OPS) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Provide centralized, automated back-office services to all 14 sectors, capturing micro-margins at scale through pure administrative arbitrage.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **ALL 13 SECTORS**: We need them to generate administrative, legal, accounting, and HR delegation requests.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: We rely on TECH to automate 90% of our workflows (auto-invoicing, auto-compliance checks).

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **ALL 13 SECTORS**: We provide:
  - Legal contract generation and review
  - Payroll processing and 1099 issuance
  - Permitting and regulatory compliance tracking
  - Centralized accounting and tax preparation

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Request**: Any venture submits `backoffice_support` delegation.
2. **Triage**: Auto-route to correct sub-agent (Legal, Accounting, HR).
3. **Execute**: Process request using automated templates and workflows.
4. **Invoice**: Bill requesting venture a flat **5-10% service fee** on the cost of the service, or a flat monthly retainer.
5. **Margin Capture**: High volume, low-touch. Margin is in the automation delta.

## KPI Targets
- Request Resolution Time: < 24 hours
- Automation Rate: > 85% of tasks handled without human intervention
- Internal Client Satisfaction: > 4.8/5
