# COMMUNITY / NON-PROFIT (COMY) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Build workforce talent pipelines, secure grants/tax credits, and generate community goodwill to accelerate municipal approvals and feed staffing operations.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **STAFFING**: Job placement opportunities for program graduates.
- **CONSTRUCTION**: Apprenticeship training sites.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Housing and program development spaces.
- **FUNDING**: Grant administration and underwriting.
- **MEDIA**: Outreach campaigns and donor acquisition.
- **EDUCATION**: Training curricula.
- **OPERATIONS**: 501(c)(3) filing compliance.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **STAFFING**: Trained, pre-vetted contractor pipelines.
- **FUNDING**: Grants and tax credit opportunities.
- **CONSTRUCTION**: Permitting approvals and apprentice labor.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Housing tax credits and community support.
- **MEDIA**: Impact content stories.
- **INVESTMENT**: Impact investment opportunities.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Source Grants**: Identify opportunities and delegate administration to Funding.
2. **Talent Output**: Place graduates in Staffing contracts.
3. **Margin Capture**: 5-10% grant admin fees + placement markup margins.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: Greenhouse · WordPress · Stripe Donors · n8n
- **Capabilities**: grant-tracking, workforce-onboarding, outreach

## KPI Targets
- Grants Secured: > $2M/year per non-profit
- Graduate Job Placement: > 80% within 90 days
- Permitting Acceleration: > 30% faster than average
- Social ROI: > 3x
