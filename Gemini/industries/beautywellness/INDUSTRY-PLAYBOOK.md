# BEAUTY & WELLNESS (BTY) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Deliver automated salon booking systems, stylist intake forms, and customer profile portals to scale wellness practices and salons.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **STAFFING**: We rely on STA for licensed stylists, therapists, and salon receptionists.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides lease structures for salon spaces and wellness suites.
- **HEALTHCARE**: Provides medical licensing oversight and clinical clearances.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **HOSPITALITY**: We provide wellness packages and corporate massage benefits.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Lead**: Customer requests appointment → Route to salon calendar (SLA < 5 mins).
2. **Staff Staffing**: Request licensed stylist from STA (SLA < 24h).
3. **Margin Capture**: Gross margin target of 30% on services and 15% on retail product sales.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: React · SQLite · Stripe · Twilio API
- **Capabilities**: booking, customer-profiles, notifications, checkout

## KPI Targets
- Rebooking Rate: > 70%
- Booking Calendar Utilization: > 80%
- Customer Retention: > 85%
