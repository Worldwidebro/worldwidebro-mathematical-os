# CONSTRUCTION (CON) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Execute physical projects (renovations, builds) using AI-dispatched labor, creating tangible assets that feed the Real Estate and Financial sectors.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **STAFFING**: We rely 100% on STA for vetted contractors. We do not hire externally unless STA fails SLA.
- **FINANCIAL**: We need project financing or milestone funding for large builds.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides the properties that need renovation/turnover.
- **OPS**: Handles permitting (LUESA), insurance, and subcontractor legal agreements.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **REAL ESTATE**: We deliver completed, rent-ready assets for RE to manage.
- **STAFFING**: We provide consistent, high-volume work for STA contractors.
- **FINANCIAL**: We provide tangible, appraised assets that FIN can use as collateral.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Acquire Project**: Receive delegation from RE or external lead.
2. **Delegate Labor**: Immediately create `labor_sourcing` delegation to STA.
3. **Execute**: Manage project, track milestones in Knowledge Graph.
4. **Handoff to RE**: Upon completion, create `property_management` delegation to RE within 24 hours.
5. **Margin Capture**: 25-35% on total project value (including STA labor markup).

## KPI Targets
- On-Time Completion: > 85%
- Project Gross Margin: > 25%
- STA Delegation Rate: 100% of labor roles
