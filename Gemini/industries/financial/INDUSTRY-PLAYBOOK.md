# FINANCIAL (FIN) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Structure capital, analyze risk, and deploy funding to the highest-arbitrage opportunities within the Worldwidebro network.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides tangible, appraised deal flow (properties needing acquisition/refinance).
- **CONSTRUCTION**: Provides project financing opportunities with clear milestone payouts.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: Provides underwriting models, data scraping, and risk analysis APIs.
- **OPS**: Provides legal structuring, SPV formation, and regulatory compliance.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **INVESTMENT**: We hand off fully structured, underwritten deals ready for capital deployment.
- **REAL ESTATE/CONSTRUCTION**: We provide the capital they need to execute, taking an advisory fee and spread.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Deal Flow**: From RE or CON via `/network/opportunities`.
2. **Underwrite**: Run automated financial models, risk assessment, and collateral verification.
3. **Structure**: Create debt/equity mix, form SPV (via OPS).
4. **Handoff to Investment**: Create `capital_deployment` delegation to INVESTMENT sector.
5. **Margin Capture**: 1-2% advisory fee + 0.5-1% success fee on capital deployed.

## KPI Targets
- Deal Underwriting Time: < 72 hours
- Capital Deployment Velocity: < 14 days from structuring
- Advisory Margin: > 1.5% average
