# HOSPITALITY (HOSP) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Deliver high-efficiency food production, commercial kitchen layout structures, and click-and-collect online ordering storefronts to scale localized food ventures.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **STAFFING**: We rely on STA for servers, prep cooks, and kitchen staff.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides premium storefront locations and commercial kitchen leases.
- **MEDIA**: Drives local customer traffic and promotional marketing.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **MEDIA**: We host promotional events and product launches at our venues.
- **BEAUTY WELLNESS**: We provide wellness catering and clean-eating menus.
- **TRANSPORTATION**: We provide local meal kits and dispatch routes.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Request**: Events or catering requested → Route via `/network/opportunities`.
2. **Staff Kitchen**: Delegate shift staffing to STA (SLA < 24h).
3. **Execute Orders**: Process through the click-and-collect Medusa dashboard.
4. **Margin Capture**: Average gross margin target of 35% on all events and orders.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: MedusaJS · Stirling-PDF · Stripe · SQLite
- **Capabilities**: menu-loaders, inventory, ticketing, scheduling

## KPI Targets
- Ticket Resolution: < 15 mins
- Order Prep Speed: < 10 mins (average)
- Kitchen Waste Ratio: < 5%
