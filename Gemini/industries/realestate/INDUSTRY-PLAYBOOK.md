# REAL ESTATE (RE) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Acquire, manage, and optimize physical assets, serving as the bridge between physical construction and financial capital.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **CONSTRUCTION**: We rely on CON to renovate and maintain our properties to maximize value.
- **STAFFING**: We rely on STA to provide property managers, leasing agents, and maintenance staff.
- **FINANCIAL**: We need acquisition capital, refinancing, and cash flow management.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: We need prop-tech dashboards for tenant portals and maintenance tracking.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **FINANCIAL**: We source off-market or value-add deals and hand them to FIN to structure.
- **INVESTMENT**: We provide stabilized, cash-flowing assets for capital deployment.
- **CONSTRUCTION**: We provide a steady pipeline of renovation and maintenance projects.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Acquire/Receive Asset**: From CON (completed project) or external market.
2. **Staff Asset**: Delegate `property_management` role to STA.
3. **Identify Capital Need**: If property needs refinance or acquisition needs funding, create `deal_sourcing` delegation to FIN.
4. **Margin Capture**: 8-12% of monthly rent + 20-30% markup on maintenance coordination.

## KPI Targets
- Occupancy Rate: > 95%
- Deal Flow to FIN: 5+ qualified deals/quarter
- Maintenance Resolution Time: < 48 hours
