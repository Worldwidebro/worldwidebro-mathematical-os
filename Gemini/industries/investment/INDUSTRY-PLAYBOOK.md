# INVESTMENT (INV) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Deploy stage-gate capital and manage algorithmic trading vaults to grow holding company assets and maximize yields on Layer 1-3 profits.

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **FINANCIAL**: Provides underwriting reports, SPVs, and advisory deals.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: Provides portfolio analytics, risk dashboards, and security scans.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **ALL 13 SECTORS**: We provide seed-stage funding, exit strategies, and growth capital.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Receive Deal**: FIN delivers structured SPV deal → INV runs final due diligence.
2. **Deploy (SLA < 7 days)**: Transfer funds to target venture account.
3. **Margin Capture**: 15-20% carry on deal exits plus 1-2% monthly management fees.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: Neo4j · Postgres · Python (NumPy/Pandas) · PyPortfolioOpt
- **Capabilities**: equity, risk-modeling, carry, trading-vaults

## KPI Targets
- Portfolio ROI: > 25% annually
- Time-to-Fund: < 7 days
- Fund Concentration Risk: < 30% in any single venture
