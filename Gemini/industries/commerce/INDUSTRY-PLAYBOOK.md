# COMMERCE (COM) Industry Playbook

## Core Mission
Launch, scale, and optimize direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands and retail operations using internal network arbitrage (marketing, logistics, financing).

## Who We Need (Inputs)
- **MEDIA**: For customer acquisition, ad creative, and SEO.
- **TRANSPORTATION**: For last-mile delivery and warehouse logistics.
- **STAFFING**: For fulfillment center workers and customer service reps.
- **TECHNOLOGY**: For Shopify/custom storefronts and inventory management.

## Who We Serve (Outputs)
- **FINANCIAL**: Provides revenue-based financing (RBF) or inventory funding.
- **REAL ESTATE**: Provides warehouse space or pop-up retail locations.
- **INVESTMENT**: Provides acquisition targets for e-commerce roll-up strategies.

## Delegation Handoff Rules
1. **Request Sourcing**: Sourcing product lists → Route via `/network/opportunities`.
2. **Setup Shop**: Tech deploys custom storefront (SLA < 48 hours).
3. **Margin Capture**: Average gross margin target of 30-40% on sales through optimized fulfillment.

## Technology Stack
- **Primary**: MedusaJS · Next.js · Stripe · TailwindCSS
- **Capabilities**: storefront, inventory, logistics-sync, payments

## KPI Targets
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): < 20% compared to standalone peers
- Order Processing: < 5 mins
- Fulfillment SLA: < 24h
