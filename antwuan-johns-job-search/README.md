## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Customize Resume for Each Role**
   - Highlight relevant experience (lead experience for foreman roles, etc.)
   - Use keywords from job description
   - Lead with strongest match

2. **Research Companies Before Applying**
   - Read Glassdoor reviews
   - Know their recent projects
   - Prepare specific questions

3. **Follow Up Strategically**
   - Wait 1 week, then email recruiter
   - Reference specific role & date applied
   - Keep it brief and professional

4. **Track Everything**
   - Date applied
   - Recruiter contact info
   - Application status
   - Interview dates
   - Follow-up dates
