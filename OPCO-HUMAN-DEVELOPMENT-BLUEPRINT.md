## Layer 1: Identity

**Ideal Founder:**
```
[SECTOR] founder who:
├─ Clear mission beyond money
├─ Deep sector understanding  
├─ Values aligned with sector
├─ Wants to build legacy
└─ Articulates unique POV
```

**Assessment (First Month):**
- Who are you in this sector? (mission)
- Why this sector? (genuine vs. financial)
- Your competitive edge?
- What will you be known for?

**OPCO Role:** Identity clarity sessions + 1-page charter document

## Layers 2-5: Internal Capacity (Emotional + Cognitive + Intuitive + Energy)

**Sector-Specific Challenges:**
- [ ] Document 3-5 main stressors in [sector]
- [ ] Map to Human OS layers (which layer fails under stress?)
- [ ] Create training curriculum

**Development Milestones:**
- Month 1-2: Emotional foundation
- Month 3-4: Cognitive tools
- Month 5-6: Intuition training
- Month 7-8: Energy management

**Success Indicators:**
- ✓ Makes sound decisions under pressure
- ✓ Handles stress without breaking
- ✓ Guts feelings + verified
- ✓ Sustainable pace (no burnout)

## Layer 6: Frequency (Observable Reliability)

**What the sector sees:**
- On-site/available presence
- Communication responsiveness
- Execution consistency
- Quality standards

**Measurement:**
- "Can the team predict when you'll be here?"
- "Do clients know what to expect?"
- "Do subcontractors/partners trust your consistency?"

---

## Layer 8: Creativity (Innovation + Problem-Solving)

**Safe Innovation Framework:**
1. Observe: What's slow/expensive/frustrating?
2. Hypothesize: "What if we tried X?"
3. Experiment: Small safe test
4. Measure: Cost/quality/safety impact?
5. Share: Tell peer founders

**Development:**
- [ ] 1 experiment/quarter (documented)
- [ ] Share learning with peers
- [ ] Fail safely (learn from it)
- [ ] Success story (innovation that stuck)

---

## Layer 9: Character (Who You Are Under Pressure)

**Sector-Specific Character Tests:**
```
Test 1: [Sector Pressure Scenario]
├─ Low Character: [Wrong response]
└─ High Character: [Integrity response]

Test 2: [Sector Pressure Scenario]
├─ Low Character: [Wrong response]
└─ High Character: [Integrity response]

[Add 3-5 sector tests]
```

**Measured By:**
- How do you handle mistakes?
- Do you cut corners when nobody watches?
- Do you tell the truth about timelines/costs?
- How do you bounce back?

**Success:** "Even when it cost me, I did the right thing."

---

## Layer 10: Legacy (What Outlasts You)

**What you're building:**
```
Individual: Your reputation/craft
Community: Jobs created, impact
Industry: Standards raised, others mentored
Worldwidebro: Model that spreads
```

**Development:**
- Year 1-2: Build with intention (things that last)
- Year 2-3: Develop others (mentor founders, share lessons)
- Year 3+: Think generationally (industry impact)
