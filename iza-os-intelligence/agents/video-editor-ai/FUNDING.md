## Buy-Borrow-Die Strategy (Long-Term Wealth)

Once equity value is established:
1. **Build equity** — profitable ventures increase in valuation
2. **Borrow against equity** — pledge business equity as collateral for low-interest loans
3. **Never sell** — selling triggers capital gains tax; borrowing does not
4. **Deduct interest** — business loan interest is tax-deductible

This is how Bezos, Musk, and Zuckerberg access billions without paying income tax.
